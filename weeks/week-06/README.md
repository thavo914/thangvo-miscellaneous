- Explain how contracts prevent breakage and how you manage evolution.
- Use 6–8 chunks around versioning, compatibility, and deprecation.
- Tell a clear story about preventing or fixing a contract breach.

## Inputs (choose 1–2)

- Article on data contracts; schema registry practices
