# Week 08 – Incident postmortem & stakeholder comms

## Outcomes (by end of week)

- Deliver a clear, blameless postmortem and action plan.
- Use 6–8 chunks around root cause, detection, remediation, prevention.
- Handle tough stakeholder questions calmly.

## Inputs (choose 1–2)

- Article on blameless postmortems
- Internal incident doc (sanitize details if needed)

## Chunk Bank (8–12)

- root cause; contributing factors; blast radius
- detection time; MTTD/MTTR; alert thresholds
- remediation; rollback; backfill; validation
- preventive action; ownership; due date
- communication plan; stakeholder updates; status page
- risk assessment; residual risk; guardrails
- lessons learned; follow-up tasks
- accountability without blame

## Grammar/Pronunciation Focus

- Grammar: Passive vs active voice choice for accountability
- Pronunciation: Calm, measured delivery; clear pauses

## Daily Plan

- Mon: Input + shadowing; chunks to SRS
- Tue: PREP: postmortem structure; 4-3-2 drill
- Wed: Role-play: tough stakeholder Q&A
- Thu: Writing-to-speaking: postmortem summary → 2-min talk
- Fri: Simulation: Exec review; action items
- Sat: Retell without notes; record
- Sun: Light review; plan next week

## Exercises & Prompts

- Problem–Solution–Impact: Incident narrative with action items
- STAR: A tough incident and what changed
- Role-play prompt:

  ```text
  You are an executive stakeholder asking for accountability and timelines. Topic: Incident postmortem & stakeholder comms. Press on owner, dates, and risks. After 6–8 minutes, give me 3 reformulations.
  ```
