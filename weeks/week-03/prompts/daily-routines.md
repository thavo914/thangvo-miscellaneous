# Full practice conversation (B1) — Daily Routines and Productivity

Direction: Read aloud in a warm, conversational tone.

Speaker 2: How do you usually start your day? Do you have a morning routine?
Speaker 1: I try to wake up early and have a quiet hour before work. I make coffee, check my calendar, and plan my top three priorities for the day.
Speaker 2: That sounds organized. What time do you usually wake up?
Speaker 1: Around 6:30 AM. It gives me time to ease into the day instead of rushing straight to work.
Speaker 2: Do you exercise in the morning?
Speaker 1: Sometimes, but I prefer to exercise in the evening. I'm not a morning person when it comes to working out.
Speaker 2: What about breakfast? Do you eat at home or grab something on the go?
Speaker 1: I usually eat at home - oatmeal with fruit or eggs. It's cheaper and healthier than buying breakfast every day.
Speaker 2: How do you stay productive during the workday?
Speaker 1: I use the Pomodoro technique - 25 minutes of focused work, then a 5-minute break. It helps me stay focused and avoid burnout.
Speaker 2: That's interesting. What do you do during your breaks?
Speaker 1: I get up and walk around, stretch, or just look out the window. Anything to give my brain a rest from the screen.
Speaker 2: How do you handle distractions when you're trying to focus?
Speaker 1: I put my phone in another room and close unnecessary browser tabs. Sometimes I use noise-canceling headphones if it's noisy.
Speaker 2: What's your evening routine like?
Speaker 1: I try to finish work by 6 PM, then cook dinner, maybe watch a show, and read before bed. I aim to be in bed by 10:30.
Speaker 2: Do you plan your meals ahead of time?
Speaker 1: I plan dinners for the week on Sunday, but breakfast and lunch are pretty routine. It saves time and reduces decision fatigue.
Speaker 2: How do you wind down before bed?
Speaker 1: I avoid screens for the last hour, read a book, and sometimes do some light stretching. It helps me fall asleep faster.

## Optional reformulations (use if you want to sound a bit more polished)

- "I find that starting the day with a quiet hour helps me feel more prepared for whatever comes up."
- "The Pomodoro technique really helps me maintain focus throughout the day."
- "Planning meals ahead saves time and reduces the stress of deciding what to cook every day."
- "Having a consistent evening routine helps me sleep better and feel more rested."

---

## Key vocabulary for daily routines

### Morning activities
- **Wake up**: to stop sleeping and become conscious
- **Ease into**: to start something gradually and comfortably
- **Rush**: to move or act quickly, often in a hurry
- **Routine**: a regular pattern of behavior or activities

### Productivity techniques
- **Pomodoro technique**: a time management method using focused work periods
- **Stay focused**: to maintain concentration on a task
- **Avoid burnout**: to prevent becoming exhausted from overwork
- **Decision fatigue**: tiredness from making too many decisions

### Evening activities
- **Wind down**: to gradually relax and prepare for sleep
- **Fall asleep**: to begin sleeping
- **Consistent**: happening regularly and predictably
- **Rested**: feeling refreshed and energetic
