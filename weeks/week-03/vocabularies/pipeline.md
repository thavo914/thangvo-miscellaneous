# Pipeline

## IPA
/ˈpaɪplaɪn/

## Definition
A complete system or process that moves data from one stage to another, from initial collection through final processing and delivery.

## Part of Speech
Noun

## Usage & Examples
- You need to understand the whole **pipeline** - from ingestion to consumption.
- The data **pipeline** processes millions of records every day.
- When something breaks in the **pipeline**, we have to trace through each step.
- Building a reliable **pipeline** takes time but saves headaches later.
- The **pipeline** includes data cleaning, transformation, and loading steps.

## Synonyms & Related Terms
- **Data flow**: the movement of data through a system
- **Process**: a series of actions or steps
- **Workflow**: the sequence of tasks that need to be completed
- **System**: a set of connected parts working together
- **Infrastructure**: the basic framework that supports operations
