# Root cause

## IPA
/rut kɔz/

## Definition
The original or fundamental reason why a problem occurs; the underlying cause that leads to other issues.

## Part of Speech
Noun phrase

## Usage & Examples
- The fix was simple, but finding the **root cause** was like detective work.
- We need to identify the **root cause** of the data quality issues, not just fix the symptoms.
- The **root cause** analysis showed that the problem started in the data ingestion process.
- Instead of just fixing the immediate problem, we should address the **root cause**.
- Understanding the **root cause** helps prevent similar problems in the future.

## Synonyms & Related Terms
- **Underlying cause**: the basic reason behind a problem
- **Source**: where something originates or comes from
- **Origin**: the beginning or cause of something
- **Fundamental issue**: the basic problem that needs to be solved
- **Core problem**: the main issue that causes other problems
