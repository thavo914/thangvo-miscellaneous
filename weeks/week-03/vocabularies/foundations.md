# Foundations

## IPA
/faʊnˈdeɪʃənz/

## Definition
The basic structure, systems, and processes that support everything else and make future work easier and more reliable.

## Part of Speech
Noun (plural)

## Usage & Examples
- The easy parts are only possible because we built solid **foundations** over time.
- We spent months setting up monitoring, testing, and error handling as our **foundations**.
- Without good **foundations**, every change would take weeks instead of days.
- It's like building a house - once the **foundations** are solid, adding rooms is much easier.
- Our data **foundations** include automated testing, monitoring, and error handling.

## Synonyms & Related Terms
- **Infrastructure**: the underlying systems and tools
- **Groundwork**: the preparation work done before starting a project
- **Base**: the fundamental support for something
- **Framework**: the basic structure that supports other parts
- **Core**: the central, most important part of something
