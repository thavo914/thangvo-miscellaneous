# Shadow

## IPA
/ˈʃædoʊ/

## Definition
To follow and observe someone more experienced in their work, learning by watching how they perform their job or handle situations.

## Part of Speech
Verb

## Usage & Examples
- New team members should **shadow experienced people** to learn best practices.
- I spent a week **shadowing** the senior data engineer to understand the deployment process.
- **Shadowing** experienced colleagues is a great way to learn company-specific workflows.
- The onboarding program includes **shadowing** sessions with different team members.
- When you **shadow** someone, pay attention to how they communicate with stakeholders.

## Synonyms & Related Terms
- **Observe**: to watch someone work without interfering
- **Follow**: to go along with someone to learn their routine
- **Mentor**: someone who guides and teaches you
- **Apprentice**: someone learning a skill from an expert
- **Onboarding**: the process of training new employees
