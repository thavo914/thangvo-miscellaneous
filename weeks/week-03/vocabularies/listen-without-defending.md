# Listen without defending

## IPA
/ˈlɪsən wɪˈðaʊt dɪˈfɛndɪŋ/

## Definition
To hear feedback or criticism without immediately trying to justify your actions, explain why you did something, or argue against the feedback.

## Part of Speech
Verb phrase

## Usage & Examples
- When receiving feedback, it's important to **listen without defending** to truly understand the other person's perspective.
- I try to **listen without defending** when colleagues point out areas for improvement.
- **Listening without defending** shows that you value the other person's input and are open to growth.
- The key to receiving constructive feedback is to **listen without defending** and ask clarifying questions instead.
- When someone gives you negative feedback, **listen without defending** first, then ask questions to understand better.

## Synonyms & Related Terms
- **Open-minded**: willing to consider new ideas or feedback
- **Receptive**: ready to accept or consider suggestions
- **Non-defensive**: not trying to protect yourself from criticism
- **Active listening**: fully concentrating on what someone is saying
- **Growth mindset**: believing you can improve through effort and feedback
