# Conflict Resolution

## IPA
/ˈkɑnflɪkt ˌrɛzəˈluʃən/

## Definition
The process of finding a peaceful solution to a disagreement or dispute between people or groups.

## Part of Speech
Noun phrase

## Usage & Examples
- **Conflict resolution** skills are essential for project managers.
- We used **conflict resolution** techniques to resolve the disagreement about project priorities.
- The team attended a **conflict resolution** workshop to improve communication.
- Good **conflict resolution** involves listening to all sides before making decisions.

## Synonyms & Related Terms
- **Dispute resolution**: formal process of solving disagreements
- **Problem solving**: finding solutions to difficult situations
- **Mediation**: helping two parties reach agreement
- **Negotiation**: discussing to reach a compromise
- **Consensus building**: getting everyone to agree on a solution
