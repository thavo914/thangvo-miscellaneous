# Routine

## IPA
/ruˈtin/

## Definition
A regular pattern of behavior or activities that you do regularly, often at the same time or in the same way.

## Part of Speech
Noun

## Usage & Examples
- I try to wake up early and have a quiet hour before work as part of my morning **routine**.
- Having a consistent evening **routine** helps me sleep better and feel more rested.
- My daily **routine** includes checking emails, planning tasks, and taking regular breaks.
- Breaking out of your usual **routine** can be refreshing and help you discover new things.
- Good **routines** help you stay organized and reduce decision fatigue.

## Synonyms & Related Terms
- **Habit**: a behavior that you do regularly without thinking
- **Schedule**: a plan of activities and when they happen
- **Pattern**: a regular way that something happens
- **Custom**: a traditional way of doing something
- **Practice**: a regular activity that you do to improve a skill
