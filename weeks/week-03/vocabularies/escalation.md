# Escalation

## IPA
/ˌɛskəˈleɪʃən/

## Definition
The process of bringing a problem, issue, or decision to a higher level of authority or management for resolution.

## Part of Speech
Noun

## Usage & Examples
- When the team couldn't agree on the project scope, we had to use **escalation** to get a decision from senior management.
- The customer service rep used **escalation** to transfer the complaint to a supervisor.
- We try to resolve conflicts at the team level before considering **escalation**.
- The project manager decided on **escalation** when the technical disagreement couldn't be resolved locally.

## Synonyms & Related Terms
- **Escalate**: to raise an issue to a higher level (verb)
- **Refer up**: to pass a decision to someone with more authority
- **Management review**: when senior leaders examine an issue
- **Appeal**: to request a higher authority to reconsider a decision
- **Chain of command**: the hierarchy of authority in an organization
