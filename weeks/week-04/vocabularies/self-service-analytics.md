# Self-Service Analytics

## Definition
Enabling business users to access and analyze data independently without requiring technical assistance from data engineers or analysts.

## Example Sentences
- "Well-documented dbt models enable self-service analytics across our organization."
- "Self-service analytics reduces the burden on our data team and empowers business users."
- "We implemented comprehensive documentation to support self-service analytics initiatives."

## Related Terms
- **Business intelligence**: Tools and processes for data analysis
- **Data democratization**: Making data accessible to all users
- **User empowerment**: Giving users tools to make decisions
- **Documentation**: Clear explanations of data models and usage

## Usage Context
- Explaining business value of dbt to stakeholders
- Discussing team efficiency improvements
- Planning documentation strategies
- Presenting ROI of data initiatives
