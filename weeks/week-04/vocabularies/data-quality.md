# Data Quality

## Definition
The overall reliability, accuracy, and consistency of data that determines its fitness for use in business decisions and analytics.

## Example Sentences
- "Our dbt testing strategy ensures high data quality across all downstream applications."
- "Poor data quality led to incorrect business insights and poor decision-making."
- "We implemented comprehensive testing to maintain data quality standards."

## Related Terms
- **Data validation**: Checking data against defined rules
- **Data integrity**: Maintaining accuracy and consistency
- **Data governance**: Framework for managing data quality
- **Data testing**: Automated verification of data conditions

## Usage Context
- Explaining testing strategies to stakeholders
- Discussing business impact of data issues
- Presenting quality improvement initiatives
- Risk assessment discussions
