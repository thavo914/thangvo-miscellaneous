# dbt Best Practices

## Overview
Practice explaining dbt concepts and benefits to different audiences, from technical team members to business stakeholders.

## Key Topics to Cover
- **Model Design**: Clean, modular structure with clear dependencies
- **Testing Strategy**: Comprehensive testing for data quality assurance
- **Documentation**: Clear descriptions and business context
- **Performance**: Optimization strategies and materialization choices
- **Version Control**: Git-based workflow and collaboration

## Speaking Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Technical Team Meeting
**Duration:** 5-7 minutes
**Audience:** Data engineers and analysts
**Goal:** Explain dbt best practices for new team members

**Key Points:**
- Project structure and organization
- Naming conventions and standards
- Testing and documentation requirements
- Code review process

**Sample Opening:**
"Let me walk you through our dbt best practices. We've established these standards to ensure consistency and maintainability across our project..."

### Scenario 2: Stakeholder Presentation
**Duration:** 3-5 minutes
**Audience:** Business users and managers
**Goal:** Explain how dbt improves data quality and accessibility

**Key Points:**
- Business value of standardized data models
- Self-service analytics enablement
- Data quality improvements
- Cost savings and efficiency gains

**Sample Opening:**
"dbt transforms how we approach data transformation. Let me show you how this tool delivers real business value..."

### Scenario 3: Project Planning Session
**Duration:** 8-10 minutes
**Audience:** Project team and stakeholders
**Goal:** Plan dbt implementation with best practices

**Key Points:**
- Implementation phases and timeline
- Resource requirements and team structure
- Success metrics and KPIs
- Risk mitigation strategies

## Practice Exercises

### Exercise 1: Best Practice Explanation
**Task:** Explain one dbt best practice in detail
**Time:** 2-3 minutes
**Focus:** Clear explanation with examples

**Choose from:**
- Testing strategy
- Documentation standards
- Model organization
- Performance optimization

### Exercise 2: Benefit Communication
**Task:** Explain the business benefits of dbt best practices
**Time:** 2-3 minutes
**Focus:** Business value and ROI

**Structure:**
- Problem solved
- Solution implemented
- Benefits achieved
- Metrics and impact

### Exercise 3: Implementation Planning
**Task:** Plan how to implement a specific best practice
**Time:** 3-4 minutes
**Focus:** Practical steps and timeline

**Include:**
- Current state assessment
- Implementation steps
- Resource requirements
- Success criteria

## Key Phrases to Practice

### Explaining Benefits
- "By implementing X, we can achieve Y..."
- "The advantage of following this practice is..."
- "This approach provides several benefits, including..."

### Describing Implementation
- "We'll implement this in phases, starting with..."
- "The implementation requires..."
- "Success depends on..."

### Making Recommendations
- "I recommend starting with..."
- "Consider implementing..."
- "The best approach would be..."

## Assessment Criteria

### Content (1-5 scale)
- **Accuracy**: Are the dbt concepts explained correctly?
- **Completeness**: Are all key points covered?
- **Examples**: Are concrete examples provided?
- **Business Value**: Is the business impact clear?

### Delivery (1-5 scale)
- **Clarity**: Is the explanation easy to understand?
- **Structure**: Is the presentation well-organized?
- **Confidence**: Does the speaker appear knowledgeable?
- **Engagement**: Is the audience interested and involved?

### Language (1-5 scale)
- **Vocabulary**: Appropriate technical and business terms?
- **Grammar**: Correct sentence structures?
- **Pronunciation**: Clear technical terminology?
- **Fluency**: Natural speaking pace and flow?
