# dbt Project Planning

## Overview
Practice presenting dbt project plans and implementation strategies to stakeholders, team members, and project managers.

## Key Planning Areas

### 1. Project Structure and Organization
- **Models**: How models will be organized and named
- **Testing**: Testing strategy and coverage requirements
- **Documentation**: Documentation standards and tools
- **Version Control**: Git workflow and branching strategy

### 2. Implementation Phases
- **Phase 1**: Core infrastructure and basic models
- **Phase 2**: Testing and documentation implementation
- **Phase 3**: Advanced features and optimization
- **Phase 4**: Monitoring and maintenance

### 3. Resource Requirements
- **Team Structure**: Roles and responsibilities
- **Skills Required**: Technical competencies needed
- **Timeline**: Realistic project milestones
- **Budget**: Cost considerations and ROI

## Speaking Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Stakeholder Presentation
**Duration:** 5-7 minutes
**Audience:** Business stakeholders and managers
**Goal:** Get approval for dbt implementation project

**Key Points to Cover:**
- Business value and ROI
- Implementation timeline and phases
- Resource requirements and costs
- Risk assessment and mitigation

**Sample Opening:**
"Let me present our dbt implementation plan. This project will transform how we handle data transformation and deliver significant business value..."

### Scenario 2: Team Kickoff Meeting
**Duration:** 8-10 minutes
**Audience:** Data engineering team
**Goal:** Align team on project goals and approach

**Key Points to Cover:**
- Project objectives and success criteria
- Technical approach and architecture
- Team roles and responsibilities
- Development workflow and standards

**Sample Opening:**
"Today we're kicking off our dbt implementation project. Let me walk you through our approach and how we'll work together..."

### Scenario 3: Project Status Update
**Duration:** 3-5 minutes
**Audience:** Project managers and stakeholders
**Goal:** Provide progress update and address challenges

**Key Points to Cover:**
- Current status and progress
- Achievements and milestones
- Challenges and roadblocks
- Next steps and timeline

**Sample Opening:**
"Let me update you on our dbt project progress. We've made significant strides and I'd like to share our current status..."

## Planning Framework

### 1. Current State Assessment
**Questions to Address:**
- What data transformation processes exist currently?
- What are the pain points and inefficiencies?
- What tools and technologies are already in place?
- What is the team's current skill level?

**Sample Language:**
"Our current data transformation process has several inefficiencies. We're spending 4 hours daily on manual processes that could be automated..."

### 2. Future State Vision
**Questions to Address:**
- What will the ideal data transformation workflow look like?
- What benefits will we achieve?
- How will this improve business outcomes?
- What metrics will measure success?

**Sample Language:**
"Our future state will enable self-service analytics, reduce processing time by 80%, and provide real-time data quality monitoring..."

### 3. Gap Analysis
**Questions to Address:**
- What capabilities do we need to develop?
- What skills does the team need to acquire?
- What infrastructure changes are required?
- What processes need to be established?

**Sample Language:**
"To bridge this gap, we need to develop testing expertise, establish CI/CD processes, and create comprehensive documentation standards..."

### 4. Implementation Strategy
**Questions to Address:**
- How will we phase the implementation?
- What are the critical success factors?
- How will we manage risks and dependencies?
- What support and training will be needed?

**Sample Language:**
"We'll implement this in three phases, starting with core infrastructure, then adding testing and documentation, and finally optimizing for performance..."

## Practice Exercises

### Exercise 1: Project Overview
**Task:** Present a 2-minute project overview to stakeholders
**Focus:** Clear objectives and business value
**Structure:** Problem, Solution, Benefits, Timeline

### Exercise 2: Implementation Planning
**Task:** Explain implementation phases in 3-4 minutes
**Focus:** Logical progression and dependencies
**Structure:** Phase 1, Phase 2, Phase 3, Success Criteria

### Exercise 3: Resource Planning
**Task:** Present resource requirements in 2-3 minutes
**Focus:** Team needs and skill development
**Structure:** Current State, Required Skills, Training Plan, Timeline

### Exercise 4: Risk Assessment
**Task:** Discuss project risks and mitigation in 2-3 minutes
**Focus:** Realistic assessment and practical solutions
**Structure:** Risk Identification, Impact Assessment, Mitigation Strategies

## Assessment Criteria

### Content Quality (1-5 scale)
- **Clarity**: Is the plan easy to understand?
- **Completeness**: Are all key areas covered?
- **Realism**: Are timelines and resources realistic?
- **Business Value**: Is the business case clear?

### Presentation Skills (1-5 scale)
- **Structure**: Is the presentation well-organized?
- **Confidence**: Does the speaker appear knowledgeable?
- **Engagement**: Is the audience interested?
- **Clarity**: Is the message easy to follow?

### Planning Quality (1-5 scale)
- **Feasibility**: Is the plan realistic and achievable?
- **Risk Management**: Are risks properly identified and addressed?
- **Resource Planning**: Are resource requirements realistic?
- **Success Metrics**: Are success criteria clearly defined?

## Common Challenges and Solutions

### Challenge 1: Unrealistic Timelines
**Solution:** Break down into phases, set realistic milestones, build in buffer time

### Challenge 2: Resource Constraints
**Solution:** Prioritize critical features, phase implementation, leverage existing skills

### Challenge 3: Stakeholder Resistance
**Solution:** Focus on business value, provide concrete examples, address concerns directly

### Challenge 4: Technical Complexity
**Solution:** Start simple, build incrementally, provide training and support
