Our pipeline is end-to-end from ingestion to consumption. We ingest events from Kafka topics and batch files from S3. A Flink job performs basic validation and writes to a bronze layer. Airflow orchestrates nightly compaction into Parquet, partitioned by date and customer. We enforce data contracts using a schema registry. A Spark job builds the silver tables with deduplication and handling of late-arriving data via watermarks. Quality checks gate promotion to gold with SLAs monitored in our observability tool. Downstream, the semantic layer exposes curated marts in BigQuery. The main bottleneck is skew during large joins, which we mitigate with salting and AQE. We optimize for reliability at the expense of a small freshness delay.
