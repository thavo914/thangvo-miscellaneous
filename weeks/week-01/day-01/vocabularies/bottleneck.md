# bottleneck

1. (IPA): /ˈbɒtlnek/

2. Definition

- The slowest step or constrained resource that limits overall throughput or increases latency in a system.

1. Part of Speech

- Noun

1. Usage & 5 Examples

- The shuffle join is our primary bottleneck during peak hours.
- Skew on customer_id created a performance bottleneck.
- We removed a storage bottleneck by compacting small files.
- Monitoring revealed the orchestration bottleneck was retries.
- Fixing the bottleneck cut end-to-end runtime by 30%.

1. Synonyms & Related Terms

- Synonyms: constraint, chokepoint, hot spot
- Related: skew, shuffle, optimization, scaling
