# watermark

1. (IPA): /ˈwɔːtəmɑːk/ (UK), /ˈwɔtɚˌmɑrk/ (US)

2. Definition

- A threshold indicating how far behind event time the system will accept data; used to manage lateness and trigger completeness.

1. Part of Speech

- Noun

1. Usage & 5 Examples

- We advanced the watermark after verifying completeness.
- A looser watermark reduced false negatives but increased latency.
- The watermark configuration depends on source variability.
- Downstream tables publish only after crossing the watermark.
- Watermark shifts are audited alongside SLAs.

1. Synonyms & Related Terms

- Synonyms: lateness threshold, completeness marker
- Related: event time, late-arriving data, windowing, SLAs
