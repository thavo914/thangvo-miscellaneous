# schema registry

1. (IPA): /ˈskiːmə ˈredʒɪstri/

2. Definition

- A centralized service that stores and versions schemas, enabling compatibility checks and safe evolution of data formats.

1. Part of Speech

- Noun phrase

1. Usage & 5 Examples

- Producers must register new versions in the schema registry.
- The schema registry blocked an incompatible change.
- Our consumers fetch schemas at read time from the registry.
- We configured backward compatibility in the registry.
- The registry integrates with our data contract checks.

1. Synonyms & Related Terms

- Synonyms: schema store, schema catalog
- Related: Avro/Protobuf, compatibility, data contracts, CI checks
