# optimize for X at the expense of Y

1. (IPA): /ˈɒptɪmaɪz fə ɛks æt ði ɪkˈspens əv waɪ/

2. Definition

- To improve performance on one criterion while knowingly trading off another criterion.

1. Part of Speech

- Verb phrase (construction)

1. Usage & 5 Examples

- We optimize for reliability at the expense of freshness in this pipeline.
- The team optimized for cost at the expense of latency.
- We can optimize for simplicity at the expense of flexibility.
- They optimized for developer speed at the expense of governance.
- We chose to optimize for accuracy at the expense of compute cost.

1. Synonyms & Related Terms

- Synonyms: trade off, prioritize X over Y, balance
- Related: SLAs, non-functional requirements, constraints, trade-offs
