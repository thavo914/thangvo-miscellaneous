# from ingestion to consumption

1. (IPA): /frəm ɪnˈdʒestʃən tə ˌkɒnˈsʌmpʃən/

2. Definition

- Describes the full span of data flow, starting when data enters the system (ingestion) until it is used by end users or applications (consumption).

1. Part of Speech

- Prepositional phrase

1. Usage & 5 Examples

- We manage quality from ingestion to consumption.
- Observability should cover the path from ingestion to consumption.
- Costs are tracked from ingestion to consumption to find waste.
- Access controls apply from ingestion to consumption.
- The contract is valid from ingestion to consumption across teams.

1. Synonyms & Related Terms

- Synonyms: end-to-end, source to sink
- Related: pipeline, producers/consumers, SLAs, lineage
