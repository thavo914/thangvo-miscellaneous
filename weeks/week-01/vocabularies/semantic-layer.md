# semantic layer

1. (IPA): /sɪˈmæntɪk ˈleɪə/

2. Definition

- A modeling layer that provides business-friendly metrics and dimensions, abstracting the underlying tables for analytics.

1. Part of Speech

- Noun phrase

1. Usage & 5 Examples

- Dashboards query the semantic layer instead of raw tables.
- The semantic layer standardizes KPI definitions across teams.
- We added a new dimension to the semantic layer for segmentation.
- Access policies are enforced in the semantic layer.
- The semantic layer sources its data from gold tables.

1. Synonyms & Related Terms

- Synonyms: metrics layer, business layer, semantic model
- Related: marts, BI, dimensions/measures, governance
