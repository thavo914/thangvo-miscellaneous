# end-to-end

1. (IPA): /ˌend tuː ˈend/

2. Definition

- Covering the entire process from the initial input to the final output; spanning all stages of a system or pipeline.

1. Part of Speech

- Adjective (also adverbial phrase)

1. Usage & 5 Examples

- We own an end-to-end pipeline from ingestion to consumption.
- The testing strategy is end-to-end, including contract tests and user validation.
- End-to-end SLAs help align producers and downstream consumers.
- We measured end-to-end latency from event time to dashboard availability.
- An end-to-end view exposed a hidden bottleneck in the join step.

1. Synonyms & Related Terms

- Synonyms: full-stack, holistic, entire, complete
- Related: upstream/downstream, latency, SLA/SLO, pipeline
