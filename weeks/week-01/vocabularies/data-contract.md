# data contract

1. (IPA): /ˈdeɪtə ˈkɒntrækt/

2. Definition

- A formal agreement specifying the schema, semantics, SLAs, and quality guarantees between data producers and consumers.

1. Part of Speech

- Noun phrase

1. Usage & 5 Examples

- The data contract prevents breaking changes from reaching consumers.
- We enforce the data contract in CI using schema checks.
- Breaching the data contract triggers alerts and blocks promotion.
- The contract defines acceptable freshness and null rates.
- We negotiated a contract update before changing the payload.

1. Synonyms & Related Terms

- Synonyms: interface, agreement, schema contract
- Related: schema registry, compatibility, SLAs, validation
