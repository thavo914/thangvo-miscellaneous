# contrastive stress

## IPA
/kənˈtræstɪv stres/

## Definition
A pronunciation technique where specific words are emphasized to show contrast, comparison, or opposition. Used to highlight differences between options and make key points stand out in speech.

## Part of Speech
Noun phrase

## Usage & Examples

**In technical discussions:**
- "**BATCH** processing is cheaper than **STREAMING**."
- "We're choosing **COST** over **SPEED**."
- "**SIMPLE** solutions are better than **COMPLEX** ones."

**In business contexts:**
- "**RELIABILITY** is more important than **FEATURES**."
- "We're prioritizing **TIME** over **PERFECTION**."
- "**BUDGET** constraints limit our **OPTIONS**."

**In presentations:**
- "**BATCH** gives us 4-hour latency, but **STREAMING** provides real-time data."
- "It's not about **TECHNOLOGY**, it's about **BUSINESS NEEDS**."
- "**COST** is critical, **SPEED** is secondary."
