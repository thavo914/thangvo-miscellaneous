# emphasis

## IPA
/ˈemfəsɪs/

## Definition
Special importance, value, or prominence given to something. In speech, it refers to the stress or prominence given to certain words or syllables to make them stand out and convey their importance.

## Part of Speech
Noun

## Usage & Examples

**In technical discussions:**
- "We need to put **emphasis** on cost-effectiveness in our decision."
- "The **emphasis** should be on reliability, not speed."
- "I want to **emphasize** that batch processing is the right choice."

**In business contexts:**
- "The **emphasis** is on getting to market quickly."
- "We're placing **emphasis** on operational simplicity."
- "Let me **emphasize** the key benefits of this approach."

**In presentations:**
- "The **emphasis** of my talk is on trade-offs and recommendations."
- "I want to **emphasize** three main points today."
- "The **emphasis** should be on business value, not technical features."
