# cleft sentence

## IPA
/kleft ˈsentəns/

## Definition
A sentence that has been divided into two parts, with one part being emphasized by placing it at the beginning after "It is" or "What." Used to highlight specific information and create emphasis in speech and writing.

## Part of Speech
Noun phrase

## Usage & Examples

**In technical discussions:**
- "It is **cost** that matters most in our decision."
- "What we need is **reliability** under pressure."
- "It is **batch processing** that I recommend for this project."

**In business contexts:**
- "It is **time to market** that drives our strategy."
- "What we're optimizing for is **operational simplicity**."
- "It is **business value** that should guide our choices."

**In presentations:**
- "It is **this approach** that will deliver the best results."
- "What really matters is **getting to production quickly**."
- "It is **cost-effectiveness** that makes this solution attractive."
