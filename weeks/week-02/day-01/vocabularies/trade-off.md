# trade-off

## IPA
/ˈtreɪd ɒf/

## Definition
A situation where you must accept something you do not want in order to get something you want, especially when making decisions between different options that each have advantages and disadvantages.

## Part of Speech
Noun

## Usage & Examples

**In technical discussions:**
- "There's a trade-off between performance and cost in this architecture."
- "We need to consider the trade-offs between batch and streaming processing."

**In business contexts:**
- "The trade-off is between speed to market and operational complexity."
- "We're evaluating the trade-offs of different data processing approaches."

**In decision making:**
- "Every architectural decision involves trade-offs."
- "The main trade-off here is between real-time capabilities and operational simplicity."
