# operational overhead

## IPA
/ˌɒpəˈreɪʃənəl ˈəʊvəhed/

## Definition
The additional work, resources, and complexity required to maintain and operate a system, including monitoring, troubleshooting, maintenance, and support activities.

## Part of Speech
Noun phrase

## Usage & Examples

**In technical discussions:**
- "Streaming systems have higher operational overhead than batch processing."
- "We need to consider the operational overhead when choosing our architecture."

**In cost analysis:**
- "The operational overhead includes 24/7 monitoring and incident response."
- "Batch processing reduces operational overhead by simplifying the system architecture."

**In decision making:**
- "The operational overhead of streaming doesn't justify the benefits for our use case."
- "We're optimizing for lower operational overhead to reduce team burden."
