# 📅 Week 14: Meeting Skills

**Dates**: May 11-17, 2026  
**Month 4: Problem Solving & Meetings**  
**Status**: [UPCOMING]

## 🎯 Week Overview

Interrupt politely, contribute ideas, summarize meetings

## 🗓️ Daily Breakdown

### Monday: Input & Shadowing
**Focus**: Shadow effective meeting facilitation
- 0-30 min: Find and watch relevant content
- 30-90 min: Intensive shadowing practice (3-level technique)
- 90-120 min: Record your own version and compare

### Tuesday: Vocabulary & Sentence Mining
**Focus**: Meeting phrases: interjections, agreements, clarifications, summaries
- 0-45 min: Read relevant materials and highlight 10 key phrases
- 45-90 min: Create 2 example sentences per phrase (general + specific)
- 90-120 min: Add to Anki and review out loud

### Wednesday: AI Role-play & Simulation
**Focus**: Participate in heated technical debate
- 0-15 min: Set up ChatGPT with appropriate scenario prompt
- 15-75 min: 3 rounds of role-play with feedback
- 75-120 min: Refine and record best version

### Thursday: Deep Listening & Transcription
**Focus**: Team meeting recording or business discussion
- 0-60 min: Transcribe 3-minute audio segment word-by-word
- 60-90 min: Compare with original and analyze mistakes
- 90-120 min: Summarize content in your own words (record it)

### Friday: Paraphrasing & Flexible Thinking
**Focus**: Practice diplomatic disagreement multiple ways
- 0-30 min: Choose 5 key concepts from this week
- 30-90 min: Paraphrase at 3 levels (simple, business, technical)
- 90-120 min: Speed thinking exercise with random words

### Saturday: Mini-Project & Synthesis
**Focus**: Facilitate mock team meeting (record it)
- 0-30 min: Plan and outline your deliverable
- 30-90 min: Practice and rehearse multiple times
- 90-120 min: Record final polished presentation

### Sunday: Passive Immersion
**Focus**: Negotiation or communication skills videos/documentaries
- 120 min: Watch relevant movie/show/documentary
- English audio + subtitles (or no subtitles if ready)
- No pressure - just enjoy and absorb!

## 📊 Week 14 Success Metrics

- ✅ Shadowing practice and recording
- ✅ 10-15 new Anki cards added
- ✅ AI role-play sessions completed
- ✅ Transcription exercise done
- ✅ Paraphrasing drills completed
- ✅ Mini-project presentation recorded
- ✅ 2 hours passive immersion

**Key Vocabulary**: 'If I may add...', 'Building on that...', 'To summarize...', 'Could you clarify...'
