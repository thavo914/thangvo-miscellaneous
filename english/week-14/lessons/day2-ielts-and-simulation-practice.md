## 🗣️ 1. IELTS Speaking Practice Questions

### Part 1 (Interview)
1. **Have you ever moved to a new city or town to live?**
   - *Tip*: Talk briefly about your transition from Sai Gon to Da Nang. State how long ago it was and how you felt.
2. **Do you prefer living in a quiet city or a busy city?**
   - *Tip*: Use preference structures like *"I prefer... to..."* or *"I would rather..."* to compare Da Nang and Sai Gon.
3. **Is it easy to find a suitable apartment to rent in your city?**
   - *Tip*: Mention the process of looking around different districts and checking options.

### Part 2 (Cue Card)
**Describe a time when you had to adapt to a major change in your life (such as moving to a new city or office).**
You should say:
- What the change was
- Why you had to make this change
- How you adapted to the new environment
- And explain how you felt about the outcome of this change.

### Part 3 (Analytical Discussion)
1. **Why do many people choose to relocate to smaller cities instead of staying in mega-cities like Sai Gon?**
2. **How can companies support employees who have recently relocated to a new regional office?**

## 🎙️ 4. Interactive Shadowing & Pacing Guide

Practice speaking these lines out loud. Use the slashes `/` for pauses, and stress the **capitalized** words.

**Line 1 (Expressing adaptation timeline)**:
> *"It took me a **WHILE** / to get used to the **QUIETER** streets / because I was used to hearing **TRAFFIC** noise / all **NIGHT** in Sai Gon."*

- **Pacing**: Pause slightly after *while* and *streets*. Keep a steady rhythm on *because*.
- **Intonation**: Rise slightly on *quieter* and fall naturally on *Sai Gon*.

**Line 2 (Giving financial tips)**:
> *"If you want to live within your **MEANS** / in Da **NANG** / you actually don't have to **TRY** / very **HARD**."*

- **Pacing**: Pause after *means* and *Nang*. Speed up slightly on *you actually don't have to*.
- **Intonation**: Stress **MEANS** and **TRY** to highlight the contrast.
