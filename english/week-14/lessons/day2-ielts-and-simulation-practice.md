## 🗣️ 1. IELTS Speaking Practice Questions

### Part 1 (Interview)
1. **Have you ever moved to a new city or town to live?**
   - *Tip*: Talk briefly about your transition from Sai Gon to Da Nang. State how long ago it was and how you felt.
   - *Answer*:
      + "Yes, I have. About two years ago, I decided to move from Sai Gon to Da Nang for a better work-life balance. At first, it was a big adjustment because I had lived in Sai Gon my whole life, but I quickly grew to love the slower pace of life here."
      + "I think moving to Da Nang was one of the best decisions I've ever made. While I do miss some things about Sai Gon, like the food scene and the convenience, I really value the quality of life here. The beaches, the mountains, and the friendly people make it a wonderful place to live."
      + "I'm really glad I made the move. The job opportunities here are great, especially in the tech industry, and I love being able to go to the beach after work. Plus, the cost of living is much more affordable than in Sai Gon, which allows me to save more money and enjoy a better lifestyle."
      
2. **Do you prefer living in a quiet city or a busy city?**
   - *Tip*: Use preference structures like *"I prefer... to..."* or *"I would rather..."* to compare Da Nang and Sai Gon.
   - *Answer*: 
      + "I’d definitely choose a quieter city. For me, peace of mind is key. Here in Da Nang, I can walk home late at night without a second thought, which is something I could never do comfortably in a bustling mega-city like Saigon. Having that sense of safety and freedom is something I really value."
      + "Honestly, I prefer a balance, but I lean toward a quieter city because of the lifestyle. In Da Nang, I can walk home late at night without worrying, which I never felt comfortable doing in Saigon. That said, I do occasionally miss the buzz of a busy city—the food scene in Saigon is incredible and there's always something going on. But for now, Da Nang is the perfect fit."
      + "In Sai Gon, you’re always surrounded by noise and traffic, and you constantly feel like you’re rushing. It’s exhausting. Here, I can actually breathe. I go for a swim in the sea after work, or just relax at a cafe by the river. It’s a much healthier lifestyle and I feel more recharged."
      
3. **Is it easy to find a suitable apartment to rent in your city?**
   - *Tip*: Mention the process of looking around different districts and checking options.
   - *Answer*: 
      + "Generally, yes, it's quite straightforward. There are many options available across different districts, and the rental prices are quite reasonable compared to Sai Gon. I found a great apartment near the beach after just a few weeks of searching, and I was able to negotiate a good price." 
      + "I looked at a few different districts before deciding on the one near the beach. Each area has its own vibe - some are more residential and quiet, while others are closer to the city center with more restaurants and nightlife." 
      + "Honestly, the process was pretty smooth. I started by looking online to get an idea of the market, and then I spent a few weekends driving around different neighborhoods. I found a few places that I liked, but I eventually settled on the one near the beach because it's close to my office and has great views." 

### Part 2 (Cue Card)
**Describe a time when you had to adapt to a major change in your life (such as moving to a new city or office).**
You should say:
- What the change was
- Why you had to make this change
- How you adapted to the new environment
- And explain how you felt about the outcome of this change.

## 🎙️ 4. Interactive Shadowing & Pacing Guide

Practice speaking these lines out loud. Use the slashes `/` for pauses, and stress the **capitalized** words.

**Line 1 (Expressing adaptation timeline)**:
> *"It took me a **WHILE** / to get used to the **QUIETER** streets / because I was used to hearing **TRAFFIC** noise / all **NIGHT** in Sai Gon."*

- **Pacing**: Pause slightly after *while* and *streets*. Keep a steady rhythm on *because*.
- **Intonation**: Rise slightly on *quieter* and fall naturally on *Sai Gon*.

**Line 2 (Giving financial tips)**:
> *"If you want to live within your **MEANS** / in Da **NANG** / you actually don't have to **TRY** / very **HARD**."*

- **Pacing**: Pause after *means* and *Nang*. Speed up slightly on *you actually don't have to*.
- **Intonation**: Stress **MEANS** and **TRY** to highlight the contrast.
