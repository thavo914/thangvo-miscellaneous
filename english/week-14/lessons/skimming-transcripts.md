## 📹 Topic 1: Adjusting to Da Nang: From Sai Gon to the Beach

### 1. Document Format & Pacing
*   **Topic**: Moving from Sai Gon to Da Nang city, adjusting to cost of living and local public transit.
*   **Target Duration**: 60 seconds
*   **Word Count**: 131 words
*   **Speaking Speed**: ~130 WPM

#### **Transcript**:
`[00:00]` Relocating from Sai Gon to Da Nang was a huge change for me. **It took me a while to get used to** the quiet evenings after work, because I **used to live** in a very noisy street near the center of Sai Gon. `[00:20]` Once I started to **look around** different districts, I realized how beautiful this city is. I eventually rented an apartment near the sea, and the lower **cost of living** here has made it much easier to **live within my means**. `[00:40]` For my daily commute, I prefer riding my motorbike, but I always remember to check the local bus route map if it starts raining heavily. I am slowly **getting used to** this new, relaxed lifestyle.

---

### 2. 10-Second Skimming Training

#### **Skimming Check**:
1. Why did the speaker struggle to adapt to Da Nang at first?
2. What helped the speaker live within their financial means?

## 📹 Topic 2: Post-Mortem: Troubleshooting Our Ingestion Failure

### 1. Document Format & Pacing
*   **Topic**: incident post-mortem and setting up system alerts.
*   **Target Duration**: 60 seconds
*   **Word Count**: 128 words
*   **Speaking Speed**: ~128 WPM

#### **Transcript**:
`[00:00]` We had a major pipeline incident yesterday. The ingestion database **had been running smoothly until** 2 PM, when a sudden replication lag spiked. It took our team several hours to **track down** the exact error, and we realized that a bad schema change in the source database was the main **point of failure**. `[00:20]` By **ruling out** database server load issues, we were able to focus on the raw files. **If we had kept a closer eye on** the logs, we **would have caught** the bug early. `[00:40]` To prevent this, we need to **get the schema validation updated** in our staging pipeline. We also need to **get new PagerDuty alerts configured** today to monitor any future schema drift.
