## 🗺️ 2. General Monologue: Adjusting to Da Nang Life

**Context**: You are explaining to a visiting colleague or manager how you adjusted your lifestyle, expenses, and commute after relocating to the Da Nang office.

**Monologue**:
> "Let me walk you through how my daily routine has changed since moving here. I live in an area that is very close to My Khe beach.
>
> I **prefer living** near the beach **to staying** in the noisy city center, so I rented a studio apartment in that area. In Sai Gon, I **was used to hearing** motorbikes at 2 AM, but here it's so quiet. **It took me a while to get used to** the silence, but now I love it.
>
> Regarding my commute to the office, I usually ride my motorbike. However, when it rains, I'd rather take the local bus than get wet. I always **remember to check** the DanaBus app for the schedule because the buses don't run very often.
>
> Finally, look at the **cost of living** here. It is much lower than in Sai Gon. **If you want to live within your means in** this city, you actually don't have to try very hard. Since I moved, I've managed to save quite a bit of my salary because I **stopped eating** at expensive tourist spots and started enjoying local street food instead.
>
> So, overall, my apartment is near the beach, my office is a short ride away, and my expenses are low. I've really managed to **settle in** well!"

### Key Vocabulary & Concepts:
- **Commute**: A regular journey of some distance to and from one's place of work.
- **Settle in**: To become familiar with a new place and feel comfortable there.
- **Commute**: (Vietnamese: Quãng đường di chuyển đi làm hàng ngày)
- **Settle in**: (Vietnamese: Ổn định cuộc sống / làm quen với môi trường mới)

## 📖 3. Band 6.5-7.0 Cue Card Sample Answer

**Prompt**: Describe a time when you had to adapt to a major change in your life (such as moving to a new city or office).

**Sample Answer**:
"I'd like to talk about a major change I experienced recently when I relocated from Sai Gon to Da Nang for my job. I was working as a data engineer, and our company opened a new regional tech branch, so I volunteered to transfer. 

At first, it was quite challenging. I **used to live** in a very loud, busy neighborhood in Sai Gon, so **it took me a while to get used to** the quietness here, especially at night. For the first week, I actually struggled to sleep because it was too quiet! I also had to **look around** several different areas before I found a suitable apartment. In the end, I chose a flat near the beach, and **I will never forget seeing** the ocean from my balcony for the first time.

Another major adjustment was the local transit. In Sai Gon, I was used to riding my motorbike everywhere in heavy traffic, but here, the roads are wider and much safer. I also started taking the local bus system, and I always **remember to check** the route map because it's still new to me. 

Ultimately, this change was very positive. The **cost of living** is much lower, which makes it easier to **live within my means** and save some money. I **prefer living** in a relaxed environment like Da Nang **to working** in the chaotic rush of Sai Gon. It gave me a fresh perspective on my lifestyle."

### Conversational Words Highlighted:
- **used to live**: previously lived there, but not anymore (Vietnamese: đã từng sống)
- **look around**: to search or check out different places or options (Vietnamese: đi tham quan, khảo sát xung quanh)
- **cost of living**: the amount of money needed to sustain a certain standard of living (Vietnamese: chi phí sinh hoạt)
- **live within my means**: to spend less than or equal to the money one earns (Vietnamese: chi tiêu trong tầm khả năng tài chính)
- **prefer... to...**: like something more than another thing (Vietnamese: thích cái gì hơn cái gì)
