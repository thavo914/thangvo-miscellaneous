# Week 14 - Combined 6-Day Lesson Overview

This week's lesson focuses on two distinct, highly practical communication scenarios for data professionals:
1. **Topic 1 (Days 1–3)**: Relocating for work, discussing moving from Sai Gon to Da Nang city, adjusting to the local lifestyle, cost of living, and public transit.
2. **Topic 2 (Days 4–6)**: Incident post-mortem, running a technical debrief with the engineering team to explain a pipeline failure and outline new monitoring alerts.

## 🗣️ 4. Core Lesson Dialogues

### Dialogue 1: Topic 1 (Moving to Da Nang from Sai Gon)
**Context:** Minh (Data Engineer who recently relocated) is chatting with Vy (a colleague based in Da Nang) during lunch.

- **Vy**: "Hey Minh! How have you been? Have you managed to **settle in** since relocating from Sai Gon?"
- **Minh**: "Hi Vy! Yes, I’m getting there. **It took me a while to get used to** the quieter streets because **I was used to hearing** traffic noise all night in Sai Gon."
- **Vy**: "I can imagine! Sai Gon is incredibly busy. How are you finding the **cost of living** here?"
- **Minh**: "It’s surprisingly affordable. In Sai Gon, **I forgot to budget** for eating out because local food was everywhere, but here, it’s much cheaper. **If you want to live within your means in** Da Nang, you actually don't have to try very hard."
- **Vy**: "That's true. Did you find a place to stay yet?"
- **Minh**: "**Before deciding to** sign a lease, **I spent a weekend looking around** Ngu Hanh Son district. I ended up renting a place near the beach. **I'll never forget walking** to the beach on my first morning."
- **Vy**: "That sounds perfect! How do you commute to the office?"
- **Minh**: "I usually ride my motorbike, but sometimes I take the local bus. **I always remember to check** the bus schedule online because they don't run as frequently as in Sai Gon."

### Dialogue 2: Topic 2 (Incident Post-Mortem Meeting)
**Context:** Nam (Lead Data Engineer) is running a post-mortem debrief with his team members, Tu and Linh, regarding a major pipeline crash.

- **Nam**: "Okay everyone, thanks for joining. Let's review yesterday's ingestion failure. **The database had been running smoothly until** 2 PM when the replication lag spiked. What did we find?"
- **Tu**: "Well, **it took us several hours to track down** the exact issue, **which was the main point of failure**. It turned out the vendor changed a field format in the source database without notifying us."
- **Linh**: "**By ruling out** network issues first, **we were able to focus on** the schema changes. The parser crashed because it received a string instead of an integer."
- **Nam**: "Right. We need to prevent this. **We need to get the schema validation updated** in our staging pipeline in order to block bad inputs."
- **Tu**: "Agreed. I will also configure our monitoring tool. **If we had kept a closer eye on** the error logs, **we would have caught the bug early**."
- **Nam**: "Yes, let's make sure we set up Slack and PagerDuty notifications for any schema drift. I'll make sure **we get the alerts configured** by end of day."
