# Day 4 - Exercise 1: Active Listening & Input Calibration (20–30 Mins)

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 1 Focus**: Acute auditory decoding of workplace technical dialogue, numerical benchmarks, system descriptions, and operational discussions.

---

## 🎧 Active Listening: TOEIC Dictation (20–30 Mins)

### Goal
Decode fast-paced technical conversations, system metrics, and architectural decisions.

### Instructions
1. Select **3 short workplace technical audio passages** (from TOEIC Part 3 or Part 4).
2. Transcribe the audio word-for-word onto paper or into a blank text editor.
3. Compare against the audio transcript and highlight any missed passive verb endings (`-ed`), prepositions, or comparative adverbs.
