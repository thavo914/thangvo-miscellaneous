# Day 1 - Exercise 5: AI Speaking Simulation & Review (75 Mins)

> [!NOTE]
> **Blocks 3 & 4 Focus**: AI Spoken Production with ChatGPT Voice Mode (60 mins) + Flashcard capture & error logging (15 mins).

---

## 🤖 Part 1: AI Speaking Practice (60 Mins)

Practice speaking with ChatGPT Voice Mode on your mobile phone or desktop. Below are 3 contextualized drills specifically calibrated to today's theme: **setting healthy work-life boundaries, managing workload stress, and polite professional refusal**.

---

### Drill 1: Rapid Q&A (Workload Habits & Reflex - 15 Mins)
* **Context**: You are reflecting on your daily working habits, how you manage requests from other departments, and how you disconnect in the evening.
* **Goal**: Answer spontaneously within 2–3 sentences without hesitating or translating mentally from Vietnamese.
* **Target Expressions to Activate**: `tend to`, `set clear boundaries`, `spread oneself too thin`, `unplug after work`, `keep up with the workload`.

> 📋 **Copy-Paste Prompt for ChatGPT Voice Mode:**
> ```text
> Act as my English speaking coach. I am a Data Engineer practicing setting work-life boundaries and managing workload stress. Ask me 5 rapid questions one by one about:
> 1. How I handle incoming ad-hoc requests when my schedule is already full.
> 2. What bad habits I tend to fall into when sprint deadlines get tight.
> 3. Why taking on too many tasks makes an engineer spread themselves too thin.
> 4. How I communicate with teammates when I need to push back on a deadline.
> 5. What routine I use to unplug after work.
> After each of my spoken answers, give me quick feedback on my grammar and suggest a more natural B1-B2 Plain English upgrade using today's target phrases.
> ```

---

### Drill 2: Instant Translation & Reverse Shadowing (Workplace Boundary Drill - 15 Mins)
* **Context**: Quick-fire verbal translation of realistic Vietnamese workplace dilemmas into natural, idiomatic English under 5 seconds.
* **Goal**: Build muscle memory for English sentence templates and eliminate awkward literal translations ("Vietlish").

> 📋 **Copy-Paste Prompt for ChatGPT Voice Mode:**
> ```text
> Act as my bilingual English trainer. Give me 4 realistic Vietnamese workplace situations one by one where a data engineer needs to set boundaries or manage workload (for example: "Ưu tiên số một của tôi là sửa pipeline nên không nhận task mới trước thứ Ba", "Nếu cứ nhận hết việc thì mình sẽ bị ôm đồm quá nhiều thứ", "Thay vì làm ẩu cho kịp giờ, tôi muốn làm cẩn thận để tránh lỗi trên production", "Hết giờ làm tôi luôn tắt máy tính để nghỉ ngơi").
> I will translate each into English out loud within 3 to 5 seconds.
> After I answer, provide the most natural Band 6.5 native English phrasing using collocations like "set clear boundaries", "spread oneself too thin", "push back on a deadline", or "instead of... I'd rather...".
> ```

---

### Drill 3: Scenario Role-Play (Pushing Back on an Urgent Stakeholder - 30 Mins)
* **Detailed Scenario**:
  * **AI Persona**: Sarah, an urgent Product Manager who needs 3 custom dashboard metrics and a customer cohort data extract delivered by 4 PM today for an executive deck. She insists it's "just a quick SQL query" and pressures you to finish it right away.
  * **Your Persona**: Thang, a Data Engineer whose top priority today is resolving an ingestion latency bottleneck on the core payment pipeline.
  * **Your Mission**:
    1. Acknowledge her urgency calmly without being defensive.
    2. Use Template 1: *"Right now, my top priority is... so I won't be able to take on... until..."*
    3. Explain the trade-off with Template 3: *"If we agree to take this on today, we'll have to push back on..."*
    4. Offer a constructive next-day alternative delivery time.

> 📋 **Copy-Paste Prompt for ChatGPT Voice Mode:**
> ```text
> Let's do a realistic workplace role-play:
> - Your Role: Sarah, an urgent Product Manager who needs 3 custom dashboard metrics and a customer extract by 4 PM today for an executive deck. You think it's a quick 10-minute SQL job and will push me to finish it today.
> - My Role: Thang, a Data Engineer whose top priority today is fixing an ingestion latency bottleneck on the core production pipeline.
> - Setting: You ping me on Slack audio asking for immediate delivery.
> - Objective: I will practice setting clear boundaries politely, stating my current priorities, explaining trade-offs, and negotiating to push back the delivery to tomorrow morning without being defensive.
> Start the conversation right now by urgently asking me for the custom metrics.
> ```

### 🎯 Speaking Checklist for Today's Voice Session:
