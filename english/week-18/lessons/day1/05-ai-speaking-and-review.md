# Day 1 - Exercise 5: AI Speaking Simulation & Review (75 Mins)

> [!NOTE]
> **Blocks 3 & 4 Focus**: AI Spoken Production with ChatGPT Voice Mode (60 mins) + Flashcard capture & error logging (15 mins).

---

## 🤖 Part 1: AI Speaking Practice (60 Mins)

Use ChatGPT Voice Mode on your mobile phone or desktop to practice speaking with AI using today's grammar rules, sentence templates, and high-yield collocations.

> **Suggested Exercises (AI Prompts):**
>
> 1. **Rapid Q&A:**
>    > "Act as my English tutor. Ask me random English questions ranging from easy to advanced (topics: daily life, work, opinions, etc.). I will answer in English as quickly as possible. After I answer, please correct my grammar and vocabulary, and suggest more natural, native-like ways to express my ideas."
>
> 2. **Instant Translation (Reverse Shadowing):**
>    > "Give me a common Vietnamese situation or daily phrase. I will try to translate it into English out loud within 3–5 seconds. After my attempt, please provide the most natural English translation so I can compare."
>
> 3. **Role-play:**
>    > "Let's do a role-play. Please choose one of these topics: ordering at a restaurant, a job interview, traveling/at the airport, or discussing work with a colleague. We will have a back-and-forth conversation entirely in English."

---

## 📝 Part 2: Review & Consolidation (15 Mins)

1. **Anki Flashcards**: Add today's 5 collocations (`set clear boundaries`, `spread oneself too thin`, `push back on a deadline`, `unplug after work`, `keep up with the workload`) and your 3 favorite sentence templates to Anki.
2. **Error Logging**: Note any hesitations or grammatical corrections from your AI voice session in your personal speaking log.
3. **Checklist Update**: Mark Day 1 tasks as completed in [checklist.md](../../checklist.md).
