# Day 1 - Exercise 1: Warm-up Reflex & Active Listening (35–45 Mins)

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 1 Focus**: Spontaneous spoken English formulation (15 mins) + Acute auditory decoding via dictation (20–30 mins).

---

## 🎙️ Part 1: Speaking Reflex - The Daily Commentator (15 Mins)

### Goal
Build spontaneous English formulation without translating from Vietnamese in your head by narrating your actions, focus priorities, and immediate thoughts out loud.

### Instructions
1. Look around your desk, open tasks, calendar, or editor.
2. Speak continuously out loud for **3 minutes** narrating what you are working on, how you plan your focus time, and how you deal with interruptions.
3. Intentionally inject at least two of today's target phrases:
   - `set clear boundaries`
   - `spread oneself too thin`
   - `push back on a deadline`
   - `unplug after work`
   - `keep up with the workload`

### 💡 Worked Example (What to Say Out Loud)
> *"Right now, I'm sitting down at my desk, looking over my task backlog for the week. In the past, I had a habit of saying yes to every incoming ping, which led to **spreading myself too thin**. Today, I'm making a conscious effort to **set clear boundaries**. I have a high-priority pipeline refactor to finish, so if non-urgent tickets come in, I'll politely **push back on the deadline**. Once I finish my focus block, it'll be much easier to **keep up with the workload** and actually **unplug after work** tonight."*

---

## 🎧 Part 2: Active Listening - TOEIC Dictation (20–30 Mins)

### Goal
Train acoustic decoding and catch natural reductions, linking, and rapid speech in professional contexts.

### Instructions
1. Open a TOEIC Short Conversations / Short Talks practice audio clip (Parts 3 or 4).
2. Listen to **3 distinct audio passages**. Pause sentence-by-sentence and transcribe every single word onto paper or into a blank text document.
3. Compare against the audio transcript and highlight missed linking sounds, prepositions, and weak forms with a red pen.
