## 🇻🇳➡️🇬🇧 Drill 2: Translation & Transformation Challenge

Translate these 3 common workplace situations into natural, professional English using today's grammar rules, sentence templates, and high-yield collocations (`set clear boundaries`, `spread oneself too thin`, `push back on a deadline`, `keep up with the workload`, `unplug after work`).

1. **Câu 1**: *Nếu mình cứ nhận thêm mọi yêu cầu phát sinh từ các phòng ban khác, mình sẽ bị ôm đồm quá nhiều việc và không thể nào bắt kịp khối lượng công việc chính được.*  
   → **Your Translation**: If I take on every extra request from other teams, I will spread myself too thin and won't be able to keep up with my core workload.

2. **Câu 2**: *Hiện tại ưu tiên lớn nhất của tôi là hoàn thành việc di chuyển dữ liệu sang hệ thống mới, nên tôi buộc phải xin lùi thời hạn bàn giao báo cáo doanh thu sang thứ Sáu.*  
   → **Your Translation**: Right now, my top priority is completing the data migration to the new system, so I had to push back on the deadline for the revenue report until Friday.
3. **Câu 3**: *Thay vì vừa ăn tối vừa kiểm tra tin nhắn công việc, tôi tập thói quen ngắt kết nối hoàn toàn sau 7 giờ tối để giữ đầu óc tỉnh táo cho ngày hôm sau.*  
   → **Your Translation**: Instead of checking work messages while eating dinner, I make it habit to completely unplug after 7 PM to keep my mind clear for the next day.

**Model Translation 1:**
> *"If we keep accepting every ad-hoc request from other teams, we'll end up **spreading ourselves too thin** and won't be able to **keep up with our core workload**."*
* *Linguistic Notes*: Uses "ad-hoc request" for yêu cầu phát sinh; replaces literal "take too many things" with the natural collocation "spread ourselves too thin".
* *Alternative Phrasing*: *"Taking on every incoming request will only spread us too thin, making it impossible to stay on top of our primary deliverables."*

**Model Translation 2:**
> *"Right now, my top priority is completing the data migration to the new system, so I had to **push back on the deadline** for the revenue report until Friday."*
* *Linguistic Notes*: Uses Template 1 structure; replaces "ask for delay" with professional "push back on the deadline".
* *Alternative Phrasing*: *"Because our core focus right now is the database migration, we'll need to push back the revenue report delivery to Friday."*

**Model Translation 3:**
> *"**Instead of** checking work messages during dinner, I've **gotten into the habit of completely unplugging after work** past 7 PM to stay sharp for the next day."*
* *Linguistic Notes*: Uses Rule 3 "Instead of + V-ing"; applies the natural idiom "unplug after work" rather than literal "turn off work communication".
* *Alternative Phrasing*: *"Rather than monitoring work chats while eating dinner, I make it a rule to unplug after 7 PM so I can recharge for tomorrow."*

## 🔍 Drill 3: Spot the Mistake (Error Correction Drill)

Find and correct the errors in the 3 sentences below:
1. *I tend to accepting too many tasks because I afraid of saying no to my manager.*  
   → **Your Correction**: I tend to accept too many tasks because I'm afraid of saying no to my manager.
2. *Right now my priority is fix the pipeline, so I am not able take new requests until tomorrow.*  
   → **Your Correction**: Right now my priority is fixing the pipeline, so I won't be able to take new requests until tomorrow.
3. *Instead of write unit tests, he prefers deploying the script directly into production.*  
   → **Your Correction**: Instead of writing unit tests, he prefers deploying the script directly into production.

<details>
<summary>💡 Click to View Explanations & Corrections</summary>

1. **Correction**: *I **tend to accept** too many tasks because I **am afraid of** saying no to my manager.*  
   *Explanation*: `tend to` must be followed by a base verb (`accept`), not a gerund (`accepting`). Also, `afraid` is an adjective and requires the auxiliary verb `am` (`am afraid of`).
2. **Correction**: *Right now my priority is **fixing** the pipeline, so I **won't be able to take** new requests until tomorrow.*  
   *Explanation*: After `priority is`, use a gerund (`fixing`). For polite professional refusals, use future modal `won't be able to take`.
3. **Correction**: *Instead of **writing** unit tests, he prefers **to deploy** (or: **prefers deploying**) the script directly into production.*  
   *Explanation*: The prepositional phrase `instead of` requires a gerund (`writing`).
</details>
