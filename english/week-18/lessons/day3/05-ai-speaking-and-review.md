## 🤖 Part 1: AI Speaking Practice (60 Mins)

Practice speaking with ChatGPT Voice Mode on your mobile phone or desktop. Below are 3 contextualized drills specifically calibrated to today's theme: **workplace boundary dialogue, the engineering mindset of saying no, and transcreation of the coffee chat with Linh**.

---

### Drill 1: Rapid Q&A (Monologue Defense & Boundary Mindset - 15 Mins)
* **Context**: You are defending the argument in today's monologue: *Why Learning to Say No Makes You a Better Engineer*.
* **Goal**: Explain why saying yes to every ad-hoc query harms core pipeline quality and team delivery.
* **Target Expressions to Activate**: `spread oneself too thin`, `set clear boundaries`, `push back on a deadline`, `keep up with the workload`, `unplug after work`.

> 📋 **Copy-Paste Prompt for ChatGPT Voice Mode:**
> ```text
> Act as my English communication coach. I am a Data Engineer practicing self-explanation and articulating professional boundaries. Ask me 5 rapid questions one by one about:
> 1. Why does saying yes to every incoming ad-hoc request make an engineer spread themselves too thin?
> 2. What happens to the quality of core data pipelines when an engineer's attention is scattered across ten minor tasks?
> 3. In what way is learning to set clear boundaries an act of professional responsibility rather than laziness?
> 4. How should an engineer politely push back on an unreasonable deadline without sounding defensive or burning bridges?
> 5. What practical steps can an engineer take to ensure they can unplug after work without constant anxiety?
> After each of my spoken answers, give me concise feedback on grammar and suggest more natural B1-B2 Plain English upgrades.
> ```

---

### Drill 2: Instant Translation & Reverse Shadowing (Coffee Chat Transcreation - 15 Mins)
* **Context**: Rapid verbal translation of realistic Vietnamese dialogue turns from the coffee chat between Thang and Linh.
* **Goal**: Build instantaneous recall of natural conversational transitions and eliminate "Vietlish" traps.

> 📋 **Copy-Paste Prompt for ChatGPT Voice Mode:**
> ```text
> Act as my bilingual speaking trainer. Give me 4 realistic Vietnamese lines from a workplace coffee chat between colleagues one by one (for example: "Dạo này em căng thẳng vì ai nhờ việc gì trên Slack em cũng nhận", "Nếu em không đặt ranh giới rõ ràng, mọi người sẽ nghĩ em lúc nào cũng rảnh", "Thay vì làm ẩu một bản vá tạm thời, em muốn dành thời gian kiểm tra kỹ trước khi bàn giao", "Thống nhất lại tiến độ xong em thấy dễ thở hơn hẳn và bắt kịp công việc").
> I will translate each into English out loud within 3 to 5 seconds.
> After I speak, show me the native conversational English model using "spread myself too thin", "set clear boundaries", "push back on a deadline", or "keep up with the workload".
> ```

### Drill 3: Scenario Role-Play (Coffee Chat Consultation with a Senior Teammate - 30 Mins)
* **Detailed Scenario**:
  * **AI Persona**: Linh, a supportive Senior Data Engineer having a 15-minute coffee break with you at the office pantry.
  * **Your Persona**: Thang, a Data Engineer who has been working late recently because of endless ad-hoc data requests from marketing and sales, feeling guilty about saying no.
  * **Your Mission**:
    1. Confess that you tend to take on every request and are spreading yourself too thin.
    2. Explain that you need to set clear boundaries to protect core sprint deliverables.
    3. Practice walking Linh through how you plan to push back on non-urgent requests politely.
    4. Conclude with a healthy boundary: wrapping up at 6 PM to work out and unplug.

> 📋 **Copy-Paste Prompt for ChatGPT Voice Mode:**
> ```text
> Let's do a realistic workplace coffee chat role-play:
> - Your Role: Linh, a seasoned Senior Data Engineer and supportive mentor having coffee with me at the office pantry.
> - My Role: Thang, a Data Engineer who has been working late recently because of endless ad-hoc data requests from marketing and sales.
> - Setting: Casual coffee break conversation.
> - Objective: I will explain my workload challenges, admit that I tend to take on too much and spread myself too thin, discuss why I need to set clear boundaries, and practice explaining how I plan to push back on non-urgent requests.
> - Tone: Friendly, collaborative, and grounded.
> Start the conversation now by asking me why I look so exhausted today.
> ```
