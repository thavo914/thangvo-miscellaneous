## 📋 Part 1: Self-Evaluation Checklist

Assess your mastery of Topic 1's structures and collocations:
- [ ] Did I use `tend to + base verb` to describe natural inclinations objectively?
- [ ] Did I use `won't be able to + base verb` paired with an alternative timeline?
- [ ] Did I use `instead of + V-ing` to contrast a better approach with a hasty one?
- [ ] Did I include all 5 target collocations naturally in my spoken drills (`set clear boundaries`, `spread oneself too thin`, `push back on a deadline`, `unplug after work`, `keep up with the workload`)?

## 🏢 Part 2: Situational Workplace Responses

### Scenario 1: Urgent Dashboard Column Request
*A business stakeholder asks you to add 5 new calculated columns to a live dashboard by the end of today, while you are in the middle of resolving an ingestion bug.*

<details>
<summary>💡 Click to View Suggested Professional Response</summary>

> *"I'd be glad to help add those columns, but right now my top priority is resolving the ingestion latency on our staging cluster. As a result, I won't be able to modify the dashboard until tomorrow morning. Instead of rushing the calculation into production today, I'd rather test the query metrics first thing tomorrow so we ensure the numbers are 100% accurate."*
</details>

---

### Scenario 2: Late Afternoon Pull Request Review
*Your teammate asks you to review a 500-line pull request while you are about to log off at 6 PM.*

<details>
<summary>💡 Click to View Suggested Professional Response</summary>

> *"I see your PR just came in. I'm actually wrapping up for the day and make it a point to unplug after six to recharge. I'll make reviewing your pull request my very first task when I log on tomorrow morning at nine."*
</details>
