# Day 3 - Exercise 1: Warm-up Reflex & Active Listening (35–45 Mins)

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 1 Focus**: Spontaneous boundary negotiation roleplay (15 mins) + Sentence-level active listening dictation (20–30 mins).

---

## 🤖 Part 1: Speaking Reflex - Unconventional AI Voice Roleplay (15 Mins)

### Goal
Build spontaneous spoken reactions under quirky or unexpected conversational conditions.

### Roleplay Scenario
You are talking to an over-caffeinated startup CEO who wants you to build an AI chatbot, fix three broken pipelines, and design an executive dashboard all by tomorrow morning. Your objective is to remain calm, apply softeners, and set firm, professional boundaries without getting flustered.

### AI Voice Prompt (ChatGPT Voice Mode)
```
Act as an energetic startup founder who wants three massive data tasks delivered by tomorrow. I am a pragmatic data engineer. Ask me to do everything at once. I will practice setting clear boundaries, explaining trade-offs, and pushing back on unreasonable deadlines.
```

---

## 🎧 Part 2: Active Listening - TOEIC Dictation (20–30 Mins)

### Goal
Sentence-level auditory processing and error checking.

### Instructions
1. Listen to **3 short workplace conversation clips** from TOEIC Part 3 or Part 4.
2. Transcribe word-for-word on paper or a text document.
3. Compare against the transcript and mark missed words or unclear vowel reductions.
