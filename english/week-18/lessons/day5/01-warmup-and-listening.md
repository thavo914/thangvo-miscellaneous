# Day 5 - Exercise 1: Warm-up Reflex & Active Listening (35–45 Mins)

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 1 Focus**: Spontaneous cognitive pivot drill (15 mins) + Technical auditory dictation (20–30 mins).

---

## 😈 Part 1: Speaking Reflex - The "Devil's Advocate" Challenge (15 Mins)

### Goal
Train cognitive flexibility and spontaneous contrast articulation in English under rapid timing constraints.

### Instructions
1. Pick a provocative technical statement (e.g., *"We should immediately rewrite our entire data warehouse in a brand-new framework"*).
2. State your agreement enthusiastically for **15 seconds**.
3. Pivot instantly using a contrast linker (*"On the other hand...", "Having said that...", "However, the real trade-off is..."*) and systematically argue against it for the remaining **30 seconds**.
4. Challenge yourself to use `trade-off between` or `phase out` naturally.

### 💡 Worked Example
> *"Rewriting our whole data stack using the latest cutting-edge framework sounds amazing because it could drastically speed up queries and make our developer experience much cooler.  
> **Having said that**, the real **trade-off between** chasing new tech and maintaining stable business delivery is huge. Our team already understands our existing Python pipelines inside and out. If we rush into a complete rewrite, we'll spend months fixing migration bugs instead of shipping core features. A much wiser approach is to **run a small proof of concept** first and **phase out** legacy parts gradually."*

---

## 🎧 Part 2: Active Listening - TOEIC Dictation (20–30 Mins)

### Goal
Decode complex numbers, technical explanations, and multi-speaker workplace meetings.

### Instructions
1. Select **3 conversation clips** from TOEIC Part 3 or Part 4 focusing on office technology or project schedules.
2. Transcribe word-for-word on paper or a text editor.
3. Compare against the transcript and note any missed technical terms or fast-spoken reductions (*could've, should be, gonna*).
