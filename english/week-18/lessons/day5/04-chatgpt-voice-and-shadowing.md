## 🎼 Part 3: Interactive Shadowing & Pacing Guide

Read these two technical lines aloud with visual stress (**BOLD CAPS**) and thought-group pauses (`/`):

### Line 1 (Delivering Benchmark Results):
> "The DuckDB pipeline is **SIGNIFICANTLY FASTER** / and uses **LESS MEMORY** / than our existing Pandas script. / For our ten-gigabyte test dataset, / runtime **DROPPED** from twenty-eight minutes / down to **UNDER FOUR MINUTES**."

* **Rhythm Cue**: Pause after *Pandas script* and *dataset* to let numerical contrasts hit clearly.
* **Emphasis**: Strongly stress *SIGNIFICANTLY FASTER*, *LESS MEMORY*, and *UNDER FOUR MINUTES*.

---

### Line 2 (Recommending Deprecation):
> "Let's write up a **SHORT MIGRATION RFC** / so our legacy Pandas scripts / **CAN BE PHASED OUT** / by the end of **NEXT MONTH**."

* **Rhythm Cue**: Emphasize *can be phased out* with steady, authoritative pacing.
