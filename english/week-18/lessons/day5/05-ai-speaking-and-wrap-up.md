## 🤖 Part 1: AI Speaking Practice (60 Mins)

Practice speaking with ChatGPT Voice Mode on your mobile phone or desktop. Below are 3 contextualized drills specifically calibrated to today's theme: **presenting POC benchmark results to the Tech Lead, defending architectural trade-offs, and scheduling pipeline deprecation**.

---

### Drill 1: Rapid Q&A (Technical POC Architecture & Benchmarks - 15 Mins)
* **Context**: You are answering sharp technical questions about your recent DuckDB + dbt proof-of-concept run against legacy Pandas jobs.
* **Goal**: Deliver precise numerical evidence and architectural rationale without hesitation.
* **Target Expressions to Activate**: `run a proof of concept (POC)`, `trade-off between`, `ease the burden on`, `phase out`, `scale up smoothly`.

> 📋 **Copy-Paste Prompt for ChatGPT Voice Mode:**
> ```text
> Act as my Technical English coach. I am a Data Engineer presenting benchmark results from a proof of concept evaluating DuckDB and dbt against legacy Pandas scripts. Ask me 5 rapid technical questions one by one about:
> 1. What specific bottlenecks in our legacy Pandas scripts prompted us to run this POC?
> 2. What did the benchmark numbers show regarding runtime and memory usage on our 10GB test dataset?
> 3. What is the primary trade-off between local embedded DuckDB execution and our shared data warehouse?
> 4. How will offloading hourly transformations ease the burden on our primary production database?
> 5. What is our recommended timeline to phase out the legacy scripts and ensure our data models scale up smoothly?
> After each of my spoken answers, critique my technical precision, verb choices, and comparative grammar.
> ```

---

### Drill 2: Instant Translation & Reverse Shadowing (Tech Transcreation Drill - 15 Mins)
* **Context**: Verbal translation of key lines from the 1-on-1 technical huddle between Thang and Alex (Tech Lead).
* **Goal**: Build muscle memory for technical idioms, benchmark phrasing, and deprecation recommendations.

> 📋 **Copy-Paste Prompt for ChatGPT Voice Mode:**
> ```text
> Act as my bilingual Technical English coach. Give me 4 realistic Vietnamese data engineering statements one by one (for example: "Luồng DuckDB nhanh hơn rõ rệt và tiêu tốn ít RAM hơn hẳn script Pandas cũ", "Thời gian chạy giảm từ 28 phút xuống dưới 4 phút trên tập dữ liệu 10GB", "Điểm đánh đổi lớn nhất nằm ở công cụ phát triển và lưu trữ tạm cục bộ", "Chúng ta nên viết tài liệu RFC để từng bước loại bỏ script cũ vào cuối tháng sau").
> I will translate each into English out loud within 3 to 5 seconds.
> After my response, provide the native Band 6.5+ technical model using "significantly faster", "trade-off between", "ease the burden on", and "phase out".
> ```

---

### Drill 3: High-Stakes Tech Lead 1-on-1 Simulation (POC Defense & RFC Approval - 30 Mins)
* **Detailed Scenario**:
  * **AI Persona**: Alex, a sharp, pragmatic Tech Lead who is protective of production stability and wants to understand the catches, developer tooling, and risks before signing off on any migration.
  * **Your Persona**: Thang, a Data Engineer presenting benchmark results from a 10GB test dataset.
  * **Your Mission**:
    1. Deliver the headline win: *"The DuckDB pipeline is significantly faster and uses less memory than our existing Pandas script."*
    2. Address trade-offs upfront: *"The main trade-off comes down to developer tooling and local storage, but dbt SQL transformations make debugging much simpler."*
    3. Emphasize warehouse relief: *"Moving these transformations out of the cluster will ease the burden on our primary warehouse."*
    4. Propose next steps: *"Let's draft an RFC so legacy Pandas scripts can be phased out by the end of next month."*
    5. Handle Alex's questions if he asks how the solution scales up under double data volume.

> 📋 **Copy-Paste Prompt for ChatGPT Voice Mode:**
> ```text
> Let's do a realistic high-stakes engineering 1-on-1 role-play:
> - Your Role: Alex, my Tech Lead. You are sharp, pragmatic, and cautious about adding new dependencies to our data platform. You want hard numbers and want to understand maintenance risks before approving any migration.
> - My Role: Thang, a Data Engineer presenting benchmark results from a 10GB POC where DuckDB and dbt dropped runtime from 28 minutes to under 4 minutes.
> - Setting: 1-on-1 engineering huddle.
> - Objective: I will present the benchmark results, acknowledge the trade-off regarding developer tooling and storage, explain how this eases the burden on the warehouse, and propose an RFC to phase out our legacy Pandas scripts by next month.
> - Challenge: Challenge my claims on maintainability and ask what happens if dataset volume doubles.
> Start the meeting now by asking me how the aggregation pipeline investigation went.
> ```
