# Day 5 - Exercise 3: Tech IELTS Speaking Preparation

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 2 (Part B)**: Topic 2 IELTS Speaking Part 1, Part 2 (Cue Card on tool evaluations and improvements), and Part 3 analytical questions.

---

## 🎙️ Part 1: Technology & Engineering Tools (3 Questions)

Answer each question out loud in 2–4 natural sentences. Aim for smooth pacing and functional clarity.

1. **What software or tools do you rely on most in your daily work?**
   * *Sample Idea*: Explain using SQL, dbt, or Python for processing tabular data, highlighting ease of maintenance.
   * *Target Phrase Integration*: Use `ease the burden on` or `scale up smoothly`.

2. **Do you prefer working with established legacy systems or testing new technologies?**
   * *Sample Idea*: Discuss balancing stability with innovation; mention running a proof of concept before adopting new tools.
   * *Target Phrase Integration*: Use `run a proof of concept` or `trade-off between`.

3. **How do you keep your technical skills up to date?**
   * *Sample Idea*: Reading engineering blogs, building sample projects, or testing tools locally.
   * *Target Phrase Integration*: Use `run a proof of concept` on weekends.

---

## 🎙️ Part 2: Individual Long Turn (Cue Card)

```
Describe a technical improvement or tool evaluation you proposed or worked on.

You should say:
- What the existing problem or bottleneck was
- What new tool or approach you decided to evaluate
- What the results of the evaluation or benchmark showed
- And explain how this improvement helped your team or system perform better.
```

### 💡 1-Minute Planning Notes Template:
* **Bottleneck**: *Hourly aggregation query taking 30 minutes in Pandas; cluster running out of memory.*
* **Tool evaluated**: *DuckDB + dbt; ran a proof of concept on 10GB dataset.*
* **Benchmark results**: *Runtime dropped to 4 mins (80% cut); reduced compute costs.*
* **Outcome**: *Eased burden on primary warehouse; legacy script phased out; pipeline scales up smoothly.*

## 🎙️ Part 3: Analytical Discussion Questions

1. **Why are many established companies hesitant to replace legacy systems even when newer tools are clearly faster?**
   * *Key Angle*: Legacy systems have hidden dependencies and battle-tested edge cases. The financial and operational risk of migration often outweighs theoretical speed improvements unless existing systems completely fail to scale.

2. **In modern tech, how should engineering teams decide between building a custom in-house solution and adopting an open-source tool?**
   * *Key Angle*: Teams must weigh the trade-off between customization and maintenance overhead. Open-source tools ease the burden of building from scratch, whereas in-house tools are only justified when the business problem is genuinely unique.
