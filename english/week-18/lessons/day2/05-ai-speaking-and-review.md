## 🤖 Part 1: AI Speaking Practice (60 Mins)

Practice speaking with ChatGPT Voice Mode on your mobile phone or desktop. Below are 3 contextualized drills specifically calibrated to today's theme: **IELTS Speaking fluency, managing heavy workloads, work-life balance, and evening routines**.

---

### Drill 1: Rapid Q&A (IELTS Part 1 & Part 3 Warm-up - 15 Mins)
* **Context**: Quick-fire practice answering introductory interview and analytical questions on working hours, digital overload, and personal limits.
* **Goal**: Answer spontaneously within 2–4 sentences with smooth pacing and zero mental translation delay.
* **Target Expressions to Activate**: `tend to`, `set clear boundaries`, `unplug after work`, `spread oneself too thin`, `push back on a deadline`.

> 📋 **Copy-Paste Prompt for ChatGPT Voice Mode:**
> ```text
> Act as my IELTS Speaking examiner. I am preparing for Band 6.5+ conversational fluency. Ask me 5 rapid questions one by one covering:
> 1. How do you typically plan and organize your daily working hours?
> 2. Is it easy or difficult for you to disconnect from work in the evening?
> 3. What do you do when you are assigned more tasks than you can complete in one day?
> 4. Why do many professionals in fast-growing tech companies struggle with work-life balance?
> 5. In your opinion, who holds primary responsibility for avoiding burnout: the employer or the employee?
> After each of my spoken answers, give me concise feedback on my fluency and grammatical precision, and suggest natural B1-B2 upgrades using collocations like "set clear boundaries", "unplug after work", or "spread oneself too thin".
> ```

---

### Drill 2: Instant Translation & Reverse Shadowing (IELTS Part 2 Ideas - 15 Mins)
* **Context**: Verbal translation of realistic IELTS Part 2 talking points from Vietnamese into native English under 5 seconds.
* **Goal**: Build instantaneous recall of high-band expressions when narrating a stressful project or heavy workload.

> 📋 **Copy-Paste Prompt for ChatGPT Voice Mode:**
> ```text
> Act as my bilingual IELTS tutor. Give me 4 realistic Vietnamese statements one by one describing handling a high-pressure workload (for example: "Đợt đó hai kỹ sư nghỉ phép đúng lúc hệ thống nguồn thay đổi schema đột ngột", "Tôi nhận ra làm việc 16 tiếng mỗi ngày là không bền vững và dễ gây lỗi", "Tôi chủ động đặt ranh giới và thông báo lùi thời hạn các yêu cầu không khẩn cấp", "Thay vì hoảng loạn vá tạm, tôi tập trung cô lập lỗi và viết lại logic chuẩn xác").
> I will translate each into English out loud within 3 to 5 seconds.
> After each attempt, provide the most natural Band 6.5–7.0 phrasing using phrases like "set clear boundaries", "flooded with urgent pings", "push back on the deadline", or "with peace of mind".
> ```

---

### Drill 3: Full IELTS Simulation (Cue Card & Analytical Discussion - 30 Mins)
* **Detailed Scenario**:
  * **Examiner Persona**: Official, professional, yet encouraging IELTS examiner.
  * **Your Persona**: Test candidate (Data Engineer) sharing authentic project experience.
  * **Test Flow**:
    1. Examiner introduces the Cue Card: *"Describe a time when you had to manage a very heavy workload or handle work stress."*
    2. You deliver a continuous 1.5 to 2-minute long turn using your [Day 2 planning notes](file:///c:/Users/Admin/source/repos/thangvo-miscellaneous/english/week-18/lessons/day2/02-ielts-speaking-prep.md#-1-minute-planning-notes-template).
    3. Examiner asks 1 follow-up question, then leads into Part 3 analytical questions comparing modern remote work expectations and company cultures.

> 📋 **Copy-Paste Prompt for ChatGPT Voice Mode:**
> ```text
> Let's conduct an authentic IELTS Speaking simulation (Part 2 and Part 3):
> - Examiner Persona: You are an official, professional, yet supportive IELTS examiner.
> - My Role: Test candidate (Data Engineer).
> - Task:
>   1. Give me the Cue Card prompt: "Describe a time when you had to manage a very heavy workload or handle work stress."
>   2. Tell me to start speaking. Let me speak for 1.5 to 2 minutes without interrupting.
>   3. Ask 1 brief follow-up question.
>   4. Seamlessly transition to Part 3 with 3 deep analytical questions on overtime expectations, remote work boundaries, and company culture.
> - At the very end, give me structured feedback on Fluency & Coherence, Lexical Resource (rewarding "unplug after work", "push back on a deadline", "spread oneself too thin"), and Grammatical Range.
> Start now by introducing the Cue Card.
> ```
