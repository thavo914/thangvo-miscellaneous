# Day 2 - Exercise 2: IELTS Speaking Preparation

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 2 (Part A)**: Topic 1 IELTS Speaking Part 1, Part 2 (Cue Card with planning notes), and Part 3 analytical questions.

---

## 🎙️ Part 1: Introductory Interview Questions
Answer each question out loud in 2–4 natural sentences. Aim for smooth pacing and functional clarity.

1. **How do you usually organize your daily working hours?**
   * *Talking Points*: Mention starting with a focus block, listing top priorities, and avoiding checking messages too early.
   * *Target Phrase Integration*: Use `tend to` or `set clear boundaries`.

2. **Is it easy for you to switch off from work in the evening?**
   * *Talking Points*: Discuss whether you check Slack at night, how you disconnect, or steps you take to relax.
   * *Target Phrase Integration*: Use `unplug after work` or `spread oneself too thin`.

3. **What do you do when you have too many tasks to complete in one day?**
   * *Talking Points*: Explain how you prioritize, notify teammates, or reschedule lower-priority items.
   * *Target Phrase Integration*: Use `push back on a deadline` or `keep up with the workload`.

---

## 🎙️ Part 2: Individual Long Turn (Cue Card)

```
Describe a time when you had to manage a very heavy workload or handle work stress.

You should say:
- What the situation was
- Why there was so much work to do
- How you managed or responded to the pressure
- And explain what you learned from that experience about managing your time.
```

### 💡 1-Minute Planning Notes Template:
* **Situation**: *Payment pipeline overhaul + sudden schema break from upstream API.*
* **Reason for load**: *Two team members on leave, multiple ad-hoc queries from sales.*
* **Actions taken**: *Talked with manager, prioritized core fix, pushed back marketing deadline, avoided spreading myself too thin.*
* **Key lesson**: *Setting boundaries early protects system stability and personal health.*

---

## 🎙️ Part 3: Analytical Discussion Questions

1. **Why do so many professionals in fast-paced industries struggle with work-life balance?**
   * *Key Angle*: The constant availability created by laptops and smartphones makes people feel obligated to respond 24/7. People tend to believe that staying online longer equals higher productivity, whereas it often leads to burnout and mistakes.

2. **In your opinion, whose responsibility is it to prevent employee burnout: the individual or the company?**
   * *Key Angle*: It's a shared responsibility. The company must provide realistic workloads and promote a culture where pushing back on unreasonable deadlines is respected. Meanwhile, individuals must take ownership of their personal habits and learn to unplug after work.
