# Day 2 - Exercise 4: Shadowing & Pacing Guide

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 2 (Part C)**: Pronunciation cadence, rhythm markers, and thought-group pausing practice.

---

## 🎼 Interactive Shadowing & Pacing Guide

Read these lines out loud **3–5 times**. Focus on natural pauses (`/`) and dynamic word stress (**BOLD CAPS**).

### Line 1 (Setting Priorities & Boundaries):
> "RIGHT NOW, / my **TOP PRIORITY** is finishing the payment pipeline refactor, / so I **WON'T BE ABLE** to take on new requests / until **NEXT TUESDAY**."

* **Rhythm Cue**: Notice the slight pause after *refactor* to let the listener absorb your primary focus before delivering the boundary.
* **Pitch Motion**: Raise pitch slightly on *TOP PRIORITY*, then maintain a calm, flat-to-falling pitch on *NEXT TUESDAY*.

---

### Line 2 (Advocating for Quality Over Haste):
> "**INSTEAD OF RUSHING** a quick fix / that **BREAKS** in production, / I'd **RATHER** take the time / to build a **RELIABLE** solution."

* **Rhythm Cue**: Emphasize *instead of rushing* and *breaks*, then drop your tone comfortably on *reliable solution*.
* **Breath Mark**: Take a half-breath after *production*.
