# Day 2 - Exercise 1: Warm-up Reflex & Active Listening (35–45 Mins)

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 1 Focus**: Spontaneous timed object pitch (15 mins) + Active listening dictation (20–30 mins).

---

## ⏱️ Part 1: Speaking Reflex - The 60-Second Random Object Pitch (15 Mins)

### Goal
Eliminate mental hesitation and build continuous English fluency without pausing for more than 2 seconds.

### Instructions
1. Pick a random item near your desk (e.g., your water bottle, mechanical keyboard, wrist watch, or noise-canceling headphones).
2. Start a **60-second timer** on your phone.
3. Speak non-stop about the item: describe its physical appearance, why you bought it, how it helps your daily focus, and how it helps you manage your routine.
4. Challenge yourself to include at least one target collocation naturally: `set clear boundaries`, `keep up with the workload`, or `unplug after work`.

### 💡 Worked Example: Noise-Canceling Headphones
> *"On my desk here, I have my black wireless noise-canceling headphones. I bought them about six months ago when our team transitioned to a hybrid work model. In an open-plan office or when working from home, ambient noise can be very distracting. When I put these on, it sends a clear signal to everyone around me that I'm in focus mode. It acts as an easy way to **set clear boundaries** during deep work sessions, helping me **keep up with the workload** and stay calm during busy sprints."*

---

## 🎧 Part 2: Active Listening - TOEIC Dictation (20–30 Mins)

### Goal
Sharpen listening comprehension and word-boundary recognition in professional dialogue.

### Instructions
1. Choose 3 short conversation clips from TOEIC Part 3 or Part 4 audio tests.
2. Transcribe the audio word-for-word on paper or a blank text file.
3. Check against the transcript and note any missed auxiliary verbs, articles, or contracted sounds.
