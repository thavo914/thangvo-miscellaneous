# Day 5: Monologue + Shadowing

## Part A - Monologue (60 min)

### Phase 1 - Warm-up (15 min)
- **Lexical Labeling:** Walk around your room/workspace and describe objects or situations using the episode's vocabulary (e.g., "That messy cable situation is bonkers," "I need to get to the bottom of why my laptop is slow").
- **Syntactic Narration:** Narrate a recent work event aloud using the grammar structures from Day 4 (e.g., "By fixing the server, we...").

### Phase 2 - Recorded Monologue (30 min)
- **Topic:** Why do people believe in conspiracy theories, and how does the rapid spread of information online (similar to data streams) affect this? Relate it to your experience with how fast data moves.
- **Structure:** 
  - Hook (Catch the listener's attention)
  - Core idea mapped to episode theme
  - Example from your professional context (e.g., trying to find truth in messy data)
  - Conclusion
- **Attempt 1:** Record for 2-3 minutes without stopping. Don't worry about mistakes.
- **Listen back:** Note 1-2 things to improve.
- **Attempt 2:** Record again immediately.

### Stage 2 - Transcribed Echoing (15 min)
*Read the transcript. Listen sentence by sentence. Pause the audio and repeat exactly as heard. Focus on the annotations.*

### Stage 3 - Synchronous Shadowing (20 min)
*Read the transcript out loud along with the audio, delaying by about 0.5 seconds. Do 10+ reps.*
- **Round 1 (Accuracy):** Focus on hitting every word.
- **Round 2 (Stress/Connected Speech):** Focus on the rhythm and linked sounds.
- **Round 3 (Emotional Tone):** Match the speaker's confidence and intonation.

### Stage 4 - Blind Shadowing (15 min)
*Put the transcript away. Shadow the audio purely by ear.*
