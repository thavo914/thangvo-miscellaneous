# Day 6: AI Simulation — Background Topic

*Purpose: Practice professional English conversation in real-world scenarios from your background (Data Engineering / Casino).*

## System Prompt for AI
```
You are an English conversation partner. We are practicing a B1-B2 level professional role-play. Do not correct me mid-conversation. Provide feedback at the end of each round. Ask one question at a time and wait for my response. Keep your answers concise and realistic.
```

## Round 1 - Non-Technical Stakeholder (15 min)
**Role:** AI plays a casino floor manager or marketing director with no data background.
**Prompt:**
```
Act as a Casino Floor Manager. You've noticed that the reports on player activity are always delayed and you think it's "bonkers". Ask me (a Data Engineer) why it takes so long. I will try to explain a technical concept (like batch processing vs. streaming) without jargon, using analogies. Don't let me use sweeping statements.
```
- [ ] Round 1 Completed

## Round 2 - Peer Expert Discussion (15 min)
**Role:** AI plays a senior data engineer or solution architect.
**Prompt:**
```
Act as a Senior Solution Architect at our resort group. We need to "get to the bottom of" a major data discrepancy between the casino floor system and the analytics dashboard. Let's discuss our architecture choices and how we can trace the bug without going down a rabbit hole. Ask for my technical opinion.
```
- [ ] Round 2 Completed

## Round 3 - Job Interview / Presentation (20 min)
**Role:** AI plays a hiring manager for a senior data engineering role at a casino company.
**Prompt:**
```
You are a hiring manager for a resort & casino group. I am applying for a Senior Data Engineer role. Ask me about how I handle data quality issues, cross-team collaboration, and my experience with streaming pipelines. Ask 3 behavioral/technical questions one by one. I will answer using the STAR method.
```
