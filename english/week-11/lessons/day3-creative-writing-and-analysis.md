## 📝 1. Creative Dialogue Writing
**Your Task:** Write a professional conversation transcript (at least 8-10 turns total) between you and your manager discussing your promotion to Level 3.
- Try to naturally integrate at least **2 target collocations** (e.g., *step up, salary bump, housing allowance*) and **2 sentence templates** from Day 1.
- Write your draft directly in the space below:

*Write your dialogue here...*
**Manager:** Thanks for coming in. I wanted to review your performance over the last quarter. You've done an excellent job with the new ETL pipelines.
**You:** Thank you! I've really enjoyed the work, and **I feel ready to step up and take on more responsibility.**
**Manager:** That's exactly what I was hoping to hear. We'd like to **promote you to Level 3**, our Senior Data Engineer position.
**You:** That's fantastic news! **If I accept the new role, will I be managing the junior team members?**
**Manager:** Yes, **in this role, you are expected to mentor the junior engineers.** Along with that, your base salary will **increase by 25%**.
**You:** Wow, a 25% **salary bump** is very generous. Are there any changes to the other **perks and benefits**?
**Manager:** Yes, since Level 3 is a senior position, you are now eligible for the **housing allowance**. We will provide you with a **private apartment**.
**You:** **A 25% salary bump and a private apartment? That's amazing!** Thank you so much, I'm fully committed to this new role.

## 🎓 2. Band 6.5 - 7.0 Cue Card Sample Answer
*Here is a realistic model response answering the Day 2 Part 2 Cue Card: **Describe a time when you discussed a promotion, a raise, or a new role with a manager.***

### Model Answer:
> "I'd like to tell you about a crucial 1-on-1 meeting I had with my Technical Director, David, about two weeks ago. We had scheduled a performance review to look back at the ETL pipelines I had built over the last year. 
> 
> During the meeting, David mentioned that he was extremely happy with my output and asked if I felt ready to **step up**. I told him that I was totally prepared to **take on more responsibility**. That's when he offered to promote me to Level 3, which is our Senior Data Engineer tier. We then discussed the **perks and benefits** that come with the new title. He explained that if I accept the new role, my base pay will increase by 25%.
> 
> What really surprised me, though, was the housing benefit. He told me that Level 3 engineers are eligible for a **housing allowance**, meaning I would move out of the shared dorms and get my own **private apartment**. A 25% **salary bump** and a private apartment? That was an amazing offer.
> 
> I felt incredibly proud and relieved. In a high-pressure casino operations environment, it's easy to feel like just a cog in the machine. Having my manager directly recognize my hard work and reward it so generously made me deeply loyal to the team and completely ready to tackle the senior architecture challenges next quarter."

### Key Conversational Phrases Used:
1. **step up** (bước lên, nhận thêm trách nhiệm): *Being ready for a higher role.*
2. **take on more responsibility** (đảm nhận thêm trách nhiệm): *Accepting harder tasks.*
3. **perks and benefits** (phúc lợi): *The non-salary rewards of a job.*
4. **housing allowance / private apartment** (trợ cấp chỗ ở / căn hộ riêng): *Real estate benefits provided by the company.*
5. **salary bump** (tăng lương): *An informal way to say a raise in pay.*
