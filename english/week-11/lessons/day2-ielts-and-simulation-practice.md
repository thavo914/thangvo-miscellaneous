## 🗣️ 1. IELTS Speaking Practice Questions

### Part 1: Interview (Career & Ambitions)
1. *Do you consider yourself an ambitious person when it comes to your career?*
- Yes, I do. I am constantly looking for ways to step up and take on more responsibility in my data engineering role. I set clear goals for myself every quarter.
- I would say I am moderately ambitious. While I enjoy a good work-life balance, I am also working hard to reach the Senior level so I can tackle more complex data pipeline challenges.
Your turn:
- I consider myself a ambitious person when it comes to my career. I am constantly looking for ways to step up and take on more responsibility in my data engineering role. My goal in the next 3 years is to be a Data Architect.
- I am ambitious but not very ambitious. I want to work hard and get promoted, but I also want to have a good work-life balance. 
2. *What is the most important factor for you when looking for a job: the salary, the benefits, or the work itself?*
- For me, it's a balance. The work itself needs to be engaging, but perks and benefits, like a housing allowance or a solid salary bump, are crucial motivators that make me feel valued.
- To be honest, a good salary bump is very important. However, as long as I have enough to live comfortably, I prioritize working on exciting cloud data infrastructure.
Your turn:
- For me, it depends on the situation. If I am young and single, I would prioritize the work itself. However, if I have a family, I would prioritize the salary and benefits.
- Right now, I think salary is the most important factor for me. I'm in my early 30s and I want to earn more money to have a good life.
- For me, it should be a balance. I think the most important factor is a good salary, but the work itself should also be interesting and challenging. 
3. *Do you think it is common for companies in your country to offer housing benefits like a private apartment?*
- Not really. Most companies just offer a base salary. That's why being promoted to Level 3 and receiving a private apartment is such a fantastic opportunity for me.
- It depends on the industry. In the casino and hospitality sector, it is more common for senior roles to receive a housing allowance to ensure they stay close to the operations center.
Your turn:
- I don't think it is common for companies in Vietnam to offer housing benefits like a private apartment. All my previous companies just offer a base salary. I'm working at an intergrated resort company and it's far from down town so it's very convenient for me to live in the provided housing area.
- In the IT industry, it's rare for companies to offer housing benefits like a private apartment. Most companies just offer a base salary.
- I think it is becoming more common, especially for expat workers or senior management positions. Companies understand that a good housing package helps attract top talent.


### Part 2: Cue Card (Long Turn)
> **Describe a time when you discussed a promotion, a raise, or a new role with a manager.**
> 
> You should say:
> - Who you spoke to and when it happened
> - What specific benefits or responsibilities you discussed
> - How you prepared for the conversation
> - And explain how you felt during and after the discussion.

**Model Idea for Data Engineers:**
I would like to talk about a recent 1-on-1 meeting I had with my technical manager, David, just last week. We sat down to discuss my performance over the past year and my goal of becoming a Senior Data Engineer. I prepared for the meeting by writing down all the successful data pipelines I had built and how they improved the company's analytics speed. During the conversation, we discussed the possibility of me stepping up to Level 3. He mentioned that if I take on more responsibility, my salary will increase by 25%, and I would also be eligible for a private apartment instead of shared staff housing. I was quite nervous beforehand, but seeing how much he valued my work made me feel incredibly proud and motivated to tackle the new challenges.
Your turn:
- I would like to talk about an annual performance review I had with my manager last year. It was a crucial conversation that helped shape my career path. I had been working at the company for 2 years and I felt ready to take on more responsibility. My team was having a new internal project that used new technology to replace the legacy system. I was very excited to work on this project because it was a good opportunity for me to learn new things and improve my skills. I spent a lot of time researching the new technology and I was able to come up with a plan to implement it in the next quarter. I also discussed with my manager about the potential promotion to senior position if I did a good job. This conversation made me realize that I was on the right track and I was confident that I could achieve my goals. Overall, I felt very happy and motivated after the discussion. 
### Part 3: Analytical Discussion (Deeper Questions)
1. *Some people argue that taking on more responsibility at work just leads to more stress, regardless of the salary bump. Do you agree?*
 - It can definitely lead to stress if you are not prepared. However, if the company provides adequate support and clear boundaries, taking on more responsibility is the only way to grow professionally. The salary bump and perks are the rewards for managing that extra pressure.
Your turn:
- I agree and disagree with this statement. On the one hand, taking on more responsibility at work can definitely lead to more stress. You may have to work longer hours, deal with more complex problems, and be responsible for the success or failure of your projects. However, if the company provides adequate support and clear boundaries, taking on more responsibility is the only way to grow professionally. The salary bump and perks are the rewards for managing that extra pressure.
- Well, I think it's a double-edged sword. In the modern day, dealing with stress is a common thing. It's not just about working harder but also about working smarter. We need to find ways to manage our time and energy effectively so that we can handle the extra pressure without burning out. On the other hand, we should also set boundaries to avoid overworking ourselves. We need to remember that taking rest is also important like working.
- I agree that taking on more responsibility often leads to more stress. When you are given a new role, you are expected to handle more tasks, meet higher expectations, and deliver better results. This can be challenging, especially if you are not used to the new responsibilities. However, the salary bump and other perks can help compensate for the extra stress. It really depends on the individual and their ability to handle pressure. For me, I think I can handle the extra stress as long as I get a fair compensation in return.
2. *How do employee benefits, like housing allowances or flexible hours, impact an employee's loyalty to a company?*
 - They have a massive impact. When a company provides a private apartment or pays for health insurance, it shows they care about the employee's well-being outside of the office. This makes it much harder for an employee to leave for a competitor.
Your turn:
- I think they have a huge impact on an employee's loyalty to a company. When the housing price gets higher and higher, the employee will find it hard to rent or buy a house if they just get a normal salary. So, housing allowance is a really attractive benefit for employees. Besides, flexible hours also help employees balance their work and life, which can increase their loyalty to the company. 
- I totally agree with that. When employees feel valued and supported by their company, they are more likely to be loyal and committed to their work.

## 🎵 4. Interactive Shadowing & Pacing Guide
*Practice reading these lines out loud. Slashes `/` represent brief natural pauses. **UPPERCASE** words indicate word stress and emphasis.*

### Line 1 (You - Expressing Readiness):
> "Thank you. // I really feel READY / to STEP UP / and take on MORE responsibility. // I'm aiming for the SENIOR Data Engineer role."

### Line 2 (Manager - Offering the Promotion):
> "Well / FIRST of all / your base pay will INCREASE / by twenty-five PERCENT. // On TOP of that / you will get a PRIVATE apartment / instead of SHARED housing."

### Line 3 (You - Accepting the Offer):
> "A twenty-five percent salary BUMP / and a PRIVATE apartment? // That's AMAZING! // I'm COMPLETELY ready / to take on the new CHALLENGES."
