## 🗣️ 1. IELTS Speaking Practice Questions

### Part 1: Interview (Career & Ambitions)
1. *Do you consider yourself an ambitious person when it comes to your career?*
- Yes, I do. I am constantly looking for ways to step up and take on more responsibility in my data engineering role. I set clear goals for myself every quarter.
- I would say I am moderately ambitious. While I enjoy a good work-life balance, I am also working hard to reach the Senior level so I can tackle more complex data pipeline challenges.
2. *What is the most important factor for you when looking for a job: the salary, the benefits, or the work itself?*
- For me, it's a balance. The work itself needs to be engaging, but perks and benefits, like a housing allowance or a solid salary bump, are crucial motivators that make me feel valued.
- To be honest, a good salary bump is very important. However, as long as I have enough to live comfortably, I prioritize working on exciting cloud data infrastructure.
3. *Do you think it is common for companies in your country to offer housing benefits like a private apartment?*
- Not really. Most companies just offer a base salary. That's why being promoted to Level 3 and receiving a private apartment is such a fantastic opportunity for me.
- It depends on the industry. In the casino and hospitality sector, it is more common for senior roles to receive a housing allowance to ensure they stay close to the operations center.

### Part 2: Cue Card (Long Turn)
> **Describe a time when you discussed a promotion, a raise, or a new role with a manager.**
> 
> You should say:
> - Who you spoke to and when it happened
> - What specific benefits or responsibilities you discussed
> - How you prepared for the conversation
> - And explain how you felt during and after the discussion.

**Model Idea for Data Engineers:**
I would like to talk about a recent 1-on-1 meeting I had with my technical manager, David, just last week. We sat down to discuss my performance over the past year and my goal of becoming a Senior Data Engineer. I prepared for the meeting by writing down all the successful data pipelines I had built and how they improved the company's analytics speed. During the conversation, we discussed the possibility of me stepping up to Level 3. He mentioned that if I take on more responsibility, my salary will increase by 25%, and I would also be eligible for a private apartment instead of shared staff housing. I was quite nervous beforehand, but seeing how much he valued my work made me feel incredibly proud and motivated to tackle the new challenges.

### Part 3: Analytical Discussion (Deeper Questions)
1. *Some people argue that taking on more responsibility at work just leads to more stress, regardless of the salary bump. Do you agree?*
 - It can definitely lead to stress if you are not prepared. However, if the company provides adequate support and clear boundaries, taking on more responsibility is the only way to grow professionally. The salary bump and perks are the rewards for managing that extra pressure.
2. *How do employee benefits, like housing allowances or flexible hours, impact an employee's loyalty to a company?*
 - They have a massive impact. When a company provides a private apartment or pays for health insurance, it shows they care about the employee's well-being outside of the office. This makes it much harder for an employee to leave for a competitor.

## 🎵 4. Interactive Shadowing & Pacing Guide
*Practice reading these lines out loud. Slashes `/` represent brief natural pauses. **UPPERCASE** words indicate word stress and emphasis.*

### Line 1 (You - Expressing Readiness):
> "Thank you. // I really feel READY / to STEP UP / and take on MORE responsibility. // I'm aiming for the SENIOR Data Engineer role."

### Line 2 (Manager - Offering the Promotion):
> "Well / FIRST of all / your base pay will INCREASE / by twenty-five PERCENT. // On TOP of that / you will get a PRIVATE apartment / instead of SHARED housing."

### Line 3 (You - Accepting the Offer):
> "A twenty-five percent salary BUMP / and a PRIVATE apartment? // That's AMAZING! // I'm COMPLETELY ready / to take on the new CHALLENGES."
