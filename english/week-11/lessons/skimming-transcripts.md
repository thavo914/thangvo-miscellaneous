# Week 11 - Skimming & Reading Fluency Transcripts

This document contains two 60-second video transcripts designed for rapid reading practice, shadowing, and vocabulary reinforcement. 

## 🎯 How to use this file
1. **Speed Reading:** Set a timer for 10 seconds. Try to skim the visual-aid boxes and identify the main topic before reading the full text.
2. **Shadowing:** Read the transcript out loud while mimicking the natural pauses (/) and stressed words (**BOLD**).
3. **Active Recall:** Cover the transcript and try to summarize the main points using the target collocations.

## 📹 Video Transcript 2: Career Mentorship: How to Become a Data Architect
*(Context: A Senior Architect giving advice on a professional networking video.)*

### ⏱️ 10-Second Skimming Visual Aid
> **Main Idea:** Moving from Engineer to Architect.
> **Key Action:** Looking at the big picture.
> **Result:** Bridging the gap between business and tech.

### 📝 Full Transcript

"So, / you're a Data Engineer, / and you're looking to **transition into** a Data Architect role. / How do you do it? / 

First, / you need to understand that an engineer focuses on building pipelines, / but an architect is much more strategic. / Instead of just writing code all day, / you need to look at the **big picture**. / **Designing** large-scale systems / requires a deep understanding of **scalability** and security. / 

Second, / you must learn to **bridge the gap**. / This is crucial. / You have to bridge the gap / between what the business stakeholders want / and what the engineering team can actually build. / You are the translator. / 

My best advice? / Start **making architectural decisions** in your current projects. / Don't just ask 'how do I code this', / ask 'why are we building this'. / Master that, / and you will be well on your way to becoming an architect."
