## 🗣️ 4. Core Lesson Dialogues

### Dialogue 1: Topic 1 (Promotion to Senior Data Engineer)
**Context:** You (Data Engineer) are having a 1-on-1 performance review with your Manager.

```text
Manager: Great work this quarter. Your data pipelines have been rock solid. I think it's time we discuss your next career step.
You: Thank you. I really feel ready to step up and take on more responsibility. I'm aiming for the Senior Data Engineer role.
Manager: I agree. We are planning to promote you to Level 3. If you move to Level 3, you'll be leading the new streaming project.
You: That sounds exciting. What about the perks and benefits associated with Level 3?
Manager: Well, first of all, your base pay will increase by 25%. On top of that, you will get a private apartment instead of shared housing.
You: A 25% salary bump and a private apartment? That's amazing! I'm completely ready to take on the new challenges.
Manager: Perfect. I'll get the paperwork started. Congratulations on the promotion!
```

---

### Dialogue 2: Topic 2 (Transitioning to Data Architect)
**Context:** You are grabbing coffee with a Lead Data Architect to get career advice.

```text
You: Hey, do you have a minute? I wanted to ask your advice on how to transition into a Data Architect role.
Architect: Sure! Making the jump from Data Engineer to Architect is exciting. Have you thought about what it requires?
You: I know I need to be less hands-on with coding and focus more on system design.
Architect: Exactly. You need to look at the big picture. An engineer builds the pipeline, but an architect decides why and how it should be built.
You: How can I start preparing for that?
Architect: Start by making architectural decisions in your current projects. Focus on scalability and security. You also need to bridge the gap between our business stakeholders and the engineering team.
You: So, I need to improve my communication skills and my system design knowledge.
Architect: That's right. Designing large-scale systems requires a lot of planning and clear communication. Let's review one of your designs next week.
You: Thanks so much! I'll put some ideas together.
```
