# Weekly English Exercise Schedule (3-Hour Focus)

Based on the `generate-english-lesson` workflow, here is the detailed breakdown of exercises across the 6-day cycle.

| Day | Block 1: Warm-up & Reflex (35-45m) | Block 2: Workplace Practice (60m) | Blocks 3 & 4: AI Speaking & Review (75m) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Day 1**<br>*(General Topic)* | • **Speaking (15m):** The Daily Commentator<br>• **Listening (20-30m):** TOEIC Dictation (3 Exercises) | • **Grammar Study:** 3 Target Rules<br>• **Vocabulary:** 5 Sentence Templates & 5 Collocations<br>• **Drills:** Production, Translation, Spot the Mistake | • **AI Speaking:** Practice with AI using today's lessons<br>• **Review:** Anki flashcard logging & error log |
| **Day 2**<br>*(General Topic)* | • **Speaking (15m):** The 60-Second Random Object Pitch<br>• **Listening (20-30m):** TOEIC Dictation (3 Exercises) | • **IELTS Practice:** Part 1, Part 2 (Cue Card), Part 3<br>• **Drills:** Interactive Shadowing & Pacing Guide | • **AI Speaking:** ChatGPT Voice Mode Roleplay (IELTS Simulation)<br>• **Review:** Feedback logging & Anki review |
| **Day 3**<br>*(General Topic)* | • **Speaking (15m):** Unconventional AI Voice Roleplay<br>• **Listening (20-30m):** TOEIC Dictation (3 Exercises) | • **Writing:** Creative Dialogue Writing (10+ turns)<br>• **Speaking Prep:** General Topic Monologue<br>• **Analysis:** Band 6.5-7.0 Cue Card Model Answer | • **AI Speaking:** Practice with AI using today's dialogue/monologue<br>• **Review:** Weekly portfolio log completion & Evaluation Checklist |
| **Day 4**<br>*(Tech Topic)* | • **Listening (20-30m):** TOEIC Dictation (3 Exercises) | • **Grammar Study:** 3 Target Rules (Tech Context)<br>• **Vocabulary:** 5 Sentence Templates & 5 Tech Collocations<br>• **Drills:** Production, Translation, Spot the Mistake | • **AI Speaking:** Practice with AI using today's tech lessons<br>• **Review:** Anki flashcard logging & error log |
| **Day 5**<br>*(Tech Topic)* | • **Speaking (15m):** The "Devil's Advocate" Challenge<br>• **Listening (20-30m):** TOEIC Dictation (3 Exercises) | • **IELTS Practice:** Part 1, Part 2 (Cue Card), Part 3 (Tech Context)<br>• **Drills:** Interactive Shadowing & Pacing Guide | • **AI Speaking:** ChatGPT Voice Mode Roleplay (Tech Simulation)<br>• **Review:** Feedback logging & Anki review |
| **Day 6**<br>*(Mock Test)* | • **Warm-up:** Mental Prep<br>• **Listening (45m):** Full TOEIC Listening Test | • **Full Test (60m):** IELTS Reading, Writing, or Speaking Practice Test | • **Review (75m):** Detailed mistake analysis and error logging<br>• **Wrap-up:** Weekly portfolio log completion |
