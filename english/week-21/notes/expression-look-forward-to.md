# 📘 Week 21 Curriculum Note: `look forward to` vs. `looking forward to`

> **Target Week**: Week 21  
> **Type**: Phrasal Verb & Professional Email / Workplace Communication Formula  
> **Core Concept**: To anticipate a future event or response with pleasure or positive expectation (`look forward to + V-ing / Noun`). *(Vietnamese: Trông mong, mong đợi điều gì đó với thái độ tích cực; phân biệt sắc thái từ trang trọng trong email đến thân mật trên Slack).*

---

## 🌟 1. Core Explanation & Mechanics

### Grammatical Structure & Core Formula

$$\mathbf{[Subject] + look(s)\ /\ 'm\ looking + forward\ to\ +\ [Noun\ Phrase\ /\ Verb\text{-}ing]}$$

### ⚠️ Quy tắc ngữ pháp số 1: Giới từ `TO` + `V-ing` (The #1 English Trap)
* Chữ **`to`** trong cụm này là **Giới từ (Preposition)**, hoàn toàn **không phải** là "to-infinitive".
* Vì là giới từ, từ đứng sau bắt buộc phải là **Danh từ (Noun Phrase)** hoặc **Danh động từ (Gerund `V-ing`)**:
  * ✅ *"I look forward to **hearing** from you."*
  * ✅ *"I look forward to **our meeting**."*
  * ❌ *Tuyệt đối không dùng*: *"I look forward to hear from you."*
  * ❌ *Tuyệt đối không dùng*: *"I look forward to see you."*

---

### Phân biệt 3 cấp độ sắc thái (Tone & Formality Spectrum)

| Dạng thức | Mức độ trang trọng | Ngữ cảnh sử dụng phù hợp nhất | Ví dụ thực tế |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Present Simple**<br>`I look forward to + V-ing` | 👔 **Trang trọng, chuyên nghiệp, lịch thiệp** | Email gửi khách hàng, đối tác bên ngoài, thư ứng tuyển tuyển dụng, email thông báo chính thức. | *"I **look forward to hearing** from you soon."*<br>*"We **look forward to working** with your team on this engagement."* |
| **2. Present Continuous**<br>`I'm looking forward to + V-ing` | 🤝 **Thân thiện, ấm áp, cởi mở** | Email nội bộ giữa đồng nghiệp, trao đổi sprint, chuẩn bị 1-on-1, nói chuyện trực tiếp (1-on-1, sync meeting). | *"I'**m really looking forward to collaborating** on the new pipeline."*<br>*"I'**m looking forward to our catch-up** tomorrow."* |
| **3. Lược bỏ chủ ngữ**<br>`Looking forward to + V-ing` | 💬 **Thân mật, nhanh gọn, hiện đại** | Tin nhắn Slack, Microsoft Teams, câu kết email ngắn với đồng nghiệp thân thiết. | *"**Looking forward to hearing** your feedback!"*<br>*"**Looking forward to catching up** tomorrow!"* |
| **4. Present Perfect Continuous**<br>`I've been looking forward to...` | ⏳ **Mong đợi từ lâu** | Nói về một sự kiện bạn đã háo hức chờ đợi suốt một thời gian dài (hội thảo, kỳ nghỉ, trận bóng). | *"I'**ve been looking forward to this tech summit** all quarter."* |

## 📚 2. Categorized Usages & Real-Life Examples

### Domain A: Professional Emails & Stakeholder Communications (`I look forward to...`)
* **Ending an Email Requesting Feedback**:
  * *"Please let me know if you need any adjustments to the architectural proposal. I **look forward to hearing from you**."*
* **Partner / Client Kickoff**:
  * *"Thank you for your prompt response. We **look forward to a productive partnership** over the coming months."*
* **Candidate / Interview Follow-up**:
  * *"Thank you for taking the time to speak with me today. I **look forward to hearing about the next steps**."*

### Domain B: Teammates, Sprint Planning & Slack Messages (`Looking forward to...`)
* **Reviewing Pull Requests / Proposals on Slack**:
  * *"I just pushed the Silver pipeline changes to GitHub. **Looking forward to your review and feedback**!"*
* **Upcoming 1-on-1 Catch-ups**:
  * *"Thanks for setting up the invite. I'**m looking forward to our 1-on-1 chat** on Thursday."*
* **Welcoming a New Teammate**:
  * *"Welcome aboard! We'**re all looking forward to working with you** on the streaming platform."*

### Domain C: Daily Life, Personal Plans & Sports
* **Weekend Plans & Resting**:
  * *"It has been an exhausting sprint; I'**m really looking forward to unplugging this weekend**."*
* **Sports & Premier League Banter (Liverpool FC)**:
  * *"Liverpool is playing a massive derby match on Sunday, and I'**ve been looking forward to it all week**."*
* **Running & Marathon Milestones**:
  * *"After weeks of base building, I'**m looking forward to running my first half-marathon** next month."*

## ⚠️ 3. Common Pitfalls & Vietlish Traps

| ❌ Common Mistake / Awkward Phrasing | ✅ Natural Native English | Why / Linguistic Reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *I look forward to hear from you.* | *I look forward to **hearing** from you.* | Lỗi phổ biến nhất. `to` là giới từ, sau nó bắt buộc là `V-ing`. |
| *I am looking forward for your reply.* | *I am looking forward **to** your reply.* | Giới từ cố định luôn là `to`, không dùng `for`. |
| *I look forward to see you tomorrow on Slack.* | *Looking forward to **seeing you** tomorrow!* / *Talk soon!* | Dùng `I look forward to...` trong chat nội bộ nghe quá xa cách và cứng nhắc như viết công văn; nên dùng `Looking forward to...` hoặc `See you tomorrow`. |
| *I wait your reply.* / *I am waiting for your reply.* | *I **look forward to your reply**.* / *Hope to hear from you soon.* | "Waiting for your reply" nghe có cảm giác hối thúc, sốt ruột hoặc gây áp lực. "Look forward to" tạo cảm giác lịch sự, tích cực. |
| *I look forward to meet you.* | *I look forward to **meeting** you.* | Động từ sau `to` phải thêm đuôi `-ing`. |

## 🎯 4. Lesson Integration Blueprint (For `/generate-english-lesson`)

### A. Grammar / Template Slot Formulas:
* **Formula 1 (Formal Email Sign-off)**:
  * `I look forward to + [hearing from you / receiving your feedback] + [regarding / on] + [Project/Topic].`
  * *Workplace Example*: *"I look forward to hearing from you regarding our proposed cloud budget."*
* **Formula 2 (Friendly Peer Collaboration)**:
  * `I'm really looking forward to + [collaborating / working] + with + [Team/Person] + to + [Base Verb Goal].`
  * *Engineering Example*: *"I'm really looking forward to working with the infra team to roll out our new Spark cluster."*
* **Formula 3 (Quick Chat Sign-off)**:
  * `Looking forward to + [catching up / syncing up] + [Time Period]!`
  * *Slack Example*: *"Looking forward to syncing up at 2:00 PM!"*

### B. Practice Drills:
* **Drill 1 (Official Email to Vendor)**:
  * **Drill Prompt**: You just sent an email to a software vendor asking for their licensing quote. Write a polite concluding sentence using the target phrase.
  * **Suggested Answer**: *"Please review the user requirements attached above. I look forward to hearing from you with the updated quotation."*
* **Drill 2 (Casual Chat to Teammate)**:
  * **Drill Prompt**: Send a quick Slack message to a colleague you will meet tomorrow morning for coffee before standup.
  * **Suggested Answer**: *"Looking forward to catching up over coffee tomorrow morning before the standup!"*

### C. Translation Prompt (Vietnamese to English):
* **Prompt**: *"Tôi rất mong nhận được phản hồi từ bạn về bản đề xuất kiến trúc dữ liệu mới này."*
* **Suggested Native Answer**: *"I look forward to hearing your feedback on this new data architecture proposal."*

### D. AI Speaking Simulation Prompt:
* *"In ChatGPT Voice Mode, act as an engineering director concluding a bi-weekly sync with me. Conclude the meeting by saying you'll review my RFC document tonight. I will practice closing naturally by using 'Looking forward to hearing your thoughts' or 'I look forward to your feedback'."*
