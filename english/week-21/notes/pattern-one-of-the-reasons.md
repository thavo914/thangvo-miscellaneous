# 📘 Week 21 Curriculum Note: `One of the reasons [I did X] was because of [Y] that was included`

> **Target Week**: Week 21  
> **Type**: Sentence Structure & Justification Formula (Decision-making & Functional Self-Explanation)  
> **Core Concept**: To clearly justify a technical, workplace, or personal purchasing decision by highlighting a bundled benefit, service, or warranty (`One of the reasons [Subject] + [Verb] + was because of [Noun] that was included`). *(Vietnamese: Một trong những lý do tôi [mua/chọn/nâng cấp cái đó] là vì [quyền lợi/gói bảo hành/tính năng] được đính kèm đi cùng).*

---

## 🌟 1. Core Explanation & Mechanics

### Grammatical Structure & Core Patterns

$$\mathbf{Pattern\ 1:\ One\ of\ the\ reasons\ [Subject] + [Past\ Verb]\ + was\ because\ of\ the\ [Item]\ that\ was\ included}$$
$$\mathbf{Pattern\ 2\ (Concise):\ One\ of\ the\ reasons\ [Subject] + [Past\ Verb]\ + was\ the\ included\ [Item]}$$
$$\mathbf{Pattern\ 3\ (That-Clause):\ One\ of\ the\ reasons\ [Subject] + [Past\ Verb]\ + was\ that\ [Item]\ was\ included}$$

### 3 Golden Grammar Rules of This Structure

1. **`One of the REASONS` (Always Plural)**:
   - Sau cụm `One of the...`, danh từ bắt buộc phải ở dạng **số nhiều** (`reasons`), vì bạn đang chọn ra "một trong số nhiều lý do".
   - ❌ *Never say*: *"One of the reason..."*

2. **Subject-Verb Agreement (`One... was`)**:
   - Chủ ngữ ngữ pháp chính của toàn bộ câu là **`One`** (số ít), **không phải** `reasons`.
   - Vì vậy, động từ to be bắt buộc phải chia số ít: **`was`** (quá khứ) hoặc **`is`** (hiện tại).
   - ❌ *Never say*: *"One of the reasons I bought it were..."*

3. **Tense Harmony (Sự hòa hợp về thì)**:
   - Nếu hành động mua/chọn đã diễn ra trong quá khứ (`bought`, `chose`, `picked`, `upgraded`), toàn bộ câu phải thống nhất ở quá khứ:
     - `bought` ➔ `was` ➔ `was included`.
   - Nếu áp dụng cho nguyên tắc chung hoặc lý do hiện tại:
     - *"One of the reasons I **like** this cloud tier **is** because automatic backup **is included**."*

## 📚 2. Categorized Usages & Real-Life Examples

### Domain A: Vendor Selection, Cloud Providers & Software Licensing
* **Enterprise Support & SLAs**:
  * *"One of the reasons we chose this database vendor **was because of the 24/7 priority support that was included** in the contract."*
* **Cloud Credits & Bundled Services**:
  * *"One of the reasons our startup migrated to AWS **was because of the $50,000 in cloud credits that were included** with the accelerator program."*
* **SaaS Tier Upgrades**:
  * *"One of the reasons we upgraded to the Business plan **was that automated compliance logging was included** by default."*
* **Training & Onboarding**:
  * *"One of the reasons we went with this BI platform **was because of the hands-on engineer training that was included**."*

### Domain B: Workplace Hardware & Office Equipment
* **External Displays & Ergonomics**:
  * *"One of the reasons I ordered this Dell monitor **was because of the built-in USB-C hub and power delivery that was included**."*
* **Laptops & Extended Protection**:
  * *"One of the reasons the IT department issued ThinkPads **was because of the next-business-day on-site repair that was included**."*
* **Peripherals & Bundles**:
  * *"One of the reasons I picked this desk bundle **was the heavy-duty cable management tray that was included**."*

### Domain C: Personal Purchases & Household Appliances (The User's Core Sentence)
* **Smart TVs & Electronics (Direct Example)**:
  * *"I had several TV models to consider, but **one of the reasons I bought it was because of the three-year warranty that was included**."*
* **Home Appliances & Free Installation**:
  * *"One of the reasons we bought this washing machine **was because free delivery and installation were included**."*
* **Gym Memberships & Lifestyle**:
  * *"One of the reasons I signed up for this fitness club **was because of the free coaching session that was included** each month."*

## ⚠️ 3. Common Pitfalls & Vietlish Traps

| ❌ Common Mistake / Awkward Phrasing | ✅ Natural Native English | Why / Linguistic Reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *One of the reason I bought it was...* | *One of the **reasons** I bought it was...* | Sau `one of the`, danh từ bắt buộc phải là số nhiều (`reasons`). |
| *One of the reasons I bought it were...* | *One of the reasons I bought it **was**...* | Chủ ngữ chính là `One` (số ít), không chia động từ theo `reasons`. |
| *One of the reasons I bought it was because the warranty was include.* | *...was because of the warranty that was **included**.* | Cần dùng thể bị động dạng tính từ / quá khứ phân từ (`included`) vì gói bảo hành "được đính kèm/bao gồm". |
| *The reason I bought it because it has warranty.* | *The reason I bought it **was that** it came with a warranty.* | Tránh thiếu động từ to be (`was`) nối giữa chủ ngữ `The reason...` và vế vị ngữ. |
| *One reason make me buy it is warranty.* | *One of the reasons I **bought it was the included warranty**.* | Câu gốc dịch thô từng từ từ tiếng Việt ("Một lý do làm tôi mua là..."). Mẫu câu chuẩn tự nhiên là `One of the reasons I bought it was...`. |

## 🎯 4. Lesson Integration Blueprint (For `/generate-english-lesson`)

### A. Grammar / Template Slot Formulas:
* **Formula 1 (Full Justification with Relative Clause)**:
  * `One of the reasons [Subject] + [Past Verb] + [Object] + was because of the + [Perk/Feature] + that was included.`
  * *Engineering Example*: *"One of the reasons we migrated to Snowflake was because of the automated clustering that was included."*
* **Formula 2 (Concise Adjective Variation)**:
  * `One of the reasons [Subject] + decided to + [Base Verb] + was the included + [Noun Phrase].`
  * *Workplace Example*: *"One of the reasons our team decided to buy this monitoring tool was the included Slack alert integration."*

### B. Practice Drills:
* **Drill 1 (Technical Vendor Decision)**:
  * **Drill Prompt**: During an architecture review, explain why your team chose a particular managed Kafka provider over self-hosting. Mention that automated disaster recovery was included.
  * **Suggested Answer**: *"One of the reasons we chose the managed Kafka provider was because of the multi-region disaster recovery that was included out of the box."*
* **Drill 2 (Workplace Hardware Justification)**:
  * **Drill Prompt**: Explain to your engineering manager why you requested a specific workstation model. Mention that a 3-year accidental damage warranty was included.
  * **Suggested Answer**: *"One of the reasons I requested this specific laptop was because of the three-year accidental damage protection that was included."*
