## 🎙️ Section 1: Workplace Monologue: Navigating High-Workload Cycles

*Context: An engineer reflects during a team retrospective on how they survived a demanding quarterly sprint while staying healthy.*

> "Whenever our engineering squad enters a major migration window, everyone knows that **these next few months will be a lot of work**. There are always unexpected schema mismatches, cluster alerts, and urgent stakeholder requests flying in from every direction.
> 
> In my earlier years, whenever someone took time off, I would automatically try to **pick up the slack** without saying a word. I’d log back in after dinner, skip my evening runs, and spend hours troubleshooting edge cases. But after a while, you start to burn out, and you genuinely **wonder if it's worth it**.
> 
> What changed my perspective was realizing that true professional maturity isn't about being a martyr. It’s about communication. Now, when an ad-hoc request comes in during a crunch week, I don't hesitate to say that our team is simply **not going to be able to** deliver it without compromising our primary sprint goals. Sticking to clear **company policy** and communicating our real capacity protects both system stability and team morale. We support our teammates when they need help, but we never **take our teammates' goodwill for granted**."

### 📚 Key Vocabulary & Concepts from Monologue
* **these next few months will be a lot of work**: Mấy tháng tới sẽ rất nhiều việc (lưu ý trật tự từ và danh từ *work* không đếm được).
* **pick up the slack**: Cáng đáng / gánh vác phần việc dôi ra khi thiếu người.
* **wonder if it's worth it**: Tự hỏi liệu có bõ công / đáng công sức bỏ ra hay không.
* **not going to be able to**: Sẽ không thể thu xếp làm được gì (từ chối khéo léo).
* **company policy**: Chính sách / quy định làm việc của công ty (danh từ bổ nghĩa).
* **take someone's goodwill for granted**: Coi thiện chí giúp đỡ của người khác là điều hiển nhiên.

## 📋 Section 2: Band 6.5–7.0 Model Answer (Day 2 Cue Card)

*Prompt: Describe a time when you had to evaluate and choose between multiple service or product options for an important event or project.*

### Model Answer
> *"I'd like to share an experience from last quarter when our data engineering squad had to evaluate cloud data storage providers for our historical analytics lake. Our existing on-premise relational database was running out of disk space, so our architect asked me to research modern cloud options.
> 
> Over the course of two weeks, I scheduled technical demos with three different cloud vendors. During the sessions, their solution architects **walked us through all of the options** available for distributed columnar storage. We looked closely at three main candidates—Snowflake, BigQuery, and Databricks—**all of which** offered seamless autoscaling and pay-as-you-go pricing models.
> 
> What made the decision straightforward was how our team weighed the trade-offs. One vendor presented an attractive commercial proposal: if our company signed an enterprise agreement **by the end of the quarter**, we would **get 25% off our compute costs**. Furthermore, their customer success lead told us: '**I have no doubt that** your ingestion pipeline latency will drop by half within the first month.'
> 
> Ultimately, we selected their platform because it offered the best balance between ease of use and cost efficiency. Looking back, going through all those options methodically was completely **worth it**, as our daily dashboards now refresh three times faster without any manual babysitting."*

### 💡 High-Yield Collocations to Note:
* **walked us through all of the options**: Dẫn dắt chúng tôi đi qua toàn bộ các lựa chọn.
* **all of which offered...**: Mà tất cả các giải pháp đó đều cung cấp... (mệnh đề quan hệ chỉ số lượng, tránh lỗi phẩy nối câu *all of them*).
* **by the end of the quarter**: Hạn chót trước khi kết thúc quý.
* **get 25% off our compute costs**: Được giảm 25% chi phí điện toán.
* **completely worth it**: Hoàn toàn bõ công / đáng giá.
