# Day 3 - Exercise 5: AI Speaking Simulation & Daily Review

> [!NOTE]
> **Blocks 3 & 4 (75 Mins)**: Put today's conversation transcreation and boundary-setting patterns into realistic conversational practice with AI, followed by logging and Anki review.

## 🎙️ Block 3: AI Spoken Simulation with ChatGPT Voice Mode (60 Mins)

Open ChatGPT on your phone or desktop, activate **Voice Mode 🎧**, and practice today's workplace conversation:

### Day 3 Primary Voice Scenario: Workload & Boundary Negotiation
> *"Act as my engineering colleague during an afternoon coffee break. Mention that the next few months will be very intense due to multiple pipeline rollouts. I will practice discussing workload realities using: 'These next few months will be a lot of work', 'wonder if it's worth it', 'pick up the slack', and 'not going to be able to'. Correct my spoken grammar and suggest punchier native phrases after each response."*

---

> **Suggested Exercises (AI Prompts):**
>
> 1. **Rapid Q&A:**
>    > "Act as my English tutor. Ask me random English questions ranging from easy to advanced (topics: daily life, work, opinions, etc.). I will answer in English as quickly as possible. After I answer, please correct my grammar and vocabulary, and suggest more natural, native-like ways to express my ideas."
>
> 2. **Instant Translation (Reverse Shadowing):**
>    > "Give me a common Vietnamese situation or daily phrase. I will try to translate it into English out loud within 3–5 seconds. After my attempt, please provide the most natural English translation so I can compare."
>
> 3. **Role-play:**
>    > "Let's do a role-play. Please choose one of these topics: ordering at a restaurant, a job interview, traveling/at the airport, or discussing work with a colleague. We will have a back-and-forth conversation entirely in English."

## 📝 Block 4: Review, Error Logging & Consolidation (15 Mins)

### 1. Spoken Error Logging Table

| What I Said | What Sounded Unnatural | Native Upgrade |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *These next months have many works...* | Countable noun error | *These next few months will be **a lot of work**...* |
| *I wonder if it has worth...* | Literal translation | *I wonder **if it's worth it**...* |
| *I cannot do that per company's policy...* | Blunt and stiff possessive | *We're **not going to be able to** take that on, per **company policy**...* |

### 2. Anki Flashcard Candidates (Top 3 for Today)
* **Front**: *Mấy tháng tới chắc chắn sẽ rất nhiều việc.*  
  **Back**: *"These next few months will be a lot of work."*
* **Front**: *Chúng tôi sẽ gánh bớt phần việc dôi ra cho anh ấy.*  
  **Back**: *"We will pick up the slack for him."*
* **Front**: *Đừng bao giờ coi sự giúp đỡ của đồng nghiệp là điều hiển nhiên.*  
  **Back**: *"Never take your colleagues' support for granted."*
