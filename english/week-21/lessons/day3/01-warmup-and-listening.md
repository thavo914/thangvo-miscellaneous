# Day 3 - Exercise 1: Warm-up Reflex & Active Listening

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 1 (35–45 Mins)**: Spoken improv roleplay to break hesitation, followed by high-intensity word-for-word audio dictation.

---

## 🤖 Part A: Speaking Reflex — Unconventional AI Voice Roleplay (15 Mins)

### Objective
Step into a high-energy, slightly quirky situational roleplay. The goal is to think on your feet, assert realistic workplace boundaries, and avoid freezing when pressed for time.

### Target Expressions to Weave In
* `These next few months will be a lot of work` *(Mấy tháng tới sẽ rất nhiều việc)*
* `not going to be able to` *(sẽ không thể thu xếp làm được)*
* `pick up the slack` *(gánh việc dôi ra)*
* `wonder if it's worth it` *(tự hỏi liệu có bõ công không)*

### Scenario: The Overambitious Project Manager
* **Setting**: Your Project Manager just asked you to take on two additional dashboard migration projects this week while your teammate is out sick.
* **Your Task**: Politely push back, cite company policy, and explain your realistic bandwidth aloud:
  > *"Hey Alex, I understand these deliverables are important, but **these next few months will already be a lot of work** for our squad. With Mark out sick, the rest of us are already **picking up the slack** to keep the core pipelines running. Honestly, I'm **not going to be able to** take on two new migrations without risking production stability. According to our team's **company policy**, we should focus on sprint stability first rather than spreading ourselves thin."*

---

## 🎧 Part B: Active Listening — TOEIC Dictation (20–30 Mins)

### Instructions
1. Open your designated TOEIC Listening audio source (Short Conversations / Part 3 on project deadlines, staffing shortages, or workplace policies).
2. Choose **3 audio clips** (30–45 seconds each).
3. **Listen & Transcribe Word-for-Word**:
   * First pass: Listen to understand the interpersonal dynamic and workplace tension.
   * Second pass: Write down every single word in your notebook, paying attention to noun adjuncts (*company policy, office hours*) vs. possessives (*company's revenue*).
   * Rewind up to 3 times for modal reductions (*won't be able to, going to be*).
4. **Compare & Red-Pen Analysis**:
   * Compare your text with the transcript and log missed words with a red pen.
