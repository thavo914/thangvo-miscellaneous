## 📋 Section 1: Mid-Week Self-Evaluation Checklist

Review your speaking and writing performance across Days 1–3:

| Linguistic Skill / Pattern | Mastered? | Confident in Spontaneous Speaking? | Notes / Personal Pitfalls |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| `will be + V-ing` for polite routine updates | [ ] | [ ] | Avoid *will work* when announcing availability. |
| Prepositions (`by corporate`, `in your shoes`, `on the team`) | [ ] | [ ] | Check articles before *corporate* and *company*. |
| `Believe it or not, I was once...` | [ ] | [ ] | Natural rhythm to break ice with juniors. |
| `walk someone through all of the options` | [ ] | [ ] | Don't insert *with* (*not: walk with you through*). |
| Relative quantifier `..., all of which + Verb` | [ ] | [ ] | Avoid comma splice (*not: ..., all of them are*). |
| `by the end of` (deadline) vs. `at the end of` | [ ] | [ ] | Use *by EOD* for task delivery commitments. |
| `get 10% off your room rental` | [ ] | [ ] | Avoid saying *reduce 10% for me*. |
| `(not) going to be able to` | [ ] | [ ] | Polite boundary-setting without sounding harsh. |

## 🎯 Section 2: Situational Workplace Responses

### Scenario 1: Declining an Out-of-Scope Reporting Request
* **Situation**: A business analyst emails you asking for a custom Excel dump of customer events by 4:00 PM today, but your squad is finalizing a sprint release.
* **Prompt**: Write a concise, professional Slack message declining the request politely while explaining your current capacity.

<details>
<summary>💡 Click to View Model Response</summary>

> *"Hi David, thanks for reaching out. Our data squad is currently at full capacity finalizing today's scheduled production release, so we're **not going to be able to** pull this custom report by 4:00 PM today. Per our team's intake process, could you please submit a ticket in our backlog so we can prioritize it for next sprint? Thanks for your understanding!"*
</details>

### Scenario 2: Offering Support to an On-Call Colleague
* **Situation**: Your teammate has been up since 3:00 AM resolving a flaky database replica. They look exhausted at the morning standup.
* **Prompt**: Offer to take over their remaining on-call shift for the rest of the day so they can get some rest.

<details>
<summary>💡 Click to View Model Response</summary>

> *"Hey Nam, you look completely drained after tackling that replica incident last night. Don't worry about the afternoon queue; I'm happy to **pick up the slack** and cover the rest of your on-call shift so you can log off early and get some rest. We've got your back!"*
</details>
