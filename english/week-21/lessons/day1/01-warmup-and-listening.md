# Day 1 - Exercise 1: Warm-up Reflex & Active Listening

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 1 (35–45 Mins)**: Warm up your vocal tract and mental English reflexes before moving into analytical grammar study.

---

## 🎙️ Part A: Speaking Reflex — The Daily Commentator (15 Mins)

### Objective
Narrate your immediate actions, mindset, and daily schedule out loud in real time without hesitation or mental translation. Treat yourself like a live sports commentator narrating your own morning routine and workplace kick-off.

### Target Vocab to Weave In
Try to naturally use at least 2 of these expressions while speaking aloud:
* `settle into the role` *(bắt nhịp với công việc)*
* `develop a work ethic` *(rèn luyện tác phong làm việc)*
* `will be stepping away` *(sẽ tạm rời máy một lúc)*
* `get to experience` *(có dịp / cơ hội được trải nghiệm)*

### Practice Prompts (Speak Aloud for 60–90 Seconds Each)
1. **Prompt 1 (Morning Setup & Availability)**:  
   Look around your desk and narrate what you are doing right now, then state your availability for the day:
   > *"Right now, I'm opening my laptop, grabbing my water bottle, and checking the Jira backlog. Today I'**ll be working remotely** on documentation, but I'**ll be stepping away** for 30 minutes at lunch..."*
2. **Prompt 2 (Welcoming a Colleague & Mentorship Reflection)**:  
   Imagine a new teammate just sat down next to you on their first day. Say a few words aloud to welcome them and reassure them:
   > *"Welcome to the team! Believe it or not, I was once in your shoes and felt nervous too. It took me a few weeks to really **settle into the role**, so don't hesitate to ask questions..."*

---

## 🎧 Part B: Active Listening — TOEIC Dictation (20–30 Mins)

### Instructions
1. Open your designated TOEIC Listening audio source (Short Conversations / Part 3 or Talks / Part 4).
2. Choose **3 short audio recordings** (30–45 seconds each).
3. **Listen & Transcribe Word-for-Word**:
   * Listen once without writing to catch the general context.
   * On the second pass, pause every 4–6 words and write down every single word you hear in your physical notebook.
   * Rewind up to 3 times for difficult connected speech, contractions, or prepositions (`in`, `on`, `at`, `by`).
4. **Compare & Red-Pen Analysis**:
   * Check your transcription against the official transcript.
   * Underline missed weak forms (*of, to, been, 'll*) with a red pen.
   * Log any unfamiliar business collocations in your notebook.
