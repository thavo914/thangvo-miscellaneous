### 1. Model Translation 1
> *"Just a quick heads-up: I'**ll be working from home** tomorrow afternoon to focus on optimizing the SQL queries for our reporting system."*
* **Linguistic Breakdown**: Dùng thì Tương lai tiếp diễn `I'll be working from home` để thông báo lịch trình nhẹ nhàng, lịch sự. Dùng giới từ `for` chỉ mục đích phục vụ hệ thống báo cáo.

### 2. Model Translation 2
> *"**Believe it or not, I was once** in your shoes when I first started, but **getting to troubleshoot** real data issues helped me **learn so much**."*
* **Linguistic Breakdown**: Mẫu câu `Believe it or not, I was once in your shoes` tạo sự đồng cảm tự nhiên. Cụm `getting to troubleshoot` nhấn mạnh việc được cọ xát với lỗi thực tế là cơ hội quý báu. Dùng `learn so much` (không dùng *learn so many* hay *learn more so much*).

### 3. Model Translation 3
> *"**I want to personally thank each and every one of you for** pulling together during the rollout of our new server cluster sponsored **by corporate**."*
* **Linguistic Breakdown**: Cụm `each and every one of you` nhấn mạnh sự tri ân đến từng cá nhân. Cụm `sponsored by corporate` thể hiện tác nhân tài trợ chuẩn xác theo giới từ `by`.

## 🔍 Drill 3: Spot the Mistake (Error Correction)

Identify and correct the grammatical or collocation error in each sentence:

1. *Don't worry, I will be leave the office a little earlier today to pick up my laptop from repair.*  
   → **Correction**: __________________________________________________
2. *During my two years on the data team, I learned more so much from our lead architect.*  
   → **Correction**: __________________________________________________
3. *Unfortunately, I have to attend that prestigious international tech conference in Singapore next month.* (Context: The speaker is excited and grateful for the company sponsorship).  
   → **Correction**: __________________________________________________

<details>
<summary>💡 Click to View Explanations & Corrections</summary>

1. **Incorrect**: *will be leave*  
   ✅ **Correct**: *Don't worry, I'**ll be leaving** the office a little earlier today to pick up my laptop from repair.*  
   *Reason*: Sau `will be`, động từ bắt buộc phải thêm đuôi `-ing` (`leaving`).
2. **Incorrect**: *learned more so much*  
   ✅ **Correct**: *During my two years on the data team, I **learned so much** (hoặc: **learned so much more**) from our lead architect.*  
   *Reason*: Không bao giờ đặt `more` trước `so much`. Chuẩn phản xạ là `learned so much` hoặc `learned a ton`.
3. **Incorrect**: *have to attend*  
   ✅ **Correct**: *I'm thrilled that I **get to attend** that prestigious international tech conference in Singapore next month!*  
   *Reason*: Khi nói về cơ hội quý báu / sự ưu ái được tài trợ đi hội nghị, dùng `get to attend` thay vì `have to attend` (mang nghĩa bị ép buộc/bổn phận nặng nề).
</details>
