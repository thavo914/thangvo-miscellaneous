# Day 1 - Exercise 5: AI Speaking Simulation & Daily Review

> [!NOTE]
> **Blocks 3 & 4 (75 Mins)**: Transform today's written knowledge into fluent spoken reflexes using ChatGPT Voice Mode, followed by error logging and Anki consolidation.

---

## 🎙️ Block 3: AI Spoken Simulation with ChatGPT Voice Mode (60 Mins)

Open ChatGPT on your mobile phone or desktop, activate **Voice Mode 🎧**, and run the targeted simulation below:

### Day 1 Primary Voice Scenario: Onboarding Mentorship
> *"Act as my new junior colleague on the engineering team. Today is your first day at the company. Ask me how I adjusted when I first joined, what my schedule looks like today, and what advice I have for learning our data pipelines. I will practice using: 'Believe it or not, I was once...', 'I'll be working...', 'get to experience', and 'What helped me most early on was...'."*

---

> [!TIP]
> **Active Speaking Framework Checklist**:
> - **5W1H (Daily Q&A & Monologues)**: Deliver a direct answer first, supported by 1–2 details (*Why, When, How*).
> - **O-R-E-O (Abstract Opinions)**: Structure views as **O**pinion ➔ **R**eason ➔ **E**xample ➔ **O**utcome.
> - **S-T-A-R (Job Interview Mode)**: Answer behavioral questions with **S**ituation ➔ **T**ask ➔ **A**ction ➔ **R**esult.

> **Suggested Exercises (AI Prompts):**
>
> 1. **Rapid Q&A:**
>    > "Act as my English tutor. Ask me random English questions ranging from easy to advanced (topics: daily life, work, opinions, etc.). I will answer in English as quickly as possible. After I answer, please correct my grammar and vocabulary, and suggest more natural, native-like ways to express my ideas."
>
> 2. **Instant Translation (Reverse Shadowing):**
>    > "Give me a common Vietnamese situation or daily phrase. I will try to translate it into English out loud within 3–5 seconds. After my attempt, please provide the most natural English translation so I can compare."
>
> 3. **Role-play:**
>    > "Let's do a role-play. Please choose one of these topics: ordering at a restaurant, a job interview, traveling/at the airport, or discussing work with a colleague. We will have a back-and-forth conversation entirely in English. If we choose a job interview, ask me behavioral questions and evaluate my responses using the STAR framework (Situation, Task, Action, Result)."

## 📝 Block 4: Review, Error Logging & Consolidation (15 Mins)

### 1. Spoken Error Logging Table
Capture any awkward phrasing, pronunciation hiccups, or grammar corrections given by AI during today's session:

| What I Said | What Sounded Unnatural / Incorrect | Native Upgrade / Correction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *I will leave early today...* | Sounded overly formal/rigid | *I'**ll be leaving** early today...* |
| *I learned more so much...* | Word order confusion | *I **learned so much**...* |
| *I have to shadow the lead...* | Sounded like a burden | *I **got to shadow** the lead...* |

### 2. Anki Flashcard Candidates (Top 3 for Today)
Add these high-impact cards into your personal Anki deck:
* **Front**: *Tôi báo trước một chút: chiều mai tôi sẽ làm việc ở nhà để tối ưu hóa SQL.*  
  **Back**: *"Just a quick heads-up: I'll be working from home tomorrow afternoon to optimize SQL queries."*
* **Front**: *Dù tin hay không, tôi cũng từng bỡ ngỡ hệt như bạn hồi mới vào nghề.*  
  **Back**: *"Believe it or not, I was once in your shoes when I first started."*
* **Front**: *Phân biệt `get to do something` vs `have to do something`.*  
  **Back**: *`get to` = được dịp/cơ hội quý báu; `have to` = bổn phận/bị buộc phải làm.*
