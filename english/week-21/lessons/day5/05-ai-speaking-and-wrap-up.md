## 📝 Block 4: Weekly Review, Error Logging & Consolidation (15 Mins)

### 1. Spoken Error Logging Table

| What I Said | What Sounded Unnatural | Native Upgrade |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *We run the job in low hours...* | Non-idiomatic timing collocation | *We **run the job off-peak** during **off-peak hours**...* |
| *We don't lower quality...* | Basic / descriptive | *We achieve this **without compromising data quality**...* |
| *We think uptime is obvious...* | Literal Vietnamese thinking | *We never **take uptime for granted**...* |
