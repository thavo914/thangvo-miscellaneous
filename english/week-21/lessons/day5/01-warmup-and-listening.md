# Day 5 - Exercise 1: Warm-up Reflex & Active Listening

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 1 (35–45 Mins)**: Fast-paced argumentative counter-thinking drill, followed by word-for-word audio dictation.

---

## 😈 Part A: Speaking Reflex — The "Devil's Advocate" Challenge (15 Mins)

### Objective
Make a bold technical assertion, then immediately pivot within **30 seconds** to present the counter-argument using sharp contrast linkers (*Having said that, At the same time, On the flip side*). This trains agile thinking in engineering debate.

### Target Expressions to Weave In
* `run off-peak` / `off-peak hours` *(chạy vào giờ thấp điểm)*
* `compromise quality` *(hạ chuẩn / làm suy giảm chất lượng)*
* `without compromising [something]` *(mà không phải đánh đổi)*
* `take [uptime] for granted` *(chủ quan coi sự ổn định là hiển nhiên)*

### Practice Rounds (Pick 2 Prompts, 45s Each)
1. **Round 1: Real-Time Streaming vs. Batch Aggregation**:
   * *Assertion*: *"We should convert all our batch data pipelines into real-time streaming jobs immediately so stakeholders have sub-second numbers."*
   * *Devil's Advocate Counter*: *"Having said that, streaming 24/7 during business hours significantly drives up compute costs. A smarter trade-off is to schedule heavy aggregations to **run off-peak** during midnight **off-peak hours**, allowing us to cut cloud spend by 30% **without compromising data quality**..."*
2. **Round 2: Rushing a Cloud Migration**:
   * *Assertion*: *"We need to finish this database migration before Friday to hit our quarterly OKR ahead of schedule."*
   * *Devil's Advocate Counter*: *"On the other hand, skipping the staging shadow-tests would severely **compromise data integrity**. We should never **take our 99.99% uptime for granted** just to meet an artificial deadline..."*

## 🎧 Part B: Active Listening — TOEIC Dictation (20–30 Mins)

### Instructions
1. Open your designated TOEIC Listening audio source (Short Talks or Conversations from Part 3/4 involving budgets, service agreements, cloud billing, or quality control).
2. Choose **3 audio clips** (30–45 seconds each).
3. **Listen & Transcribe Word-for-Word**:
   * First pass: Grasp the financial and operational trade-offs being negotiated.
   * Second pass: Write down every word in your notebook, paying attention to connected prepositions (*without-compromising*, *off-peak-hours*).
   * Rewind up to 3 times for contracted auxiliaries (*we'll, shouldn't*).
4. **Compare & Red-Pen Analysis**:
   * Check your transcription against the transcript.
   * Highlight missed words and high-yield collocations in red.
