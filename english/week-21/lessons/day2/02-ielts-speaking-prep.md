# Day 2 - Exercise 2: IELTS Speaking Practice & Workplace Pitching

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 2 (Part A)**: Build structured spoken fluency for service evaluations, negotiations, and decision-making using IELTS-style prompts.

---

## 🎙️ Part 1: Introductory Interview (3–4 Minutes)

### Question 1: How do you usually choose a venue or service for a special celebration?
* **Model Response**:  
  > *"When choosing a venue, I usually look for clear packages where the coordinator is willing to **walk me through all of the options** upfront. For instance, having transparent pricing for **room rental** and catering makes it much easier to decide without hidden fees."*
* **Key Expressions**:
  * `walk me through all of the options` *(dẫn dắt tôi đi qua toàn bộ các gói lựa chọn)*
  * `room rental` *(chi phí thuê địa điểm)*

### Question 2: Do you prefer booking services well in advance or waiting for last-minute deals?
* **Model Response**:  
  > *"I definitely prefer booking well in advance. Many event spaces offer seasonal incentives—for example, if you confirm your date **by the end of the week**, you might **get 10% to 15% off** the total cost. It gives me peace of mind."*
* **Key Expressions**:
  * `by the end of the week` *(hạn chót trước khi hết tuần)*
  * `get 10% to 15% off` *(được giảm 10% đến 15%)*

### Question 3: What makes a good customer presentation when buying high-value services?
* **Model Response**:  
  > *"A great presenter doesn't overwhelm you with jargon. Instead, they present two or three solid recommendations, **all of which** fit your specific requirements, and explain the trade-offs clearly."*
* **Key Expressions**:
  * `all of which fit your specific requirements` *(mà tất cả những phương án đó đều phù hợp với yêu cầu của bạn)*

## 📋 Part 2: Individual Long Turn (Cue Card)

### Candidate Task Card

```text
Describe a time when you had to evaluate and choose between multiple service or product options for an important event or project.

You should say:
• What the event or project was
• What options were presented to you
• How the choices were explained to you
And explain why you decided on that final option and how satisfied you were.
```

### 🎯 1-Minute Preparation Scratchpad
* **Topic**: Choosing an event venue (Juniper Hall) or choosing a cloud data warehouse (Snowflake vs. BigQuery).
* **Key Target Vocab to Include**:
  * *walked us through all of the options*
  * *three distinct packages, all of which had...*
  * *I had no doubt that...*
  * *if we signed by the end of the day, we would get 10% off*

### Model Band 6.5–7.0 Spoken Response
> *"I'd like to talk about a time last year when my family and I had to choose an event venue for my sister's wedding reception. We looked at several locations around town, but the most memorable experience was visiting a boutique venue called Juniper.
> 
> When we arrived, the event coordinator sat down with us and **walked us through all of the options** available for hosting a private reception. She showed us three distinct room layouts—the outdoor garden terrace, the grand banquet hall, and an intimate mezzanine—**all of which** could be arranged to seat over 150 guests comfortably.
> 
> What really impressed me was how transparent she was. She explained what was included in each package and reassured us that their culinary team could handle customized menus. She told us: '**I have no doubt that** your guests will be blown away by the natural lighting and historic architecture.'
> 
> To top it off, she offered an early-bird incentive: if we confirmed our reservation **by the end of the day**, we would **get 10% off our room rental** fee. Because the venue was stunning and the pricing was clear, we decided to book it on the spot. Looking back, it was a fantastic decision; the event went off without a hitch, and everyone loved the atmosphere."*

## 💬 Part 3: Two-Way Analytical Discussion (4–5 Minutes)

### Question 1: Why do some businesses offer discounts if customers decide "by the end of the day"?
* **Model Response**:  
  > *"From a commercial perspective, offering a discount **by the end of the day** creates urgency. It prevents prospective clients from shopping around endlessly. At the same time, if the customer gets a meaningful reduction—like **getting 10% off**—both parties feel like they achieved a win-win deal."*

### Question 2: In professional settings, how can someone present complex options without confusing their audience?
* **Model Response**:  
  > *"The key is structure. A skilled professional will categorize solutions into tiers, **all of which** address the core problem from different angles. By guiding the listener step-by-step—literally **walking them through** each scenario—the client can make an informed decision without feeling overwhelmed."*
