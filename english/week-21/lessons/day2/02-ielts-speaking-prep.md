# Day 2 - Exercise 2: IELTS Speaking Practice & Workplace Pitching

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 2 (Part A)**: Build structured spoken fluency for service evaluations, negotiations, and decision-making using IELTS-style prompts.

---

## 🎙️ Part 1: Introductory Interview (3–4 Minutes)

> [!TIP]
> **5W1H Framework Rule**: For each Part 1 question, deliver a crisp **Direct Answer** immediately, followed by 1–2 supporting details addressing **Why**, **When**, or **How**.

### Question 1: How do you usually choose a venue or service for a special celebration?
* **Framework Breakdown (5W1H)**:
  * **[Direct Answer - What/How]**: *"When choosing a venue, I usually look for clear packages where the coordinator is willing to **walk me through all of the options** upfront."*
  * **[Supporting Detail - Why/How]**: *"Having transparent pricing for **room rental** and catering makes it much easier to decide without hidden fees or surprise surcharges."*
* **Full Spoken Flow**:  
  > *"When choosing a venue, I usually look for clear packages where the coordinator is willing to **walk me through all of the options** upfront. For instance, having transparent pricing for **room rental** and catering makes it much easier to decide without hidden fees or surprise surcharges."*
* **Key Expressions**:
  * `walk me through all of the options` *(dẫn dắt tôi đi qua toàn bộ các gói lựa chọn)*
  * `room rental` *(chi phí thuê địa điểm / phòng ốc)*

### Question 2: Do you prefer booking services well in advance or waiting for last-minute deals?
* **Framework Breakdown (5W1H)**:
  * **[Direct Answer - What]**: *"I definitely prefer booking well in advance rather than waiting for last-minute offers."*
  * **[Supporting Detail - Why/When]**: *"Many event spaces offer early-bird incentives—for example, if you confirm your date **by the end of the week**, you might **get 10% to 15% off** the total cost. It gives me peace of mind and saves budget."*
* **Full Spoken Flow**:  
  > *"I definitely prefer booking well in advance rather than waiting for last-minute offers. Many event spaces offer early-bird incentives—for example, if you confirm your date **by the end of the week**, you might **get 10% to 15% off** the total cost. It gives me peace of mind and saves budget."*
* **Key Expressions**:
  * `by the end of the week` *(hạn chót trước khi hết tuần)*
  * `get 10% to 15% off` *(được giảm 10% đến 15%)*

### Question 3: What makes a good customer presentation when buying high-value services?
* **Framework Breakdown (5W1H)**:
  * **[Direct Answer - What/How]**: *"A great presenter avoids confusing jargon and explains solutions step-by-step."*
  * **[Supporting Detail - Why/How]**: *"Instead of dumping too much data, they present two or three solid recommendations, **all of which** fit your specific requirements, and explain the trade-offs clearly so you can choose with confidence."*
* **Full Spoken Flow**:  
  > *"A great presenter avoids confusing jargon and explains solutions step-by-step. Instead of dumping too much data, they present two or three solid recommendations, **all of which** fit your specific requirements, and explain the trade-offs clearly so you can choose with confidence."*
* **Key Expressions**:
  * `all of which fit your specific requirements` *(mà tất cả những phương án đó đều phù hợp với yêu cầu của bạn)*

## 📋 Part 2: Individual Long Turn (Cue Card)

### Candidate Task Card

```text
Describe a time when you had to evaluate and choose between multiple service or product options for an important event or project.

You should say:
• What the event or project was
• What options were presented to you
• How the choices were explained to you
And explain why you decided on that final option and how satisfied you were.
```

### 🎯 1-Minute Preparation Scratchpad (Structured via 5W1H)

| 5W1H Question | Scratchpad Brainstorming Notes |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Who** | My sister, my family, and the event coordinator at Juniper Hall |
| **What** | Evaluating event spaces and banquet packages for a wedding reception |
| **Where** | Juniper Hall (a boutique historic venue downtown) |
| **When** | Last year during our event planning window |
| **Why** | Needed transparent pricing, flexible seating for 150+ guests, and no hidden fees |
| **How** | Coordinator *walked us through all of the options*; showed 3 rooms *all of which* fit; gave 10% off *by the end of the day*; booked on the spot |

### Model Band 6.5–7.0 Spoken Response (5W1H Progression)

* **[When & Who & Where - Context]**:  
  > *"I'd like to talk about a time last year when my family and I had to choose an event venue for my sister's wedding reception. We looked at several locations around town, but the most memorable experience was visiting a boutique venue called Juniper."*

* **[What happened & How choices were presented]**:  
  > *"When we arrived, the event coordinator sat down with us and **walked us through all of the options** available for hosting a private reception. She showed us three distinct room layouts—the outdoor garden terrace, the grand banquet hall, and an intimate mezzanine—**all of which** could be arranged to seat over 150 guests comfortably."*

* **[Why we were impressed & Reassurance]**:  
  > *"What really impressed me was how transparent she was. She explained what was included in each package and reassured us that their culinary team could handle customized menus. She told us: '**I have no doubt that** your guests will be blown away by the natural lighting and historic architecture.' "*

* **[How it concluded & Final Outcome]**:  
  > *"To top it off, she offered an early-bird incentive: if we confirmed our reservation **by the end of the day**, we would **get 10% off our room rental** fee. Because the venue was stunning and the pricing was clear, we decided to book it on the spot. Looking back, it was a fantastic decision; the event went off without a hitch, and everyone loved the atmosphere."*

## 💬 Part 3: Two-Way Analytical Discussion (4–5 Minutes)

> [!TIP]
> **O-R-E-O Framework Rule**: For abstract and analytical Part 3 questions, follow the **O-R-E-O** structure:
> 1. **O (Opinion)**: Direct, clear stance/thesis.
> 2. **R (Reason)**: Logical explanation or cause (*Because / The reason is...*).
> 3. **E (Example)**: Concrete real-life instance or illustration (*For example / In practice...*).
> 4. **O (Outcome / Synthesis)**: Final takeaway or summarized impact (*Ultimately / As a result...*).

### Question 1: Why do some businesses offer discounts if customers decide "by the end of the day"?

* **O - Opinion**: *"From a commercial perspective, offering a discount **by the end of the day** creates a healthy sense of urgency that accelerates purchasing decisions."*
* **R - Reason**: *"The reason is that when prospective clients leave a consultation without committing, they often get lost in endless comparison shopping and postpone the project indefinitely."*
* **E - Example**: *"For instance, event spaces and software vendors frequently offer immediate incentives—such as **getting 10% off room rental** or installation fees—if the client confirms before 5:00 PM."*
* **O - Outcome**: *"Ultimately, both parties win: the vendor secures committed revenue early, while the buyer locks in substantial cost savings."*

* **Full Spoken Flow**:  
  > *"From a commercial perspective, offering a discount **by the end of the day** creates a healthy sense of urgency that accelerates purchasing decisions. The reason is that when prospective clients leave a consultation without committing, they often get lost in endless comparison shopping and postpone the project indefinitely. For instance, event spaces and software vendors frequently offer immediate incentives—such as **getting 10% off room rental** or installation fees—if the client confirms before 5:00 PM. Ultimately, both parties win: the vendor secures committed revenue early, while the buyer locks in substantial cost savings."*

### Question 2: In professional settings, how can someone present complex options without confusing their audience?

* **O - Opinion**: *"The most effective way to present complex choices is to organize them into structured tiers and guide the audience step-by-step."*
* **R - Reason**: *"This is because human working memory easily gets overloaded when too many technical variables are presented all at once, leading to decision fatigue."*
* **E - Example**: *"In practice, an experienced presenter will literally **walk you through all of the options** by highlighting two or three distinct packages, **all of which** address the core business goal from different cost and feature angles."*
* **O - Outcome**: *"As a result, the client feels empowered rather than overwhelmed, enabling them to make an informed decision with complete confidence."*

* **Full Spoken Flow**:  
  > *"The most effective way to present complex choices is to organize them into structured tiers and guide the audience step-by-step. This is because human working memory easily gets overloaded when too many technical variables are presented all at once, leading to decision fatigue. In practice, an experienced presenter will literally **walk you through all of the options** by highlighting two or three distinct packages, **all of which** address the core business goal from different cost and feature angles. As a result, the client feels empowered rather than overwhelmed, enabling them to make an informed decision with complete confidence."*
