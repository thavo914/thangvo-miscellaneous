## 🎙️ Block 3: AI Spoken Simulation with ChatGPT Voice Mode (60 Mins)

Use the prompt from [03-chatgpt-voice-simulation.md](file:///c:/Users/Admin/source/repos/thangvo-miscellaneous/english/week-21/lessons/day2/03-chatgpt-voice-simulation.md) to conduct a full 20–30 minute voice consultation roleplay. Spend the remaining 30 minutes cycling through the 3 core drills below:

---

> [!TIP]
> **Active Speaking Framework Checklist**:
> - **5W1H (Part 1 & Part 2)**: Direct answer + 1–2 supporting details (*Why, When, How*) for Part 1; structured *Who, What, Where, When, Why, How* flow for Part 2.
> - **O-R-E-O (Part 3 / Opinions)**: Structure analytical viewpoints as **O**pinion ➔ **R**eason ➔ **E**xample ➔ **O**utcome.
> - **S-T-A-R (Job Interview Mode)**: Answer behavioral questions with **S**ituation ➔ **T**ask ➔ **A**ction ➔ **R**esult.

> **Suggested Exercises (AI Prompts):**
>
> 1. **Rapid Q&A:**
>    > "Act as my English tutor. Ask me random English questions ranging from easy to advanced (topics: daily life, work, opinions, etc.). I will answer in English as quickly as possible. After I answer, please correct my grammar and vocabulary, and suggest more natural, native-like ways to express my ideas."
>
> 2. **Instant Translation (Reverse Shadowing):**
>    > "Give me a common Vietnamese situation or daily phrase. I will try to translate it into English out loud within 3–5 seconds. After my attempt, please provide the most natural English translation so I can compare."
>
> 3. **Role-play:**
>    > "Let's do a role-play. Please choose one of these topics: ordering at a restaurant, a job interview, traveling/at the airport, or discussing work with a colleague. We will have a back-and-forth conversation entirely in English. If we choose a job interview, ask me behavioral questions and evaluate my responses using the STAR framework (Situation, Task, Action, Result)."

## 📝 Block 4: Review, Error Logging & Consolidation (15 Mins)

### 1. Spoken Error Logging Table
Capture any awkward phrasing, preposition slips, or corrections from ChatGPT Voice Mode:

| What I Said | Why It Felt Awkward | Native Upgrade |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *I will walk through the options with you...* | Word order slightly clunky | *I'll **walk you through all the options**...* |
| *We have 3 halls, all of them can be used...* | Comma splice error | *We have 3 halls, **all of which** can be used...* |
| *If you book at the end of the day...* | Wrong preposition for deadline | *If you book **by the end of the day**...* |

### 2. Anki Flashcard Candidates (Top 3 for Today)
Add these high-impact cards into your personal Anki deck:
* **Front**: *Hôm nay tôi sẽ dẫn dắt hai bạn đi qua toàn bộ các lựa chọn cho lễ cưới của mình tại Juniper.*  
  **Back**: *"Today, I'll walk you through all of the options for your wedding here at Juniper."*
* **Front**: *Chúng tôi có 3 sảnh tiệc, mà tất cả các sảnh đó đều có thể tùy biến linh hoạt.*  
  **Back**: *"We offer three distinct halls, all of which can be customized."*
* **Front**: *Nếu bạn đặt chỗ trước cuối ngày hôm nay, bạn sẽ được giảm 10% phí thuê phòng.*  
  **Back**: *"If you book with me by the end of the day, you will get 10% off your room rental."*
