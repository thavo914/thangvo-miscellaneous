# Day 2 - Exercise 4: Interactive Shadowing & Pacing Guide

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 2 (Part C)**: Train spoken intonation, thought-group rhythm, and native sentence stress. Read each line aloud 5 times following the pause markers (`/`) and stress indicators.

---

## 🎵 Sentence 1: The Service Walkthrough & Relative Clause

> *"Today, / I'm going to **WALK** you through / **ALL** of the **OP**tions / we have for your **WED**ding / here at **JU**niper."*

### Rhythm & Phrasing Breakdown:
* **Thought Group 1**: `Today, /` *(Slight rising intonation, brief pause to orient the listener).*
* **Thought Group 2**: `I'm going to WALK you through /` *(Contract "I'm going to" into /aɪm gənə/, stress the verb WALK).*
* **Thought Group 3**: `ALL of the OPtions /` *(Emphatic stress on ALL and the first syllable of OP-tions).*
* **Thought Group 4**: `we have for your WEDding /` *(Smooth connection /fə jər/, stress on WED-ding).*
* **Thought Group 5**: `here at JU-ni-per.` *(Falling intonation signals completeness).*

---

## 🎵 Sentence 2: The Multi-Option Bridge (`all of which`)

> *"We offer **THREE** distinct **SPAC**es, / **ALL** of which / can be **CUS**tomized / for your **SPE**cial day."*

### Rhythm & Phrasing Breakdown:
* **Thought Group 1**: `We offer THREE distinct SPAC-es, /` *(Stress on number THREE and noun SPAC-es. Comma pause).*
* **Thought Group 2**: `ALL of which /` *(Quick rhythmic link /ɔːl əv wɪtʃ/. Do not hesitate between 'of' and 'which').*
* **Thought Group 3**: `can be CUS-tom-ized /` *(Weak modal /kən bi/, primary stress on CUS-).*
* **Thought Group 4**: `for your SPE-cial day.` *(Soft landing, clear falling intonation on SPE-cial day).*

---

## 🎵 Sentence 3: The Urgency & Discount Incentive

> *"Remember, / if you **BOOK** with me / by the **END** of the day, / you will get **TEN** percent off / your **ROOM** rental."*

### Rhythm & Phrasing Breakdown:
* **Thought Group 1**: `Remember, /` *(Warm conversational prompt).*
* **Thought Group 2**: `if you BOOK with me /` *(Stress action verb BOOK).*
* **Thought Group 3**: `by the END of the day, /` *(Clear /baɪ ði end əv ðə deɪ/. Stress END).*
* **Thought Group 4**: `you will get TEN percent off /` *(Stress the discount quantity TEN).*
* **Thought Group 5**: `your ROOM rental.` *(Stress ROOM, clean stop).*
