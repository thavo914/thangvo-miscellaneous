# Day 2 - Exercise 3: ChatGPT Voice Mode Simulation

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 2 (Part B)**: Test your spoken fluency in an interactive AI voice consultation. Practice presenting service tiers and closing a deal.

---

## 🎙️ Voice Simulation Prompt

Open ChatGPT on your phone or desktop, turn on **Voice Mode 🎧**, and paste/read this prompt:

```text
Act as a couple touring an event venue. Ask me about your wedding packages and hall spaces. I will walk you through all of the options, use all of which, and offer a 10% discount if you book by the end of the day. Correct my speaking naturally after each answer.
```

*(Character count: 268 characters | Hyphens: 0)*

---

## 📝 Spoken Feedback Logging Table

Use this table to log corrections received from ChatGPT during your voice consultation:

| Phrase Attempted | What Felt Clunky / Vietlish | AI Suggested Native Alternative |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *I will explain you all options...* | Missing preposition & article | *Let me **walk you through all of the options**...* |
| *We have 3 rooms, all of them can be used...* | Comma splice error | *We have 3 rooms, **all of which** can be tailored...* |
| *If you book at the end of the day...* | Wrong preposition for deadline | *If you book **by the end of the day**...* |
| *They reduce 10% for your room rental...* | Literal translation of "giảm" | *You'll **get 10% off your room rental**...* |
