# Day 2 - Exercise 1: Warm-up Reflex & Active Listening

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 1 (35–45 Mins)**: Rapid spoken fluency warm-up followed by word-for-word audio dictation.

---

## ⏱️ Part A: Speaking Reflex — The 60-Second Random Object Pitch (15 Mins)

### Objective
Pick an everyday item or a workplace service and speak continuously about it for **60 seconds without pausing for more than 2 seconds**. The goal is rapid fluency, seamless sentence linking, and continuous idea generation.

### Target Collocations to Weave In
* `all of the options` *(toàn bộ các gói lựa chọn)*
* `walk you through` *(hướng dẫn từng bước một)*
* `I have no doubt that...` *(tôi hoàn toàn tin chắc rằng)*
* `all of which` *(mà tất cả những thứ đó)*

### Practice Rounds (Pick 2 Prompts, 60s Each)
1. **Round 1: Pitching the Event Hall at Juniper**:  
   Imagine you are holding an open house brochure for Juniper Event Hall. Pitch the venue to a prospective couple looking for a wedding reception space:
   > *"Welcome to Juniper! Today, I'll **walk you through all of the options** we have for your special day. We offer three distinct spaces—the garden terrace, the main ballroom, and the mezzanine—**all of which** can be tailored to your guest count. **I have no doubt that** you'll fall in love with our natural lighting and banquet amenities..."*
2. **Round 2: Pitching a Dual-Cloud Architecture**:  
   Pitch an enterprise architecture that leverages both AWS and GCP for data redundancy:
   > *"Today I want to present our cross-cloud infrastructure. We evaluated three data lake storage tiers, **all of which** provide 99.999% durability. If we commit to reserving capacity **by the end of the day**, we can **get 20% off** our compute billing..."*

## 🎧 Part B: Active Listening — TOEIC Dictation (20–30 Mins)

### Instructions
1. Open your designated TOEIC Listening audio source (Part 3 Conversations involving customer service, booking flights, or event inquiries).
2. Choose **3 audio clips** (30–45 seconds each).
3. **Listen & Transcribe Word-for-Word**:
   * First pass: Listen completely without pausing to understand the booking or service context.
   * Second pass: Write down every single word in your notebook, paying attention to linking sounds (*walk-you-through*, *all-of-which*).
   * Rewind up to 3 times for contracted modals (*you'll get, we'd like to*).
4. **Compare & Red-Pen Analysis**:
   * Compare your transcript against the official answer key.
   * Underline any missed prepositions (*by vs. at*) in red ink.
