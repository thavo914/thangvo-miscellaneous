# Day 4 - Exercise 1: Warm-up Reflex & Active Listening

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 1 (30–35 Mins)**: Focused technical listening dictation to train your ear on hardware troubleshooting and operational terminology before studying technical grammar rules.

---

## 🎧 Part A: Active Listening — TOEIC Dictation (20–30 Mins)

### Instructions
1. Open your designated TOEIC Listening audio source (Short Talks or Conversations from Part 3/4 involving technical support, equipment upgrades, railway maintenance, or IT server outages).
2. Choose **3 audio clips** (30–45 seconds each).
3. **Listen & Transcribe Word-for-Word**:
   * First pass: Listen to catch the operational failure mode or maintenance schedule.
   * Second pass: Write down every single word in your notebook, paying special attention to passive verb forms (*is being replaced*, *are being upgraded*) and phrasal verbs (*hook up, swap out*).
   * Rewind up to 3 times for weak syllables in continuous forms (*be-ing, -ing*).
4. **Compare & Red-Pen Analysis**:
   * Check your transcript against the answer key.
   * Underline any missed passive auxiliaries in red.

---

## 🎙️ Part B: Rapid Spoken Diagnostic Drill (5 Mins)

Before moving to grammar, speak aloud for 60 seconds explaining an everyday or technical item that is currently being replaced or repaired:
> *"Right now, the train tracks between Wilmslow and Levenshulme **are being replaced**, so commuters have to take a replacement bus. Meanwhile in our datacenter, secondary storage node 04 **keeps making a beeping sound**, so the technician is on-site **hooking up** a replacement drive..."*
