## 🧠 Section 1: Block 2 - Grammar Focus (90 Mins)

### 1. Relative Clauses (Defining vs. Non-Defining)
- **Quote**: *"What I really wanted wasn't the title, but the feeling of growth."*
- **Explanation**: Relative clauses add detail about a noun. Nominal relative clauses starting with *What* act as the subject/object to place strong emphasis on the core desire or idea.
- > [!WARNING]
  > **Common Pitfall**: Do not add unnecessary pronouns inside relative clauses.
  > *Incorrect*: What I really wanted it was the feeling of growth.
  > *Correct*: What I really wanted was the feeling of growth.

#### 💡 Real-Life Examples:
1. *Everyday*: **What I really enjoyed about the trip** was spending time near the beach.
2. *Data Engineering*: **What the client actually needed** was accurate real-time metrics, not complex charts.
3. *Workplace*: **What makes our team effective** is open and honest communication.

---

### 2. Reflexive Pronouns for Emphasis
- **Quote**: *"We just have to remind ourselves that the journey is the destination."*
- **Explanation**: Use reflexive pronouns (*myself, yourself, himself, herself, ourselves, themselves*) when the subject and object are the same person, or to emphasize self-reliance.
- > [!WARNING]
  > **Common Pitfall**: Avoid using object pronouns where reflexive pronouns are required.
  > *Incorrect*: We have to remind us to stay calm.
  > *Correct*: We have to remind ourselves to stay calm.

#### 💡 Real-Life Examples:
1. *Everyday*: I had to force **myself** to get up early and go for a run.
2. *Data Engineering*: The pipeline automatically recovers **itself** after a network drop.
3. *Workplace*: We should give **ourselves** credit for solving that complex bug.

---

### 3. Double Comparatives (`The more..., the more...`)
- **Quote**: *"The more you step out of your comfort zone, the easier it gets."*
- **Explanation**: Expresses cause-and-effect relationships where an increase in one area causes a proportional change in another.
- > [!WARNING]
  > **Common Pitfall**: Mixing up sentence order or forgetting *the*.
  > *Incorrect*: More you practice, more you get better.
  > *Correct*: The more you practice, the better you get.

#### 💡 Real-Life Examples:
1. *Everyday*: **The more you read**, **the more knowledgeable you become**.
2. *Data Engineering*: **The more you automate tests**, **the fewer bugs you deploy to production**.
3. *Workplace*: **The more transparent you are**, **the more trust you build with stakeholders**.

## 📐 Section 2: Sentence Structure Templates (5 Patterns)

### Template 1: `What I really wanted wasn't + [Noun Phrase 1], but + [Noun Phrase 2]`
* **Communicative Function**: Clarifying core priorities or intentions.
* **Examples**:
  1. *Everyday*: What I really wanted wasn't a fancy car, but a reliable commute.
  2. *Data Engineering*: What I really wanted wasn't another BI tool, but clean data pipelines.
  3. *Workplace*: What I really wanted wasn't micro-management, but clear ownership.

### Template 2: `It's amazing what can happen when you + [Verb Phrase]`
* **Communicative Function**: Expressing positive surprise at personal progress.
* **Examples**:
  1. *Everyday*: It's amazing what can happen when you sleep 8 hours a night.
  2. *Data Engineering*: It's amazing what can happen when you index your foreign keys properly.
  3. *Workplace*: It's amazing what can happen when you step out of your comfort zone.

### Template 3: `I used to think that + [Clause 1], but now I realize + [Clause 2]`
* **Communicative Function**: Contrast past misconceptions with current wisdom.
* **Examples**:
  1. *Everyday*: I used to think that buying things made me happy, but now I realize experiences matter more.
  2. *Data Engineering*: I used to think that writing complex SQL was impressive, but now I realize simplicity is key.
  3. *Workplace*: I used to think that taking things personally was normal, but now I realize it's just feedback.

### Template 4: `The joy isn't in + [Verb-ing/Noun], it's the process of + [Verb-ing]`
* **Communicative Function**: Shifting focus from end results to continuous learning.
* **Examples**:
  1. *Everyday*: The joy isn't in finishing the book, it's the process of reading it.
  2. *Data Engineering*: The joy isn't in deploying to production, it's the process of debugging and building.
  3. *Workplace*: The joy isn't in getting promoted, it's the process of growing your skills.

### Template 5: `The more you + [Verb Phrase], the more + [Adj/Noun Phrase] + you become`
* **Communicative Function**: Highlighting progressive personal development.
* **Examples**:
  1. *Everyday*: The more you travel, the more open-minded you become.
  2. *Data Engineering*: The more you automate manual tasks, the more productive you become.
  3. *Workplace*: The more you step out of your comfort zone, the more confident you become.
