## 📹 Script 1: Finding Joy in the Process (Topic 1)

### Metadata
- **Topic**: Personal Growth & Overcoming Misconceptions
- **Target Duration**: 60 seconds
- **Word Count**: 135 words
- **Speaking Speed**: ~130 WPM

### Transcript
`[00:00]` For a long time, **I used to think that achieving big goals was all that mattered**, but recently I realized something important. **What I really wanted wasn't the final title, but the feeling of growth** along the way. `[00:20]` When you're constantly chasing the next milestone, it's easy to **let yourself go** and get stressed over minor setbacks. Now, whenever someone criticizes my effort, I try to treat it like **water off a duck's back**. `[00:40]` **The joy isn't in reaching the finish line, it's the process of learning every single day**. **The more you step out of your comfort zone, the more confident you become**. Remind yourself to **keep things in perspective** and enjoy the journey!

#### Skimming Check
1. What was the speaker's shift in perspective?
2. What happens when you step out of your comfort zone?
