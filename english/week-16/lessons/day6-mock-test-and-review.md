# Day 6: Mock Test & Review (Weekend)

> [!NOTE]
> **Total Daily Target**: 3 Hours (180 Minutes)
> Today is a mock test day. Ensure you are in a quiet environment and try to simulate real testing conditions.

---

## 🎧 Section 1: Block 1 - Full TOEIC Listening Test (45 Mins)

* **Goal**: Build endurance for long audio sections and practice real-time comprehension.
* **Instructions**:
  1. Prepare a blank answer sheet numbered 1-100.
  2. Play a full 45-minute TOEIC Listening Test audio from start to finish without pausing.
  3. Mark your answers as you go.

---

## 📝 Section 2: Block 2 - IELTS Practice Test (60 Mins)

* **Goal**: Practice test-taking strategies under strict time limits.
* **Instructions**: Choose ONE of the following skills to focus on today:
  - **Option A (Reading)**: Complete a full 60-minute Academic Reading test (3 passages, 40 questions).
  - **Option B (Writing)**: Complete Task 1 (20 mins) and Task 2 (40 mins) under timed conditions.
  - **Option C (Speaking)**: Record yourself doing a full 15-minute speaking mock test. Use the remaining time to self-evaluate using the band descriptors.

---

## 🔍 Section 3: Block 3 & 4 - Detailed Mistake Analysis (75 Mins)

### Mistake Review (60 Mins)
* **Instructions**:
  1. Score your TOEIC and IELTS tests using the answer keys.
  2. For every incorrect answer, find the exact line in the transcript or reading passage that contains the answer.
  3. Write down *why* you got it wrong (e.g., missed vocabulary, lost focus, tricky distractor).

### Weekly Wrap-up (15 Mins)
* Log any new essential vocabulary from the tests into your Anki deck.
* Complete your weekly portfolio log and prepare for tomorrow's rest day!
