## ✍️ Section 1: Block 2 - Creative Dialogue Writing (90 Mins)

### 💡 Worked Example Dialogue (10 Turns):
*Setting: Chris and Morgan are having lunch near the office after a difficult project debrief.*

**Chris:** Man, that client review was brutal today. I felt like everything we built was put under a microscope.
**Morgan:** I know, but try not to **take things personally**, Chris. They were just stressed about their launch date.
**Chris:** I know, but I used to think that as long as code worked, people would be happy. Now I realize stakeholder management is half the job.
**Morgan:** Exactly. **What I really wanted wasn't just to write code, but to solve real business problems**. 
**Chris:** True. I guess I just need to let harsh comments be like **water off a duck's back**.
**Morgan:** That's the spirit! **The more you step out of your comfort zone, the more resilient you become**.
**Chris:** You're right. I need to **keep things in perspective**. One tough meeting doesn't define our whole project.
**Morgan:** Exactly. **The joy isn't in having zero problems, it's the process of solving them together**.
**Chris:** Thanks, Morgan. By the way, let me know if you want to grab coffee later—don't work through lunch and **let yourself go**!
**Morgan:** Haha, deal! Let me wrap up this pull request first.

## 🎙️ Section 2: Monologue (General Life / Workplace Balance)

### Sample Monologue: Learning to Enjoy the Process
"For a long time, **I used to think that achieving milestones was all that mattered**, but recently **I've realized that the joy isn't in reaching the destination, it's the process of getting there**. Whenever I feel overwhelmed by unexpected delays or harsh feedback, **I try to keep things in perspective**. Criticism used to bug me, but now **it's like water off a duck's back**. Ultimately, **the more you step out of your comfort zone, the more resilient you become**."

## 🌟 Section 3: Band 6.5-7.0 IELTS Cue Card Sample Answer

### Prompt: Describe a time when you stepped out of your comfort zone.

**Model Answer**:
"I'd like to share an experience from last year when I decided to deliver a technical presentation in English to our international stakeholders. Normally, I feel comfortable working silently behind my laptop, but **what I really wanted wasn't to stay safe, but to grow professionally**. 

Before the meeting, I was quite anxious about making mistakes. But I reminded myself that **it's amazing what can happen when you let go of that fear**. During the Q&A session, a senior manager asked a few tough questions. In the past, I would have taken those questions personally, but this time **I kept things in perspective** and treated them as helpful feedback. 

Looking back, **the more I stepped out of my comfort zone, the easier it was to speak naturally**. It made me realize that **the joy isn't just in giving a flawless presentation, it's the process of pushing your boundaries**."
