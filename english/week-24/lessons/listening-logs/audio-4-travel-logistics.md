## 🔖 `Audio 4 - Attempt 1 (Fresh Listen)` — 2026-11-18

### 📝 Submission (Verbatim)

The conversation is between a woman and a man; they seem to be employees of a company and they were talking about the company's new office; the woman started with compliments for the new office; it has natural light, and the space is about 2000 metres; the man agreed that the office has great views that can see the whole city; but he wondered about the noise since it near the street; the woman said that the glasses will be replaced by another has double something that I couldn't hear; The man said it can reassuaring, and asked about the park; that's all I can hear"*

### ⚖️ Score Breakdown (Strict Grading): **5.0 / 10**
1. **Precision Detail Coverage (2.5 / 5.0 Pts)**:
   * ✅ **Nailed:** Hai người nói về văn phòng, có ánh sáng tự nhiên, con số `2,000`, view nhìn toàn thành phố, lo lắng về tiếng ồn đường phố, kính hai lớp (`double-...`), cảm thấy yên tâm (`reassuring`).
   * ⚠️ **Missed / Shifted (-2.5 Pts):**
     * *(-0.5)* **Nhầm vai trò nhân vật:** Không phải 2 nhân viên nội bộ — người nữ là **nhân viên cho thuê văn phòng (`leasing agent`)** đang dẫn **khách thuê (`prospective tenant`)** đi xem mặt bằng.
     * *(-0.5)* **Sai đơn vị & Sai thì:** Đơn vị là **`2,000 square feet`** (không phải *metres*); cửa kính **`were replaced last year`** (đã thay năm ngoái rồi!), không phải `will be replaced` (sẽ thay).
     * *(-0.5)* **Sót chi tiết kính & lý do lo ồn:** `floor-to-ceiling windows`, lý do lo ồn (*Our staff need to make a lot of phone calls*), và cụm **`double-glazed, soundproof glass`** (kính 2 lớp cách âm).
     * *(-0.5)* **Nghe nhầm `parking` thành `park`:** Anh ấy hỏi về **`parking`** (bãi đỗ xe dưới hầm), không phải công viên (*park*)!
     * *(-0.5)* **Sót hoàn toàn lượt cuối:** Chỗ đỗ xe có **5 chỗ dành riêng dưới hầm (`five reserved spaces in the underground garage`)** và có thể thuê thêm nếu cần.
2. **Grammar & Reporting Accuracy (1.0 / 2.5 Pts)**:
   * *(-0.5)* **Run-on sentences & Semicolons:** Vẫn nối cả bài bằng dấu `;` thay vì tách câu bằng dấu chấm (`.`).
   * *(-0.25)* **Thiếu động từ & Chính tả:** `since it near the street` $\rightarrow$ **`since it was near a busy street`**; `reassuaring` $\rightarrow$ **`reassuring`**; `it can reassuaring` $\rightarrow$ **`that was reassuring`**.
   * *(-0.5)* **Lỗi ngữ pháp cấu trúc:**
     * `the glasses` $\rightarrow$ **`the windows`** hoặc **`the glass`** (`glasses` = mắt kính đeo hoặc ly cốc!).
     * `by another has double...` $\rightarrow$ Thiếu đại từ quan hệ `that` hoặc dùng giới từ `with`.
     * `views that can see the whole city` $\rightarrow$ Cái view không tự "nhìn" được! Dùng **`views where you can see the whole city`** (hoặc **`views of the whole city`**).
3. **Natural Phrasing & Flow (1.5 / 2.5 Pts)**:
   * ✅ Mở bài rất mượt (`I've just finished...`, `The conversation is between...`).
   * *(-0.5)* `compliments for` $\rightarrow$ **`highlighted / pointed out the features of`** (người môi giới giới thiệu điểm mạnh văn phòng).
   * *(-0.5)* `that's all I can hear` $\rightarrow$ **`that's all I could catch / remember`**.

### 🔬 Phân Tích & Nâng Cấp Chi Tiết Từng Câu (6 Đoạn)

1. **Đoạn 1 — Mở bài & Vai trò nhân vật:**
   * *Gốc:* *"I've just finished the 4th audio in this file. The conversation is between a woman and a man; they seem to be employees of a company and they were talking about the company's new office;"*
   * *Sửa:* Bỏ `in this file` cho gọn; sửa ngắt câu bằng `.`; sửa `employees` thành đúng ngữ cảnh (hoặc giữ phán đoán của bạn nếu viết theo những gì nghe được thì dùng câu tách biệt).
   * *Nâng cấp:* **"I've just finished listening to the fourth audio. The conversation is between a woman and a man who seem to be coworkers talking about a new office."** *(Thực tế: a leasing agent showing an office to a prospective tenant).*

2. **Đoạn 2 — Ánh sáng & Diện tích:**
   * *Gốc:* *"the woman started with compliments for the new office; it has natural light, and the space is about 2000 metres;"*
   * *Sửa:* Dùng `started by highlighting` (bắt đầu bằng việc nêu bật điểm cộng); sửa `metres` $\rightarrow$ **`square feet`**.
   * *Nâng cấp:* **"The woman started by highlighting the office's features, noting that it has plenty of natural light and is about 2,000 square feet."**

3. **Đoạn 3 — View thành phố & Nỗi lo tiếng ồn:**
   * *Gốc:* *"the man agreed that the office has great views that can see the whole city; but he wondered about the noise since it near the street;"*
   * *Sửa:* `views that can see` $\rightarrow$ **`great views of the whole city center`**; `since it near` $\rightarrow$ **`since it was near a busy street`**.
   * *Nâng cấp:* **"The man agreed that the space offers great views of the whole city, though he was concerned about street noise since it is on a busy street."**

4. **Đoạn 4 — Giải thích về cửa kính cách âm:**
   * *Gốc:* *"the woman said that the glasses will be replaced by another has double something that I couldn't hear;"*
   * *Sửa:* `the glasses` $\rightarrow$ **`the windows`**; `will be replaced` $\rightarrow$ **`had already been replaced`** (hoặc **`were replaced last year`**); nối mượt với `with double-something glass`.
   * *Nâng cấp:* **"The woman reassured him that the windows had been replaced with double-something glass—I couldn't catch the exact word—making it quiet inside."**

5. **Đoạn 5 — Phản ứng yên tâm & Hỏi chỗ đỗ xe:**
   * *Gốc:* *"The man said it can reassuaring, and asked about the park; that's all I can hear"*
   * *Sửa:* `it can reassuaring` $\rightarrow$ **`that was reassuring`**; `the park` $\rightarrow$ **`parking`**; `that's all I can hear` $\rightarrow$ **`that's all I could catch`**.
   * *Nâng cấp:* **"The man said that was reassuring and then asked about parking. That's all I could catch."**
