### 🔬 Phân Tích & Nâng Cấp Chi Tiết Từng Câu (5 Đoạn)

1. **Đoạn 1 — Mở bài:**
   * *Gốc:* *"I've finished listening to the third audio twice; for this audio I can understand most of things they said, but still miss some details; that what I heard:"*
   * *Sửa:* Ngắt câu bằng dấu chấm (`.`); `most of things` $\rightarrow$ **`most of what they said`**; `miss` $\rightarrow$ **`missed`** (quá khứ); `that what` $\rightarrow$ **`that's what`**.
   * *Nâng cấp:* **"I've finished listening to the third audio twice. I understood most of what they said, though I still missed a few details. Here's what I heard."**

2. **Đoạn 2 — Nhân vật (Thiếu vế 2 sau `between`):**
   * *Gốc:* *"the conversation is between a male patient who wanted to change his appointment at the Riverside clinic;"*
   * *Sửa:* Có `between` bắt buộc phải có **2 đối tượng (`A and B`)**! Chuyển `change` thành **`reschedule`** cho chuẩn y tế/công sở.
   * *Nâng cấp:* **"The conversation is between a male patient and a receptionist at Riverside Clinic. The man wanted to reschedule his appointment."**

3. **Đoạn 3 — Lịch cũ & Việc đột xuất:**
   * *Gốc:* *"the original appointment was at 2 o'clock Thursday, but he had something came up with his work,"*
   * *Sửa:* Bỏ `had` dư thừa trước `something came up`; dùng **`at work`** (không dùng *with his work*); thêm `on` trước thứ (`at 2:00 PM on Thursday`).
   * *Nâng cấp:* **"His original appointment was at two o'clock on Thursday, but something came up at work."**

4. **Đoạn 4 — Hỏi lịch phù hợp:**
   * *Gốc:* *"then the accosiate asked him what the schedule will suite him;"*
   * *Sửa:* Chính tả `receptionist` / `associate` và **`suit`** (không phải *suite*); lùi thì `will` $\rightarrow$ `would`; đổi `what the schedule` thành **`what day or time`**.
   * *Nâng cấp:* **"The receptionist checked availability and asked what day or time would suit him better."**

5. **Đoạn 5 — Phần quên & Chốt lịch mới:**
   * *Gốc:* *"the next conversation I can't remmember but finally they agreed that Tueday 9 o'clock suits with the man schedule"*
   * *Sửa:* `the middle part` (thay cho *the next conversation*); `Tueday` $\rightarrow$ **`Tuesday`**; **`suit` KHÔNG đi với `with`**; sở hữu cách **`the man's schedule`**; chia quá khứ **`suited`**.
   * *Nâng cấp:* **"I can't remember the middle part, but in the end, they agreed that Tuesday at nine o'clock suited the man's schedule."**
