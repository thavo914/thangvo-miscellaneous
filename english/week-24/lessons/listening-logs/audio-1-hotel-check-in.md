### 🔬 Phân Tích & Nâng Cấp Chi Tiết Từng Câu (7 Đoạn)

1. **Đoạn 1 — Mở bài:**
   * *Gốc:* `"I've just completed listening 2 times for the first audio;"`
   * *Lỗi:* `completed listening` hơi cứng $\rightarrow$ `finished listening`; `2 times` $\rightarrow$ `twice`; `listen` đi với **`to`** (không phải *for*); ngắt dấu `.`.
   * *Nâng cấp:* **"I've just finished listening to the first audio twice."** / *"I just listened to the first clip twice."*

2. **Đoạn 2 — Nhân vật (Who):**
   * *Gốc:* `"the conversation was about a man and a woman who's the receptionist at a hotel;"`
   * *Lỗi:* Giữa 2 người dùng **`between [A] and [B]`** (không dùng *about*); lệch thì `who's` với `was`; rút gọn thành `a hotel receptionist`.
   * *Nâng cấp:* **"The conversation was between a male guest and a hotel receptionist."**

3. **Đoạn 3 — Yêu cầu đặt phòng:**
   * *Gốc:* `"the man asked about the reservation he booked online under his name, Michael Preterson;"`
   * *Lỗi:* Chính tả *Preterson* $\rightarrow$ `Peterson`; đặt phòng trước lúc check-in dùng quá khứ hoàn thành `had booked`; bổ sung `last week`.
   * *Nâng cấp:* **"The man asked about an online reservation he had booked last week under his name, Michael Peterson."**

4. **Đoạn 4 — Đánh vần & Không tìm thấy hồ sơ:**
   * *Gốc:* `"then the woman asked him to spell his last name, which was P-E-T-E-R-S-O-N; but the woman could't find his reservation under that name,"`
   * *Lỗi:* Chính tả *could't* $\rightarrow$ `couldn't`; lặp từ `the woman`; sửa logic: lễ tân không thấy trước rồi mới nhờ đánh vần; tránh comma splice.
   * *Nâng cấp:* **"When she couldn't find anything under Peterson, she asked him to spell his last name—P-E-T-E-R-S-O-N—but still couldn't locate it."**

5. **Đoạn 5 — Đưa email xác nhận:**
   * *Gốc:* `"the man then said that he had a confirmation number that was sent to his email if it would help;"`
   * *Lỗi:* Nghe lệch chi tiết (`confirmation email right here on my phone`); cụm `if it would help` đứng cuối hơi lủng củng $\rightarrow$ dùng **`offered to show`**.
   * *Nâng cấp:* **"So he offered to show her the confirmation email right on his phone, hoping that would help."**

6. **Đoạn 6 — Tìm ra lỗi nhầm tên & Phản ứng của khách:**
   * *Gốc:* `"then the woman finally find out his reservation but it was under his first name instead of his last name, the man said no problem as long as he can have a room;"`
   * *Lỗi:* `find out` $\rightarrow$ **`found`** (chia quá khứ + tìm hồ sơ dùng `find`); dùng `and realized` thay `but`; bổ sung `apologized for the confusion`; lùi thì `can` $\rightarrow$ `could` (hoặc `got`).
   * *Nâng cấp:* **"With that, she finally found his booking and realized it had been entered under his first name instead of his last name. She apologized for the confusion, and he said no problem as long as he could get a room."**

7. **Đoạn 7 — Chốt phòng & Chỉ thang máy:**
   * *Gốc:* `"the woman then said his number room was 412 or somewhat and point him where the elavator was."`
   * *Lỗi:* `number room` $\rightarrow$ **`room number`**; `or somewhat` $\rightarrow$ bỏ hoặc `or something`; `point` $\rightarrow$ **`pointed him toward`**; chính tả *elavator* $\rightarrow$ `elevator`; bổ sung `key card` + `just around the corner to your left`.
   * *Nâng cấp:* **"Finally, she told him he was all set in Room 412, handed him his key card, and pointed him toward the elevator just around the corner to his left."**
