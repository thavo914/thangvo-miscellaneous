## ⏰ Weekly Study Schedule (90–120 Mins/Day)

Every day follows a balanced, action-packed 4-block engine focusing on active speaking and reflexes:
* **Block 1 (15–20 Mins)**: Story Mission & Pattern Activation 🎯 (Deconstructing core formulas from notes + slot-filling).
* **Block 2 (35–45 Mins)**: 3-Second Quick-Fire Reflex Drills ⚡ (15 rapid situational prompts + De-Vietlish traps).
* **Block 3 (20–25 Mins)**: Storyline Transcreation & Dialogue Breakdown 🎭 (Multi-turn conversational roleplay transcreation).
* **Block 4 (25–35 Mins)**: ChatGPT Voice Mode Real-Time Simulation 🎙️ (Hands-free voice battle + Daily Anki reflection).
* **Listening & Active Recall Companion**: Pair this week's practice with [listening-gist-catching-reference.md](file:///c:/Users/adm.thangvm/repos/thangvo-miscellaneous/english/week-24/lessons/listening-gist-catching-reference.md) and [verbal-recall-cues.md](file:///c:/Users/adm.thangvm/repos/thangvo-miscellaneous/english/week-24/lessons/verbal-recall-cues.md).
