## 🧩 Part 1: Vocabulary & Collocation Slot-Filling (20 Points — 2 Pts / Question)

*Fill in each blank with the exact missing word, preposition, or phrasal verb particle from Week 24.*

1. We've just received __________ from management that we need to cut the overall budget __________ fifteen percent.
2. Let me pull __________ the catering quotes and see where we can __________ some adjustments.
3. A technician was scheduled to fix the copier yesterday, but she called to cancel __________ the last minute.
4. I think the design looks great, though I do need to __________ one minor revision to the pricing slide.
5. Finding someone with __________ the experience and the exact software skills we need __________ been difficult.
6. What matters now is that I need the camera __________ and __________ before our 10:00 AM meeting.
7. Instead, a technician will guide me __________ the installation steps __________ the phone.
8. We can switch __________ a sit-down dinner __________ a cocktail reception with lighter refreshments.
9. They're expecting something __________, so moving the event to an office room might look like we're cutting __________.
10. Our data volume has completely __________ our legacy tool, so migrating now will definitely pay __________ in the long run.

<details><summary>🔍 Part 1 Answer Key & Explanations</summary>

1. **word** *(uncountable noun, no "a")* / **by** *(preposition of percentage margin)*
2. **up** *(open file/dashboard on screen)* / **make** *(fixed collocation: MAKE adjustments)*
3. **at** *(fixed idiom: at the last minute)*
4. **make** *(fixed collocation: MAKE a revision, never "do")*
5. **both** *(correlative conjunction both A and B)* / **has** *(gerund subject "Finding..." takes singular verb)*
6. **up** / **running** *(idiom: up and running = operational)*
7. **through** *(guide someone through steps)* / **over** *(over the phone)*
8. **from** / **to** *(switch from Option A to Option B)*
9. **upscale** *(post-nominal adjective after "something")* / **corners** *(idiom: cut corners)*
10. **outgrown** *(outgrow a legacy tool)* / **off** *(pay off in the long run)*

## ⚠️ Part 2: Vietlish Trap Detection & Deconstruction (20 Points — 4 Pts / Scenario)

*Each sentence below contains a subtle grammatical error or unnatural Vietlish phrasing. Identify the error, briefly explain WHY it is wrong, and write the natural native English correction.*

1. **Sentence**: *"I wonder if does this supplier offer the same deals as its physical locations."*
   * **Your Correction & Explanation**: ________________________________________________

2. **Sentence**: *"I'm not sure too. Why don't you ask IT to install it tomorrow?"* (Context: Agreeing with someone who said "I'm not sure either.")
   * **Your Correction & Explanation**: ________________________________________________

3. **Sentence**: *"I will have my teammate to check the database, and I'll get QA test the release."*
   * **Your Correction & Explanation**: ________________________________________________

4. **Sentence**: *"Because of how great the team performed, we will be letting everyone to leave early on Friday."*
   * **Your Correction & Explanation**: ________________________________________________

5. **Sentence**: *"I received my new webcam yesterday, but I still can't have installed it."*
   * **Your Correction & Explanation**: ________________________________________________

<details><summary>🔍 Part 2 Answer Key & Deconstructions</summary>

1. **Correction**: *"I wonder if **this supplier offers** the same deals **at** its physical locations."*
   * **Why**: (1) Sau `I wonder if`, mệnh đề danh từ **cấm đảo trợ động từ** (*does*). (2) `deals` là ưu đãi, không thể so sánh trực tiếp ngang bằng *as* với địa điểm vật lý (`physical locations`) — phải dùng giới từ chỉ nơi chốn `at`.
2. **Correction**: *"I'm not sure **either**."*
   * **Why**: Đồng tình với câu phủ định bắt buộc dùng **`either`** ở cuối câu, tuyệt đối không dùng *too*.
3. **Correction**: *"I will **have** my teammate **check** the database, and I'll **get** QA **to test** the release."*
   * **Why**: Quy tắc Causative ngược nhau: `HAVE someone DO` (**cấm `to`**) $\leftrightarrow$ `GET someone TO DO` (**bắt buộc `to`**).
4. **Correction**: *"...we will be **letting everyone leave** early on Friday."*
   * **Why**: Sau động từ truyền khiến `let + [Object]`, động từ theo sau luôn ở dạng nguyên mẫu không `to` (bare infinitive).
5. **Correction**: *"...but I still **can't have it installed**."* (or *can't get it installed*)
   * **Why**: `can't have installed it` là cấu trúc suy đoán quá khứ ("chắc chắn là đã không cài"), sai nghĩa hoàn toàn. Phải dùng Causative bị động kẹp tân ngữ (`have + it + V3/ed`) để chỉ trạng thái chưa thu xếp cài đặt xong.

</details>
