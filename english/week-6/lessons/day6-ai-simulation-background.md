# Day 6 — AI Simulation (Background Topic)
## Week 6 | Is this the era of distrust?
*Technique: Professional Role-Play — Data Engineering / Casino | ~60 minutes total*

> **Purpose:** Transfer episode vocabulary and critical thinking into your professional world. Trust, reliability, competence, and judgment are central themes in data engineering — they map directly onto pipeline quality, stakeholder communication, and system design.
>
> **Rules:**
> - B1–B2 level English throughout.
> - No mid-conversation corrections — ask for feedback after each round.
> - Use at least **2 vocabulary words from this week** in each round: *trustworthiness, gullible, trust to luck, indiscriminately, competence, reliability.*

## Round 1 — Non-Technical Stakeholder (15 min)

**Goal:** Explain data reliability and pipeline trustworthiness to someone with no data background.

### Prompt (paste into your AI chat):

> "Let's do a role-play in English. You are a casino floor manager at a luxury resort. You have no background in data or technology. I am the company's Senior Data Engineer. You've recently heard that 'the data reports can't be trusted' and you're frustrated. Ask me what trustworthiness means in the context of data pipelines, and challenge me if I use jargon you don't understand. Use B1–B2 level English. Don't correct me during the conversation — give feedback at the end."

### Vocabulary to use naturally:
- *"Our pipeline's reliability means it runs correctly every single time, not just sometimes."*
- *"If we trust the data indiscriminately — without checking the source — we end up with bad decisions."*
- *"Competence in data engineering means building systems that deliver accurate results, not just fast ones."*

## Round 2 — Peer Expert Discussion (15 min)

**Goal:** Discuss a technical design decision with a senior data engineer or solution architect.

### Prompt (paste into your AI chat):

> "Let's have a technical discussion in English. You are a senior solution architect with 15 years of experience in data warehousing for the hospitality industry. I am a data engineer presenting a design proposal for a new patron data pipeline using dbt and Airflow. Challenge my decisions, ask about data quality controls, reliability mechanisms, and how I ensure the pipeline's trustworthiness under failure conditions. Use B1–B2 level English. Don't correct me during the conversation — give feedback at the end."

### Topics to be ready to discuss:
- How do you handle failures and retries in Airflow DAGs?
- What does "competence" look like in a dbt model — what makes it correct vs. merely functional?
- How do you build trust with business stakeholders who've been burned by bad data before?
- When have you "trusted to luck" in a design choice and what happened?
