## Phase 2 — Recorded Monologue (30 min)

**Goal:** Speak continuously for 2–3 minutes on one chosen topic. Record yourself.

### Choose ONE Topic:

**Option A — Data Pipeline Trustworthiness**
> *"Describe how you ensure that the data you produce is trustworthy — covering honesty (accuracy), competence (correctness of logic), and reliability (consistent delivery). Use a real pipeline or project as your example."*

**Option B — Earning Stakeholder Trust After a Data Failure**
> *"Tell the story of a time when stakeholders lost trust in your data. What went wrong, how did you diagnose it, and what did you do to rebuild their confidence? What did this teach you about the three ingredients of trustworthiness: honesty, competence, and reliability?"*

**Option C — The Danger of Trusting Data Indiscriminately**
> *"Describe a situation (real or hypothetical) where data was consumed or reported indiscriminately — without proper quality checks — and the harmful results that followed. How should data engineers approach trust differently?"*

---

### Timing Structure

| Segment | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0:00 – 0:30 | Open with a strong statement, question, or anecdote that grabs attention |
| Main Point | 0:30 – 1:30 | Introduce your core idea with the 3-part structure (honesty, competence, reliability) |
| Example | 1:30 – 2:30 | Share a specific, concrete example from your work |
| Conclusion | 2:30 – 3:00 | Close with a reflection or lesson learned |

---

### Recording Instructions

1. Set a timer for 3 minutes.
2. Record on your phone or computer.
3. **Do not stop or restart** if you make a mistake — keep going.
4. Complete **2 full recording attempts**.
5. Listen back to Attempt 2.
