# Day 5 — Monologue + Shadowing
## Week 6 | Is this the era of distrust?

**Goal:** Speak about the episode topic in your own words (Monologue), then internalise the speaker's rhythm, stress, and connected speech (Shadowing).
**Time:** ~120 min total — split into two sittings if needed. *Monologue in the morning, Shadowing in the evening works well.*

---

## PART A — Monologue (60 min)

### Phase 1 — Warm-Up (15 min)
*Goal: Relax the vocal cords and activate your English lexicon before recording.*

- [ ] **Lexical Labeling (5 min):** Look at your working environment. Describe 3–5 things aloud that connect to the theme of trust or reliability.
  - *"My laptop is a tool I rely on — if it crashes with no backup, I've been trusting to luck."*
  - *"This dashboard must be trustworthy — the finance team makes decisions based on it every morning."*
- [ ] **Syntactic Narration (10 min):** Narrate your current professional situation aloud:
  - *"Right now I'm working on a data pipeline. I need to make sure it's reliable — not just running, but running correctly, every single time. That takes honesty about its limits, competence in how I build it, and reliability in how I monitor it."*

---

### Phase 2 — The Monologue (30 min)
*Goal: Apply episode vocabulary fluently in a professional context. Speed beats perfection.*

**Topic:** *"How do you build trust in data? Describe what trustworthiness means for a data pipeline or reporting system — using honesty, competence, and reliability as your framework."*

**Instructions:**
- Aim for **2–3 minutes** of continuous speaking.
- Use at least **4 target words** from the episode vocabulary.
- Record Attempt 1 without stopping. Listen back. Then record Attempt 2 immediately.

**Structure Template:**

| Section | Time | Key Phrase to Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | 0:00–0:30 | *"trust in data is not just about accuracy — it's about…"* |
| **Honesty** | 0:30–1:00 | *"An honest pipeline is one that…"* |
| **Competence** | 1:00–1:30 | *"Competence means not just running, but…"* |
| **Reliability** | 1:30–2:00 | *"Reliability is the boring one — that's just…"* |
| **Danger of gullibility** | 2:00–2:30 | *"Stakeholders who trust dashboards indiscriminately…"* |
| **Conclusion** | 2:30–3:00 | *"Ultimately, trustworthiness in data requires…"* |

**Record to file:** `week6-day5-monologue-v1.m4a` → listen → `week6-day5-monologue-v2.m4a`

## PART B — Shadowing (55 min)

**Primary Clip:** Refer to `lesson-transcript.md` → `[TARGET]` section (~50s, the Onora O'Neill + David Edmonds exchange on gullibility and the three ingredients).

---

### Stage 1 — Auditory Priming (5 min)
> No transcript. No reading. Just listen.

- Play the primary shadowing clip **3 times**.
- Close your eyes on the third play. Focus only on rhythm, stress, and pace.
- Write 3 words that describe how the speakers sound:

```
1. _______________   2. _______________   3. _______________
```

---

### Stage 2 — Transcribed Echoing (15 min)
> Open `lesson-transcript.md` to the `[TARGET]` section. Read along sentence by sentence.

- After each sentence, **pause and repeat from memory**.
- Pay attention to annotation markers: `_` catenation · `(t)`/`(d)` elision · **BOLD** stress · `[up]`/`[down]` intonation

**Focus phrases for this clip:**

| Phrase | What to imitate |
|---|---|
| *"…gullible…"* | Stress on first syllable: **GUL**-i-bl |
| *"…indiscriminately…"* | Stress: in-dis-**CRIM**-in-ate-ly — don't rush |
| *"…three ingredients…"* | Rising intonation on "three"; list follows |
| *"That's the boring one."* | Flat, dry delivery — "boring" is ironic |
| *"…episodically honest…"* | ep-i-**SOD**-ic-al-ly — say it slowly at first |

---

### Stage 3 — Synchronous Shadowing (20 min)
> Speak **0.5 seconds behind** the audio. Transcript in front of you.

| Round | Focus | Reps |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Follow along — don't worry about accuracy | 3× |
| 4–6 | Match stress and connected speech patterns | 3× |
| 7–10 | Match emotional tone and pace exactly | 4×+ |

> 💡 If you fall behind, skip ahead — don't stop. Keeping pace is the training.

---

### Stage 4 — Blind Shadowing (15 min)
> No transcript. Record both attempts.

- **Attempt 1:** Shadow from memory. Focus on fluency over accuracy.
- **Attempt 2:** After a short break, shadow again. Notice what improved.

**Record to file:** `week6-day5-shadow-attempt1.m4a` and `week6-day5-shadow-attempt2.m4a`
