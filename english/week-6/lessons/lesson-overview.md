## Learning Objectives

| Skill | Objective |
|---|---|
| **Listening** | Follow an analytical discussion about trust, misinformation, and societal values |
| **Vocabulary** | Use 6 target words (trustworthiness, gullible, trust to luck, indiscriminately, competence, reliability) accurately in formal and professional contexts |
| **Pronunciation** | Master weak forms, linked speech, and stress patterns in abstract noun phrases |
| **Speaking** | Discuss trust, data quality, and professional reliability using episode vocabulary |
| **Thinking** | Evaluate who and what deserves trust using Onora O'Neill's 3-criteria framework |

---

## Target Vocabulary

| Word | POS | Meaning | Episode Sentence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **trustworthiness** | noun | The quality of being able to be trusted as honest and reliable | *"We'll be hearing from a philosopher who believes the problem is not about trust itself but about trustworthiness."* |
| **gullible** | adjective | Easy to deceive because you trust and believe people too quickly | *"We have another word, which is gullible… you simply place trust indiscriminately without making a judgement."* |
| **trust to luck** | phrase (verb) | Believe or hope that things will happen for the best without any judgement | *"Just trusting to luck as we say, is probably not a virtue."* |
| **indiscriminately** | adverb | Done in a random way that does not show care or judgement, usually with harmful results | *"Their biggest mistake was to trust him indiscriminately."* |
| **competence** | noun | The ability to do something well, in a satisfactory or effective way | *"Second, competence. Beyond honesty and competence there's a third element to trustworthiness."* |
| **reliability** | noun | The quality of being trusted to do what you say you will, all the time | *"That's the boring one. That's just being honest and competent each time."* |

## Ground Rules

1. **One artifact, many angles** — apply every technique to this same episode.
2. **Record your output** — voice memos count. You are your own evaluator.
3. **Resist translation** — describe unknown words in English first.
4. **Quantity beats quality** — 3 imperfect recordings beat 1 over-rehearsed one.
