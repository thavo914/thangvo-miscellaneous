## Phase 3 — Phonetic Drill (15 min)

**Goal:** Build muscle memory for 4 key phrases from the episode.

### Instructions:
- For each phrase below, say it aloud **5 times**, paying attention to the annotation markers.
- Aim for natural connected speech rhythm, not word-by-word reading.

| # | Phrase | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | *"just **trusting** to **luck** as_we **say**"* | Rhythm: stress on content words; schwa on "as" |
