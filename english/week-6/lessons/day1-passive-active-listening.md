## Week 6 | Is this the era of distrust?
*Technique: Listening + Dictation A | ~50 minutes total*

---

## Phase 1 — Passive Listening (15–20 min)

**Goal:** Build familiarity. Do not force comprehension.

- [ ] Play the episode **2–3 times as background audio** while doing light tasks (stretching, tidying, commuting).
- Do NOT read the transcript or vocabulary list.
- Let your ears absorb the rhythm, tone, and natural speed of the conversation.

> 💡 **Tip:** Notice the topic — trust and distrust in society. Let impressions form naturally.

---

## Phase 2 — Active Listening (25 min)

**Goal:** Extract meaning from the spoken audio using structured questions.

### Global Listening — Listen once, then answer from memory:

1. What is the central question this episode explores?
2. What does philosopher Onora O'Neill say is the real problem — trust or trustworthiness?

---

### Targeted Listening — Listen again (1–2 times), then answer:

3. According to the episode, what are the **three ingredients** of trustworthiness?
4. What does being *gullible* mean, and how does it relate to trust?
5. How did Bernie Madoff exploit people's trust? What mistake did his victims make?
6. What is the difference between being *reliable* and being *episodically honest*?

---

## Phase 3 — Micro-Dictation Part A (25 min)

**Goal:** Write what you hear for the primary shadowing clip. Maximum 3 replays. Do NOT use the transcript.

### Instructions:
1. Find the segment starting with **"We have another word, which is gullible…"** and ending at **"…not others."** (the Onora O'Neill + David Edmonds exchange — approx. 50 seconds).
2. Play the clip once for orientation.
3. Write down every word you can catch on your first attempt. Leave blanks for uncertain parts.
4. Play up to **2 more times** to fill in gaps.
