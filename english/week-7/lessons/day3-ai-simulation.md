# Day 3: AI Simulation

*Set up your AI voice assistant (e.g., ChatGPT Voice, Gemini Live) for these 3 rounds. The tone should be at a B1-B2 level. Request feedback at the very end of all rounds, not during the flow.*

## Prompt for AI Assistant
> "Let's practice my English. I want to discuss the 6 Minute English episode 'Will Covid-19 change cities?'. We will do 3 short rounds. Please speak to me at a B1-B2 English level. Don't correct me during the rounds; keep the conversation flowing. At the end of the session, please review my major mistakes and give me 3 improved sentences to practice. Let me know when you're ready for Round 1."

## Round 1 - Discussion (15 min)
**Role:** AI plays a curious peer who also listened to this episode.
**Goal:** Share initial thoughts and recap the main ideas of the episode.
**Discussion points:**
- How did diseases historically change cities like Paris or London?
- What was surprising about the fact that antibiotics were discovered so late?
- What happened to the cities during the Covid-19 lockdown?

## Round 2 - Expert Role-play (15 min)
**Role:** AI plays Professor Beatriz Colomina or Dr Vaidehi Tandel. You play an interviewer or city planner.
**Goal:** Dig deeper into the structural or social challenges of cities.
**Discussion points:**
- Ask the expert about how tuberculosis affected dense cities.
- Discuss the idea of the "15-minute city" and whether it is feasible.
- Talk about why people are reluctant to move away from the city centre due to their livelihoods and the cost of the commute.

## Round 3 - Devil's Advocate / Debate (20 min)
**Role:** AI strongly argues that cities should be decentralized and people should move out, you argue that we must fix the city centres so people can keep their jobs. 
**Goal:** Practice debate language, agreeing, disagreeing, and defending a point.
**Focus vocabulary to try using:** *metropolis, commute, livelihood, spread like wildfire, airborne*
