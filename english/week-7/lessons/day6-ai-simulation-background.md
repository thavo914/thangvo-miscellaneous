# Day 6: AI Simulation — Background Topic

*Purpose: Practice professional English conversation in real-world scenarios from your background (Data Engineering / Casino).*
*Set up your AI voice assistant. Request B1-B2 level English. Remind it not to correct you mid-conversation and ask for feedback after all rounds.*

## 🗣️ Background Prompt Bank
**Instructions for AI Setup:** Provide the AI with the appropriate prompt below for each round.

### Round 1 - Non-Technical Stakeholder (15 min)
**Prompt for AI:** "You are a Casino Floor Manager with no technical background. I am a Data Engineer. I need to explain to you how a recent software bug spread through our POS systems and how we are redesigning the network to prevent it — similar to how cities built sewers to stop cholera. Ask me questions and ask for non-technical analogies if you don't understand my technical terms."
**Your Goal:** Explain complex system architecture (network isolation, firewalls) using the city-planning and disease metaphors from the episode.

### Round 2 - Peer Expert Discussion (15 min)
**Prompt for AI:** "You are a Senior Data Engineer at our company. Let's discuss our current monolithic database architecture. I think it's becoming a congested 'metropolis' and want to pitch treating our data pipelines like decentralized cities to reduce the data's 'commute' time. Engage in a technical debate about latency, microservices, and system bottlenecks."
**Your Goal:** Use episode concepts (metropolis, commute, spread) in a highly technical architecture discussion.

### Round 3 - Job Interview / Presentation (20 min)
**Prompt for AI:** "You are a hiring manager for a large resort & casino group. I am applying for a Senior Data Engineer role. Ask me 3 questions about: 1) A time I had to stop data errors from spreading quickly. 2) How I ensure my data pipelines support the business's 'livelihood'. 3) My remote work communication style. Use the STAR method to evaluate me."
**Your Goal:** Answer the questions smoothly using the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result). Incorporate the target vocabulary where natural.
