# Day 5: Monologue + Shadowing

*Two-part session (~120 min total). Split into two sittings if needed.*

## Part A - Monologue (60 min)

### Phase 1 - Warm-up (15 min)
- **Lexical Labeling**: Describe your workspace aloud using episode-related words (e.g., is your office a small *metropolis*? Does news *spread like wildfire*?).
- **Syntactic Narration**: Narrate a professional context aloud (e.g., "The new data warehouse was designed to...").

### Phase 2 - Recorded Monologue (30 min)
- **Topic**: Use the episode theme (disease changing spaces, commutes, livelihoods) but adapt it to your domain. 
  - *Example Idea:* "How the Covid pandemic permanently changed the way casino floors are operated, or how it shifted data traffic as everyone worked remotely."
  - *Example Idea:* "Why commuting to the office is no longer necessary for Data Engineers, and how remote work has changed our livelihoods."
- **Goal**: Aim for 2–3 minutes.
- **Recording**: 
  1. Record Attempt 1 without stopping. Listen back.
  2. Record Attempt 2 immediately.
- **Structure**: Hook → Core idea mapped to episode theme → Example from professional context → Conclusion.

### Stage 2 - Transcribed Echoing (15 min)
Sentence-by-sentence with the transcript. Pause and repeat. Note specific focus phrases and rhythms.

### Stage 3 - Synchronous Shadowing (20 min)
Follow the speaker with text, using a 0.5s delay. Do at least 10 repetitions.
- **Round A:** Focus on accuracy.
- **Round B:** Focus on stress and connected speech.
- **Round C:** Focus on emotional tone and pacing matching.
