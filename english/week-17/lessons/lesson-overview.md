## 🗣️ 4. Core Lesson Dialogues

### Dialogue 1 (Topic 1: Seeking Mentor Advice Over Coffee Before the Review)
*Setting: Nam (Senior Engineer/Mentor) and Thang (Data Engineer) are having coffee near the office.*

**Nam:** Hey Thang, how's your prep going for the annual performance review next Thursday?  
**Thang:** Honestly Nam, I'm feeling a bit nervous. I know **I have consistently delivered** key projects this year, but I always find it awkward to talk about my own achievements without sounding boastful.  
**Nam:** That's completely normal, but remember that reviews are meant for self-advocacy. You shouldn't be shy about how your work **contributes to** the team.  
**Thang:** True. **What I'm most proud of is how** our team stabilized the daily reporting pipelines. In fact, we **delivered key updates ahead of schedule** and kept uptime above 99.8%.  
**Nam:** Exactly! Point to concrete metrics. You also **took on extra tasks** when you stepped in during the payment gateway migration. That really made your work **stand out** to the whole team.  
**Thang:** Thanks, Nam. You're right. With my **history of delivering** pipeline optimizations, I should focus on clear facts and results rather than trying to sound fancy.  
**Nam:** Spot on. If you present hard data with confidence, your manager will see the full value you provide.

---

### Dialogue 2 (Topic 2: 1-on-1 Performance Review & 25% Salary Negotiation with Manager)
*Setting: Thang meets with Sarah, his Engineering Manager, in a conference room for the annual evaluation.*

**Sarah:** Thanks for coming in, Thang. Overall, your feedback from product and analytics has been fantastic. You've had a standout year.  
**Thang:** Thanks so much, Sarah. I really appreciate the feedback. Over the past twelve months, I've really enjoyed **taking on more ownership** across our core data infrastructure.  
**Sarah:** It definitely shows. The data warehouse refactoring was a massive win for us.  
**Thang:** Yes, **by optimizing the storage tier and warehouse queries, I was able to deliver significant bottom-line impact**, cutting our monthly cloud spend by over thirty percent.  
**Sarah:** That was huge for our quarterly department budget, no doubt.  
**Thang:** Building on that, Sarah, **based on my expanded scope of ownership and current market benchmarks, I'd like to make a strong case for a 25% salary adjustment**.  
**Sarah:** Wow, twenty-five percent is quite a significant jump, Thang. Our standard company merit increase is usually between five and ten percent.  
**Thang:** **While I understand standard company bands**, my responsibilities have evolved from routine pipeline maintenance to leading architectural overhauls and unblocking the analytics team. When I **benchmark against the market** for this level of ownership, a 25% adjustment aligns directly with the value delivered.  
**Sarah:** You've clearly done your homework and the results speak for themselves. Let me take these numbers to HR and the director, and see how close we can get to that target.  
**Thang:** Thank you, Sarah. I'm very excited about our roadmap for next year and committed to continuing this level of impact.
