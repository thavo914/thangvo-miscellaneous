## 🗣️ 1. IELTS Speaking Practice Questions

### Part 1 (Interview)
1. **Do you often have to explain complex things to other people?**
   - *Tip*: Mention how you bring stakeholders up to speed on technical issues.
   - *Answer*: "Yes, quite often actually. In my job as a data engineer, I frequently have to explain technical issues, like data discrepancies, to business analysts. It's important to simplify things so I can put their minds at ease and bring them up to speed quickly."
   
2. **How do you handle difficult situations at work?**
   - *Tip*: Use the target collocations like "look into something" and "root cause".
   - *Answer*: "When a problem arises, I try to stay calm and tell my manager that I totally understand their concern. Then, I look into the issue immediately to find the root cause, so we can iron out the problems as fast as possible."

3. **Are you good at working under pressure?**
   - *Tip*: Mention how you reassure people using the Future Continuous.
   - *Answer*: "I think so. Even when things go wrong, like a system failure, I focus on the solution. I just assure everyone that we will be working on it and will have it resolved shortly. It helps to keep everyone calm."

### Part 2 (Cue Card)
**Describe a time when you had to solve a problem for someone else.**
You should say:
- What the problem was and who it affected
- What caused the problem
- What steps you took to solve it
- And explain how the other person felt after you resolved it.

### Part 3 (Analytical Discussion)
1. **Why is it important for technical staff to have good communication skills in modern companies?**
2. **How can a company rebuild trust with its clients after a major mistake or service failure?**

## 🎙️ 4. Interactive Shadowing & Pacing Guide

Practice speaking these lines out loud. Use the slashes `/` for pauses, and stress the **capitalized** words.

**Line 1 (Empathizing and providing the reason)**:
> *"I totally understand your **CONCERN** / about the missing **DATA**. / What caused the discrepancy / was **LATE**-arriving events."*

- **Pacing**: Pause after *concern*, *data*, and *discrepancy*.
- **Intonation**: Show empathy on *concern*, then bring a firm, explanatory tone on *late*.

**Line 2 (Reassuring and setting a deadline)**:
> *"Rest **ASSURED** / the missing records have been **BACKFILLED** / and we will be monitoring the pipeline / **CLOSELY**."*

- **Pacing**: Pause slightly after *assured* and *backfilled*. Speed up slightly on *and we will be monitoring the pipeline*.
- **Intonation**: Stress **ASSURED** and **BACKFILLED** to convey confidence and completion.
