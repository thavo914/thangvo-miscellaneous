## 🗣️ 1. IELTS Speaking Practice Questions

### Part 1 (Interview)
1. **Do you think you are a patient person?**
   - *Tip*: Talk about your journey and whether you enjoy the process or just the result.
   - *Answer*: "To be honest, I used to be quite impatient, always chasing the next goal. But as I've grown older, I've realized that the joy isn't in reaching the destination, it's the process of getting there. So, yes, I think I'm much more patient now."
   
2. **How do you usually handle criticism or negative feedback?**
   - *Tip*: Use the target collocations like "water off a duck's back" or "take things personally".
   - *Answer*: "I used to take things personally, but these days I try to keep things in perspective. I remind myself that feedback is just an opportunity to grow, so now, unfair criticism is like water off a duck's back to me."

3. **Do you like trying new things?**
   - *Tip*: Mention stepping out of your comfort zone and double comparatives.
   - *Answer*: "Absolutely! I strongly believe that the more you step out of your comfort zone, the more opportunities appear. It's amazing what can happen when you let go of your fear and just try something new."

### Part 2 (Cue Card)
**Describe a time when you changed your mind about what was important in life.**
You should say:
- What your original goal or mindset was
- What caused you to change your perspective
- How your new perspective affects your daily life
- And explain how you feel about this change.

### Part 3 (Analytical Discussion)
1. **Why do you think many people in modern society are obsessed with reaching a certain level of wealth or status?**
2. **How can parents teach their children to find joy in the process rather than just focusing on winning?**

## 🎙️ 4. Interactive Shadowing & Pacing Guide

Practice speaking these lines out loud. Use the slashes `/` for pauses, and stress the **capitalized** words.

**Line 1 (Expressing a shift in mindset)**:
> *"What I really **WANTED** / wasn't the **TITLE** / but the feeling of **GROWTH**."*

- **Pacing**: Pause naturally after *wanted* and *title*.
- **Intonation**: Stress **WANTED**, **TITLE**, and **GROWTH** to highlight the contrast.

**Line 2 (Discussing the value of the journey)**:
> *"The joy isn't in reaching the **GOAL** / it's the **PROCESS** / of getting **THERE**."*

- **Pacing**: Pause after *goal* and *process*. Keep the rhythm steady.
- **Intonation**: Rise slightly on *goal*, and bring your pitch down naturally on *there*.
