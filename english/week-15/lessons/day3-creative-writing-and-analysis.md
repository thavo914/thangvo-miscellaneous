## 🎤 2. General Monologue: The Journey is the Destination

*Read the following monologue out loud. Pay attention to the bolded grammar structures and phrases.*

"I used to be obsessed with the idea of 'making it'. I thought that if I just got the right job, the perfect house, and enough money in the bank, I would finally be happy. **What I really wanted wasn't the money, but the feeling of security.** But the funny thing is, every time I achieved a goal, the happiness only lasted for a few days. Then I was right back to chasing the next thing. It was exhausting.

Then, one day, I had this realization. **The joy isn't in reaching the peak of the mountain, it's the process of climbing it.** You have to learn to enjoy the struggle, the small wins, and even the failures. I stopped **taking things personally** when things went wrong, and started to treat setbacks like **water off a duck's back**. It completely changed my life. **The more you step out of your comfort zone, the more you realize that growth is the actual reward.** If you're not learning and evolving, you are just stagnating. So now, I try to remind **myself** to stay present and just enjoy the ride."

### Key Vocabulary & Concepts
* **Making it**: (Idiom) Trở nên thành công.
* **Stagnating**: (Verb) Trì trệ, không phát triển.
* **Stay present**: (Phrase) Sống trong hiện tại.

## 📝 3. Band 6.5-7.0 Cue Card Sample Answer

**Prompt**: *Describe a time when you changed your mind about what was important in life.*

**Sample Answer**:
"I'd like to talk about a significant shift in my mindset that happened a few years ago. For a long time, I was heavily focused on career progression and climbing the corporate ladder. I believed that success and a high salary were the most important things in life. 

However, everything changed when I started working on a really demanding project. I was putting in crazy hours, and honestly, I was starting to **let myself go**. I wasn't exercising, I rarely saw my friends, and I was constantly stressed. One day, a senior colleague, who is someone I deeply respect, pulled me aside. She told me that I needed to **keep things in perspective**. She pointed out that while hard work is good, **if you're not enjoying the daily work, you are just wasting your time**. 

That conversation really struck a chord with me. I realized that **I used to think that achieving the end goal was everything, but now I realize the journey itself is what matters**. I started to focus more on the process—learning new skills, collaborating with my team, and finding balance. 

This new perspective has completely changed my daily life. **It's amazing what can happen when you let go of the pressure to be perfect.** I feel much more fulfilled and far less anxious. Now, when I face challenges at work, I don't **take them personally**. I just view them as part of the learning curve."

### Conversational Words Highlighted
* **Climbing the corporate ladder**: Thăng tiến trong công việc/công ty.
* **Struck a chord with me**: (Idiom) Làm tôi thức tỉnh / Có sự đồng cảm sâu sắc.
* **Learning curve**: Quá trình học hỏi (thường ám chỉ giai đoạn đầu khó khăn khi học cái mới).
