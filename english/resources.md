## 💬 Speaking & Practice Tools

### AI Conversation Partners
- **ChatGPT Voice Mode** - Your tireless practice partner, available 24/7
  - Use for role-plays, simulations, feedback
  - Best for: Wednesday (Role-play day)
  
### Pronunciation Tools
- **ELSA Speak** - AI pronunciation coach
  - Use 10-15 minutes daily as warm-up
  - Best for: Monday (Shadowing day) warm-up

- **Otter.ai** - Speech-to-text transcription
  - Records your voice and converts to text
  - If AI can't transcribe you, your pronunciation needs work
  - Best for: All recording sessions

## 📝 Memory & Vocabulary

### Anki (Essential!)
- **Purpose**: Spaced repetition system for long-term retention
- **What to add**: Full sentences, NOT isolated words (Sentence Mining)
- **Daily commitment**: 15-30 minutes reviewing cards
- **Best for**: Tuesday (Vocabulary day) plus daily review

### Sentence Mining Resources
- Job descriptions from MGM, Las Vegas Sands (for Week 1-4)
- Documentation from your actual work projects
- Podcast transcripts
- ChatGPT-generated example sentences

## 💡 Content Curation Tips

1. **Shadowing** (Monday): Choose 1-2 minute video clips with clear speakers
2. **Vocabulary** (Tuesday): Focus on 10-15 high-value phrases weekly
3. **Role-play** (Wednesday): Create realistic scenarios from your actual work
4. **Listening** (Thursday): Challenge yourself with 3-minute segments
5. **Passive** (Sunday): Choose content you genuinely enjoy
