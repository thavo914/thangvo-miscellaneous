## 📊 2. Band 6.5-7.0 Spoken Model Answer

### Sample Response for the Day 2 Part 2 Cue Card:
"I want to talk about a difficult choice I had to make at my last job as a data engineer. 

About a year ago, our database was running very slow during our busiest hours on Saturday nights. This was a big problem because the marketing team couldn't see how many people were playing in the casino, so they couldn't give out VIP rewards on time. I proposed that we move all our active data to a new cloud server to make the system faster. 

This was quite a big **calculated risk** because moving databases while the system is running can cause it to crash, and if that happened, the company would lose a lot of money. Before making the move, I sat down with my team to **weigh the options** and see what could go wrong. We spent a few days planning everything carefully to **deal with the risks** of system downtime. 

We eventually decided to do the migration at 2 AM on a Sunday. I was really nervous, but luckily, everything went perfectly. The database became much faster, and the marketing team could see the player ratings instantly. 

In the end, I felt super relieved and happy with the result. This decision **played a big part in** making our system better, and it helped me prove to my manager that we can **make a smart choice** when we prepare well and use facts rather than just guessing."

### Conversational Vocabulary Highlighted
- **calculated risk** *(n)*: *A risk that you take after thinking carefully about the possible results.*
  - *Vietnamese:* Rủi ro có tính toán kỹ lưỡng.
- **weigh the options** *(v)*: *To think carefully about different choices before choosing one.*
  - *Vietnamese:* Cân nhắc các lựa chọn.
- **deal with the risks** *(v)*: *To manage or handle dangers or problems.*
  - *Vietnamese:* Xử lý rủi ro.
- **plays a big part in** *(v)*: *To be very important or have a big influence in a result.*
  - *Vietnamese:* Đóng vai trò lớn/quan trọng trong.
- **make a smart choice** *(v)*: *To make a good, logical decision based on information.*
  - *Vietnamese:* Đưa ra lựa chọn thông minh.
