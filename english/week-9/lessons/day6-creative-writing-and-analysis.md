## 📊 2. Band 6.5-7.0 Spoken Model Answer

### Sample Response for the Day 5 Part 2 Cue Card:
"I want to talk about a simple habit I have that helps **protect the environment**, which is cutting down on household energy waste and sorting our daily rubbish.

I started doing this about two years ago after reading an online article about how plastic waste can seriously **damage the environment** and hurt ocean life. The steps are actually quite simple: my family and I always separate plastic bottles and paper from regular food waste. We also make sure to unplug all our electronics when we aren't using them, and we decided to use energy-saving LED bulbs to cut down on electricity.

At first, it felt like a bit of extra work, but we **have been doing** this for over two years now, so it has become a completely natural part of our daily routine. 

In the end, I feel very positive and happy about this habit. I know that an individual effort might seem small, but if every household decides to **adopt a green lifestyle**, we can collectively **leave a small footprint** on the planet and make a real difference."

### Conversational Vocabulary Highlighted
- **protect the environment** *(v)*: *To do things that prevent damage to the air, water, and land.*
  - *Vietnamese:* Bảo vệ môi trường.
- **damage the environment** *(v)*: *To cause harm or destruction to the natural world.*
  - *Vietnamese:* Gây hại cho môi trường.
- **cut down on** *(v)*: *To reduce the size, amount, or quantity of something.*
  - *Vietnamese:* Cắt giảm.
- **leave a small footprint** *(v)*: *To have a very minimal, non-destructive effect on the planet's ecosystems.*
  - *Vietnamese:* Để lại dấu chân (tác hại) sinh thái nhỏ.
- **adopt a green lifestyle** *(v)*: *To live in a way that minimizes environmental harm.*
  - *Vietnamese:* Áp dụng lối sống xanh.
