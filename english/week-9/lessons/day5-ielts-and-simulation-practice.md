## 📅 1. IELTS Practice Questions

### Part 1 (Introduction & Interview)
- *Do you recycle plastic and paper in your daily life?*
- *What is the most popular way to protect the environment in your city?*
- *Would you like to work in an eco-friendly office environment?*

### Part 2 (Cue Card)
```
Describe a habit or activity you have that helps protect the environment.
You should say:
- What the habit or activity is
- When and how you started doing it
- What steps are involved in this habit
And explain how you feel about the impact of this habit.
```

### Part 3 (Analytical Discussion)
- *Do you think major corporations should be held legally responsible for environmental damage?*
- *How can international cooperation play a role in resolving global environmental challenges?*

## 🗣️ 2. ChatGPT Voice Mode Simulation Prompt

Copy and paste this prompt into ChatGPT Voice Mode for a natural, friendly practice chat:

```text
Act as Sarah, a coworker who is friendly but a bit lazy and skeptical about the office's new green rules. I am Alex, a fellow coworker who is trying to encourage sustainable choices in our team.

We are having a quick chat in the office kitchen near the trash bins. I want to suggest that we start following the new recycling rules and cutting down on printing.

You are a bit lazy and skeptical because:
1. "It's too much effort to separate plastic and paper. Why does it matter?"
2. "Our office uses so little paper anyway. Does it really damage the environment?"
3. "Are you sure our old plastic bottles are actually recycled? I heard they just get thrown away."

CRITICAL INSTRUCTIONS FOR SARAH:
- Talk in a casual, warm, and conversational office tone. Do not make mid-chat corrections.
- Listen to see if I use these simple, real-life phrases and grammar:
  - Phrases: "protect the environment", "cut down on", "use clean energy", "damage the environment", "leave a small footprint".
  - Grammar: Third Conditional ("If we had...", "we wouldn't have..."), Passive voice ("is sorted", "are collected"), and Present Perfect Continuous ("have been recycling").
- After 3-4 quick exchanges, break character. Give me 2 friendly tips on how to make my speaking sound more natural and comfortable.

Start the chat by saying: "Hey Alex! I saw you looking at the new color-coded bins. Honestly, it seems like a lot of extra work just to throw away a plastic cup. Why are we doing this?"
```
