# 📅 6-Month English Learning Schedule

**Start Date**: February 9, 2026  
**End Date**: July 26, 2026  
**Daily Commitment**: 2 hours  
**Total Duration**: 24 weeks

## 🎯 Program Philosophy: "Muscle Confusion" for Language

Just like in fitness training, varying your daily focus prevents plateaus and ensures comprehensive skill development. Each day targets a different aspect of English mastery.

## 🗓️ Weekly Cycle Pattern

All 24 weeks follow this proven structure:

| Day           | Focus                            | Duration | Goal                              |
| ------------- | -------------------------------- | -------- | --------------------------------- |
| **Monday**    | Input & Shadowing                | 2h       | Master pronunciation & intonation |
| **Tuesday**   | Vocabulary Mining                | 2h       | Build contextual vocabulary       |
| **Wednesday** | AI Role-play & Simulation        | 2h       | Practice real-world scenarios     |
| **Thursday**  | Deep Listening & Transcription   | 2h       | Train your ear for natural speech |
| **Friday**    | Paraphrasing & Flexible Thinking | 2h       | Express ideas multiple ways       |
| **Saturday**  | Mini-Project & Synthesis         | 2h       | Apply week's learning             |
| **Sunday**    | Passive Immersion                | 2h       | Relax with English content        |

## 🎯 Pacing & Customization

### Weekly Time Allocation
- **Weekdays** (Mon-Fri): 2 hours each = 10 hours
- **Saturday**: 2 hours (Mini-Project)
- **Sunday**: 2 hours (Passive, relaxed)
- **Total**: 14 hours/week

### Flexibility Guidelines
- **Fell behind?** Extend current week, don't skip
- **Progressing fast?** Add difficulty, don't rush ahead
- **Burnout risk?** Take a maintenance week (reduce to 1 hour/day)
- **Plateaued?** Increase challenge or change resources
