# Day 3: Creative Writing & Analysis (World Cup)

## 1. Creative Dialogue Writing
**Instructions**: Write a new, original 10-turn conversation between you and another data engineer in your team. You are discussing the recent World Cup matches while waiting for a heavy data query to finish.
**Requirement**: You must naturally include at least 2 of the target grammar rules (Present Continuous for Future, Adverbs of Degree, First Conditional) and 3 of the target collocations (*kick off, root for, stay up late, pull off an upset, knock out*).

## 2. Band 6.5-7.0 Cue Card Sample Answer

**Prompt**: Describe a sports event you watched that was very exciting.

**Sample Answer**:
"I'd like to talk about a match I watched recently. The World Cup just **kicked off** a few days ago, and I am a huge football fan. I usually **root for** Spain, so I made sure to watch their opening game. Because of the time difference between the host country and Vietnam, I had to **stay up late**—the game didn't start until 2 AM! 

I watched it alone in my living room, trying not to wake up my family. The event was absolutely brilliant. Everyone thought Spain would easily win, but the opposing team almost **pulled off an upset** in the first half by scoring an early goal. The thing that surprised me most was how calm Spain remained under pressure. In the second half, they changed their tactics and eventually won 2-1. If they keep playing like this, I don't think they'll be **knocked out** anytime soon. It was so exciting because the tension was incredibly high, and I'm really glad I stayed up for it."

### Conversational Words Highlighted:
- **kicked off**: started (Vietnamese: bắt đầu)
- **root for**: support/cheer for (Vietnamese: cổ vũ cho)
- **stay up late**: not go to bed until late (Vietnamese: thức khuya)
- **pulled off an upset**: surprisingly defeated a stronger opponent (Vietnamese: tạo ra bất ngờ)
