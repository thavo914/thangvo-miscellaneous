# Day 2: IELTS Speaking & Voice Simulation (World Cup)

## 1. IELTS Speaking Practice Questions

**Part 1 (Interview)**
- Do you enjoy watching international sports tournaments like the World Cup?
- Which team are you rooting for this year, and why?
- Do you prefer watching matches at home or at a pub with friends?

**Part 2 (Cue Card)**
Describe a sports event you watched that was very exciting.
You should say:
- What the event was
- Who you watched it with
- What happened during the event
- And explain why it was so exciting.

**Part 3 (Analytical Discussion)**
- Why do you think global sports events like the World Cup are so popular worldwide?
- Do you think professional athletes are paid too much money?
