# Day 5: IELTS Speaking & Voice Simulation (Career Jump)

## 1. IELTS Speaking Practice Questions

**Part 1 (Interview)**
1. What kind of work do you do?
- I work as a data engineer at an integrated resort company.
- Currently, I am a data engineer working for a large integrated resort company based in Central Vietnam.
- My main responsibility is to maintain and optimize the company's data pipelines.
2. Do you have any plans to change your role or career in the future?
- Actually, I'm planning a career shift from a data engineer to a data architect.
- Yeah, I have plans to change my role. Because I want to be a data architect in the future.
- In the near future, I plan to take on more responsibilities and gain more experience to become a data architect.

3. What skills are most important for your current job?
- I think technical skills like Python, SQL, and cloud platforms are essential for my current role as a data engineer.
- In my current role as a data engineer, programming skills, especially Python and SQL, are really important. But to become a data architect, I also need to improve my system design and cloud architecture skills.
- For me, the most important skills for my current job as a data engineer are strong programming skills, especially in Python and SQL. On top of that, knowledge of cloud platforms like AWS or Azure is also essential for managing data pipelines effectively. However, to transition into a data architect role, I would need to develop skills in system design and cloud architecture as well.
**Part 2 (Cue Card)**
Describe a goal you have for your career in the next five years.
You should say:
- What the goal is
- Why you want to achieve it
- What you need to do to achieve it
- And explain how you will feel when you achieve it.
**My answer**:
- The main goal I want to achieve in the next five years is to make the jump to Data Architect. 
- The primary reason I want to achieve this goal is because I'm really passionate about designing big picture systems and I want to take on more responsibilities. 
- To achieve this goal, I need to study system design and cloud architecture, and gain more experience in designing and implementing data pipelines.
- When I achieve this goal, I will feel proud of myself and I will be able to contribute more to my company.
**Transcript**: 
My main goal right now is to make the jump from data engineer to data architect. The primary reason I want to achieve this goal is because I'm really passionate about designing big picture systems and I want to take on more responsibilities. To achieve this goal, I need to study system design and cloud architecture, and gain more experience in designing and implementing data pipelines. When I achieve this goal, I will feel proud of myself and I will be able to contribute more to my company.
**Part 3 (Analytical Discussion)**
1. Do you think it's better to specialize in one skill or have a broad range of skills?
**Answer**: 
- It really depends on the field. For example, in some fields like medicine or law, deep specialization is often required. However, in the tech industry, a broad range of skills is often more beneficial.
- Personally, I believe that having a broad range of skills is more important, especially in today's fast-paced world.
2. How has technology changed the way people plan their careers?
**Answer**: 
- Technology has made it much easier for people to access information and resources related to their careers. For example, online learning platforms and professional networking sites allow people to research different industries and roles.
- It has also made it easier for people to connect with others in their field and to find job opportunities. Some platforms like Linkedin help people find jobs and connect with others in their field. 
- It has also made it easier for people to work remotely, which allows them to have more flexibility in their work-life balance.
