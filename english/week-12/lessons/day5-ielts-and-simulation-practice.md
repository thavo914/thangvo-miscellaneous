# Day 5: IELTS Speaking & Voice Simulation (Career Jump)

## 1. IELTS Speaking Practice Questions

**Part 1 (Interview)**
- What kind of work do you do?
- Do you have any plans to change your role or career in the future?
- What skills are most important for your current job?

**Part 2 (Cue Card)**
Describe a goal you have for your career in the next five years.
You should say:
- What the goal is
- Why you want to achieve it
- What you need to do to achieve it
- And explain how you will feel when you achieve it.

**Part 3 (Analytical Discussion)**
- Do you think it's better to specialize in one skill or have a broad range of skills?
- How has technology changed the way people plan their careers?
