# Day 6: Creative Writing & Analysis (Career Jump)

## 1. Creative Dialogue Writing
**Instructions**: Write a new, original 10-turn conversation between you and your manager discussing your performance review and future goals.
**Requirement**: You must naturally include at least 2 of the target grammar rules (Future Perfect, Comparatives with much/a lot, Gerunds after prepositions) and 3 of the target collocations (*make the jump, big picture, bridge the gap, level up, take the lead on*).

## 2. Band 6.5-7.0 Cue Card Sample Answer

**Prompt**: Describe a goal you have for your career in the next five years.

**Sample Answer**:
"A major goal I have for my career over the next five years is to **make the jump** from being a Data Engineer to becoming a Data Architect. Right now, I spend most of my time building individual data pipelines, but I am much more interested in designing the **big picture** architecture for the entire company. 

I want to achieve this because it feels like a natural progression for me, and I enjoy solving complex, high-level system problems rather than just writing code. In order to achieve this, I need to **bridge the gap** in my knowledge regarding cloud security and large-scale distributed systems. I'm currently taking a few courses to **level up** my skills. By the time I turn 30, I will have completed my AWS Solution Architect certification, which I think will help a lot. 

Once I reach this goal, I will feel incredibly proud and fulfilled, especially when I finally get to **take the lead on** designing a massive data platform from scratch."

### Conversational Words Highlighted:
- **make the jump**: transition to a higher or different role (Vietnamese: bước nhảy vọt)
- **big picture**: the overall perspective or broad view (Vietnamese: bức tranh toàn cảnh)
- **bridge the gap**: connect two things or fill a missing piece of knowledge (Vietnamese: thu hẹp khoảng cách)
- **level up**: improve skills or advance (Vietnamese: nâng cao kỹ năng)
- **take the lead on**: assume the primary responsibility for something (Vietnamese: dẫn dắt / chịu trách nhiệm chính)
