# Day 4: Grammar and Structures (Career Jump)

## 1. Grammar Focus (3 Target Rules)

### 1. Future Perfect for Career Milestones
- **Quote**: "By the time the next review cycle comes around, I will have finished my AWS certification."
- **Rule Explanation**: Use the Future Perfect (`will have + Past Participle`) to talk about an action that will be completed *before* a specific point in the future. Often used with "By the time..." or "By next year..."
- **Common Pitfall Box**: 
  > **Incorrect**: By next year, I will finish my certification.
  > **Correct**: By next year, I will have finished my certification.
**Your Turn**:
- By the time I turn 35, I will have become a Senior Architect.
- By the time you get home, I will have cooked dinner.
- By the end of Q4, we will have migrated all the databases.
### 2. Comparatives with 'much/a lot'
- **Quote**: "I'm much more interested in designing the big picture systems..."
- **Rule Explanation**: To emphasize a comparative adjective, use *much*, *a lot*, or *far* before the comparative form (e.g., *much more interested*, *a lot harder*, *far better*).
- **Common Pitfall Box**: 
  > **Incorrect**: The architect role is very harder than the engineer role.
  > **Correct**: The architect role is much harder than the engineer role.
**Your turn**:
- The architect role is much harder than the engineer role.
- I'm much more interested in designing the big picture systems than writing SQL all day.
- I'm much more interested in playing sports than watching them on TV.
### 3. Gerunds after Prepositions
- **Quote**: "It's not just about technical skills, but also about communication."
- **Rule Explanation**: If a verb follows a preposition (like *about, in, for, of, at*), the verb must take the -ing form (gerund). Example: "I am interested *in* learn*ing*."
- **Common Pitfall Box**: 
  > **Incorrect**: I want to take the lead on design the new platform.
  > **Correct**: I want to take the lead on designing the new platform.
**Your Turn**:
- I'm interested in learning Python. I want to take the lead on designing the new platform.
- I'm interested in playing sports. I want to take the lead on playing sports.
- I'm interested in designing the new platform. I want to take the lead on designing the new platform.

## 2. Sentence Structure Templates (5 Patterns)

### 1. Stating Career Objectives
- **Communicative Function**: To clearly state what you want to achieve next in your career.
- **Slot-Filling Formula**: `My main goal right now is to + [Verb Phrase].`
- **Examples**:
  1. *Data Engineering Context*: My main goal right now is to make the jump to Data Architect.
  2. *Everyday Context*: My main goal right now is to save enough money for a house.
  3. *General Workplace Context*: My main goal right now is to improve my presentation skills.

### 2. Explaining Steps to a Goal
- **Communicative Function**: To explain the necessary actions required to achieve an objective.
- **Slot-Filling Formula**: `In order to + [Verb Phrase], I need to + [Verb Phrase].`
- **Examples**:
  1. *Data Engineering Context*: In order to level up, I need to understand cloud security better.
  2. *Everyday Context*: In order to run the marathon, I need to train five days a week.
  3. *General Workplace Context*: In order to get promoted, I need to take on more leadership tasks.

### 3. Comparing Interests
- **Communicative Function**: To state a strong preference for one type of work over another.
- **Slot-Filling Formula**: `I'm much more interested in + [Noun/Verb-ing] + than + [Noun/Verb-ing].`
- **Examples**:
  1. *Data Engineering Context*: I'm much more interested in designing architecture than writing SQL all day.
  2. *Everyday Context*: I'm much more interested in playing sports than watching them on TV.
  3. *General Workplace Context*: I'm much more interested in strategy than daily operations.

### 4. Setting Deadlines for Goals (Future Perfect)
- **Communicative Function**: To commit to completing something by a future date.
- **Slot-Filling Formula**: `By the time + [Clause/Noun], I will have + [Past Participle].`
- **Examples**:
  1. *Data Engineering Context*: By the time I turn 30, I will have become a Senior Architect.
  2. *Everyday Context*: By the time you get home, I will have cooked dinner.
  3. *General Workplace Context*: By the end of Q4, we will have migrated all the databases.

### 5. Contrasting Two Important Aspects
- **Communicative Function**: To emphasize that two different things are both necessary.
- **Slot-Filling Formula**: `It's not just about + [Noun/Verb-ing], but also about + [Noun/Verb-ing].`
- **Examples**:
  1. *Data Engineering Context*: It's not just about coding fast, but also about writing maintainable code.
  2. *Everyday Context*: It's not just about eating healthy, but also about getting enough sleep.
  3. *General Workplace Context*: It's not just about the salary, but also about the company culture.

## 3. Production & Translation Drills

### "Your Turn" Exercises
- My main goal right now is to ______________________.
My main goal right now is to get a promotion.
- In order to level up, I need to ______________________.
In order to level up, I need to understand cloud security better.
- I'm much more interested in ______________________ than ______________________.
I'm much more interested in designing the big picture systems than writing SQL all day.
- By the time next year comes, I will have ______________________.
By the time next year comes, I will have finished my certification.
- Being a good leader is not just about ______________________, but also about ______________________.
Being a good leader is not just about delegating tasks, but also about mentoring the team.

### Translation / Transformation Challenge
1. Tôi muốn thực hiện bước nhảy vọt (make the jump) để trở thành Data Architect vào năm tới.
   *(Translate into English)*
- I want to make the jump to Data Architect next year.
2. Vào thời điểm dự án kết thúc, tôi sẽ hoàn thành (Future Perfect) việc thiết kế hệ thống.
   *(Translate into English)*
- By the time the project finishes, I will have completed the system design.
3. Công việc này không chỉ về lập trình, mà còn về việc nhìn nhận bức tranh toàn cảnh (big picture).
   *(Translate into English using the target template)*
- This job is not just about programming, but also about looking at the big picture.
