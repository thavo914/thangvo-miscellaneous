# Job Interview Introduction Script

## Original Text
"I'm a Data Engineer with eight years of experience. Right now, I'm the solo engineer at a casino company, building their data warehouse from scratch using Python, Airflow, and dbt. I handle everything from architecture to real-time streaming. I love solving complex problems, which is why I'm interested in this position."

## Sentence Expansion (More Detail)
If the interviewer asks why you are leaving or what you are looking for:

"Although I enjoy the autonomy of being a solo engineer, I am looking to join a larger team where I can share knowledge and tackle even bigger data challenges."
