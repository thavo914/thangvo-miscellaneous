## 🔑 5 Target Vocabulary Words

| Word           | Part of Speech | Meaning                                             | From the episode                                                                        |
| -------------- | -------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **initially**  | adverb         | at first; in the beginning                          | *"Initially I felt the main thing an internet giant had to do was just to be neutral."* |
| **neutral**    | adjective      | not taking sides; not controlling                   | *"Just be a platform… just be neutral."*                                                |
| **anonymity**  | noun           | the state of keeping your identity hidden           | *"...when it comes to social networks, people have anonymity."*                         |
| **ameliorate** | verb           | to make something better; to improve                | *"...the issue could be hugely ameliorated by tweaking the way the thing works."*       |
| **tweak**      | verb           | to make a small change to how something works       | *"By tweaking the way in which people give or receive kudos."*                          |
| **kudos**      | noun           | praise and appreciation for something you have done | *"Give them more kudos for being constructive."*                                        |

> **💡 Bonus word**: *propagated* — spread or transmitted (used about how retweets spread online)

## 📅 6-Day Protocol (Applying the Six Techniques)

Each day applies a different technique from the Six Techniques framework to the **same** 6-minute audio. This deep multi-angle processing is how vocabulary and pronunciation get locked into long-term memory.

| Day             | Technique                           | Core Activity                              |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| **Day 1 — Mon** | Passive & Active Listening          | Immersion + First Decode                   |
| **Day 2 — Tue** | Dictation                           | Transcription + Connected Speech Analysis  |
| **Day 3 — Wed** | Shadowing                           | Articulatory Sync with BBC Hosts           |
| **Day 4 — Thu** | AI Simulation                       | Debate & Discussion with ChatGPT           |
| **Day 5 — Fri** | Cognitive Restructuring + Monologue | Think & Speak in English                   |
| **Day 6 — Sat** | Grammar & Sentence Structures       | Extract + reuse 7 patterns from transcript |

## 💡 Ground Rules for this Week

1. **One artifact, many angles** — every technique is applied to this same episode. Do not jump to new content before exhausting this one.
2. **Record your output** — voice memos on your phone count. You are your own evaluator.
3. **Resist translation** — when you don't know a word in the exercise, describe it in English first, then look it up.
4. **Quantity beats quality** — 3 imperfect recordings are better than 1 over-rehearsed one.
