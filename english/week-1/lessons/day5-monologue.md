# 🧠 Day 5 — Cognitive Restructuring + Monologue

**Techniques**: Thinking in English + Autonomous Monologue (Self-Talk)  
**Episode**: BBC 6 Minute English — "Is the Internet Good or Bad?"  
**Total time**: ~60 minutes  
**Equipment**: Phone camera / voice recorder (mandatory for recording)

---

## The Goal of Today

You have listened, decoded, shadowed, and debated. Now you must **own** the content — not as material you studied, but as **ideas you are capable of expressing independently** in English.

Today there is no script. No transcript. No AI to correct you in real time.  
Just you, your thoughts, and the English language.

---

## Phase 1: Cognitive Restructuring Warm-Up (15 min)

### Exercise A — Lexical Labeling (5 min)

Look around the room you are in right now. Mentally label **everything you see** in English, out loud in a whisper:

*"laptop, desk, coffee cup, window, traffic outside, clouds, the light — it's too dim..."*

If you don't know a word, do NOT switch to Vietnamese. Instead, **describe it in English**:  
*"That thing you use to keep papers together"* = stapler  
*"The thing that controls the temperature of the room"* = thermostat / AC remote

Do this for 5 minutes continuously.

---

### Exercise B — Syntactic Narration (10 min)

Now narrate your actions and observations in full English sentences — aloud — as if you are a journalist describing what you see:

*"I am sitting at my desk. The coffee is getting cold — I should drink it. Outside the window, the street looks busy this morning. I can see someone on their phone — they are probably scrolling social media, which is exactly what Tim Berners-Lee was worried about..."*

Connect what you observe around you to the **topic of this week's episode** — the internet, social media, anonymity.  
Keep your internal monologue in English for all 10 minutes. Forward momentum only — no stopping to perfect grammar.

---

## Phase 2: The Recorded Monologue (30 min)

### 🎤 Topic: "The Internet — Is It Good or Bad? My Personal View"

**Set up your phone or recording device NOW** (before you start thinking about what to say).

You will record yourself speaking for **2 minutes without stopping**.

---

### Monologue Structure (Build this mentally — do not write it out)

Use this structure to guide yourself:

| Section                      | Time | What to say                                                            |
| ---------------------------- | ---- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Opening**                  | 15s  | State your position clearly — are you more positive or negative?       |
| **First point**              | 30s  | One reason the internet has been good for the world (concrete example) |
| **Counterpoint**             | 30s  | One way it has caused harm — use ANONYMITY                             |
| **Tim Berners-Lee's idea**   | 20s  | Explain his "tweak the kudos" suggestion in your own words             |
| **Your opinion on his idea** | 20s  | Do you think tweaking kudos would work? Why / why not?                 |
| **Closing**                  | 5s   | One final sentence to wrap up                                          |

---

### 🎙️ Recording Instructions

**Attempt 1** — First take. Speak for 2 minutes. Do not stop. Do not restart.  
If you lose the thread, say: *"What I mean is..."* or *"Let me put it differently..."*  
These are **natural recovery phrases** — use them instead of going silent.

**Listen back** — Do not cringe. Listen analytically:
- Did you use any of the 6 target words?
- Did you pause too long between sentences?
- Did you fall back into Vietnamese structure? (e.g., putting verbs in the wrong place)
- Was there a point where you clearly got stuck?

**Attempt 2** — Record again. Try to fix the one biggest problem you noticed.

**Attempt 3** — Optional, but record a third time if you want to feel the improvement.

## Phase 3: Self-Evaluation (15 min)

Listen to your 2 best recordings side by side. Fill in:

### Target Vocabulary Audit
Check the box each time you used a target word correctly:

- [ ] **ameliorate** — used? (This is the hard one — don't skip it)
