# 🎧 Day 1 — Passive & Active Listening

**Technique**: Passive Listening → Active Listening  
**Episode**: BBC 6 Minute English — "Is the Internet Good or Bad?"  
**Total time**: ~60 minutes  
**Transcript**: Do NOT open [lesson-transcript.md](./lesson-transcript.md) today — ears first!

---

## Phase 1: Passive Exposure (During commute or morning routine — 20 min)

**Your only job**: Press play. Let it wash over you.

**Play the episode 2–3 times** while doing something else (commuting, coffee, getting ready).

What to notice *without trying*:
- How many different voices can you hear? (Rob, Neil, Tim Berners-Lee)
- What is the general emotion — serious? light-hearted? concerned?
- Which words seem to be repeated a lot?
- Does it sound fast or slow compared to normal movies/shows?

> **Do not pause. Do not look words up. Do not take notes.**  
> This is auditory priming — you are teaching your brain what British broadcast English *feels like*.

---

## Phase 2: Active Listening — First Decode (30 min)

Now sit down and give the episode your full attention. **No transcript.**

### 🔊 Listen 1 — Global Comprehension
Play the full episode. After it ends, write down (in English):

1. **Topic**: What was this episode about in one sentence?
2. **Main speakers**: Who were they?
3. **The question**: What question did Rob ask at the start about Berners-Lee?
4. **Tim Berners-Lee's concern**: What was his main worry about the internet?

*Don't worry if you missed things — this is a diagnostic, not a test.*

---

### 🔊 Listen 2 — Targeted Comprehension
Play the episode again. This time, try to catch:

**Part A – The quiz**  
- What year options were given? (a / b / c)
- What was Neil's guess?
- What was the correct answer?

**Part B – Key ideas**  
- What does Berners-Lee mean by the internet giants being "neutral"?
- What does he say happens when people have anonymity on social networks?
- What are "retweets" and "kudos" used to illustrate?

**Part C – Vocabulary hunt**  
Note down any unfamiliar words you heard — even if you can only write them phonetically (how they sounded). Try to catch at least 3.

---

### 🔊 Listen 3 — Focus on Tim Berners-Lee's clip
Find the section where Tim Berners-Lee speaks directly (not Rob or Neil paraphrasing him). Listen **only** to his voice.

- Is his speech faster or slower than Rob and Neil?
- What is his tone — hopeful, optimistic, disappointed, angry?
- Write down **one full sentence** you think you heard him say.

---

## Phase 3: Comprehension Check + Vocabulary (10 min)

After your 3 listens, answer these questions from memory in English:

1. What does the word **"initially"** mean? (Rob and Neil defined it in the episode)
2. What does **"anonymity"** allow people to do?
3. What is **"kudos"** according to Berners-Lee?
4. In your own words, what does it mean to **"tweak"** something?

Write 2–3 sentences answering: *"Do you think the internet has been good or bad overall? Why?"*  
*(Write it in English, as freely as you can — accuracy doesn't matter today.)*

## 💡 Self-Coaching Notes

After finishing, ask yourself:
- What percentage of the episode did you understand? (rough estimate %)
- Which part was hardest to follow?
- Which speaker was easiest to understand: Rob, Neil, or Tim Berners-Lee?

*Keep these notes — you will compare them at the end of the week.*
