# 📅 Week 1: Professional Identity & Casino Introduction

**Dates**: Feb 09-15, 2026  
**Month 1**: Communication & Business Foundation  
**Status**: [CURRENT]

## 🎯 Week Overview

This week launches your English journey by establishing your professional identity as a Data Engineer in a casino/hospitality environment. You'll master how to introduce yourself clearly and confidently.

**Key Goal**: Perfect a natural, engaging 1-minute self-introduction

## 🗓️ Daily Breakdown

### Monday: Input & Shadowing (Pronunciation & Intonation Focus)

**Goal**: Master the sound and rhythm of professional self-introductions

#### 0-30 minutes: Input
- Watch 3 YouTube videos on "Self-introduction for Data Engineers" or "Professional interview introduction"
- Choose ONE video with a voice/accent you like
- Examples to search:
  - "Data Engineer self introduction"
  - "Introduce yourself professionally in English"
  - "Job interview introduction examples"

#### 30-90 minutes: Intensive Shadowing
- Transcribe the introduction (about 1 minute of speech)
- Mark linking sounds (word connections: "an engineer" → "an_engineer")
- Mark stress patterns and intonation (↗ rising, ↘ falling)
- **Shadowing technique** (3 levels):
  1. **Read along** while watching (5-10 times)
  2. **Repeat without text**, sentence by sentence (10-15 times)
  3. **Shadow simultaneously** at same speed (10+ times until natural)

#### 90-120 minutes: Recording & Self-Correction
- Create YOUR version (real name, real job, real company/casino)
- Record yourself 3-5 times
- Compare with original
- Note differences in pronunciation and rhythm
- Re-record until satisfied

**Deliverable**: One polished 1-minute self-introduction recording

---

### Tuesday: Vocabulary & Sentence Mining

**Goal**: Build a foundation of professional Data Engineering vocabulary in casino context

#### 0-45 minutes: Reading & Mining
- Find 2 job descriptions for "Senior Data Engineer" at casino companies
  - Search: "MGM Data Engineer job"
  - Search: "Las Vegas Sands Data Engineer careers"
- Highlight 10 valuable phrases, for example:
  - "orchestrate data pipelines"
  - "ensure data integrity"
  - "optimize latency"
  - "real-time analytics"
  - "ETL workflows"

#### 45-90 minutes: Active Usage (Sentence Creation)
- For each of the 10 phrases, create 2 example sentences:
  - **Sentence 1**: General context
  - **Sentence 2**: Specific to your work (PMS, slot machines, casino data)
- Example:
  - General: "I orchestrate data pipelines to move information efficiently."
  - Specific: "I orchestrate data pipelines from slot machines to our analytics warehouse."

#### 90-120 minutes: Anki Flashcard Creation
- Create 10 Anki cards (one per key phrase)
- **Front**: Vietnamese hint or English context prompt
  - "Giải thích vai trò xử lý dữ liệu (pipelines)"
- **Back**: Full English sentence from your examples
  - "I orchestrate data pipelines from slot machines to our analytics warehouse."
- Review cards out loud (speak each sentence 3 times)

**Deliverable**: 10 new Anki cards with contextual sentences

### Wednesday: Role-play & AI Simulation

**Goal**: Practice spontaneous professional conversation under realistic conditions

#### 0-15 minutes: Setup ChatGPT Voice Mode
Use this prompt:
```
Act as a friendly but busy Casino Floor Manager named Mike. 
I am a new Data Engineer who just started this week.
You meet me at the coffee machine during morning break.
Ask me casual questions about what I do and how my data work can help the casino floor.
Speak naturally, use some casino jargon, and gently challenge me if my explanations are too technical.
Keep the conversation around 10 minutes.
```

#### 15-75 minutes: Role-play Loop (3 rounds)
**Round 1** (10 min conversation):
- Have the conversation naturally
- Try to use yesterday's vocabulary

**Get Feedback**:
Ask ChatGPT: "What grammar mistakes did I make? How can I sound more professional and natural?"

**Round 2** (10 min conversation):
- Apply the feedback
- Try different responses

**Get Feedback** again

**Round 3** (10 min conversation):
- Best effort - natural and confident
- Use new vocabulary phrases

#### 75-120 minutes: Refinement
- Write down the "best version" conversation script (what you *should* have said)
- Practice this script standing in front of a mirror 5 times
- Focus on body language and eye contact
- Record final version (audio or video)

**Deliverable**: Improved role-play recording showing natural conversation

### Thursday: Deep Listening & Transcription

**Goal**: Train your ear to catch every word, including connected speech and reduced sounds

#### 0-60 minutes: Deep Listening
- Choose ONE 3-minute audio segment from:
  - "Slick Talk: The Hospitality Podcast"
  - YouTube: "How Slot Machines Work"
  - Any casino/hospitality themed content

- **Dictation exercise**:
  - Listen and type EVERY word you hear
  - Pause/replay maximum 3 times per sentence
  - Mark words you couldn't catch with [???]

#### 60-90 minutes: Correction & Analysis
- Find the official transcript or use YouTube auto-captions
- Compare with your dictation
- Circle words/phrases you missed or heard incorrectly
- Analyze WHY you missed them:
  - New vocabulary?
  - Connected speech? ("want to" → "wanna")
  - Speed too fast?
  - Unclear pronunciation?

#### 90-120 minutes: Summary
- Listen to the segment again (now that you have the correct transcript)
- Record yourself summarizing the content in 2 minutes
- Focus: What was the main point? What did you learn?

**Deliverable**: Transcription comparison document + 2-minute summary recording

---

### Friday: Paraphrasing & Flexible Thinking

**Goal**: Express the same idea in multiple ways to avoid getting stuck on one word

#### 0-30 minutes: Input
Choose 5 technical sentences you commonly use (or from your work):
- Example: "The ETL process failed due to a schema mismatch in the source database."

#### 30-90 minutes: 3-Level Paraphrasing Drill
For each sentence, rephrase it at 3 levels:

**Level 1 - For Non-Technical Person** (grandma test):
- "We can't move the data because the format changed."

**Level 2 - For Business Manager**:
- "There's a delay in the report because of a system update."

**Level 3 - For Technical Colleague**:
- "Ingestion broke because of an upstream schema change."

Practice saying all 3 versions out loud multiple times

#### 90-120 minutes: Speed Thinking Game
- Use a random word generator online
- When a word appears, immediately (5 seconds max) create a sentence related to Data/Casino work containing that word
- Do this for 20-30 words
- Record the session

**Deliverable**: 5 technical concepts paraphrased 3 ways + speed thinking recording

---

### Saturday: Mini-Project & Synthesis

**Goal**: Combine all week's learning into a polished deliverable

#### 0-30 minutes: Preparation
Create an outline for a 3-minute presentation:
**Topic**: "My Role as a Data Engineer in this Casino"

Suggested structure:
1. Introduction (who you are)
2. What you do (main responsibilities)
3. How it helps the casino (business value)
4. Current projects or interests
5. Closing

#### 30-90 minutes: Rehearsal (Progressive practice)
- **Attempt 1**: Speaking while reading outline
- **Attempt 2**: Speaking without outline, record it
- **Attempt 3**: Listen to recording, identify issues
- **Attempt 4-5**: Re-record until smooth and natural

#### 90-120 minutes: Final Delivery & Simulation
- Imagine you're in a Monday morning team standup meeting
- Record your final 3-minute introduction in a "meeting simulation"
  - Greet the team
  - Give your introduction
  - Thank them

**Save this recording** - you'll compare it to Week 24!

**Deliverable**: Polished 3-minute "My Role as Data Engineer" presentation recording
