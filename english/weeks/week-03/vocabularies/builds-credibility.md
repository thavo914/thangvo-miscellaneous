# Builds credibility

## IPA
/bɪldz ˌkrɛdəˈbɪləti/

## Definition
To develop trust and confidence in your abilities, knowledge, or reliability through consistent actions and honest communication over time.

## Part of Speech
Verb phrase

## Usage & Examples
- Being transparent about challenges **builds credibility** with stakeholders and team members.
- Delivering on promises consistently **builds credibility** and trust in your professional relationships.
- Admitting mistakes and learning from them **builds credibility** more than trying to hide them.
- **Building credibility** takes time, but it's essential for long-term career success.
- When you **build credibility**, people are more likely to trust your judgment and recommendations.

## Synonyms & Related Terms
- **Earn trust**: to gain confidence through reliable behavior
- **Establish reputation**: to create a positive image over time
- **Gain confidence**: to make others believe in your abilities
- **Prove reliability**: to demonstrate consistent performance
- **Develop trustworthiness**: to become someone others can depend on
