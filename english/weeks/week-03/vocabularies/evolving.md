# Evolving

## IPA
/ɪˈvɑlvɪŋ/

## Definition
Changing and developing over time; gradually becoming more advanced or complex.

## Part of Speech
Verb (present participle)

## Usage & Examples
- The field keeps **evolving** with new tools and approaches.
- Our data architecture is constantly **evolving** as we learn from experience.
- Technology is **evolving** so fast that it's hard to keep up sometimes.
- The team's skills are **evolving** as we work on more complex projects.
- We need to stay flexible because requirements are always **evolving**.

## Synonyms & Related Terms
- **Developing**: growing or becoming more advanced
- **Changing**: becoming different over time
- **Progressing**: moving forward or improving
- **Advancing**: moving to a higher level
- **Growing**: increasing in size, skill, or complexity
