# Edge cases

## IPA
/ɛdʒ keɪsɪz/

## Definition
Unusual or unexpected situations that don't follow normal patterns and can cause problems or failures in systems.

## Part of Speech
Noun phrase (plural)

## Usage & Examples
- Some **edge cases** only show up when data volumes spike or when we get unexpected data formats.
- We need to test for **edge cases** to make sure our system handles unusual situations.
- The bug was caused by an **edge case** we hadn't considered during development.
- **Edge cases** are often the hardest problems to solve because they're unexpected.
- Good testing should cover both normal scenarios and **edge cases**.

## Synonyms & Related Terms
- **Corner cases**: unusual situations that are difficult to handle
- **Unusual scenarios**: situations that don't happen often
- **Exceptional cases**: cases that are different from the normal pattern
- **Boundary conditions**: situations at the limits of what a system can handle
- **Rare situations**: circumstances that don't occur frequently
