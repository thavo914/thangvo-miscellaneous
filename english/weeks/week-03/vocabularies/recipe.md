# Recipe

## IPA
/ˈrɛsəpi/

## Definition
A set of instructions for making a particular dish or food item, including a list of ingredients and the steps to follow.

## Part of Speech
Noun

## Usage & Examples
- I started with basic **recipes** and gradually tried more complex dishes.
- This **recipe** calls for 20 ingredients and takes 3 hours to make.
- I found a great **recipe** for homemade pizza on YouTube.
- Always read the **recipe** carefully before you start cooking.
- I learned this **recipe** from my mom, who learned it from her grandmother.

## Synonyms & Related Terms
- **Instructions**: step-by-step directions for doing something
- **Method**: a way of doing something
- **Formula**: a set of steps to achieve a result
- **Directions**: guidance on how to do something
- **Guide**: something that shows you how to do something
