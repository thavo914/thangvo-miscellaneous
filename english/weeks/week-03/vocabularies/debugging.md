# Debugging

## IPA
/diˈbʌɡɪŋ/

## Definition
The process of finding and fixing problems or errors in code, data, or systems.

## Part of Speech
Noun (gerund)

## Usage & Examples
- **Debugging** data quality issues can be really challenging and time-consuming.
- I spent most of yesterday **debugging** why the pipeline was running slowly.
- Good monitoring tools make **debugging** much easier and faster.
- **Debugging** is like detective work - you follow the clues to find the problem.
- The team is still **debugging** the issue with missing data in the reports.

## Synonyms & Related Terms
- **Troubleshooting**: finding and solving problems systematically
- **Problem-solving**: finding solutions to difficult situations
- **Investigation**: examining something to find the truth
- **Diagnosis**: identifying the cause of a problem
- **Fix**: to repair or correct something that's broken
