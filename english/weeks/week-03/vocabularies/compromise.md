# Compromise

## IPA
/ˈkɑmprəˌmaɪz/

## Definition
An agreement or settlement of a dispute that is reached by each side making concessions or giving up some of their demands.

## Part of Speech
Noun and verb

## Usage & Examples
- We reached a **compromise** where the business team gets weekly updates instead of daily ones.
- Both teams had to **compromise** to find a solution that worked for everyone.
- The **compromise** involved extending the deadline by one week but reducing the scope.
- Sometimes you need to **compromise** on quality to meet the deadline.
- Finding a good **compromise** requires understanding both sides' priorities.

## Synonyms & Related Terms
- **Concession**: something given up in a negotiation
- **Middle ground**: a position between two extremes
- **Give and take**: mutual concessions in a relationship
- **Balance**: finding the right proportion between different needs
- **Trade-off**: exchanging one thing for another
