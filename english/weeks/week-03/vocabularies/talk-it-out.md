# Talk it out

## IPA
/tɔk ɪt aʊt/

## Definition
To discuss a problem or disagreement openly and honestly in order to resolve it or reach an understanding.

## Part of Speech
Phrasal verb

## Usage & Examples
- When team members disagree, it's better to **talk it out** than to let problems build up.
- We had different opinions about the project timeline, so we sat down to **talk it out**.
- Instead of avoiding the issue, let's **talk it out** and find a solution together.
- The manager encouraged us to **talk it out** rather than sending angry emails.

## Synonyms & Related Terms
- **Discuss**: to talk about something in detail
- **Work through**: to solve a problem step by step
- **Hash out**: to discuss thoroughly until agreement is reached
- **Sort out**: to resolve a problem or misunderstanding
- **Clear the air**: to remove tension or misunderstanding through discussion
