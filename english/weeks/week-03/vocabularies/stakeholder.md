# Stakeholder

## IPA
/ˈsteɪkˌhoʊldər/

## Definition
A person, group, or organization that has an interest or concern in a project, business, or organization.

## Part of Speech
Noun

## Usage & Examples
- **Primary stakeholders** include customers, employees, and shareholders.
- The **key stakeholders** for this data migration project are the business users and IT team.
- We need to **engage stakeholders** early to understand their requirements.
- The project manager will **update stakeholders** on progress weekly.

## Synonyms & Related Terms
- **Investor**: someone who puts money into a project
- **Partner**: someone who works with you on a project
- **Client**: someone who uses your services
- **User**: someone who will use the final product
- **Sponsor**: someone who supports and funds a project
