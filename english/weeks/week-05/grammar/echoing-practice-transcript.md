## Section 3: Complex Sentences (3 minutes)

### Technical Explanations
- Dynamic task mapping allows us to scale based on data volume, which reduces DAG complexity significantly.
- Deferrable operators free up worker slots while waiting for external conditions, improving overall resource utilization.
- Cascading failures across DAGs create a domino effect that's hard to debug and affects multiple downstream systems.

### Problem-Solution Statements
- We had frequent task failures, so we implemented comprehensive retry logic, which resulted in 90% fewer failures.
- The DAG was too complex, so we broke it into smaller task groups, which improved maintainability and debugging.

### Trade-off Discussions
- While dynamic task mapping improves scalability, it comes with the trade-off of increased complexity.
- Deferrable operators optimize resource usage, however they require careful state management.

## Tips for Effective Echoing Practice

1. **Start Slow**: Begin with single words, then progress to phrases, then sentences
2. **Focus on Stress**: Pay attention to syllable stress in technical terms
3. **Practice Linking**: Notice how words connect in natural speech
4. **Record Yourself**: Compare your pronunciation with the original
5. **Be Consistent**: Practice daily for best results
6. **Use a Mirror**: Watch your mouth movements for clear articulation
7. **Vary Your Practice**: Mix vocabulary, sentences, and intonation patterns
8. **Stay Relaxed**: Tension affects pronunciation and fluency

Remember: The goal is not perfection, but improvement. Focus on clear communication and natural rhythm rather than perfect accent imitation.
