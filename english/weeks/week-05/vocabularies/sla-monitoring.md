# SLA Monitoring

## 1. IPA
/ɛs ɛl eɪ ˈmɒnɪtərɪŋ/ (S-L-A MON-i-ter-ing)

## 2. Definition
SLA monitoring is the process of tracking and alerting on Service Level Agreement violations, ensuring that data pipelines meet their performance, availability, and quality commitments within specified timeframes.

## 3. Part of Speech
**Noun** (uncountable)
- **SLA monitoring**: The process of monitoring SLAs
- **SLA**: Service Level Agreement (countable)

## 4. Usage & Examples (5 examples)

1. "SLA monitoring is critical for maintaining data pipeline reliability."
2. "Our SLA monitoring system alerts us when tasks exceed their time limits."
3. "The SLA monitoring dashboard shows real-time compliance metrics."
4. "SLA monitoring alerts notify us of violations."
5. "SLA monitoring metrics show performance trends."

## 5. Synonyms & Related Terms

### Synonyms
- **Service level monitoring**: "Service level monitoring tracks compliance."
- **Performance monitoring**: "Performance monitoring ensures SLA compliance."
- **Compliance tracking**: "Compliance tracking monitors SLA adherence."

### Related Terms
- **SLA**: Service Level Agreement defining performance commitments
- **SLO**: Service Level Objective defining target performance
- **SLI**: Service Level Indicator measuring actual performance
- **Alerting**: System that notifies of SLA violations
- **Compliance**: Adherence to SLA requirements

### Technical Variations
- **SLA monitoring configuration**: Settings for SLA tracking
- **SLA monitoring reporting**: Generating compliance reports
- **SLA monitoring optimization**: Improving monitoring efficiency
