# Incident Response

## 1. IPA
/ˈɪnsɪdənt rɪˈspɑns/

## 2. Definition
The systematic process of identifying, analyzing, and resolving data quality incidents to minimize impact and restore normal operations quickly.

## 3. Part of Speech
Noun phrase

## 4. Usage & Examples
- "Our incident response team was activated within minutes of detecting the data quality issue."
- "We have a well-defined incident response process for handling data quality emergencies."
- "The incident response procedure helped us resolve the issue in under two hours."
- "Effective incident response requires clear communication and rapid decision-making."
- "We conduct regular incident response drills to ensure our team is prepared."

## 5. Synonyms & Related Terms
- **Emergency response**: Immediate action taken during critical incidents
- **Crisis management**: Coordinated response to significant data quality issues
- **Incident handling**: Process of managing and resolving incidents
- **Problem resolution**: Systematic approach to solving data quality problems
- **Incident management**: Overall framework for handling incidents
