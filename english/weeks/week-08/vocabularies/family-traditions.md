# Family Traditions

## 1. IPA
/ˈfæməli trəˈdɪʃənz/

## 2. Definition
Customary practices, rituals, or activities that are passed down through generations within a family, creating shared experiences and strengthening family bonds.

## 3. Part of Speech
Noun phrase

## 4. Usage & Examples
- "Our family traditions include Sunday morning breakfasts and annual holiday gatherings."
- "Family traditions help create lasting memories and strengthen family connections."
- "We've established new family traditions since the children were born."
- "Family traditions provide a sense of continuity and belonging across generations."
- "Sharing family traditions with new family members helps them feel included and valued."

## 5. Synonyms & Related Terms
- **Family rituals**: Regular practices or ceremonies within the family
- **Family customs**: Traditional behaviors and practices specific to a family
- **Family heritage**: Cultural and traditional practices passed down through generations
- **Family celebrations**: Special events and observances that bring family together
- **Family practices**: Regular activities and behaviors that define family culture
