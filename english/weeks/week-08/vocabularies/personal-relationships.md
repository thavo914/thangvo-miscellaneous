# Personal Relationships

## 1. IPA
/ˈpɜrsənəl rɪˈleɪʃənʃɪps/

## 2. Definition
Close, meaningful connections between individuals that involve emotional bonds, mutual support, and shared experiences, particularly within family and close personal circles.

## 3. Part of Speech
Noun phrase

## 4. Usage & Examples
- "Strong personal relationships are essential for emotional well-being and life satisfaction."
- "I've learned valuable lessons about communication through my personal relationships."
- "Personal relationships require effort, understanding, and mutual respect to thrive."
- "My personal relationships with family members have taught me the importance of empathy."
- "Building and maintaining personal relationships is a skill that improves with practice."

## 5. Synonyms & Related Terms
- **Close relationships**: Intimate connections with family and friends
- **Personal connections**: Emotional bonds with significant people in one's life
- **Family relationships**: Connections and bonds within the family unit
- **Interpersonal relationships**: Connections between individuals
- **Meaningful relationships**: Significant connections that provide emotional support
