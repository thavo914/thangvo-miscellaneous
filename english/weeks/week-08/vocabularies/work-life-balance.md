# Work-Life Balance

## 1. IPA
/wɜrk laɪf ˈbæləns/

## 2. Definition
The equilibrium between professional responsibilities and personal life, ensuring that work demands do not overwhelm personal relationships, health, and well-being.

## 3. Part of Speech
Noun phrase

## 4. Usage & Examples
- "Achieving work-life balance has become increasingly challenging in today's fast-paced work environment."
- "I've been thinking a lot about work-life balance lately and how to spend more quality time with my family."
- "Good work-life balance contributes to better job performance and personal satisfaction."
- "Many companies are implementing policies to support employees' work-life balance."
- "Work-life balance requires setting clear boundaries between work and personal time."

## 5. Synonyms & Related Terms
- **Life balance**: Overall equilibrium between different life areas
- **Work-life integration**: Blending work and personal life in a healthy way
- **Personal time**: Time dedicated to personal activities and relationships
- **Family time**: Quality time spent with family members
- **Life management**: Strategies for managing various life responsibilities
