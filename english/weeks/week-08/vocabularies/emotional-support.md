# Emotional Support

## 1. IPA
/ɪˈmoʊʃənəl səˈpɔrt/

## 2. Definition
The provision of comfort, encouragement, and understanding to help someone cope with emotional challenges, stress, or difficult situations, particularly within family relationships.

## 3. Part of Speech
Noun phrase

## 4. Usage & Examples
- "Family members provide crucial emotional support during difficult times."
- "I've learned to offer emotional support to my family members when they need it."
- "Emotional support from loved ones can make a significant difference in overcoming challenges."
- "We create a supportive environment where family members can share their feelings openly."
- "Emotional support involves listening, understanding, and providing comfort without judgment."

## 5. Synonyms & Related Terms
- **Family support**: Encouragement and assistance provided by family members
- **Emotional comfort**: Relief and solace provided during emotional distress
- **Personal support**: Individual assistance and encouragement from close relationships
- **Moral support**: Encouragement and confidence provided during difficult situations
- **Psychological support**: Mental and emotional assistance for coping with challenges
