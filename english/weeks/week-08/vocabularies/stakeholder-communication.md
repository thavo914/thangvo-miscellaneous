# Stakeholder Communication

## 1. IPA
/ˈsteɪkhoʊldər kəˌmjunəˈkeɪʃən/

## 2. Definition
The process of sharing information, updates, and insights about data quality with business users, decision-makers, and other relevant parties who depend on the data.

## 3. Part of Speech
Noun phrase

## 4. Usage & Examples
- "Effective stakeholder communication is crucial for maintaining trust in our data systems."
- "We provide regular stakeholder communication through weekly quality reports."
- "Stakeholder communication about data quality issues requires clear, non-technical language."
- "Our stakeholder communication strategy includes both proactive updates and reactive notifications."
- "Good stakeholder communication helps prevent misunderstandings and builds confidence in our data."

## 5. Synonyms & Related Terms
- **Business communication**: Sharing information with business stakeholders
- **User communication**: Communicating with data users and consumers
- **Quality reporting**: Providing regular updates on data quality status
- **Transparency**: Open communication about data quality issues and improvements
- **Status updates**: Regular communication about current data quality status
