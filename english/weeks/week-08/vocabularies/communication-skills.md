# Communication Skills

## 1. IPA
/kəˌmjunəˈkeɪʃən skɪlz/

## 2. Definition
The ability to effectively express ideas, thoughts, and feelings while also listening and understanding others, essential for building and maintaining relationships.

## 3. Part of Speech
Noun phrase

## 4. Usage & Examples
- "Strong communication skills are crucial for both professional success and personal relationships."
- "I've been working on improving my communication skills to better connect with my family."
- "Effective communication skills include active listening, clear expression, and empathy."
- "Good communication skills help resolve conflicts and build stronger relationships."
- "Communication skills can be developed through practice and self-awareness."

## 5. Synonyms & Related Terms
- **Interpersonal skills**: Abilities for interacting effectively with others
- **Relationship skills**: Competencies for building and maintaining relationships
- **Social skills**: Abilities for interacting successfully in social situations
- **Listening skills**: Abilities for understanding and responding to others
- **Expression skills**: Abilities for clearly communicating thoughts and feelings
