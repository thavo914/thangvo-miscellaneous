# Data Quality Monitoring

## 1. IPA
/ˈdeɪtə ˈkwɑləti ˈmɑnɪtərɪŋ/

## 2. Definition
The continuous process of observing, measuring, and evaluating data quality metrics to ensure data meets established standards and requirements.

## 3. Part of Speech
Noun phrase

## 4. Usage & Examples
- "We have been implementing data quality monitoring across all our critical pipelines."
- "Data quality monitoring has helped us identify issues before they impact business operations."
- "Our data quality monitoring system runs automated checks every hour."
- "The finance team relies on our data quality monitoring reports for decision-making."
- "Effective data quality monitoring requires both automated tools and human oversight."

## 5. Synonyms & Related Terms
- **Quality assurance**: Ensuring data meets standards through systematic processes
- **Data validation**: Checking data for accuracy, completeness, and consistency
- **Quality control**: Ongoing processes to maintain data quality standards
- **Data governance**: Overall framework for managing data quality and compliance
- **Monitoring system**: Technical infrastructure for continuous quality assessment
