# Quality Threshold

## 1. IPA
/ˈkwɑləti ˈθreʃhoʊld/

## 2. Definition
A predefined minimum standard or limit for data quality metrics that, when breached, triggers alerts or corrective actions to maintain acceptable data quality levels.

## 3. Part of Speech
Noun phrase

## 4. Usage & Examples
- "We have set quality thresholds for each data source to ensure consistent quality standards."
- "When data quality drops below our quality threshold, the system automatically triggers alerts."
- "Our quality threshold for data completeness is set at 95% for critical pipelines."
- "The quality threshold serves as an early warning system for potential data quality issues."
- "We regularly review and adjust our quality thresholds based on business requirements."

## 5. Synonyms & Related Terms
- **Quality standards**: Established criteria for acceptable data quality levels
- **Quality limits**: Maximum acceptable levels of data quality issues
- **Quality benchmarks**: Reference points for comparing data quality performance
- **Quality targets**: Desired levels of data quality performance
- **Quality criteria**: Specific requirements that data must meet
