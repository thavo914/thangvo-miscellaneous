# Week 08 – Data Quality Monitoring & Family Relationships

## Outcomes (by end of week)

- Confidently communicate about data quality monitoring systems and quality metrics to stakeholders.
- Use 8–10 chunks around data quality monitoring, automated validation, and family relationship management.
- Handle data quality incidents and explain remediation plans clearly.
- Discuss family dynamics and personal relationships with natural, meaningful communication.

## Inputs (choose 1–2)

- Article/talk on data quality monitoring best practices and automated validation frameworks
- Internal documentation on data quality metrics, SLAs, and incident response procedures
- Case study on stakeholder communication during data quality incidents

## Grammar/Pronunciation Focus

- **Grammar**: Present perfect for ongoing monitoring ("We have been tracking data quality for months...")
- **Grammar**: Past tense for family stories and experiences ("When I was growing up, my family always...")
- **Grammar**: Conditional sentences for quality scenarios ("If data quality drops below the threshold, we...")
- **Grammar**: Comparative structures for family dynamics ("My relationship with my parents is closer than...")
- **Pronunciation**: Clear articulation of technical quality terms and family relationship vocabulary
- **Echoing**: Practice data quality monitoring and family conversation patterns
