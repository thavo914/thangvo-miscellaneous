# Speaking Scenarios - Week 08: Data Quality Monitoring & Family Relationships

## Overview
This file provides comprehensive speaking practice scenarios for Week 08. Practice these scenarios to develop confidence in discussing data quality monitoring professionally and family relationships personally. Each scenario includes role-play situations, presentation topics, and discussion prompts.

---

## PART 1: PROFESSIONAL SCENARIOS

### **Scenario 1: Data Quality Incident Response Meeting**

**Context:** You're leading a meeting to discuss a data quality incident that occurred last night.

**Your Role:** Data Engineer (Incident Response Lead)
**Audience:** Platform team, Business stakeholders, Management

**Key Points to Cover:**
- What happened and when
- Root cause analysis
- Impact assessment
- Remediation steps taken
- Prevention measures for the future

**Speaking Framework:** Problem → Impact → Solution → Prevention

**Practice Questions:**
1. How would you explain the technical details to non-technical stakeholders?
2. What would you say to reassure business users about data reliability?
3. How would you communicate the timeline for full resolution?

**Key Phrases to Use:**
- "We have been investigating this issue since..."
- "The root cause has been identified as..."
- "We have implemented temporary fixes to..."
- "Moving forward, we will..."

## PRACTICE GUIDELINES

### **General Practice Tips:**

1. **Start with familiar scenarios** and gradually move to more challenging ones
2. **Practice both roles** in each scenario to develop flexibility
3. **Use the speaking frameworks** as a guide but adapt them naturally
4. **Focus on clear communication** rather than perfect grammar
5. **Record yourself** and listen for natural flow and confidence
6. **Practice with different audiences** to develop versatility

### **Professional Communication:**
- **Be confident and authoritative** about technical knowledge
- **Use business language** appropriate for the audience
- **Provide concrete examples** and specific details
- **Maintain professional tone** even when discussing challenges
- **Focus on solutions** rather than just problems

### **Personal Communication:**
- **Be authentic and genuine** in your expression
- **Use warm, personal language** appropriate for relationships
- **Share personal experiences** and feelings openly
- **Show empathy and understanding** for others' perspectives
- **Focus on connection** and relationship building

### **Daily Practice Routine:**
- **Morning:** Professional scenarios (30 minutes)
- **Afternoon:** Personal scenarios (30 minutes)
- **Evening:** Mixed context scenarios (30 minutes)
- **Total:** 90 minutes per day

### **Success Indicators:**
- **Natural, confident delivery** in both contexts
- **Appropriate tone and language** for each audience
- **Clear, organized presentation** of ideas
- **Effective use of grammar patterns** from Week 08
- **Engaging and authentic communication** style

Remember: The goal is to develop natural, confident communication skills that work effectively in both professional and personal contexts!
