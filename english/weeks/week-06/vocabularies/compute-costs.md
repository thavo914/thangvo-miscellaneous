# compute costs

## 1. IPA
/kəmˈpjut kɔsts/

## 2. Definition
The expenses associated with processing power, including CPU usage, memory, and computational resources for running queries and data transformations.

## 3. Part of Speech
noun phrase

## 4. Usage & Examples
- "Compute costs increased by 25% due to more complex query patterns."
- "We implemented auto-scaling to better control our compute costs."
- "Compute costs make up 35% of our total data warehouse expenses."
- "The team optimized queries to reduce compute costs by 20%."
- "Our compute costs are highest during peak business hours."

## 5. Synonyms & Related Terms
- **processing costs**: Alternative term for compute costs
- **CPU costs**: Costs specifically related to CPU usage
- **computational expenses**: Broader term for processing-related costs
- **resource costs**: General term for all computing resource expenses
