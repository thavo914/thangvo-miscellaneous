# budget allocation

## 1. IPA
/ˈbʌdʒɪt ˌæləˈkeɪʃən/

## 2. Definition
The process of distributing financial resources across different departments, projects, or cost categories according to a predetermined plan.

## 3. Part of Speech
noun phrase

## 4. Usage & Examples
- "Our budget allocation for data infrastructure is 60% for storage and 40% for compute."
- "The finance team reviewed our budget allocation for the upcoming quarter."
- "We need to adjust our budget allocation to accommodate the new data sources."
- "The budget allocation across departments is based on their actual usage patterns."
- "Our budget allocation strategy focuses on maximizing ROI while minimizing waste."

## 5. Synonyms & Related Terms
- **resource allocation**: Distribution of resources (not just financial)
- **budget distribution**: How budget is divided among different areas
- **cost allocation**: Assigning costs to specific departments or projects
- **budget planning**: The process of creating and managing budgets
