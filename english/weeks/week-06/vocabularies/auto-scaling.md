# auto-scaling

## 1. IPA
/ˈɔtoʊ ˈskeɪlɪŋ/

## 2. Definition
The automatic adjustment of computing resources based on current demand, allowing systems to scale up or down as needed without manual intervention.

## 3. Part of Speech
noun phrase

## 4. Usage & Examples
- "Auto-scaling helps us control compute costs by matching resources to demand."
- "We implemented auto-scaling to handle variable query workloads efficiently."
- "Auto-scaling reduced our compute costs by 40% during off-peak hours."
- "The auto-scaling configuration needs to be tuned for our specific use case."
- "Auto-scaling ensures we have enough resources during peak times without over-provisioning."

## 5. Synonyms & Related Terms
- **automatic scaling**: The full term for auto-scaling
- **dynamic scaling**: Scaling that responds to changing conditions
- **elastic scaling**: Scaling that can expand and contract as needed
- **resource scaling**: General term for adjusting computing resources
