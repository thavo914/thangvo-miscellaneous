# on-premises

## 1. IPA
/ɑn ˈprɛməsəz/

## 2. Definition
Infrastructure and software that is installed and runs on the organization's own physical servers and data centers, rather than in the cloud.

## 3. Part of Speech
adjective

## 4. Usage & Examples
- "Our data warehouse runs on-premises using Linux servers and Docker containers."
- "On-premises infrastructure gives us more control over data security and compliance."
- "We're comparing on-premises costs with cloud alternatives for our next budget cycle."
- "The on-premises solution requires more upfront investment but lower ongoing costs."
- "Our MSSQL database is hosted on-premises for better performance and data governance."

## 5. Synonyms & Related Terms
- **on-prem**: Abbreviated form of on-premises
- **in-house infrastructure**: Infrastructure managed internally
- **local infrastructure**: Infrastructure located at the organization's facilities
- **self-hosted**: Software and services hosted on own servers
