# Airflow DAGs

## 1. IPA
/ˈɛrfloʊ dægz/

## 2. Definition
Directed Acyclic Graphs in Apache Airflow that define workflows for data processing tasks, including scheduling, dependencies, and execution order.

## 3. Part of Speech
noun phrase

## 4. Usage & Examples
- "Our Airflow DAGs orchestrate the execution of dbt models on a daily schedule."
- "The Airflow DAGs are deployed in Docker containers on our Linux servers."
- "We monitor Airflow DAG execution costs as part of our budget management."
- "The Airflow DAGs handle data extraction, transformation, and loading processes."
- "Failed Airflow DAGs can impact our data warehouse costs due to retry mechanisms."

## 5. Synonyms & Related Terms
- **workflows**: General term for automated processes
- **data pipelines**: Pipelines that process data
- **orchestration workflows**: Workflows that coordinate multiple tasks
- **scheduled jobs**: Jobs that run on a schedule
