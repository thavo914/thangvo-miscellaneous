# ROI analysis

## 1. IPA
/ˌɑr oʊ ˈaɪ əˈnæləsɪs/

## 2. Definition
Return on Investment analysis; a method of evaluating the efficiency and profitability of an investment by comparing the gain or loss relative to its cost.

## 3. Part of Speech
noun phrase

## 4. Usage & Examples
- "Our ROI analysis shows a 280% return on the data warehouse investment."
- "The finance team requested a detailed ROI analysis for the proposed infrastructure upgrade."
- "According to our ROI analysis, the project will pay for itself within 18 months."
- "We conduct quarterly ROI analysis to track the business value of our data initiatives."
- "The ROI analysis revealed that cloud migration would be more cost-effective than on-premises."

## 5. Synonyms & Related Terms
- **return on investment**: The full term for ROI
- **cost-benefit analysis**: Evaluation of costs versus benefits
- **business value assessment**: Measuring the value delivered to the business
- **investment evaluation**: The process of assessing investment opportunities
