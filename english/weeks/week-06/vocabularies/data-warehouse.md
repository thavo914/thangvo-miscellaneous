# data warehouse

## 1. IPA
/ˈdeɪtə ˈwɛrhaʊs/

## 2. Definition
A centralized repository that stores integrated data from multiple sources, designed for querying and analysis rather than transaction processing.

## 3. Part of Speech
noun phrase

## 4. Usage & Examples
- "Our data warehouse stores all customer information from the past five years."
- "The data warehouse is designed to support business intelligence and analytics."
- "We need to optimize our data warehouse to handle the increased query load."
- "The data warehouse architecture includes both structured and semi-structured data."
- "Our team is responsible for maintaining the data warehouse infrastructure."

## 5. Synonyms & Related Terms
- **data repository**: A general term for any system that stores data
- **data mart**: A subset of a data warehouse focused on specific business functions
- **data lakehouse**: A modern architecture combining data lake and warehouse features
- **analytical database**: A database optimized for analytical queries rather than transactions
