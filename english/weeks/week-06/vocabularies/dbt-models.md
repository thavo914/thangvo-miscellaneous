# dbt models

## 1. IPA
/ˈdi bi ti ˈmɑdəlz/

## 2. Definition
Data transformation models created using dbt (data build tool) that define how raw data is transformed into analytics-ready tables and views.

## 3. Part of Speech
noun phrase

## 4. Usage & Examples
- "Our dbt models transform raw data into clean, analytics-ready tables."
- "We have over 200 dbt models in our data warehouse project."
- "The dbt models are executed by Airflow DAGs on a scheduled basis."
- "We need to optimize our dbt models to reduce execution time and costs."
- "The dbt models are materialized as tables in our MSSQL database."

## 5. Synonyms & Related Terms
- **data models**: General term for data transformation models
- **transformation models**: Models that transform data
- **analytics models**: Models designed for analytics purposes
- **dbt transformations**: Specific transformations using dbt
