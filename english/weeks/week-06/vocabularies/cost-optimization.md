# cost optimization

## 1. IPA
/kɔst ˌɑptɪməˈzeɪʃən/

## 2. Definition
The process of reducing expenses while maintaining or improving performance, quality, and functionality of data systems.

## 3. Part of Speech
noun phrase

## 4. Usage & Examples
- "We implemented several cost optimization strategies to reduce our data warehouse expenses."
- "Cost optimization is a continuous process that requires regular monitoring and adjustment."
- "The team achieved 30% savings through effective cost optimization techniques."
- "Our cost optimization efforts focused on storage, compute, and data transfer expenses."
- "The finance team requested a detailed cost optimization plan for next quarter."

## 5. Synonyms & Related Terms
- **cost reduction**: The act of decreasing expenses
- **expense management**: The process of controlling and monitoring costs
- **budget optimization**: Maximizing value within budget constraints
- **resource optimization**: Efficient use of available resources
