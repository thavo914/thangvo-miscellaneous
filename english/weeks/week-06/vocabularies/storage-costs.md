# storage costs

## 1. IPA
/ˈstɔrɪdʒ kɔsts/

## 2. Definition
The expenses associated with storing data, including disk space, backup systems, and data retention services.

## 3. Part of Speech
noun phrase

## 4. Usage & Examples
- "Storage costs increased by 35% due to our new data retention policies."
- "We need to implement data compression to reduce our storage costs."
- "Storage costs account for 45% of our total data warehouse budget."
- "The team is exploring cheaper storage tiers to control storage costs."
- "Our storage costs are projected to double next year if we don't optimize."

## 5. Synonyms & Related Terms
- **data storage expenses**: Alternative term for storage costs
- **disk space costs**: Costs specifically related to disk storage
- **retention costs**: Expenses for keeping data over time
- **infrastructure costs**: Broader term including all infrastructure expenses
