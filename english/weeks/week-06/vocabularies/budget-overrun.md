# budget overrun

## 1. IPA
/ˈbʌdʒɪt ˈoʊvərˌrʌn/

## 2. Definition
A situation where actual expenses exceed the planned or approved budget for a project or initiative.

## 3. Part of Speech
noun phrase

## 4. Usage & Examples
- "We're facing a 40% budget overrun on our data warehouse project."
- "The budget overrun was caused by unexpected data volume growth."
- "We need to present a plan to address the budget overrun to the steering committee."
- "The team is working to minimize the budget overrun through cost optimization."
- "Budget overruns are common in data projects due to changing requirements."

## 5. Synonyms & Related Terms
- **cost overrun**: Alternative term for budget overrun
- **budget variance**: The difference between planned and actual costs
- **expense overrun**: When expenses exceed the budget
- **budget shortfall**: When there's not enough budget allocated
