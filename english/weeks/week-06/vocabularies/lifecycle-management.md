# lifecycle management

## 1. IPA
/ˈlaɪfsaɪkəl ˈmænɪdʒmənt/

## 2. Definition
The process of managing data through its entire lifecycle, from creation to archival or deletion, including storage tier optimization and cost control.

## 3. Part of Speech
noun phrase

## 4. Usage & Examples
- "Data lifecycle management helps us control storage costs by moving old data to cheaper tiers."
- "We implemented lifecycle management to automatically archive data older than two years."
- "Lifecycle management policies ensure data is stored in the most cost-effective tier."
- "Our lifecycle management strategy reduces storage costs by 30% annually."
- "The team is responsible for defining lifecycle management rules for different data types."

## 5. Synonyms & Related Terms
- **data lifecycle**: The stages data goes through over time
- **data archiving**: Moving data to long-term storage
- **tiered storage**: Using different storage types based on data age
- **data retention**: Policies for how long to keep data
