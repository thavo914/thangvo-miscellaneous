# Sentence Structure Practice - Data Warehouse & Budget Management

## Overview
Practice transforming and constructing sentences about data warehouse costs and budget management using the grammar patterns from this week's lessons.

## Exercise 1: Present Perfect Continuous

### Instructions
Transform these simple present sentences into present perfect continuous to emphasize ongoing activities.

### Examples

**Original:** "We track our data warehouse costs."
**Transformed:** "We have been tracking our data warehouse costs for the past six months."

**Original:** "The team monitors storage usage."
**Transformed:** "The team has been monitoring storage usage since the migration."

### Practice Sentences

1. **Original:** "I analyze the budget performance."
   **Your answer:** I have been analyzing the budget performance.

2. **Original:** "We work on cost optimization."
   **Your answer:** We have been working on cost optimization.

3. **Original:** "The finance team reviews our spending."
   **Your answer:** The finace team have been review our spending

4. **Original:** "We track ROI metrics."
   **Your answer:** We have been tracking ROI metrics

5. **Original:** "I study the cost breakdown."
   **Your answer:** I have been study the cost breakdown.

### Answers
1. I have been analyzing the budget performance for the past quarter.
2. We have been working on cost optimization since last month.
3. The finance team has been reviewing our spending throughout the year.
4. We have been tracking ROI metrics for the past 12 months.
5. I have been studying the cost breakdown all week.

## Exercise 3: Passive Voice

### Instructions
Convert these active voice sentences to passive voice for more formal reporting.

### Examples

**Active:** "The team completed the cost analysis."
**Passive:** "The cost analysis was completed by the team."

**Active:** "We achieved significant savings."
**Passive:** "Significant savings were achieved through optimization."

### Practice Sentences

1. **Active:** "The finance committee approved the budget increase."
   **Passive:** _________________________________

2. **Active:** "We allocated costs across departments."
   **Passive:** _________________________________

3. **Active:** "The team reduced storage costs by 40%."
   **Passive:** _________________________________

4. **Active:** "Management reviewed our budget proposal."
   **Passive:** _________________________________

5. **Active:** "We implemented cost optimization strategies."
   **Passive:** _________________________________

### Answers
1. The budget increase was approved by the finance committee.
2. Costs were allocated across departments.
3. Storage costs were reduced by 40% by the team.
4. Our budget proposal was reviewed by management.
5. Cost optimization strategies were implemented.

## Practice Tips

### Daily Practice Routine
1. **Morning:** Complete 2-3 exercises from different sections
2. **Afternoon:** Practice the sentences aloud, focusing on pronunciation
3. **Evening:** Review any mistakes and practice the correct forms

### Speaking Practice
1. **Record yourself** reading the completed sentences
2. **Focus on natural rhythm** and stress patterns
3. **Practice in context** by using these patterns in your own examples

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
1. **Present Perfect Continuous:** Don't forget the "been" (have been tracking, not have tracking)
2. **Conditionals:** Match the tense correctly (if + past simple, would + base form)
3. **Passive Voice:** Use the correct form of "be" + past participle
4. **Comparatives:** Use "than" for comparisons, "as...as" for equality

Remember to practice these patterns regularly to make them natural and automatic in your speech!
