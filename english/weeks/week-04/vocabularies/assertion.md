# Assertion

## Definition
A test that validates specific conditions about your data (e.g., uniqueness, not null, relationships) to ensure data quality and integrity.

## Example Sentences
- "We added assertions to ensure customer IDs are always unique and never null."
- "The assertion failed because we found duplicate records in our customer table."
- "Custom assertions help us validate complex business logic that generic tests can't cover."

## Related Terms
- **Data test**: Automated verification of data conditions
- **Validation**: Process of checking data against rules
- **Generic test**: Built-in dbt tests like unique, not_null
- **Custom test**: User-defined tests for specific business logic

## Usage Context
- Explaining testing strategies to team members
- Discussing data quality improvements
- Code review conversations
- Planning testing coverage
