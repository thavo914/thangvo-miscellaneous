# late-arriving data

1. (IPA): /ˌleɪt əˈraɪvɪŋ ˈdeɪtə/

2. Definition

- Records that arrive after their expected event time window, often due to network delays, retries, or device buffering.

1. Part of Speech

- Noun phrase

1. Usage & 5 Examples

- We configured a two-hour window to handle late-arriving data.
- Late-arriving data is reconciled during the backfill.
- Watermarks help us bound how we treat late-arriving data.
- KPIs tolerate small amounts of late-arriving data.
- We saw spikes in late-arriving data during outages.

1. Synonyms & Related Terms

- Synonyms: delayed events, tardy records
- Related: watermark, backfill, deduplication, event time
