# bronze / silver / gold

1. (IPA): /brɒnz/ · /ˈsɪlvə/ · /ɡəʊld/

2. Definition

- A layered modeling convention: bronze (raw/staging), silver (cleaned/conformed), gold (curated marts for analytics/BI).

1. Part of Speech

- Noun phrases (naming data layers)

1. Usage & 5 Examples

- The job promotes data from bronze to silver after validation.
- Gold tables are refreshed nightly to feed dashboards.
- Schema drift is tolerated in bronze but not in silver.
- Only gold is exposed in the semantic layer.
- Backfills start at bronze and cascade to silver and gold.

1. Synonyms & Related Terms

- Synonyms: raw/cleaned/curated layers
- Related: staging, conformed layer, marts, semantic layer
