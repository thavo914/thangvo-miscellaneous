# batch processing

## IPA
/bætʃ ˈprəʊsesɪŋ/

## Definition
A method of data processing where data is collected and processed in groups (batches) at scheduled intervals, rather than being processed immediately as it arrives.

## Part of Speech
Noun phrase

## Usage & Examples

**In technical discussions:**
- "We're implementing batch processing for our daily ETL pipelines."
- "Batch processing runs every four hours to update our data warehouse."

**In business contexts:**
- "Batch processing is more cost-effective for our current requirements."
- "The batch processing schedule aligns with our business reporting needs."

**Comparing approaches:**
- "Batch processing offers better reliability than streaming for our use case."
- "We chose batch processing over real-time processing due to cost constraints."

## Synonyms & Related Terms

**Synonyms:**
- Scheduled processing
