# streaming

## IPA
/ˈstriːmɪŋ/

## Definition
A method of data processing where data is processed continuously and in real-time as it arrives, rather than being collected into batches for later processing.

## Part of Speech
Noun (gerund form of verb "stream")

## Usage & Examples

**In technical discussions:**
- "We're considering streaming for real-time analytics."
- "Streaming requires more complex infrastructure than batch processing."

**In business contexts:**
- "Streaming provides immediate insights but at higher operational cost."
- "The streaming architecture enables real-time decision making."

**Comparing approaches:**
- "Streaming offers lower latency but higher complexity than batch processing."
- "We need to evaluate if streaming justifies the additional operational overhead."
