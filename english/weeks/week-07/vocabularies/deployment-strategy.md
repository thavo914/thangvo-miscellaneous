# Deployment Strategy

## 1. IPA
/dɪˈplɔɪmənt ˈstrætədʒi/

## 2. Definition
A planned approach for releasing software, systems, or data pipelines to production, including timing, rollback plans, testing procedures, and risk mitigation.

## 3. Part of Speech
noun phrase

## 4. Usage & Examples
- "I'm planning to deploy the ELT pipeline for gaming data next week."
- "Our deployment strategy includes thorough testing and validation."
- "The deployment strategy ensures minimal downtime and risk."
- "I'm confident about the deployment strategy for the ELT pipeline."
- "We need to review the deployment strategy before going live."

## 5. Synonyms & Related Terms
- **Release strategy**: Plan for releasing software or systems
- **Go-live strategy**: Strategy for launching new systems
- **Production deployment**: Deploying to live environment
- **Rollout plan**: Detailed plan for system rollout
