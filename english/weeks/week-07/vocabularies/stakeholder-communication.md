# Stakeholder Communication

## 1. IPA
/ˈsteɪkhəʊldər kəˌmjuːnɪˈkeɪʃən/

## 2. Definition
The process of sharing information, updates, and decisions with people who have an interest in or are affected by a project, including team members, managers, clients, and other relevant parties.

## 3. Part of Speech
noun phrase

## 4. Usage & Examples
- "Stakeholder communication is crucial for project success."
- "I'm improving my stakeholder communication skills through practice."
- "The stakeholder communication includes regular updates and meetings."
- "Effective stakeholder communication keeps everyone aligned and informed."
- "I'm working on stakeholder communication for the ELT project."

## 5. Synonyms & Related Terms
- **Project communication**: Communication about project activities
- **Team communication**: Communication within the project team
- **Status communication**: Sharing project status information
- **Progress communication**: Communicating project progress
