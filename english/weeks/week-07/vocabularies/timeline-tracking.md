# Timeline Tracking

## 1. IPA
/ˈtaɪmlaɪn ˈtrækɪŋ/

## 2. Definition
The process of monitoring and managing project schedules, deadlines, and milestones to ensure projects stay on track and meet delivery commitments.

## 3. Part of Speech
noun phrase

## 4. Usage & Examples
- "I'm confident about the timeline for the ELT project deployment."
- "Timeline tracking helps us stay on schedule with the project."
- "The timeline tracking shows we're ahead of schedule."
- "I use timeline tracking to monitor project progress and deadlines."
- "Timeline tracking is essential for project management success."

## 5. Synonyms & Related Terms
- **Schedule management**: Managing project schedules and deadlines
- **Progress tracking**: Monitoring project advancement
- **Milestone tracking**: Tracking specific project milestones
- **Deadline management**: Managing project deadlines
