# Team Integration

## 1. IPA
/tiːm ˌɪntɪˈɡreɪʃən/

## 2. Definition
The process of a new team member becoming part of the team, including learning team dynamics, building relationships, understanding processes, and contributing effectively to team goals.

## 3. Part of Speech
noun phrase

## 4. Usage & Examples
- "The team integration has been going really well since I joined."
- "I'm focusing on team integration while working on the ELT project."
- "The team has been really welcoming, which helps with team integration."
- "Team integration is important for new employees to be successful."
- "I'm enjoying the team integration process and learning from everyone."

## 5. Synonyms & Related Terms
- **Onboarding**: The process of integrating new employees
- **Team building**: Activities to strengthen team relationships
- **Cultural integration**: Adapting to team culture and values
- **Workplace integration**: General integration into work environment
