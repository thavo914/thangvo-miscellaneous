# Data Quality

## 1. IPA
/ˈdeɪtə ˈkwɒlɪti/

## 2. Definition
The degree to which data meets the requirements for its intended use, including accuracy, completeness, consistency, timeliness, and validity. Data quality is crucial for reliable analytics and decision-making.

## 3. Part of Speech
noun phrase

## 4. Usage & Examples
- "The data quality looks excellent, and I've implemented proper validation."
- "We need to ensure data quality throughout the ELT pipeline."
- "I've built in validation checks to maintain data quality."
- "Data quality is essential for accurate business insights."
- "The gaming data quality has exceeded our expectations."

## 5. Synonyms & Related Terms
- **Data integrity**: The accuracy and consistency of data
- **Data validation**: Process of checking data quality
- **Data accuracy**: How correct the data is
- **Data completeness**: How complete the data is
