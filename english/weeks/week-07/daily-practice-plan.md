# Daily Practice Plan - Week 07: Office Conversations & Project Reporting

## Overview
This week focuses on office conversations and project reporting as a new Data Engineer. You'll practice lunch conversations with your boss, project status meetings, and general office interactions centered around your ELT project progress and DBT transformation work.

---

## Key Grammar Patterns to Practice

### **Present Perfect for Progress:**
- "I have completed the extraction part"
- "I've been working on the transformation layer"
- "I've learned a lot about DBT best practices"

### **Future Tense for Planning:**
- "I will deploy the ELT pipeline next week"
- "I'm planning to complete the DBT models this week"
- "I'm going to finish the testing by Friday"

### **Conditional Sentences:**
- "If everything goes smoothly, I will deploy next week"
- "If we encounter issues, I can fix them quickly"
- "If the testing passes, we can proceed with deployment"

### **Passive Voice for Technical Descriptions:**
- "The data transformations are performed by DBT"
- "The data quality is validated by the system"
- "The pipeline is deployed to the production environment"

### **Business Language Expressions:**
- "I'm happy to report that we've made progress"
- "I'd like to provide an update on the project"
- "Looking ahead, I'm planning to complete the testing"

## Practice Tips

### **Speaking Practice:**
1. **Record yourself** - listen for pronunciation and fluency
2. **Practice both roles** - be comfortable as speaker and listener
3. **Use natural language** - avoid memorized scripts
4. **Focus on clarity** - prioritize clear communication
5. **Build confidence** - speak with authority about your knowledge

### **Grammar Practice:**
1. **Start with basic patterns** - master present perfect and future tense
2. **Add complexity gradually** - practice conditionals and passive voice
3. **Use in context** - apply patterns in real conversations
4. **Focus on accuracy** - get the grammar right before speed
5. **Build fluency** - practice speaking naturally and confidently

### **Vocabulary Practice:**
1. **Learn in context** - understand how words are used
2. **Practice pronunciation** - focus on clear articulation
3. **Use actively** - incorporate new words in conversations
4. **Review regularly** - reinforce learning through repetition
5. **Apply immediately** - use new vocabulary in practice sessions

Remember, the goal is to develop natural, confident communication skills for office conversations and project reporting. Regular practice with these patterns will significantly improve your professional communication abilities!
