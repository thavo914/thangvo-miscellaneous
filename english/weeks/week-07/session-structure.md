# Session Structure - Week 07: Office Conversations & Project Reporting

## Overview
This document outlines the structure for 90-minute English practice sessions focused on office conversations and project reporting. Each session is designed to maximize speaking practice while building confidence in professional communication.

---

## Session 1: Project Progress Reporting (90 minutes)

### **Warm-up (10 minutes)**
- **4-3-2 Drill on yesterday's topic:**
  - 4 minutes: Explain your ELT project progress
  - 3 minutes: Same topic, more concise
  - 2 minutes: Same topic, most concise
- **1 minute reflection:** What improved in each retelling?

### **Input (15 minutes)**
- **Read article on project status reporting:**
  - Focus on business communication patterns
  - Identify key phrases and structures
  - Take notes on professional language

### **Shadowing (15 minutes)**
- **Practice 8-10 sentences from article:**
  - Focus on business tone and professional delivery
  - Practice stress patterns and intonation
  - Record yourself and compare with original

### **Vocabulary (10 minutes)**
- **Add 5-6 new terms to vocabulary list:**
  - Technical terms: ELT pipeline, DBT models, data extraction
  - Business terms: project status, progress update, timeline tracking
  - Practice pronunciation and usage

### **Speaking Task (25 minutes)**
- **PREP Presentation:**
  - Point: ELT project progress update
  - Reason: Need to inform stakeholders of current status
  - Example: Specific accomplishments and next steps
  - Point: Timeline and confidence in delivery
- **Practice 2-3 minute presentation**
- **Record and review**

### **Pronunciation (10 minutes)**
- **Focus on technical vocabulary:**
  - ELT: /iː el tiː/
  - DBT: /diː biː tiː/
  - Pipeline: /ˈpaɪpˌlaɪn/
  - Transformation: /ˌtrænsfərˈmeɪʃən/
- **Practice stress patterns in business sentences**

### **Log (5 minutes)**
- **Record:**
  - One new chunk used: "I have completed the data extraction phase"
  - One error to fix: [specific grammar or pronunciation issue]
  - One goal for tomorrow: [specific improvement target]

## General Session Guidelines

### **Speaking Practice Focus:**
1. **60%+ speaking time** - maximize oral communication
2. **Natural language** - avoid memorized scripts
3. **Context awareness** - adapt tone to situation
4. **Confidence building** - speak with authority
5. **Error correction** - learn from mistakes

### **Grammar Integration:**
1. **Present perfect** - for project progress
2. **Future tense** - for planning and timelines
3. **Conditional sentences** - for problem-solving
4. **Passive voice** - for technical descriptions
5. **Comparative structures** - for tool comparisons

### **Vocabulary Development:**
1. **Technical terms** - clear pronunciation
2. **Business language** - professional expressions
3. **Office communication** - natural conversation
4. **Context usage** - appropriate word choice
5. **Active practice** - use new words immediately

### **Pronunciation Focus:**
1. **Technical vocabulary** - clear articulation
2. **Stress patterns** - emphasize key words
3. **Linking** - connect words smoothly
4. **Intonation** - use appropriate tone
5. **Rhythm** - maintain natural pacing

Remember, the goal is to develop natural, confident communication skills for office conversations and project reporting. Each session builds on the previous one, creating a comprehensive learning experience that prepares you for real-world professional communication!
