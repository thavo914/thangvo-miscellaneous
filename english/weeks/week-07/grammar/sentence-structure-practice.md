# Sentence Structure Practice - Office Communication Patterns

## Overview
This practice focuses on sentence structures commonly used in office conversations and project reporting. You'll practice transforming sentences, combining ideas, and using appropriate grammar patterns for different communication contexts.

---

## 4. Comparative Structure Transformations

### **Exercise 7: Basic Comparisons**
Transform these sentences using comparative structures.

**Instructions:** Use "more/less + adjective + than" or "better/worse + than" structures.

1. **Original:** "DBT is organized. Ad-hoc scripts are not organized."
   **Transform to:** "DBT is more organized than ad-hoc scripts."

2. **Original:** "The new approach is good. The previous approach is not good."
   **Transform to:** "The new approach is better than the previous one."

3. **Original:** "This method is efficient. The old method is efficient."
   **Transform to:** "This method is as efficient as the old one."

4. **Original:** "The performance is good this week. The performance was not good last week."
   **Transform to:** "The performance is better this week than last week."

5. **Original:** "The data quality is high. The data quality was low before."
   **Transform to:** "The data quality is higher than before."

### **Exercise 8: Advanced Comparisons**
Create more complex comparative sentences.

**Instructions:** Use comparative structures with additional context.

1. **Original:** "DBT is organized. Ad-hoc scripts are not organized. This helps with maintenance."
   **Transform to:** "DBT is more organized than ad-hoc scripts, which helps with maintenance."

2. **Original:** "The new approach is good. The previous approach is not good. The team prefers it."
   **Transform to:** "The new approach is better than the previous one, and the team prefers it."

3. **Original:** "This method is efficient. The old method is efficient. Both work well."
   **Transform to:** "This method is as efficient as the old one, and both work well."

4. **Original:** "The performance is good this week. The performance was not good last week. We fixed the issues."
   **Transform to:** "The performance is better this week than last week because we fixed the issues."

5. **Original:** "The data quality is high. The data quality was low before. We implemented validation."
   **Transform to:** "The data quality is higher than before because we implemented validation."

## 8. Practice Guidelines

### **How to Use These Exercises:**

1. **Start with simple transformations** - master basic patterns first
2. **Practice out loud** - say the transformed sentences
3. **Record yourself** - listen for natural pronunciation
4. **Use in context** - apply patterns in real conversations
5. **Build complexity gradually** - start simple, add complexity

### **Common Mistakes to Avoid:**

1. **Present Perfect vs. Past Simple** - use present perfect for current relevance
2. **Future tense consistency** - don't mix "will" and "going to" unnecessarily
3. **Passive voice structure** - remember "be" + past participle
4. **Comparative forms** - use "more/less" with long adjectives
5. **Business vs. casual tone** - match the formality to the context

### **Progression Tips:**

1. **Week 1** - Focus on present perfect and future tense
2. **Week 2** - Add passive voice and comparative structures
3. **Week 3** - Practice business language and office conversations
4. **Week 4** - Combine all patterns in complex sentences

Remember, the goal is to communicate clearly and naturally. These sentence structures will help you sound more professional and confident in office conversations and project reporting!
