# Day 5 - Exercise 4: Voice Simulation & Shadowing Guide (15 Mins)

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 2 (Part C)**: ChatGPT Voice Mode simulation prompt (under 300 characters, zero hyphens) and interactive spoken rhythm shadowing.

---

## 🎙️ Section 1: ChatGPT Voice Mode Simulation Prompt

Use the prompt below to initiate an interactive, spoken technical discussion with ChatGPT Voice Mode on iOS/Android or Desktop:

```text
Act as my tech lead. We are discussing whether to buy an expensive cloud tool or build an in-house open source pipeline to save costs. Ask me about the vendor estimate and our budget trade-offs, and let's have a realistic spoken conversation in English.
```

---

## 🎵 Section 2: Interactive Shadowing & Pacing Guide

Read these two technical rationale sentences aloud. Observe the **pauses** marked by slashes (`/`), and emphasize the **CAPITALIZED** words.

### Sentence 1: Justifying Cost-Effective Architecture
> *"Instead of signing / an expensive vendor **CON**tract / self-hosting an open-source tool / is **MUCH** more cost-effective / and gives us the **MOST BANG** for our **BUCK**."*

* **Rhythm Breakdown**:
  * `/ Instead of signing /`: Clean transition into the contrast clause.
  * `/ an expensive vendor CONtract /`: Crisp primary stress on the first syllable of *CON-tract*.
  * `/ self-hosting an open-source tool /`: Smooth, even pacing across technical terms.
  * `/ is MUCH more cost-effective /`: Punch *MUCH* to highlight the contrast.
  * `/ and gives us the MOST BANG for our BUCK /`: Deliver *MOST BANG* and *BUCK* with lively, energetic stress.

---

### Sentence 2: Urgent Remediation & Decision Making
> *"Their promotional discount / **EX**pires this **FRI**day / so we had **BET**ter act **QUICK**ly / before our trial **LAP**ses."*

* **Rhythm Breakdown**:
  * `/ Their promotional discount /`: Quick, unstressed flow over *promotional*.
  * `/ EXpires this FRIDAY /`: Strong stress on *EX-pires* and *FRI-day*.
  * `/ so we had BETter act QUICKly /`: Fast delivery of *we had*; crisp stress on *BET-ter* and *QUICK-ly*.
  * `/ before our trial LAPses /`: Downward pitch contour on *LAP-ses* to conclude cleanly.
