# Day 5 - Exercise 1: Warm-up Reflex & Active Listening (35–45 Mins)

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 1 Focus**: Fast-thinking counter-argument drill with the "Devil's Advocate" Challenge (15 mins) + Final TOEIC dictation sprint (20–30 mins).

---

## 😈 Part 1: Speaking Reflex - The "Devil's Advocate" Challenge (15 Mins)

### Goal
Force flexible thinking and rapid speech restructuring by defending a technical proposition, and then immediately arguing against it within 30 seconds using contrast linkers (*on the flip side, however, having said that, at the same time*).

### Challenge Topic: Paying for Fully Managed SaaS vs. Self-Hosting In-House Open-Source Tools
* **Side A (Pro-SaaS - 30s Out Loud)**: Explain why buying a managed cloud tool saves engineering hours and provides 24/7 vendor support.
* **Side B (Pro-Self-Hosting - 30s Out Loud)**: Immediately pivot and argue why proprietary SaaS tools **break the bank** as data grows, and why self-hosting an open-source tool **for internal use** is far more **cost-effective** and **gives us the most bang for our buck**.

### 💡 Model Spoken Delivery
> *(Side A)*: *"On one hand, subscribing to a fully managed cloud service makes total sense when engineering bandwidth is tight. You don't have to worry about patching servers or debugging infrastructure at 2 AM, and the vendor takes full accountability for system uptime."*  
>
> *(Pivot & Side B)*: *"However, looking at the numbers from our latest vendor estimate, that approach is going to **break the bank** once our ingestion volume doubles. **I'm a little bit stressed that we won't be able to afford** their per-row charges next year. Deploying an open-source solution **for internal use** is significantly more **cost-effective**, gives our team complete control over data privacy, and ultimately **gives us the most bang for our buck**."*

---

## 🎧 Part 2: Active Listening - TOEIC Dictation (20–30 Mins)

### Goal
Consolidate listening accuracy on technical specifications, commercial contracts, and fast-paced workplace meetings.

### Instructions
1. Load 3 short TOEIC conversation clips dealing with project deadlines, software purchasing, or operational emergencies.
2. Complete full word-for-word dictation on paper or blank editor.
3. Compare against the transcript and analyze errors to ensure total comprehension of modal verbs and prepositions.
