# Day 2 - Exercise 1: Warm-up Reflex & Active Listening (35–45 Mins)

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 1 Focus**: Fast-paced oral fluency with the 60-Second Random Object Pitch (15 mins) + Intensive auditory dictation (20–30 mins).

---

## ⏱️ Part 1: Speaking Reflex - The 60-Second Random Object Pitch (15 Mins)

### Goal
Force fluid, uninhibited speech production under time pressure without pausing longer than 2 seconds, reinforcing high-frequency descriptors and natural rhythm.

### Instructions
1. Choose an everyday object within arm's reach (e.g., your desk mug, mechanical keyboard, wireless headphones, or monitor stand).
2. Start a 60-second stopwatch.
3. Speak out loud continuously for the full 60 seconds explaining why this object is essential to your daily productivity, using natural linking words (*honestly, what makes it great is, it really takes the edge off, at the end of the day*).
4. **Golden Rule**: If you hesitate, stumble, or freeze for more than 2 seconds, reset the timer and start over!

### 💡 Worked Example (What to Say Out Loud)
> *"This coffee mug right on my desk is basically my lifeline during morning deployments. What makes it special isn't just that it holds twelve ounces of dark roast, but that it keeps my coffee hot for hours while I'm deep in database queries. In our team, we often joke that having reliable coffee is like **bringing a trusted teammate on board**—it **takes the mental strain off your plate** when things get chaotic. It’s sturdy, reliable, and has **never left me high and dry** during an early production incident. At the end of the day, it's a simple tool, but it fits into my daily routine perfectly."*

---

## 🎧 Part 2: Active Listening - TOEIC Dictation (20–30 Mins)

### Goal
Refine auditory parsing of rapid unstressed function words, reduced auxiliary verbs (`have been`, `could've`, `was wondering`), and natural connected speech.

### Instructions
1. Load 3 short TOEIC conversation clips focusing on workplace decisions, personnel changes, or team coordination.
2. Listen to each clip sentence by sentence, pausing to write down every spoken word verbatim.
3. Review against the script and highlight omissions with a red pen to diagnose your listening blind spots.
