# Day 2 - Exercise 4: Shadowing & Rhythm Practice (15 Mins)

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 2 (Part C)**: Pacing, thought-group boundaries, and syllable stress practice to overcome monotonous or robotic English delivery.

---

## 🎵 Interactive Shadowing Guide

Read these two key sentences aloud. Observe the **pauses** marked by slashes (`/`), and place extra vocal energy on the **CAPITALIZED** stress words.

### Sentence 1: Advocating for Team Support
> *"The time has **COME** / to bring someone on **BOARD** / who can take routine maintenance / off our **PLATE**."*

* **Rhythm Breakdown**:
  * `/ The time has COME /`: Build pitch slightly on *COME* to signal urgency.
  * `/ to bring someone on BOARD /`: Smooth linking between *someone* and *on*; stress *BOARD*.
  * `/ who can take routine maintenance /`: Quick, unstressed flow over *routine maintenance*.
  * `/ off our PLATE /`: Emphasize *PLATE* with a clear downward pitch to signal completion.

---

### Sentence 2: Communicating Professional Loyalty
> *"I have been **TREATED** so **WELL** / by my **CUR**rent team / that I would **NEV**er want / to leave them **HIGH** and **DRY**."*

* **Rhythm Breakdown**:
  * `/ I have been TREATED so WELL /`: Notice the weak reduction of *have been* (`/əv bɪn/`), then strong stress on *TREATED* and *WELL*.
  * `/ by my CURrent team /`: Sharp stress on the first syllable of *CUR-rent*.
  * `/ that I would NEVer want /`: Clean pause before the final contrast clause.
  * `/ to leave them HIGH and DRY /`: Equal rhythmic weight on *HIGH* and *DRY* to deliver the idiom naturally.

---

## 🎯 Pacing Drill Instructions
1. **Slow Shadow (3 times)**: Whisper along with the pause markers, checking tongue and lip placement.
2. **Speed Shadow (3 times)**: Read at full native conversational speed (130–150 words per minute) without stopping at thought-group boundaries.
3. **Record & Compare**: Record your voice on your smartphone and compare your pitch contour against the stress markers above.
