# Day 2 - Exercise 3: ChatGPT Voice Mode Simulation Prompt (15 Mins)

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 2 (Part B)**: Voice simulation prompt for ChatGPT Voice Mode on iOS/Android or Desktop.

---

## 🎙️ Voice Simulation Prompt

Use the prompt below to initiate an interactive, spoken conversation with ChatGPT Voice Mode.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Prompt Calibration**: Meets all system constraints (under 300 characters and fewer than 10 hyphens).

```text
Act as my tech lead. We are discussing team expansion and hiring a new data engineer to take tasks off our plate. Ask me what qualities I look for in a candidate, and have a short, natural back and forth conversation with me in English.
```

---

## 📋 Spoken Performance Goals During Simulation
While conversing with ChatGPT, actively incorporate:
1. `The time has come to bring someone on board because...`
2. `That candidate seemed like a great match for our culture...`
3. `Having someone take routine table tasks off our plate will help us...`
4. `I have been treated well by this team, so I want to make sure everyone succeeds.`
