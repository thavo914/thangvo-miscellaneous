# Day 4 - Exercise 1: Active Listening - TOEIC Dictation (30 Mins)

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 1 Focus**: Deep auditory decoding, identifying vendor negotiations and cost management phrasing in spoken English.

---

## 🎧 TOEIC Audio Dictation Protocol

### Goal
Tune your ears to rapid numerical details, cost projections, and modal structures in fast-paced professional audio clips.

### Instructions
1. Select **3 distinct TOEIC listening passages** (Parts 3 or 4) focusing on budget approvals, software licensing, vendor quotes, or office equipment procurement.
2. Listen to the first passage without pausing to grasp the global context.
3. On the second pass, pause every 5–8 words and transcribe the speech verbatim.
4. On the third pass, verify punctuation and check for missed function words (`for`, `at`, `won't`, `'d better`).
5. Open the official transcript. Use a red pen or highlight tool to log words that your ear merged or skipped.
