**Model Translation 1:**
> *"**We reached out to the software vendor for an estimate**, but their annual licensing quote is going to **break the bank** for our engineering department."*
* *Linguistic Notes*: Uses "reached out to ... for an estimate"; replaces literal "break our budget" with the natural idiom "break the bank".
* *Alternative Phrasing*: *"We requested an estimate from the vendor, but their pricing model is far too high for our departmental budget."*

**Model Translation 2:**
> *"**Instead of** signing an expensive third-party contract, self-hosting an open-source solution **for internal use** is much more **cost-effective** and **gives us the most bang for our buck**."*
* *Linguistic Notes*: Uses Template 3; specifies "for internal use"; pairs "cost-effective" with the high-yield idiom "gives us the most bang for our buck" (đáng đồng tiền bát gạo).
* *Alternative Phrasing*: *"Deploying an in-house open-source tool is far more cost-effective than proprietary vendor licenses, maximizing the return on our infrastructure budget."*

**Model Translation 3:**
> *"The streaming pipeline **has been acting up** since this morning, so **we had better act quickly** before tomorrow's financial report numbers get corrupted."*
* *Linguistic Notes*: Replaces literal "dở chứng" with the natural phrasal verb "has been acting up"; uses "(had) better act quickly" for urgent technical remediation.
* *Alternative Phrasing*: *"Our streaming job is acting up, so we'd better troubleshoot the issue right away before the morning accounting batch runs."*

## 🔍 Drill 3: Spot the Mistake (Error Correction Drill)

Identify and correct the errors in these 3 sentences:

1. *We had better to act quickly before the server crashes.*  
   → **Your Correction**: ____________________

2. *This staging cluster is configured only for use internally.*  
   → **Your Correction**: ____________________

3. *I am very stressed that we can't afford to buying that license next year.*  
   → **Your Correction**: ____________________

<details>
<summary>💡 Click to View Explanations & Corrections</summary>

1. **Correction**: *We **had better act quickly** before the server crashes.*  
   *Explanation*: `(had) better` is an auxiliary modal and must be followed directly by a base verb (`act`), never an infinitive with "to" (`to act`).
2. **Correction**: *This staging cluster is configured strictly **for internal use**.*  
   *Explanation*: The standard technical modifier is `for internal use`, which functions as a compact prepositional phrase.
3. **Correction**: *I am a little bit stressed that we **won't be able to afford that license** (or: **afford to buy that license**) next year.*  
   *Explanation*: Use `won't be able to afford` to project future financial constraints smoothly. After `afford`, use either a direct noun (`afford that license`) or a to-infinitive (`afford to buy`), never a preposition + gerund (`afford to buying`).
</details>
