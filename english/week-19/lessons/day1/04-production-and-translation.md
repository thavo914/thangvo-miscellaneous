**Model Translation 1:**
> *"**The time has come to bring a data engineer on board** to **take daily pipeline maintenance off our plate**."*
* *Linguistic Notes*: Uses "the time has come to" for urgent transition; replaces formal "recruit/hire" with conversational "bring on board"; uses the natural idiom "take something off our plate" instead of literal "reduce our work".
* *Alternative Phrasing*: *"It's clear that the time has come to hire an additional engineer so they can take routine operational tasks off our hands."*

**Model Translation 2:**
> *"**I have been treated well by my current company**, so I would never want to **leave my teammates high and dry** in the middle of our data warehouse launch."*
* *Linguistic Notes*: Uses Present Perfect passive "have been treated well by"; applies the idiom "leave high and dry" rather than literal "abandon my team in difficulty".
* *Alternative Phrasing*: *"Because I've been treated with so much fairness by this organization, I refuse to leave the team high and dry while our project deadline is right around the corner."*

**Model Translation 3:**
> *"**Based on yesterday's interview**, that candidate seems like **a great match for our team culture** because he communicates clearly and is eager to **pull his weight**."*
* *Linguistic Notes*: Uses "a great match for"; uses "pull his weight" (làm tròn trách nhiệm / cùng gánh vác) rather than literal "take responsibility".
* *Alternative Phrasing*: *"Judging from our chat yesterday, she looks like a fantastic culture fit for our squad because she has strong technical fundamentals and a collaborative attitude."*

## 🔍 Drill 3: Spot the Mistake (Error Correction Drill)

Find and correct the errors in the 3 sentences below:

1. *I am treated very good by my company for two years, so I don't leave them high and dry.*  
   → **Your Correction**: ____________________

2. *The time has come to hiring someone on board because we have too much work.*  
   → **Your Correction**: ____________________

3. *I was wonder if we can make a special exception to work from home tomorrow.*  
   → **Your Correction**: ____________________

<details>
<summary>💡 Click to View Explanations & Corrections</summary>

1. **Correction**: *I **have been treated very well** by my company for two years, so I **would never leave** them high and dry.*  
   *Explanation*: Ongoing positive experience requires the Present Perfect (`have been treated`), not Present Simple (`am treated`). Use the adverb `well` to modify `treated`, not the adjective `good`. Use `would never leave` for a respectful conditional commitment.
2. **Correction**: *The time has come **to bring** someone on board (or: **to hire** someone) because we have too much work.*  
   *Explanation*: `The time has come to` must be followed by a base infinitive verb (`bring` or `hire`), not a gerund (`hiring`).
3. **Correction**: *I **was wondering if we could** make a special exception to work from home tomorrow.*  
   *Explanation*: The polite softening formula uses the Past Continuous (`was wondering`) followed by conditional modal (`could`), not the abrupt Present Simple (`was wonder... can`).
</details>
