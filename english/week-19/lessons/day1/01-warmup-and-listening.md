# Day 1 - Exercise 1: Warm-up Reflex & Active Listening (35–45 Mins)

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 1 Focus**: Spontaneous spoken English formulation without internal translation (15 mins) + Acute auditory decoding via dictation (20–30 mins).

---

## 🎙️ Part 1: Speaking Reflex - The Daily Commentator (15 Mins)

### Goal
Build spontaneous English formulation without translating from Vietnamese in your head by narrating your current workspace, team capacity, and daily thoughts out loud.

### Instructions
1. Look around your workstation, open IDE windows, terminal screens, or calendar schedule.
2. Speak continuously out loud for **3 minutes** narrating what you are working on, how your team workload feels, and why bringing in reinforcements matters.
3. Intentionally inject at least two of today's target phrases:
   - `bring someone on board`
   - `take something off [someone's] plate`
   - `a great match`
   - `be treated well by`
   - `leave someone high and dry`

### 💡 Worked Example (What to Say Out Loud)
> *"Looking at our sprint board right now, I see over twenty open backlog tickets. Just the two of us are maintaining the streaming pipeline, and we're definitely feeling the pressure. In our morning standup, I mentioned that we really need to **bring someone on board** to **take routine maintenance off our plate**. The candidate we spoke to last week looked like **a great match for our engineering team**. Someone joked whether I would look for another job, but honestly, I've **been treated well by my current company**. I have no intention of jumping ship and **leaving my team high and dry** during such a critical infrastructure migration."*

---

## 🎧 Part 2: Active Listening - TOEIC Dictation (20–30 Mins)

### Goal
Train acoustic decoding, catch rapid spoken reductions, and notice natural rhythm markers in realistic workplace English dialogues.

### Instructions
1. Open a TOEIC Short Conversations / Short Talks practice audio clip (Parts 3 or 4).
2. Listen to **3 distinct audio passages**. Pause sentence-by-sentence and transcribe every single word onto paper or into a blank text document.
3. Compare against the audio transcript and highlight missed linking sounds, prepositions, and weak forms with a red pen.
