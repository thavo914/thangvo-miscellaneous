# Day 1 - Exercise 5: AI Speaking Simulation & Review (75 Mins)

> [!NOTE]
> **Blocks 3 & 4 Focus**: AI Spoken Production with ChatGPT Voice Mode (60 mins) + Flashcard capture & error logging (15 mins).

---

## 🤖 Part 1: AI Speaking Practice (60 Mins)

Practice speaking with ChatGPT Voice Mode on your mobile device or laptop. Below are 3 contextualized drills tailored to today's core focus: **team expansion, candidate evaluation, and workplace loyalty**.

---

### Drill 1: Rapid Q&A (Hiring & Workload Reflex - 15 Mins)
* **Context**: You are talking through your team's current bandwidth, why you need more hands, and how you evaluate candidate fit.
* **Goal**: Answer spontaneously within 2–3 sentences without hesitating or translating mentally from Vietnamese.
* **Target Expressions to Activate**: `bring someone on board`, `take something off someone's plate`, `a great match`, `be treated well by`, `leave someone high and dry`.

> 📋 **Copy-Paste Prompt for ChatGPT Voice Mode:**
> ```text
> Act as my English speaking coach. I am a Data Engineer practicing talking about team expansion, candidate fit, and workplace loyalty. Ask me 5 rapid questions one by one about:
> 1. When do you know that the time has come to bring someone on board?
> 2. How can adding an extra engineer take routine maintenance off your plate?
> 3. What qualities make a candidate a great match for a tech team?
> 4. Why is it important not to leave your teammates high and dry during a major release?
> 5. How does being treated well by a company affect your commitment to stay?
> After each of my spoken answers, give me quick feedback on my grammar and suggest a more natural B1-B2 Plain English upgrade using today's target phrases.
> ```

---

### Drill 2: Instant Translation & Reverse Shadowing (Workplace Team Scenarios - 15 Mins)
* **Context**: Rapid verbal translation of realistic Vietnamese workplace statements into natural, spoken English under 5 seconds.
* **Goal**: Build muscle memory for slot templates and eliminate awkward word-for-word translation.

> 📋 **Copy-Paste Prompt for ChatGPT Voice Mode:**
> ```text
> Act as my bilingual English trainer. Give me 4 realistic Vietnamese workplace situations one by one where an engineer discusses hiring, team workload, or company loyalty (for example: "Đã đến lúc phải tuyển thêm người gánh bớt việc bảo trì cho team", "Công ty đối xử với tôi rất tốt nên tôi không muốn bỏ mặc họ lúc nước sôi lửa bỏng", "Ứng viên hôm qua rất phù hợp với văn hóa nhóm mình", "Tôi tính hỏi sếp xem có thể tạo một ngoại lệ cho trường hợp này không").
> I will translate each into English out loud within 3 to 5 seconds.
> After I answer, provide the most natural Band 6.5 native English phrasing using collocations like "bring someone on board", "take something off our plate", "a great match", or "leave them high and dry".
> ```

---

### Drill 3: Scenario Role-Play (Pitching Headcount to Engineering Manager - 30 Mins)
* **Detailed Scenario**:
  * **AI Persona**: Mark, an Engineering Manager who is protective of departmental budget but open to compelling, practical arguments.
  * **Your Persona**: Thang, a Senior Data Engineer whose team is operating at full capacity while managing critical streaming ingestion pipelines.
  * **Your Mission**:
    1. Acknowledge budget constraints politely.
    2. Explain that *"the time has come to bring someone on board"* because current velocity is bottlenecked.
    3. Illustrate how hiring will *"take routine operational tasks off our plate"*, freeing you to focus on high-impact cloud migrations.
    4. Mention that the recent candidate interviewed was *"a great match for our culture"*.
    5. Reiterate your commitment to the team: you have *"been treated well by the company"* and want to build a sustainable data platform without burning out.

> 📋 **Copy-Paste Prompt for ChatGPT Voice Mode:**
> ```text
> Let's do a realistic workplace role-play. You are Mark, my Engineering Manager. I am Thang, a Data Engineer on your team. I am meeting with you to discuss our team's workload and make a case for bringing an additional engineer on board. You should express concern about budget limits, and I will persuade you using phrases like "the time has come to", "take routine tasks off our plate", and "a great match for the team". Keep your responses concise (2 to 3 sentences) so we can have a natural, interactive spoken conversation. Start by asking me how our sprint is progressing.
> ```

### 📌 Universal AI Speaking Engine (Mandatory Exercises)

> **Suggested Exercises (AI Prompts):**
>
> 1. **Rapid Q&A:**
>    > "Act as my English tutor. Ask me random English questions ranging from easy to advanced (topics: daily life, work, opinions, etc.). I will answer in English as quickly as possible. After I answer, please correct my grammar and vocabulary, and suggest more natural, native-like ways to express my ideas."
>
> 2. **Instant Translation (Reverse Shadowing):**
>    > "Give me a common Vietnamese situation or daily phrase. I will try to translate it into English out loud within 3–5 seconds. After my attempt, please provide the most natural English translation so I can compare."
>
> 3. **Role-play:**
>    > "Let's do a role-play. Please choose one of these topics: ordering at a restaurant, a job interview, traveling/at the airport, or discussing work with a colleague. We will have a back-and-forth conversation entirely in English."
