# Day 3 - Exercise 1: Warm-up Reflex & Active Listening (35–45 Mins)

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 1 Focus**: Spontaneous dramatic reflex speaking via Unconventional AI Roleplay (15 mins) + Concentrated listening dictation (20–30 mins).

---

## 🤖 Part 1: Speaking Reflex - Unconventional AI Voice Roleplay (15 Mins)

### Goal
Break emotional rigidity and practice flexible spoken English under unusual, high-stakes conversational circumstances.

### Scenario: The Overzealous Headhunter
* **Context**: An aggressive tech headhunter calls you offering double your salary to jump ship immediately and start tomorrow at a stealth AI startup.
* **Your Role**: Maintain calm, polite composure. Explain that while the compensation is flattering, you **have been treated well by your current team**, that you are currently working on critical infrastructure, and that you would never **leave your teammates high and dry**.
* **Instructions**:
  1. Open ChatGPT Voice Mode or speak aloud to an imaginary headhunter.
  2. Speak for **3 minutes** handling the recruiter's persistent counter-arguments.
  3. Intentionally activate:
     - `I have been treated well by...`
     - `leave them high and dry`
     - `the time has come to...`
     - `a great match`

### 💡 Model Spoken Rebuttal
> *"Look, David, I really appreciate you reaching out, and the compensation package sounds impressive on paper. But for me, professional ethics and culture matter just as much as base salary. I've **been treated really well by my current leadership**, especially when our team had to redesign our data architecture. We're right in the middle of a major production migration, and walking away tomorrow would **leave my teammates high and dry**. In fact, our department is growing right now, and **the time has come to bring another senior engineer on board**. So if you know anyone who would be **a great match for our culture**, feel free to send their resume my way instead."*

---

## 🎧 Part 2: Active Listening - TOEIC Dictation (20–30 Mins)

### Goal
Decode natural conversational flow, contractions (`I've been`, `wouldn't`, `didn't`), and rhythm in office discussions.

### Instructions
1. Select 3 TOEIC audio clips depicting colleagues discussing staffing, scheduling, or project handovers.
2. Transcribe the audio word-for-word onto paper or an editor.
3. Compare against the transcript and note words where pronunciation differed from your mental expectation.
