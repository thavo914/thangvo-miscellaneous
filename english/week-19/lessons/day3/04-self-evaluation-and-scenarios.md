## ✅ Self-Evaluation Checklist

Check off each item once you can comfortably use it aloud in unscripted conversation:

- [ ] I can naturally use `the time has come to...` to indicate an urgent transition or needed action.
- [ ] I can describe a candidate or colleague as `a great match for [role/culture]` with clear reasons.
- [ ] I can use `bring someone on board` instead of repetitive words like *hire* or *recruit*.
- [ ] I can express workload relief using `take [task] off [someone's] plate`.
- [ ] I can articulate personal commitment using `have been treated well by [team/company]`.
- [ ] I can naturally deploy the idiom `leave someone high and dry`.
- [ ] I can use conversational softeners like `I was wondering if...` and `I've been meaning to...`.

## 💼 Tech Workplace Situational Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Post-Interview Debrief with Engineering Manager
* **Context**: You just wrapped up a 45-minute technical system design and live coding interview with an applicant for your data platform squad. Your manager asks: *"So Thang, what's your verdict on this candidate?"*
* **Your Task**: Recommend advancing the candidate by explaining why their attitude and experience make them a great match who can take operational tasks off the team's plate.

<details>
<summary>💡 Click to View Model Response</summary>

> *"Honestly, Mark, I think he’s **a great match for our team**. Technically, his understanding of partition keys and SQL performance tuning was very sharp. But what stood out most was how well he listened and asked clarifying questions before jumping into code. Bringing him on board will immediately **take routine ingestion monitoring off our plate**, which frees us up to focus on our warehouse migration. I definitely recommend moving him to the final offer stage."*
</details>

### Scenario 2: Responding to an Aggressive Headhunter on LinkedIn
* **Context**: A recruiter messages you offering a lucrative package at a fast-paced fintech startup, urging you to interview immediately while your current team is in the middle of a critical database cutover.
* **Your Task**: Write a courteous, professional refusal expressing your dedication to seeing your current project through and not leaving your teammates stranded.

<details>
<summary>💡 Click to View Model Response</summary>

> *"Hi Rachel, thank you for reaching out with this opportunity. The role and project sound fascinating. However, right now my full focus is dedicated to my current team. I **have been treated really well by my organization**, and we are currently right in the middle of an architectural overhaul. I take team commitments seriously, and I would never want to **leave my colleagues high and dry** during such an important phase. I wish you the best of luck with the search, and let's stay connected for the future."*
</details>
