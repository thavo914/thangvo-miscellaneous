## 🗣️ 4. Core Lesson Dialogues

### Dialogue 1 (Topic 1: Team Growth, Hiring & Loyalty Over Lunch)
*Setting: Thang and his teammate Lan are grabbing lunch to go near the office.*

**Lan:** Thanks for walking down with me, Thang. Are you getting your iced coffee **to go**?  
**Thang:** Yeah, let's grab it **to go** so we can eat in the park. **By the way**, have you heard back from engineering management about our headcount request?  
**Lan:** Not yet, but our manager admitted that **the time has come to hire a Senior Data Engineer**. We're managing ten pipelines with just the two of us, and sprint velocity is dropping.  
**Thang:** Exactly. **I've been meaning to talk to him about bringing someone on board** who can **take routine maintenance off our plate**. That candidate we interviewed last Tuesday seemed like **a great match for our culture**.  
**Lan:** I agree! He was sharp, humble, and communicated clearly. Honestly, someone asked me yesterday if you were planning to jump ship with all this workload, but I told them no.  
**Thang:** Definitely not. **I have been treated well by my current company**, especially when I needed flexible hours during our database migration. Management even **made a special exception** for my remote setup. **I would never want to leave them high and dry** in the middle of our warehouse overhaul.  
**Lan:** That shows genuine professional integrity, Thang. Let's push for that new hire so both of us can thrive.

### Dialogue 2 (Topic 2: Reviewing Vendor Estimates & Cloud Budgets)
*Setting: Thang and Alex, the Tech Lead, meet in a huddle room to review third-party tool proposals.*

**Alex:** Thanks for pulling the numbers together, Thang. Did the third-party Change Data Capture (CDC) vendor get back to you?  
**Thang:** Yes, **we reached out to their sales team for an estimate**, but the quote came in surprisingly high. If our transactional database grows by 30% this year, their licensing model is going to **break the bank**.  
**Alex:** Ouch. How high are we talking?  
**Thang:** Nearly four thousand dollars a month. Honestly, **I'm a little bit stressed that we won't be able to afford their cloud tier** on our departmental budget.  
**Alex:** That's a valid concern. What are our alternatives?  
**Thang:** **Instead of signing a multi-year vendor contract, self-hosting Debezium on our existing Kubernetes cluster is much more cost-effective**. It gives us **the most bang for our buck**, and it's already battle-tested **for internal use**.  
**Alex:** That sounds solid, but didn't their sales rep say their introductory discount expires this Friday?  
**Thang:** Exactly. **We had better act quickly** to decline the paid proposal politely and kick off our internal pilot before the sprint ends.  
**Alex:** Let's do it. I'll give you the green light to deploy the internal Debezium cluster this afternoon.
