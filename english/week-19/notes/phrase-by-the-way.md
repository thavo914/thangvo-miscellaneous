# 📘 Week 19 Curriculum Note: `by the way`

> **Target Week**: Week 19  
> **Type**: Adverb Phrase / Discourse Marker  
> **Core Concept**: Used to introduce a new subject into a conversation, to add an incidental thought, or to mention an extra piece of relevant information casually. *(Vietnamese: nhân tiện / tiện thể / à mà này)*

---

## 🌟 1. Core Explanation & Mechanics

This discourse marker is essential for smoothly transitioning topics in both spoken and written (Slack/email) communication without sounding abrupt.

* **Formula**: `[Main point]. By the way, [incidental thought/question]?`
* **Key Principle**: It signals that what follows is an afterthought or a secondary, but related, point. In writing, it is often abbreviated as BTW.

---

## 📚 2. Categorized Usages & Real-Life Examples

### Domain A: Tech Workplace & Code Reviews
* **Adding Context or Reminders**:
  * *Example*: *"Thanks for reviewing the SQL query so quickly—by the way, did you notice that the staging pipeline had a schema mismatch this morning?"*
  * *Example*: *"I've finished backfilling the user activity tables; by the way, don't forget that the cloud database is scheduled for maintenance tonight at 10 PM."*
* **Standup Updates**:
  * *Example*: *"We wrapped up the sprint planning meeting early; by the way, did anyone remember to update the JIRA tickets for the backlog?"*

### Domain B: Personal Life, Sports & Running
* **Running & Training Conversations**:
  * *Example*: *"I'm aiming for a sub-four-hour marathon in December; by the way, which running shoes did you end up buying for your long runs?"*
  * *Example*: *"My training pace felt remarkably smooth during this morning's 15 km tempo run—by the way, are you joining the local running club this weekend?"*
* **Football (Liverpool FC)**:
  * *Example*: *"Liverpool have three crucial Premier League fixtures coming up this month; by the way, are you heading over to Anfield for any of them?"*

## ⚠️ 3. Common Pitfalls & Vietlish Traps

| ❌ Common Mistake / Awkward Phrasing | ✅ Natural Native English | Why / Linguistic Reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *Conveniently, did you notice the error?* | ***By the way**, did you notice the error?* | "Conveniently" is a false friend translation of "nhân tiện / tiện thể" in this context. "By the way" is the natural conversational filler. |
| *By the way that...* | ***By the way,** [subject] [verb]...* | It stands alone as a phrase, usually separated by a comma; it doesn't take a "that" clause. |
