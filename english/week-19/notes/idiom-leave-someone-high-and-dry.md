# 📘 Week 19 Curriculum Note: `leave someone high and dry`

> **Target Week**: Week 19  
> **Type**: Idiom / Verb Phrase  
> **Core Concept**: To leave someone in a difficult or helpless situation, especially without the help or resources they were expecting. *(Vietnamese: bỏ mặc ai đó trong tình thế khó khăn / bơ vơ)*

---

## 🌟 1. Core Explanation & Mechanics

This idiom describes abandonment in a time of need. It metaphorically evokes a ship stranded on land after the tide has gone out.

* **Formula**: `leave [someone] high and dry`
* **Key Principle**: It emphasizes the unexpected nature of the abandonment and the vulnerability of the person left behind. It’s often used passively or actively with consequences.

---

## 📚 2. Categorized Usages & Real-Life Examples

### Domain A: Workplace & Engineering
* **Unexpected Resignations / Outages**:
  * *Example*: *"When the senior architect quit right before the major cloud migration, it left the rest of our data team completely high and dry."*
  * *Example*: *"We couldn't believe the vendor abruptly shut down the legacy service—it left our whole data engineering department high and dry just when we needed to run end-of-month reports."*
* **API & System Deprecation**:
  * *Example*: *"If we deprecate the old API without providing a clear migration guide, we're going to leave our downstream analysts high and dry when their dashboards break."*

### Domain B: Sports & Running
* **Races & Training**:
  * *Example*: *"I was relying on the aid station at mile 18 for hydration, but they had completely run out of water, leaving me high and dry in the sweltering heat."*
  * *Example*: *"My pacing partner cramped up and decided to drop out at the halfway mark, leaving me high and dry for the hardest part of the marathon."*
* **Football / Team Sports**:
  * *Example*: *"The referee's controversial decision to send off our key defender early in the first half really left the squad high and dry against a relentless attack."*

---

## ⚠️ 3. Common Pitfalls & Vietlish Traps

| ❌ Common Mistake / Awkward Phrasing | ✅ Natural Native English | Why / Linguistic Reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *They left me alone in a difficult situation.* | *They **left me high and dry**.* | While grammatically fine, the idiom "high and dry" is much more expressive and native for feeling abandoned when needing support. |
| *He left high and dry.* | *He **was left high and dry**.* / *They **left him high and dry**.* | "High and dry" is the state resulting from someone else's action; it must take an object (leave *someone*) or be passive. |
