# 📘 Week 22 Curriculum Note: Social Collocation & Phrasal Pattern — `Appetizers That Are Being Passed Around`

> **Target Week**: Week 22  
> **Type**: Social Collocation, Phrasal Verb & Reduced Relative Passive  
> **Core Concept**: Using `appetizers` (món khai vị / đồ ăn nhẹ đãi tiệc) combined with the phrasal verb `pass around` in the continuous passive (`(that are) being passed around`) to invite people warmly to eat and drink during office mixers, corporate dinners, networking events, and team celebrations. *(Vietnamese: Dùng cụm "appetizers (that are) being passed around" để mời đồng nghiệp/khách thưởng thức các món khai vị, đồ ăn nhẹ đang được bưng đi mời hoặc chuyền tay nhau quanh phòng tiệc).*

---

## 🌟 1. Core Explanation & Mechanics

### Grammatical Structure & Core Formula

$$\mathbf{[Polite\ Invitation]} + \mathbf{enjoy\ /\ help\ yourself\ to} + \mathbf{the\ appetizers\ /\ snacks} + \mathbf{(that\ are)\ being\ passed\ around}$$

* **Full Relative Clause**: *"Please enjoy the appetizers **that are being passed around**."*
* **Reduced Relative Clause (More Common in Spoken English)**: *"Feel free to enjoy the appetizers **being passed around**."*
* **Active Reflex (Host / Servers)**: *"The staff is **passing around** trays of appetizers."*

---

### Deep Dive: Breakdown of Key Components

#### 1. `Appetizers` (Noun, plural)
* **Definition**: Small portions of savory food served before a main meal, or served continuously throughout social cocktail parties, office happy hours, and company year-end mixers.
* **Spoken Shortening**: Native speakers, especially in US workplaces and casual dining, frequently shorten it to **`apps`** (*"Let's grab a table and order some apps"*).
* **Nuance vs. Other Food Terms**:
  * `Appetizers` / `Apps`: General term for starters or cocktail bites (US standard).
  * `Finger food` / `Light bites`: Food eaten directly with fingers without utensils (mini sandwiches, skewers, egg rolls).
  * `Hors d'oeuvres` (/ɔːr ˈdɜːrvz/): French loanword used for upscale or formal corporate receptions.
  * `Starters`: British / Commonwealth English standard for the first course of a meal.

#### 2. Phrasal Verb `Pass around` (Separable Phrasal Verb)
* **Meaning**: To offer or distribute something to everyone in a room, group, or meeting table so each person can take one or examine it.
* **Passive Voice Nuance (`are being passed around`)**:
  * Emphasizes the **ongoing action in progress**: waiters or colleagues are walking around the room carrying platters of food/drinks right now.
  * The presence of **`being`** is crucial: without `being` (*"appetizers that are passed around"*), it sounds like a generic permanent routine rather than food actively circulating in front of you.

## 📚 2. Categorized Usages & Real-Life Examples

### Domain A: Corporate Socials, Team Dinners & Networking Mixers
* **Event Opening / Welcome Announcement**:
  * *"Thank you all for coming tonight. Our key presentation will start in about 15 minutes; in the meantime, **please enjoy the appetizers that are being passed around**."*
* **Welcoming Late Arrivals at a Mixer**:
  * *"Hey, glad you could make it! Grab a drink from the bar, and **feel free to help yourself to the appetizers being passed around**."*
* **Casual Coworker Banter at a Celebration**:
  * *"Have you tried those smoked salmon bites **they're passing around**? They're really good."*
* **Hosting an Informal Office Celebration / Friday Drinks**:
  * *"We have some **passed appetizers** coming out from the kitchen, so don't be shy—dig in!"*

### Domain B: Workplace Meetings & Team Collaboration (`Pass around`)
* **Sharing Meeting Handouts & Agendas**:
  * *"I'm going to **pass around the agenda** and the updated project timeline so everyone has a physical copy."*
* **Engineering Architecture Review / RFC**:
  * *"Before we finalize this database migration, let's **pass this draft design around the team** for quick feedback."*
* **Sign-Up Sheets & Attendance Logs**:
  * *"Could you please sign your name and **pass the attendance sheet around the table**?"*
* **Hardware & Security Key Sharing**:
  * *"We have one physical test device; let's **pass it around** so each engineer can verify the build."*

### Domain C: Daily Life, Sports Gatherings & Personal Outings
* **Watching Football at a Sports Pub (Liverpool Matchday)**:
  * *"We grabbed a booth, ordered a pitcher of beer, and asked the kitchen for a basket of **appetizers** to share before kickoff."*
* **Family Dinners & Potlucks**:
  * *"Could you **pass that bowl of salad around** so everyone can get a serving?"*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Polite Host / MC (Formal)** | `enjoy the appetizers that are being passed around` | Formal announcements, company galas, client receptions | *"Please **enjoy the appetizers that are being passed around** by our catering team."* |
| **Warm Casual Host (Modern Office)** | `help yourselves to the appetizers / bites coming around` | Warm, approachable tone during team happy hours and meetups | *"Make yourselves at home and **help yourselves to the appetizers coming around**."* |
| **Rapid Spoken Reflex** | `grab some apps / grab some bites` | ⚡ Fast, punchy daily speech among close coworkers and friends | *"Let's **grab some apps** while we wait for the rest of the team to show up."* |
| **Colloquial Idiomatic Reflex** | `doing the rounds` | Extremely common native phrasing when drinks or snacks circulate | *"There are mini sliders **doing the rounds**, so keep an eye out for the tray."* |
| **Workplace Paperwork Reflex** | `pass around [the document / sheet]` | Handing physical or shared items around a conference table | *"I'll **pass around** the printouts of today's architecture diagram."* |

## ⚠️ 4. Common Pitfalls & Vietlish Traps

| ❌ Common Mistake / Awkward Phrasing | ✅ Natural Native English | Why / Linguistic Reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *Please eat the open-food passing around.* | *Please enjoy the **appetizers being passed around**.* | ❌ **Literal Translation Trap**: Dịch từ "món khai vị" thành "open food". Trong tiếng Anh, phải dùng `appetizers`, `starters`, hoặc `finger food`. |
| *Enjoy the appetizers are being passed around.* | *Enjoy the appetizers **that are being passed around*** OR *Enjoy the appetizers **being passed around**.* | ❌ **Lỗi ngữ pháp 2 động từ chính**: Một câu không thể có 2 động từ chính đi liền kề mà không có đại từ quan hệ (`that/which`) hoặc rút gọn mệnh đề quan hệ dạng V-ing (`being passed around`). |
| *Please enjoy the appetizers that passed around.* | *Please enjoy the appetizers that **are being passed around**.* | ❌ **Lỗi sai thì & dạng bị động**: Thức ăn không thể tự "passed around". Nếu đang có người bưng đi mời lúc nói, bắt buộc phải dùng Hiện tại tiếp diễn bị động `are being passed around`. |
| *He passed out the food to everyone.* | *He **passed around** the food / appetizers to everyone.* | ⚠️ **Nhầm lẫn Phrasal Verb nghiêm trọng**: `pass out` có nghĩa là **ngất xỉu** hoặc **say bất tỉnh** (hoặc phát bài thi từng người). Khi chuyền thức ăn quanh bàn hoặc bưng đồ ăn vòng quanh phòng, từ chuẩn xác là `pass around`. |
