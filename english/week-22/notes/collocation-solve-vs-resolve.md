# 📘 Week 22 Curriculum Note: Verbal Distinction & Workplace Collocations — `Solve` vs. `Resolve`

> **Target Week**: Week 22  
> **Type**: Verbal Distinction, Problem-Solving Collocations & Workplace Phrasing  
> **Core Concept**: Distinguishing between `solve` (finding a concrete answer or technical fix for an equation, bug, or puzzle) and `resolve` (settling conflicts, disputes, or closing Jira incident tickets), alongside natural workplace phrasing for team capacity (`new hires`, `tackle an issue`, `take pressure off`). *(Vietnamese: Phân biệt bản chất giữa "solve" - tìm ra lời giải/fix lỗi kỹ thuật và "resolve" - dàn xếp bất đồng/đóng ticket sự cố, kết hợp phản xạ tự nhiên khi nói về nhân sự mới "new hires" giải quyết áp lực công việc).*

---

## 🌟 1. Core Explanation & Mechanics

### The Core Cognitive Distinction

```
[SOLVE]   ---> Finding the correct answer / technical fix (Lời giải kỹ thuật, đáp án triệt để)
               Focus: The "How" and "Why" — producing a Solution.
               Objects: math problems, puzzles, mysteries, bugs, bottlenecks, memory leaks.

[RESOLVE] ---> Reaching an agreement / settlement / closure (Dàn xếp ổn thỏa, hòa giải, đóng trạng thái)
               Focus: The "Agreement" and "Closure" — producing a Resolution.
               Objects: conflicts, disputes, disagreements, complaints, Git merge conflicts, incident tickets.
```

### Key Principles & Nuances:

1. **`Solve` + Technical / Logical Problems**:
   * You **solve** something that has an objective, logical answer waiting to be discovered.
   * *Formula*: `[Subject] + solved + [bug / puzzle / technical bottleneck / equation]`
   * *Example*: *"Our senior engineer spent the morning tracking down and **solving the memory leak** in the Spark executor."*

2. **`Resolve` + Human Friction / Operational States**:
   * You **resolve** something involving multiple parties, differing opinions, friction, or an open ticket status that needs to be officially closed.
   * *Formula*: `[Subject] + resolved + [conflict / dispute / disagreement / ticket / complaint]`
   * *Example*: *"The tech lead stepped in to **resolve the architectural disagreement** between the backend and data teams."*
   * *Git Collocation*: *"We need to **resolve merge conflicts** before pushing to `main`."*

3. **Workplace Vocabulary Upgrade: `new employees` $\rightarrow$ `new hires`**:
   * Instead of the stiff, textbook phrase *"new employees"*, native professionals and tech teams almost exclusively say:
     * **`the new hires`** (nhân sự mới được tuyển vào)
     * **`bringing on new hires`** (tuyển thêm người mới về đội)
     * **`onboarding new team members`** (hướng dẫn hội nhập cho thành viên mới)

## 📚 2. Categorized Usages & Real-Life Examples

### Domain A: Data Engineering & Systems Troubleshooting
* **Solving Bugs & Performance Bottlenecks (`Solve`)**:
  * *"We finally **solved the latency issue** by indexing the query filter columns in Trino."*
  * *"Can anyone help me **solve this PySpark serialization error**?"*
* **Resolving Incident Tickets & System Outages (`Resolve`)**:
  * *"The on-call engineer mitigated the outage and **resolved the Sev-1 incident ticket** within 30 minutes."*
  * *"Once the database replica resyncs, we can **resolve the monitoring alerts**."*
* **Git Version Control & Schema Conflicts (`Resolve`)**:
  * *"I pulled the latest changes from `origin/main`, but I have three merge conflicts to **resolve** in the DDL scripts."*

### Domain B: Workplace Collaboration, Team Capacity & Deadlines
* **New Hires & Team Pressure (`tackle` / `take pressure off`)**:
  * *"I think having the **new hires** on board will help us **tackle this backlog**."*
  * *"Bringing on two **new hires** this quarter will really **take some pressure off** the on-call rotation."*
* **Resolving Disagreements & Setting Alignment (`Resolve`)**:
  * *"Let's hop on a quick 5-minute huddle to **resolve this conflict** before the sprint ends."*
  * *"We held a sync with the product manager to **resolve our conflicting priorities**."*

### Domain C: Daily Life, Customer Service & Personal Friction
* **Daily Brain Teasers & Puzzles (`Solve`)**:
  * *"He enjoys **solving the daily Wordle** or crossword puzzle with his morning coffee."*
* **Customer Service & Disputes (`Resolve`)**:
  * *"The airline representative offered travel vouchers to **resolve the customer's lost baggage complaint**."*

## 💡 3. Proactive Native Alternatives & Variations

| Style / Register | Expression / Collocation | Practical Nuance / When to Use | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Descriptive / Formal** | `solve the problem` | Standard, universal; but can sound repetitive or like textbook English | *"We must **solve this problem** quickly."* |
| **Punchy Spoken Reflex (Action)** | `tackle this issue` | ⚡ Energetic, active, highly natural in standups and sprint planning | *"The new hires will help us **tackle this issue**."* |
| **Conversational Phrasal Verb** | `sort this out` / `sort it out` | Friendly, everyday spoken English for untangling a messy situation | *"Don't worry about the scheduling mess; we'll **sort it out**."* |
| **Analytical Phrasal Verb** | `figure it out` | Focuses on the cognitive process of deducing or discovering the solution | *"Give me twenty minutes with the logs and I'll **figure it out**."* |
| **Negotiation Phrasal Verb** | `iron out [discrepancies / kinks]` | Resolving small frictions, mismatches, or disagreements smoothly | *"We just need to **iron out** a few details with the vendor."* |
| **Team Capacity Idiom** | `take pressure off [the team]` | Explaining the direct human benefit of additional headcount | *"Onboarding new hires will **take pressure off** our seniors."* |

## ⚠️ 4. Common Pitfalls & Vietlish Traps

| ❌ Common Mistake / Awkward Phrasing | ✅ Natural Native English | Why / Linguistic Reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *I solved the disagreement with my manager.* | *I **resolved the disagreement** with my manager.* | ❌ **Sai bản chất**: Bất đồng ý kiến không phải bài toán logic để tìm "lời giải" (`solve`), mà là xung đột cần sự hòa giải / dàn xếp êm đẹp (`resolve`). |
| *Please resolve this math equation.* | *Please **solve this math equation**.* | ❌ **Sai bản chất**: Phương trình toán học cần tìm ra ẩn số $x, y$ (solution), không thể "dàn xếp" (`resolve`) được. |
| *We must resolve the bug in the Spark job.* | *We must **solve / fix / track down the bug** in the Spark job.* | ⚠️ **Hơi gượng gạo**: Với lỗi code cụ thể, kỹ sư nói `fix the bug` hoặc `solve the bug`. Ta chỉ dùng `resolve` khi nói về `resolve the ticket / incident` (đóng ticket báo lỗi). |
| *The new employees will solve this.* | *The **new hires** will help us **tackle / sort this out**.* | ⚠️ **Textbook / Vietlish**: "New employees" nghe nặng tính hành chính/HR; trong nhóm làm việc thực tế, người bản xứ luôn nói `new hires` hoặc `new team members`. |

## 🎯 5. Lesson Integration Blueprint (For `/generate-english-lesson`)

### A. Grammar & Slot-Filling Formulas:
* **Formula 1 (Capacity & New Hires)**:
  $$\text{[Subject: Bringing on / Having]} + \text{the new hires (on board)} + \text{will help us [tackle / sort out]} + \text{[Target Issue / Backlog]}.$$
  * *Engineering Example*: *"Having the new hires on board will help us tackle the pipeline migration backlog much faster."*
* **Formula 2 (Resolving System / Team Friction)**:
  $$\text{We need to resolve the [conflicting requirements / merge conflicts / incident ticket]} + \text{before we can [Proceed / Deploy]}.$$
  * *Engineering Example*: *"We need to resolve the Git merge conflicts in the dbt models before we can deploy to staging."*

### B. Practice Drills:
* **Drill 1 (Standup Update on Team Headcount & Backlog)**:
  * *Vietnamese*: *"Tôi nghĩ việc có thêm các bạn nhân sự mới vào đội sẽ giúp chúng ta xử lý dứt điểm đống backlog này và giảm bớt áp lực cho đợt trực ban tới."*
  * *Model Native English*: *"I think having the new hires on board will really help us tackle this backlog and take some pressure off the next on-call rotation."*
* **Drill 2 (Differentiating Solve vs. Resolve in Post-Mortem)**:
  * *Vietnamese*: *"Chúng tôi đã tìm ra nguyên nhân và fix lỗi rò rỉ bộ nhớ lúc 2 giờ chiều, sau đó chính thức đóng ticket sự cố lúc 3 giờ."*
  * *Model Native English*: *"We solved the memory leak root cause at 2 PM, and then officially resolved the incident ticket by 3 PM."*
