## 💡 2. Detailed Breakdown of Newly Suggested Additions

### A. Topic 1 Enriched Additions (Logistics, Availability & Customer Exceptions)
* **`track down a package/ticket`** *(tìm ra / lần theo dấu vết bưu kiện)*: Complements `expecting a delivery`. In office English, natives say *"Let me track that down for you"* instead of the stiff *"I will search for the status"*.
* **`keep an eye out for`** *(để mắt canh chừng / chờ đón)*: Pairs naturally with `sometime tomorrow afternoon` (e.g., *"Keep an eye out for the courier around 2 PM"*).
* **`work around [constraint]`** *(tìm cách xử lý linh hoạt / đi đường vòng)*: Complements `extenuating circumstance` and `make an exception`. Avoids textbook *"circumvent"*.
* **`held up in traffic`** *(bị giữ chân / kẹt cứng giữa đường)*: Natural native spoken alternative to *"stuck in traffic"*, pairing with `He said he's on his way`.

### B. Topic 2 Enriched Additions (Payment Systems, Permissions & Operational Fixes)
* **`integrate payment gateway / process transactions`** *(tích hợp cổng thanh toán / xử lý giao dịch)*: Complements `receive payments via` and `card scanners/readers`.
* **`tackle the root cause`** *(xử lý tận gốc nguyên nhân)*: Direct contrast to surface-level patch fixes; pairs perfectly with `solve vs. resolve`.
* **`run into a permission error / access denial`** *(gặp lỗi phân quyền truy cập)*: Complements `locked out of [system]` and `approval/denial word families`.

## 🎙️ 3. Spoken Practice & Roleplay Plan

* **Day 2 Speaking Reflex (60s Pitch / Voice Simulation)**:
  - *Context*: A client calls demanding urgent delivery before 12:00 PM, but warehouse policy requires 24 hours. The learner must use `can't normally do this`, `extenuating circumstance`, and `put a special note` to resolve it politely without making unauthorized promises.
* **Day 3 Vietnamese-to-English Transcreation Dialogue**:
  - *Scenario*: Two event organizers frantic about missing conference badges and presentation materials. One colleague is `held up in traffic`, while the other must `drop off` the spare flash drive and explain that the keynote speaker `was supposed to arrive an hour ago`.
* **Day 5 Tech War Room & S-T-A-R Incident Response**:
  - *Scenario*: Payment webhook failure on production. Multiple checkout transactions are failing `for the fifth month in a row` after a scheduled deployment. The on-call data engineer is `locked out of the production bastion host`.
  - *S-T-A-R Drill*: Describe a time you had to `resolve` a cross-team conflict regarding delayed deliverables (`a couple weeks behind schedule`) and execute hard changes (`if that's what needs to be done`).
