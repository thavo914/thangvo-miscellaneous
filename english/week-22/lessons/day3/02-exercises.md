## 🎙️ Block 1: Warm-up Reflex & Shadowing (20–25 Mins)

### Part A: Speaking Reflex — Reverse Shadowing (15 Mins)
* **Instructions**: Read each Vietnamese thought aloud, then immediately say the natural English equivalent within 3–5 seconds without translating word-for-word.
1. *"Anh ấy vừa gọi bảo là đang trên đường tới rồi."*  
   → English: *"He just called and said he's on his way."*
2. *"Đường cao tốc tắc cứng rồi, không có cách nào đi vòng qua được."*

## 🇻🇳➡️🇬🇧 Block 2: Conversation Transcreation Drill (35–40 Mins)

### The Workplace Scenario (8 Turns)
Read this dialogue between two project coordinators on the morning of a major partner kickoff meeting. Transcreate it into natural B1–B2 Plain English:

* **Turn 1 (Huy)**: *"Thảo ơi, tài liệu in và thẻ tên khách mời David mang tới chưa? Khách sắp vào hội trường rồi."*  
* **Turn 2 (Thảo)**: *"Chưa Huy ơi. David vừa gọi cho em lúc nãy, bảo là anh ấy đang bị kẹt xe cứng ngắc ở cầu Sài Gòn."*  
* **Turn 3 (Huy)**: *"Trời đất, theo kế hoạch là anh ấy phải thả tập tài liệu đó ở bàn tiếp tân lúc 8 giờ mà!"*  
* **Turn 4 (Thảo)**: *"Vâng, nhưng có một vụ va quẹt trên cầu nên hoàn toàn không có cách nào đi vòng qua chỗ kẹt xe được."*  
* **Turn 5 (Huy)**: *"Thế bây giờ anh ấy đang ở đâu rồi? Liệu có kịp trước khi hội nghị bắt đầu lúc 8 giờ rưỡi không?"*  
* **Turn 6 (Thảo)**: *"Anh ấy vừa bảo là qua khỏi cầu rồi và đang rẽ vào đường chính. Em tin chắc là anh ấy sẽ tới trước khi chúng ta cần phát tài liệu."*  
* **Turn 7 (Huy)**: *"May quá. Trong lúc chờ anh ấy, em với anh đi kiểm tra lại micro và màn hình chiếu đi."*  
* **Turn 8 (Thảo)**: *"Ok anh, David vừa bước vào cửa là em sẽ chạy ra lấy tài liệu ngay."*

### Model Native English Transcreation:
* **Huy**: *"Thao, has David dropped off the printed handouts and guest badges yet? Guests are already heading into the auditorium."*
* **Thao**: *"Not yet, Huy. David called a few minutes ago and said he's stuck in gridlock on the bridge."*
* **Huy**: *"Oh man, he was supposed to drop off those handouts at the reception desk by 8:00 AM!"*
* **Thao**: *"I know, but there was a minor collision on the bridge, so there was no way to bypass the traffic jam."*
* **Huy**: *"Where is he right now? Do you think he can make it before the opening remarks start at 8:30?"*
* **Thao**: *"He said he just crossed the bridge and is turning onto the main avenue. I'm sure he'll get here by the time we need them."*
* **Huy**: *"What a relief. While we wait, let's double-check the microphones and projector display."*
* **Thao**: *"Sounds good. As soon as David walks in, I'll grab the handouts and head out to the front desk."*

### Contrastive Linguistic Notes:
1. *"Khách sắp vào hội trường rồi"*: Translate naturally as *"Guests are already heading into the auditorium"* instead of stiff *"Guests are going to enter the room"*.
2. *"theo kế hoạch là anh ấy phải..."*: Use `he was supposed to drop off` instead of *"according to plan he must"*.
3. *"hoàn toàn không có cách nào..."*: Use `there was no way to bypass` instead of *"completely had no way"*.
</details>

### Speaking Part 2 Cue Card (5W1H Framework)
* **Prompt**: Describe an occasion when traffic or a transport issue caused you to be late for an important event.
* **Model 90-Second Speech (5W1H Flow)**:
  > *"I’d like to talk about a time last year when I was caught in severe traffic on the way to our quarterly company town hall.  
  > **Who**: I was traveling with our team’s senior backend engineer.  
  > **What happened**: We **were supposed to** deliver the kickoff presentation for our new data platform.  
  > **Where & When**: It happened on a rainy Monday morning on the main expressway leading to district 1.  
  > **Why**: An overturned delivery truck blocked two lanes, and **there was no way to bypass the traffic jam**. We were completely **held up in traffic** for nearly forty minutes.  
  > **How it ended**: Luckily, my teammate called our project lead and **said we were on our way**. We managed to take a back alley as soon as we exited, and we arrived just **by the time our presentation slot started**. It was stressful, but everything worked out in the end."*

## 🧩 Block 4: Structural Drills & Pitfall Correction (15–20 Mins)

### Spot the Mistake:
1. *"He just called me and said he was on his way, so wait a few minutes."* *(Context: He is currently driving right now).*
2. *"I'm sure the report will be ready by the time the manager will arrive."*
3. *"There was no way to bypass because of the heavy traffic jam."*

<details>
<summary>💡 Click to View Corrections & Explanations</summary>

1. **Correction**: *"He just called me and said **he is on his way**, so wait a few minutes."*  
   *Explanation*: Since the action is actively continuing right now, present relevance preserves the present tense (`is`).
2. **Correction**: *"I'm sure the report will be ready by the time the manager **arrives**."*  
   *Explanation*: After time conjunctions like `by the time`, always use Simple Present, never future `will`.
3. **Correction**: *"There was no way to **bypass the traffic jam**."*  
   *Explanation*: `Bypass` is a transitive verb that directly takes the obstacle as its object (*bypass the traffic jam / bypass the security check*).
</details>
