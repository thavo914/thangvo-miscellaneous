### Part A: Spoken Technical Warm-up (15 Mins)
* **Objective**: Explain the checkout options of your company or an e-commerce platform you frequently use aloud in 60 seconds without long hesitations.
* **Target expressions to weave in**:
  * `reach out regarding`
  * `receive payments via`
  * `have the option to`
* **Sample Warm-up Delivery**:
  > *"When customers visit our checkout page, we want them to **have the option to** pay using multiple methods. On mobile, **it is possible to receive payments via** instant QR codes and digital wallets. For retail shops, we use **card scanners and readers** connected to our core billing system..."*

4. **Red-pen check**: Compare against the transcript and highlight missed prepositions (`via`, `regarding`, `at`) and weak contractions.

### Drill 2: Spot the Pitfall (Quantifier & Preposition Traps)
Find and fix the error in each sentence:
1. *"Almost people who shop on our website prefer paying with bank transfers."*
2. *"I am writing to reach out regarding to the small business loan."*
3. *"My uncle has been doing a retail business for over ten years."*

<details>
<summary>💡 Click to View Explanations & Corrections</summary>

1. **Correction**: *"**Most people** who shop on our website prefer paying with bank transfers."* *(Or: *"Almost all people..."*)*  
   *Explanation*: `Almost` is an adverb and cannot modify a noun directly. Use `most people` or `almost all people`.
2. **Correction**: *"I am writing to reach out **regarding the small business loan**."*  
   *Explanation*: `Regarding` is already a preposition; do not add `to` or `about` after it.
3. **Correction**: *"My uncle has been **running a retail business** for over ten years."*  
   *Explanation*: English speakers `run a business` or `own a business`, not `do a business`.
</details>
