## 🎙️ Block 1: Warm-up Reflex & Shadowing (20–25 Mins)

### Part A: Speaking Reflex — The Daily Commentator (15 Mins)
* **Objective**: Look around your desk, imagine receiving new office or tech gear today, and narrate your setup out loud in real time for 60–90 seconds without pausing.
* **Target Expressions to weave in**:
  * `expecting a delivery`
  * `should be back by`
  * `keep an eye out for`
* **Prompt to Speak Aloud**:
  > *"Right now, I'm setting up my work station for the morning. I'm **expecting a delivery** of some cables, so I need to **keep an eye out for** the courier. I have a sync at noon, but I **should be back by** 1:30 PM..."*

### Part B: Interactive Shadowing & Pacing (10 Mins)
Read these two key lines aloud 3–5 times. Pay attention to the rhythm slashes `/` (breathing pauses) and **CAPITALIZED** stress markers:
1. *"I was CALLING to check on an order / that was placed under the name MINH VO."*
2. *"I have a lunch meeting at twelve-thirty, / but I should be BACK by two P.M."*

### Drill 2: Spot the Pitfall (Error Correction)
Find and correct the mistake in each sentence:
1. *"I am calling because I am expecting for an important package today."*
*Correct*: I was calling because I'm expecting an important package today.
2. *"Can you check the order? It is placed with my name, Thang Vo."*
*Correct*: Can you check the order? It is placed under my name, Thang Vo.
3. *"I will be away from my desk until 3:00 PM, but I will return until 3:30 PM."*
*Correct*: I will be away from my desk until 3:00 PM, but I will return by 3:30 PM.

<details>
<summary>💡 Click to View Explanations & Corrections</summary>

1. **Correction**: *"I am calling because I am **expecting an important package** today."*  
   *Explanation*: `Expect` is a transitive verb; do not add the preposition `for`.
2. **Correction**: *"Can you check the order? It is placed **under** my name, Thang Vo."*  
   *Explanation*: Official records and reservations exist *under* a name.
3. **Correction**: *"I will be away from my desk until 3:00 PM, but I will return **by** 3:30 PM."*  
   *Explanation*: Returning is a single point of completion, so use `by` (no later than), not `until`.
</details>

## 🗣️ Block 4: Spoken Response Frameworks (25–30 Mins)

### 1. General Follow-up (5W1H Framework)
* **Prompt**: *"When you're waiting for an urgent package or hardware delivery at work, how do you handle your schedule?"*
* **Response Scaffold (Direct Answer + 2 Details)**:
  - **Direct Answer**: I usually schedule my calls and focus time around the estimated delivery window.
  - **Supporting Detail 1 (When/How)**: For instance, if the courier is arriving sometime in the morning, I make sure to stay near my desk.
  - **Supporting Detail 2 (Why)**: That way, I won't miss the delivery driver if he calls my phone.
* **Practice**: Speak your answer aloud using this 3-part flow in under 45 seconds.

### 2. Analytical Discussion (O-R-E-O Framework)
* **Prompt**: *"Do you think couriers should provide an exact delivery hour instead of a wide time window?"*
* **Scaffold**:
  - **O (Opinion)**: In my view, having a tighter time window is definitely better for busy professionals.
  - **R (Reason)**: The main reason is that waiting around for an entire afternoon disrupts your deep work.
  - **E (Example)**: For example, yesterday I had to postpone a code review because I wasn't sure when the package would drop off.
  - **O (Outcome)**: Overall, tighter delivery slots help everyone plan their workday much more effectively.
* **Practice**: Speak aloud following the O-R-E-O steps.
