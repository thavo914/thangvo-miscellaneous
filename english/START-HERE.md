# 🚀 English Learning Project - START HERE

Welcome to your 6-month English mastery journey! This structured program will transform your communication skills from hesitant to natural and professional.

## 📋 Quick Overview

- **Duration**: 6 months (24 weeks)
- **Daily Commitment**: 2 hours
- **Philosophy**: "Muscle Confusion" - Different focus each day for comprehensive development
- **Start Date**: February 10, 2026 (Week 1)
- **Target**: Natural, professional English communication in Data Engineering & Hospitality contexts

## 🗓️ The Weekly Cycle

Each week follows this proven pattern:

| Day           | Focus                   | What You'll Do                                      |
| ------------- | ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **Monday**    | Input & Shadowing       | Master pronunciation and intonation                 |
| **Tuesday**   | Vocabulary Mining       | Build contextual vocabulary through sentence mining |
| **Wednesday** | Role-play & AI Practice | Real-world simulation with AI feedback              |
| **Thursday**  | Deep Listening          | Train your ear with transcription exercises         |
| **Friday**    | Paraphrasing            | Develop flexible expression to avoid word blocks    |
| **Saturday**  | Mini-Project            | Synthesize the week's learning into a deliverable   |
| **Sunday**    | Passive Immersion       | Relax with English content (movies, shows)          |

## 📚 Your Learning Roadmap

### Month 1: Foundation (Weeks 1-4)
Build professional identity and basic hospitality communication

### Month 2: Industry Knowledge (Weeks 5-8)

Capstone projects and mock interviews

## 🎯 How to Use This System

### Daily Routine

1. **Check your current week**: Open the week schedule (e.g., `week-1-schedule.md`)
2. **Follow the day's structure**: Each day has 3 blocks (0-30, 30-90, 90-120 minutes)
3. **Track your progress**: Update `progress-tracker.md` after each session
4. **Review and reflect**: Log challenges and achievements

### Weekly Routine

1. **Monday**: Start fresh with shadowing exercises
2. **Throughout the week**: Add vocabulary to Anki daily
3. **Saturday**: Complete your mini-project
4. **Sunday**: Enjoy passive immersion without pressure
5. **End of week**: Review and prepare for next week's theme

## 💡 Success Tips

1. **Consistency over intensity**: 2 hours daily beats 10 hours on weekends
2. **Use sentence mining**: Never learn isolated words - always full sentences
3. **Embrace AI**: ChatGPT Voice Mode is your tireless practice partner
4. **Record yourself**: Weekly recordings reveal progress you can't see otherwise
5. **Don't skip Sunday**: Passive immersion builds intuition without burnout
