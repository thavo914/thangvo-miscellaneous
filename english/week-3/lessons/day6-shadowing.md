# Day 6: Shadowing (60 min)

## Stage 1: Auditory Priming (5 min)
*Goal: Mental mapping of natural business speech rhythm.*

**Instructions**:
- Play the **Primary Shadowing Clip** (Ethan Mollick, Part 4) 3-5 times.
- **Transcript closed. No speaking.**

---

## Stage 2: Transcribed Echoing (15 min)
*Goal: Articulatory precision for complex business terms.*

**Instructions**:
- Open `lesson-transcript.md` to Part 4.
- Pause after each logical thought group and repeat aloud.
- Repeat each sentence **3-5 times**.

### Focus Sentences
| Sentence | Feature to Copy |
| :--- | :--- |
| "There’s all these platforms th[a]t let you build..." | Speed: The word "PLATFORMS" is stressed; "all these" is fast. |
| "...build_en-tr[a]-pr[a]-NEU-ri-al ventures..." | Articulation: Master the 5-syllable "entrepreneurial". |
| "The growth (o)f things like crowdfunding..." | Schwa: The word "OF" is nearly invisible /əv/. |
| "...entrepreneurship more accessible..." | Stress: /ˌɒntrəprəˈnɜːʃɪp/ - primary stress on the /-nɜː-/. |
| "...cost (o)f launching new businesses dropped..." | Intonation: A slight falling intonation on "dropped". |

---

## Stage 3: Synchronous Shadowing with Text (20 min)
*Goal: Synchronization and pace control.*

**Instructions**:
- Play the clip on a loop.
- Read simultaneously with the audio, trailing by 0.5 seconds.
- Repeat **10+ times**. Don't stop if you miss a word—keep the rhythm!

---

## Stage 4: Blind Shadowing (15 min)
*Goal: Pure auditory-motor mastery.*

**Instructions**:
- **CLOSE the transcript.**
- Shadow purely by ear.
- Record 2 attempts.

---
## Day 6 Deliverables
- [ ] 10+ Synchronous reps.
- [ ] 2 Blind shadowing recordings.

> **Key Insight for Today**: Shadowing the "experts" makes their confidence rub off on your English. If you can shadow at their speed, you can speak in a meeting at their speed.
