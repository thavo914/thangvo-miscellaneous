## Structure 1: Defining a Group
- **Quote**: *"millennials - the name given to the current generation of young people..."* (Rob)
- **Function**: To categorize or define a group of people or objects using a passive descriptive phrase.
- **Pattern**: `[Group Name] - the name given to [Category/Description].`
- **Examples**:
  - *"Full-stack developer - the name given to engineers who work on both front-end and back-end."* (Work context)
  - *"Crowdfunding - the name given to the process of raising money online."*

## Structure 2: Describing Dissatisfaction
- **Quote**: *"Some of the millennial generation are dissatisfied with the old ways of doing things..."* (Sam)
- **Function**: To express professional disagreement or a need for change.

- **Examples**:

## Structure 3: Explaining Origins/Causes
- **Quote**: *"...asked business professor Ethan Mollick to explain how this situation came about."* (Rob)

## Structure 4: Listing Opportunities
- **Quote**: *"Entrepreneurs see opportunities for products and services not being supplied by existing companies..."* (Sam)
- **Function**: To identify a gap or a specific chance for improvement.
- **Pattern**: `[Subject] see opportunities f[a]r [Project/Target] not be-ing [supplied/done] by [Competitor/Previous system].`
- **Examples**:
  - *"We see opportunities for automation not being explored by current software."* (Work context)
  - *"She sees opportunities for education not being provided by schools."*

## Structure 5: Intent and Growth
- **Quote**: *"start-ups - newly formed businesses intended to grow rapidly..."* (Sam)

- **Examples**:

## Structure 6: Caution Against Overemphasis
- **Quote**: *"...Charles Kenny cautions against overemphasising individual business over governments..."* (Rob)
- **Function**: To warn someone about focusing too much on one factor while ignoring another.
- **Pattern**: `[Person/Expert] cau-tions_a-gainst_o-ver-EM-pha-si-sing Word A_**O**-ver Word B.`
- **Examples**:
  - *"The architect cautions against overemphasising speed over security."* (Work context)
  - *"Scientists caution against overemphasising growth over quality of life."*

## Structure 7: Collective Necessity
- **Quote**: *"It’s not something that any one individual can do, it has to be a collective effort..."* (Charles Kenny)
- **Function**: To emphasize that a task is too big for a single person and requires teamwork.
- **Pattern**: `It’s not [something] th[a]t_**A**-ny **ONE** [individual] c[a]n **DO**, it **HAS** t[a] b[a]_a [COLLECTIVE EFFORT].`
- **Examples**:
  - *"Fixing the legacy system is not something any one developer can do; it has to be a collective effort."* (Work context)
