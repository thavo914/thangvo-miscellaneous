# Day 3: AI Simulation (60 min)

## Guidelines
- Roleplay as an **Entrepreneur**.
- AI Level: **B1-B2 Intermediate**.
- Request feedback on **Business Vocabulary** and **Persuasiveness**.

---

## Round 1: Pitching to an Investor (15 min)
**Role**: You are a young entrepreneur with a new 'start-up'. The AI is a potential investor.
**Prompt**:
> "I want to practice my business English. I am a millennial entrepreneur pitching a new start-up idea to you, a serious investor. My business provides a service that big companies currently ignore. I used crowdfunding for the first prototype, but now I need real investment. You are skeptical but curious. Start by asking me: 'Why should I invest in your start-up instead of a more established company?' Don't correct me during the chat. At the end, tell me if you would 'invest' based on my language and point out 3 business words I used correctly."

---

## Round 2: The Social Responsibility Debate (15 min)
**Role**: You believe businesses can solve world problems better than governments. The AI is a cynical economist.
**Prompt**:
> "You are a government economist who thinks individual businesses are not enough to fix the world. I am a 'smart, committed' young person who believes start-ups are the answer. We are debating whether 'collective effort' through government is better than individual 'entrepreneurship'. Challenge my view. Use the word 'corrupt' in your argument. After 3 exchanges, give me feedback on my ability to use formal linking words like 'however' and 'furthermore'."

---

## Round 3: Silicon Valley Role-play (20 min)
**Role**: You are job hunting at a tech firm. The AI is the manager.
**Prompt**:
> "Role-play a job interview. I am applying for a Data Engineering role at your fast-growing start-up in Silicon Valley. You are the hiring manager. You care about my technical skills but also my passion for 'quality of life' and 'social impact'. Ask me 3 behavioral questions. Evaluate my responses for 'startup culture' vocabulary and grammatical precision. Suggest 2 more natural phrases I could have used to sound more enthusiastic."

---

## Review (10 min)
1. Look at the "corrected" business phrases the AI gave you.
2. Record yourself saying them **3 times** each.

---
## Day 3 Deliverables
- [ ] Investor pitch (3+ exchanges).
- [ ] Economist debate completed.
- [ ] Startup interview completed.

> **Key Insight for Today**: AI is the perfect "safe" place to practice your elevator pitch. If the AI investor says 'No', you haven't lost money—you've just gained knowledge.
