# Lesson Overview: Millennials and business (Restructured)

## Metadata
- **Title**: Millennials and business
- **Source**: BBC Learning English (6 Minute English)
- **CEFR Level**: B1-B2 (Intermediate)
- **Topic**: The attraction of millennials to entrepreneurship and Social Responsibility.

## Learning Objectives
| Category | Objective |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Listening** | Understand the motivations behind millennial start-ups and the role of crowdfunding. |
| **Vocabulary** | Master 7 target words related to business, entrepreneurship, and social impact. |
| **Pronunciation** | Master connected speech in fast-paced business explanations. |
| **Speaking** | Discuss the pros and cons of individual entrepreneurship vs. government action. |
| **Thinking** | Directly associate business concepts with English terms. |

## Target Vocabulary
| Word | POS | Meaning | Episode Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **millennials** | noun | current generation of young people (aged 24-38) | "...that’s the dream for many **millennials**..." |
| **entrepreneur** | noun | someone who starts their own business | "...easier to become an **entrepreneur**..." |
| **start-ups** | noun | newly formed businesses intended to grow rapidly | "...led to tons of new **start-ups**." |
| **crowdfunding** | noun | getting funding via internet from many people | "...this can be solved with **crowdfunding**..." |
| **corrupt** | adjective | acting immorally/illegally for money/power | "...governments can be **corrupt**..." |
| **collective effort** | noun phrase | a group acting together for a common goal | "...it has to be a **collective effort**..." |
| **quality of life** | noun phrase | level of personal satisfaction and comfort | "...raise people’s **quality of life**..." |
