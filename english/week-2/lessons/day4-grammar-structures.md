## Structure 1: Hedged Opinion
- **Quote**: *"I wouldn’t say relaxing exactly, but I get really involved..."* (Georgina)
- **Function**: To provide a nuanced opinion without being too blunt or direct. It allows you to disagree slightly or refine your point.
- **Pattern**: `I wouldn't say [Adjective] exactly, but [Alternative opinion/feeling].`
- **Examples**:
  - *"I wouldn't say the database migration was easy exactly, but we finished on time."* (Work context)
  - *"I wouldn't say I'm an expert exactly, but I know how to use the FMRI scanner."*

## Structure 2: Definition in Speech
- **Quote**: *"‘Bibliotherapy’ is the prescription of books as a remedy to sickness."* (Neil)
- **Function**: To explain a complex or new term immediately after introducing it.
- **Pattern**: `[Term] is the [Simple definition/Category] of [Substance/Action].`
- **Examples**:
  - *"Data engineering is the architecture and maintenance of data pipelines."* (Work context)
  - *"Scanning is the rapid reading of text to find specific facts."*

## Structure 3: Time Contrast/Progress
- **Quote**: *"It has been around since 2013, when the UK charity... published a list..."* (Neil)
- **Function**: To anchor a current situation in a specific historical starting point.
- **Pattern**: `[Situation] has been around since [Year/Date], when [Historical Event].`
- **Examples**:
  - *"This legacy system has been around since 2018, when the company first expanded."* (Work context)
  - *"The reading group has been around since last Tuesday, when Neil suggested it."*

## Structure 4: The Conditional (Implicit Recommendation)
- **Quote**: *"if you’re just looking for information, you go fast, it’s very easy..."* (Philip Davis)
- **Function**: To describe a result that always or usually happens under certain conditions.
- **Pattern**: `If you [Do X], you [Get Result Y/Go Z].`
- **Examples**:
  - *"If you run the query without an index, it takes much longer."* (Work context)
  - *"If you read literature, your brain comes to life."*

## Structure 5: Passive Recommendation
- **Quote**: *"It's so successful that it's about to be extended to children as well."* (Georgina)
- **Function**: To announce a future expansion or change where the "agent" (who is doing it) is less important than the "object" (what is being extended).
- **Pattern**: `[Subject] is about to be [Past Participle] to [New target/group].`
- **Examples**:
  - *"The new dashboard is about to be deployed to the production server."* (Work context)
  - *"The bibliotherapy scheme is about to be extended to schools."*

## Structure 6: Describing Measured Results
- **Quote**: *"...the oxygen indicates again changes in the configuration of the brain..."* (Philip Davis)
- **Function**: To report scientific or technical findings based on a specific indicator.
- **Pattern**: `The [Indicator/Metric] indicates [Change/Result] in the [Object/System].`
- **Examples**:
  - *"The error log indicates a failure in the API connection."* (Work context)
  - *"The ECG indicates a leap in brain activity."*

## Structure 7: Concession and Focus
- **Quote**: *"Above all, that it’s not to do with scanning... but when literature begins to do something..."* (Philip Davis)
- **Function**: To emphasize the most important point by first rejecting a less important one.
- **Pattern**: `Above all, it's not [Point A], but [Point B].`
- **Examples**:
  - *"Above all, it's not just about the code, but about the data quality."* (Work context)
  - *"Above all, it's not to do with speed, but with focus."*
