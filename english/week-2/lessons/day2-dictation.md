## Phase 3: Drill (10 min)
*Goal: Neuromuscular habit. If you can't hear it, learn to say it.*

Pick 4 phrases from the clip and drill them 5 times each. Focus on the natural-speech version.

1. **Catenation**: "...working_at..."
   - *Natural*: /wɜːkɪŋæt/ (Link the 'ng' to 'a')
2. **Elision**: "Different parts"
   - *Natural*: /dɪfrən pɑːts/ (Skip the 't')
3. **Weak Form**: "It's not to do with"
   - *Natural*: /ɪts nɒt tə duː wɪð/ (The 'to' is a very short schwa)
4. **Rhythm**: "Above all..."
   - *Natural*: /əˈbʌv ɔːl/ (Stress the second syllable of 'above')
