# Day 3: Shadowing (60 min)

## Stage 1: Auditory Priming (5 min)
*Goal: Map the music of the speech before you try to sing it.*

**Instructions**:
- Play the **Primary Shadowing Clip** (Philip Davis section) 3-5 times.
- **NO transcript. NO speaking.**
- Close your eyes. Listen for the "hills and valleys" of his voice. Where does he speed up? Where does he emphasize?

---

## Stage 2: Transcribed Echoing (15 min)
*Goal: Slow down to speed up. Master the mechanics of the sentences.*

**Instructions**:
- Open `lesson-transcript.md` to Part 4.
- Play one sentence -> Pause -> Read aloud.
- Repeat each sentence **3-5 times** until you can say it without stumbling.

### Focus Sentences
| Sentence | Feature to Copy |
| :--- | :--- |
| "Above all, that it’s not to do with scanning..." | Contrast: "ABOVE all" (high stress) vs "not to do with" (reduced). |
| "...when literature begins to do something more complicated..." | Rhythm: The word "LITERATURE" has a specific British cadence. |
| "...in an area that emotionally you care about." | Catenation: "in_an_area", "care_about". |
| "The brain begins to work from different parts..." | Elision: Skip the 't' in "different". |
| "...you can see the brain coming to life..." | Melody: Rising pitch on "coming" and "life". |

---

## Stage 3: Synchronous Shadowing with Text (20 min)
*Goal: Overcome the urge to over-enunciate. Match the native pace.*

**Instructions**:
- Play the clip continuously.
- Read the transcript out loud **simultaneously** with the audio.
- Target a **0.5-second delay** (the "shadow").
- Repeat at least **10 times**.

### Common Mistakes
| Problem | Fix |
| :--- | :--- |
| You're falling behind the audio. | Use more schwas (weak forms). Don't say every sound clearly. |
| You're pronouncing the 't' too hard. | Practice the elision: "differen_ parts". |
| Your voice sounds flat/monotone. | Exaggerate the stress on the BOLD words in the transcript. |
| You stumble on "hemisphere". | Drill the word isolated x5: /ˈhemɪsfɪə/. |

---

## Stage 4: Blind Shadowing (15 min)
*Goal: Pure auditory-motor synchronization. No visual crutch.*

**Instructions**:
- **CLOSE the transcript.**
- Play the clip. Shadow it purely by ear.
- Record your last 2 attempts on your phone.

**Self-Evaluation**:
1. Did I match the rhythm? [ ]
2. Did I hit the primary stressed words? [ ]
3. Which word was the hardest to say at full speed? ________________

**Shadow the original, then say your version with the SAME rhythm.**
