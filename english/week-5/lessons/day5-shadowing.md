# Day 5 — Shadowing
**Episode**: No More Bosses | BBC 6 Minute English | B1-B2 Intermediate

---

## Primary Shadowing Clip
> **Target**: The Doug Kirkpatrick / Dina Newman exchange.
> **Start**: *"The first principle was that human beings should not use force or coercion..."*
> **End**: *"...they can be held accountable by anyone."*
> **Duration**: ~50 seconds
> **Why this clip?** Dense target vocabulary, a mix of two speakers, complex sentence rhythm, and a strong contrast between formal principle-stating and natural conversational challenge-response.

---

## Stage 1 — Auditory Priming (5 min)
> **No transcript. No writing. Eyes closed.**

1. Play the clip **3 times straight through**.
2. Focus only on **rhythm and music** — which syllables are stressed? Where do phrases speed up or slow down?
3. After the third play: Hum or tap the rhythm without any words.

**Notice:**
- Doug's measured, deliberate pace when stating principles ("The **first** principle was…")
- Dina's rising intonation in her challenge question ("Would it be **true** to say…?")
- Doug's confident, slightly faster pace when defending his position

---

## Stage 2 — Transcribed Echoing (15 min)
> Use the annotated transcript (`lesson-transcript.md`). Work **sentence by sentence**.

**Method:**
1. Play one sentence.
2. **Pause**.
3. Repeat the sentence aloud — match the speed, stress, and linking sounds.
4. Move to the next sentence only when this one feels natural.

**Focus points:**

| Phrase | Key Feature to Match |
| :--- | :--- |
| *"human beings should not use force or coercion"* | Stress on **force**, then **co-er-cion** (3 syllables: /kəʊˈɜːʃən/) |
| *"people should keep the commitments they make to each other"* | Linking: commitments_they, make_to_each |
| *"they are not subject to force and coercion"* | Weak form: "they are" → /ðər/ |
| *"they can be held accountable by anyone"* | Stress: **held** ac-**count**-able |

---

## Stage 3 — Synchronous Shadowing (20 min)
> **With text. 0.5-second delay after each word group. 10+ repetitions of the full clip.**

**Instructions:**
1. Play the audio and read along — speak simultaneously with the speaker, staying ~0.5 seconds behind.
2. Do NOT pause — keep going even when you fall behind. Re-engage on the next phrase.
3. Complete at least **10 full passes** of the clip.
4. After rep 5: Put one hand on your throat and feel the vibration — are you speaking at the same volume and energy as the speaker?

**Self-correction checklist:**
- [ ] Am I matching the speed of the connected speech?
- [ ] Am I blending words that should be linked? (e.g., *held_accountable*)
- [ ] Am I using the schwa correctly on unstressed syllables?
- [ ] Does my stress pattern match the speaker's? (strong beats on content words)

---
