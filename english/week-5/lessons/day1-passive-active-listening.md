# Day 1 — Passive + Active Listening & Dictation Draft A
**Episode**: No More Bosses | BBC 6 Minute English | B1-B2 Intermediate

---

## Phase 1 — Passive Listening (10–15 min)
> Play the episode 2–3 times as background audio. Do NOT take notes. Simply let the sounds, rhythm, and voices become familiar.

- Play while doing something light: walking, stretching, tidying your desk.
- Notice the overall mood and pace of the speakers.
- Don't pause. Don't rewind. Just absorb.

---

## Phase 2 — Active Listening (25 min)

### Global Questions (First listen — no pausing)
Listen once and answer from memory:

1. What kind of company is featured in this episode, and where is it based?
2. What word does Doug Kirkpatrick use instead of "bosses" or "rules"?

### Targeted Questions (Second listen — pause allowed)
Listen again more carefully and answer:

3. What were the **two principles** that Morning Star adopted as its governance rules?
4. According to Neil, what are the **risks** of having no bosses for some employees?
5. What is the estimated annual cost of excess management and bureaucracy to the US economy?
6. Why does Doug Kirkpatrick say he does not accept the phrase "left behind"?

### Reflection
After answering, write 1–2 sentences about what you found **most surprising** in this episode.

1. Listen to the clip **once all the way through** without writing.
2. Play again and write what you hear. **Maximum 3 replays.** Do NOT use the transcript.
3. You are writing a **rough draft** — spelling mistakes and gaps are expected.
