# Lesson Overview: No More Bosses (Restructured)

## Metadata
- **Title**: No More Bosses
- **Source**: BBC 6 Minute English
- **CEFR Level**: B1-B2 (Intermediate)
- **Topic**: Companies that have eliminated managers and adopted self-management – benefits, risks, and accountability.

## Learning Objectives
| Category | Objective |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Listening** | Understand how self-managing companies operate and what the real-world trade-offs are. |
| **Vocabulary** | Master 6 target words related to workplace structures, power, and personal responsibility. |
| **Pronunciation** | Master connected speech and emphatic stress in B1-B2 level opinion and discussion language. |
| **Speaking** | Confidently discuss workplace management styles, autonomy, and accountability. |
| **Thinking** | Connect the concept of self-management to your professional context in data engineering and casino operations. |

## Target Vocabulary
| Word | POS | Meaning | Episode Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **hierarchy** | noun | a system of organising people according to their level of importance | "most companies are **hierarchies** – systems of organising people according to their level of importance." |
| **coercion** | noun | the use of force to persuade someone to do something they do not want to do | "decisions are made by all employees equally without **coercion**." |
| **commitment** | noun | a promise or firm decision to do something | "employees must keep their **commitments** – promises or firm decisions to do something." |
| **left behind** | phrasal verb | remain at a lower level than others because you are not as quick to improve or progress | "some employees may be **left behind** – remain at a lower level...because they are not as quick to develop." |
| **take full advantage (of)** | phrase | make good use of an opportunity to progress or achieve a goal | "other workers enjoy managing themselves and **take full advantage** of the system." |
| **be held accountable** | phrase | accept responsibility for the consequences of your actions | "if they don't act according to their commitments they can **be held accountable** by anyone." |
