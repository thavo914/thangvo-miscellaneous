## 🧩 Part 1: Vocabulary & Collocation Slot-Filling (20 Points — 2 Pts / Question)

*Fill in each blank with the exact missing word, preposition, or phrasal verb particle from Week 25.*

1. It's __________ a month __________ I subscribed __________ Phenix Stream, and their subscription prices have skyrocketed __________ the last year.
2. This age group gets online to seek __________ recommendations __________ household items.
3. The concert tickets sold out __________ five minutes __________ going __________ sale.
4. By the way, Ron has been a __________ fan of the band for years, and he's been trying to get his __________ on a ticket all week.
5. Please be mindful __________ your clothes and bags when walking around the museum, and we need to meet __________ here at 5:30.
6. Note that you should fix the power cord __________ place __________ twisting it clockwise.
7. I should've read the manual __________ the __________ place!
8. When __________ data producers push a breaking schema change without warning, all __________ data consumers suffer broken reports.
9. I would like to hire all senior roles __________ __________ the company, so it is a great opportunity to __________ your career.
10. If you would like to be __________ for a role, please email your resume to california@buzzmarketing.com __________ November 29th.

<details><summary>🔍 Part 1 Answer Key & Explanations</summary>

1. **been** / **since** / **to** / **in** *(Pattern: `It's been [Time] since + Past Simple`; `subscribe to`; `in the last year` with Present Perfect)*
2. **out** / **on** *(Phrasal verb: `seek out` = actively search for; collocation: `recommendations on [topic]`)*
3. **within** / **of** / **on** *(Pattern: `within [Time] of [V-ing]`; idiom: `go on sale` = launch for purchase)*
4. **diehard** / **hands** *(Collocations: `a diehard fan of`; `get one's hands on` with plural `hands`)*
5. **of** / **back** *(Fixed preposition: `be mindful of`; directional adverb: `meet back here` = regroup at starting point)*
6. **in** / **by** *(Collocation: `fix [something] in place` = secure firmly; method: `by + V-ing`)*
7. **in** / **first** *(Idiom at end of sentence: `in the first place` = đáng lẽ ngay từ đầu)*
8. **upstream** / **downstream** *(Data Engineering architecture pair: `upstream data producers` $\rightarrow$ `downstream data consumers`)*
9. **from** / **within** / **advance** *(Compound preposition: `hire from within the company`; collocation: `advance your career`)*
10. **considered** / **by** *(Passive voice: `be considered for a role`; deadline preposition: `by November 29th`)*

<details><summary>🔍 Part 2 Answer Key & Deconstructions</summary>

1. **Correction**: *"Cloud storage prices **have skyrocketed in the last year** (or **skyrocketed last year**), and we ran **a few commercials** on TV."*
   * **Why**: (1) `skyrocket` là nội động từ (intransitive verb) ➔ **tuyệt đối cấm chia bị động** (*were skyrocketed* ❌). (2) Trước danh từ số nhiều không có từ hạn định (`commercials`), dùng **`a few commercials`** (cấm có `of`).
2. **Correction**: *"Guess what **I got** here! She had to **give them up** because **she'll be hosting** a training seminar this weekend."*
   * **Why**: (1) Sau `Guess what`, mệnh đề danh từ giữ nguyên trật tự `S + V` (**cấm đảo trợ động từ** *did I get*). (2) Đại từ nhân xưng `them` bắt buộc phải **kẹp giữa** separable phrasal verb (`give them up`). (3) Dùng Tương lai tiếp diễn `will be hosting` cho lịch trình đã cố định từ trước.
3. **Correction**: *"According to **museum rules**, you are not allowed to touch any of the **artwork**, or any other **item** you see."*
   * **Why**: (1) Dùng Noun Adjunct `museum rules` chỉ quy định thể chế khách quan. (2) `artwork` là danh từ khối không đếm được (uncountable mass noun) ➔ **cấm thêm `-s`**. (3) Sau cụm bao trùm `or any other`, dùng danh từ đếm được số ít (`item`).
4. **Correction**: *"I **tried unplugging** the power cord and **plugging it back in**, but it just **won't** power on."*
   * **Why**: (1) Thử nghiệm một giải pháp xem có sửa được lỗi không phải dùng **`try + V-ing`** (`tried unplugging... and plugging`), không dùng `try to V0`. (2) Đại từ `it` đặt ngay sau động từ (`plugging it back in`). (3) Dùng modal `won't power on` để diễn tả máy móc "lì lợm từ chối lên nguồn" ngay lúc bấm.
5. **Correction**: *"If you would like **to be considered for a role from within** the company, you'll **complete** a short interview, **with the plan to start** training **at** the beginning of the year."*
   * **Why**: (1) Ứng viên phải dùng bị động `be considered for a role`. (2) Tuyển nội bộ là `from within the company`, không nói *inside*. (3) Dùng `complete an interview`, không dùng *make*. (4) `with the plan to + V0` + **`at`** `the beginning of the year`.

</details>
