# 🚀 Day 2: Concert Ticket Frenzy, Schedule Conflicts & Office Banter
> **Weekly Topic 1**: Data-Driven Campaign Strategy, Rapid Ticket Banter & High-EQ Compliance Briefings  
> **Today's Focus**: Opening lively conversations (`Guess what I got here!`), describing rapid timeframes (`within five minutes of going on sale`), expressing disbelief (`Don't tell me... from scalpers`), explaining pre-scheduled conflicts (`had to give them up because she'll be hosting...`), and inviting teammates (`a diehard fan`, `get his hands on`, `the three of us`).  
> **Total Time Budget**: 90–120 Minutes across 4 action blocks.

## 🎯 Block 1: Story Mission, Linearthinking Dissection & Pattern Activation (15–20 Mins)

### 📖 Today's Story Brief:
> *It's Tuesday at the office coffee machine. You walk up to your teammate Sarah holding three coveted concert tickets for a rock band whose show sold out within five minutes of going on sale. Sarah is shocked and assumes you paid crazy prices to scalpers. You explain that you actually got them at face value from Chloe in HR—she had to give them up because she'll be hosting a weekend training seminar. Then you suggest inviting Ron from Data Engineering, who has been a diehard fan for years and has been trying to get his hands on a ticket all week, so the three of you can go together!*

### 🧠 Linearthinking Dissection (`Simplify: S - V - O Skeleton + Causal Link`)
* **Full Sentence**: *"She had to give them up because she'll be hosting a training seminar this weekend."*
  * **Core Skeleton 1 (`Effect`)**: `She / had to give up / them (the tickets).`
  * **Logical Marker**: `because` *(Reason / Pre-scheduled Conflict)*
  * **Core Skeleton 2 (`Cause`)**: `she / will be hosting / a seminar.` *(Time modifier: `this weekend`)*
  * **Sound-Block Reconstruction (`/`)**: `She had to` / `give them up` / `because she'll be hosting` / `a training seminar` / `this weekend.`

### 🛠️ 6 Core Target Formulas from Notes:

#### 1. Mở lời gây tò mò (Cấm đảo ngữ): `Guess what I got here!`
* **Công thức**: `Guess + [what / who / where] + [Subject + Verb (normal order)]!`
* **Chức năng & Quy tắc**: Dùng để khoe tin vui hoặc món đồ bất ngờ. Mệnh đề sau `Guess what` là mệnh đề danh từ ➔ **BẮT BUỘC giữ nguyên `S + V`** (*Guess what I got*), tuyệt đối **cấm đảo trợ động từ** (*Guess what did I get* ❌).
* **📖 Note chi tiết**: [pattern-conversation-guess-what-i-got-here.md](file:///c:/Users/Admin/source/repos/thangvo-miscellaneous/english/week-25/notes/pattern-conversation-guess-what-i-got-here.md)
* **Slot-Filling**:
  1. Guess what `[I got here]`!
  2. Guess who `[I ran into at the coffee shop]` this morning!

#### 2. Tốc độ chớp nhoáng & Mở bán: `[Subject] sold out within [Time] of going on sale.`
* **Công thức**: `[Action / Event] + within + [Time Duration] + OF + [V-ing / Noun].`
* **Chức năng & Quy tắc**: Giới từ nối giữa khoảng thời gian và mốc kích hoạt bắt buộc là **`OF`** (không dùng *after* trong cụm `within X of Y`). Phân biệt **`go on sale`** (bắt đầu mở bán) vs **`be on sale`** (đang giảm giá/khuyến mãi).
* **📖 Note chi tiết**: [pattern-speed-within-time-of-ving-and-go-on-sale.md](file:///c:/Users/Admin/source/repos/thangvo-miscellaneous/english/week-25/notes/pattern-speed-within-time-of-ving-and-go-on-sale.md)
* **Slot-Filling**:
  1. The tickets sold out `[within five minutes of going on sale]`.
  2. The on-call engineer resolved the alert `[within ten minutes of receiving the page]`.

#### 3. Phản ứng ngạc nhiên / Không thể tin nổi: `Don't tell me you bought them from scalpers!`
* **Công thức**: `Don't tell me + (that) + [Subject + Past/Present Verb]!`
* **Chức năng**: Bày tỏ sự sửng sốt hoặc "sợ điều mình đoán là thật" (*"Đừng nói với mình là bạn đã... đấy nhé!"*). `scalpers` = dân phe vé / cò hàng đầu cơ. Đối lập: mua đúng giá gốc là **`at face value`**.
* **📖 Note chi tiết**: [pattern-reaction-dont-tell-me-and-scalpers.md](file:///c:/Users/Admin/source/repos/thangvo-miscellaneous/english/week-25/notes/pattern-reaction-dont-tell-me-and-scalpers.md)
* **Slot-Filling**:
  1. Don't tell me `[you bought them from scalpers]`!
  2. Don't tell me `[you ran the drop-table script in production]`!

#### 4. Kẹt lịch định sẵn (Tương lai tiếp diễn): `She had to give them up because she'll be hosting...`
* **Công thức**: `[Subject] + had to + give [Pronoun] up + because + [Subject] + will be + [V-ing] + [Time].`
* **Chức năng & Quy tắc**:
  1. Đại từ `them / it` bắt buộc phải **kẹp ở giữa** phrasal verb: `give them up` (cấm nói *give up them* ❌).
  2. Dùng **Future Continuous (`will be hosting`)** thay vì `will host` để nhấn mạnh một sự kiện đã lên lịch cố định từ trước và sẽ đang diễn ra tại thời điểm đó.
* **📖 Note chi tiết**: [pattern-schedule-conflict-give-up-and-will-be-ving.md](file:///c:/Users/Admin/source/repos/thangvo-miscellaneous/english/week-25/notes/pattern-schedule-conflict-give-up-and-will-be-ving.md)
* **Slot-Filling**:
  1. She had to `[give them up]` because `[she'll be hosting a training seminar this weekend]`.
  2. I had to `[give my seat up]` because `[I'll be running the database migration tonight]`.

#### 5. Chuyển ý mượt mà, Fan cứng & Nỗ lực săn lùng: `By the way, [S]'s been a diehard fan... and he's been trying to get his hands on...`
* **Công thức**: `By the way, + [Subject] + has been a diehard fan of [X], and [Subject] + has been trying to get [one's] hands on + [a ticket / item].`
* **Chức năng**: Dùng `has been` cho đam mê kéo dài tới hiện tại; `has been trying` (HTHT tiếp diễn) cho nỗ lực săn tìm liên tục; thành ngữ `get one's hands on` = kiếm/săn bằng được thứ quý hiếm.
* **📖 Note chi tiết**: [pattern-topic-shift-by-the-way-and-diehard-fan.md](file:///c:/Users/Admin/source/repos/thangvo-miscellaneous/english/week-25/notes/pattern-topic-shift-by-the-way-and-diehard-fan.md) | [pattern-persistence-has-been-trying-get-hands-on.md](file:///c:/Users/Admin/source/repos/thangvo-miscellaneous/english/week-25/notes/pattern-persistence-has-been-trying-get-hands-on.md)
* **Slot-Filling**:
  1. By the way, `[Ron's been a diehard fan of the band]`, and `[he's been trying to get his hands on a ticket]`.
  2. I've been trying to `[get my hands on the latest benchmark report]` all morning.

#### 6. Đại từ chỉ nhóm xác định: `The three of us are going to have so much fun!`
* **Công thức**: `The + [Number (two / three / four)] + of + [us / you / them] + [Plural Verb].`
* **Chức năng & Quy tắc**: Có **`THE`** (`The three of us`) = **cả 3 người chúng ta** (nhóm chỉ có đúng 3 người). Không có `the` (`Three of us`) = 3 người trong một tập thể lớn hơn.
* **📖 Note chi tiết**: [grammar-group-pronouns-the-three-of-us-and-variations.md](file:///c:/Users/Admin/source/repos/thangvo-miscellaneous/english/week-25/notes/grammar-group-pronouns-the-three-of-us-and-variations.md)
* **Slot-Filling**:
  1. `[The three of us]` are going to have so much fun!
  2. `[The two of us]` can handle the schema review while the rest of the team tests the API.
