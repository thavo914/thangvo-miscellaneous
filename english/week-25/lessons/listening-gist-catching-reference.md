## 🎯 How to Run Each Audio Block (10–12 Mins / Block)

Mỗi bài nghe bên dưới đã được đóng gói trọn vẹn thành **1 Audio Block khép kín 4 bước** (không cần cuộn lên xuống):
1. **Step 1 — Read & Understand 100% (`🧱 Chunked Transcript`, 2m)**: Đọc hiểu trọn vẹn cốt truyện và các nhịp ngắt `/` trước khi bật băng.
2. **Step 2 — Audio-Text Sync (2m)**: Bật audio **1.0x**, mắt dò theo dấu `/` để khớp mặt chữ với nối âm/nuốt âm bản ngữ.
3. **Step 3 — Shadowing & Blind Speed Check (4m)**: Nhại đuổi 0.5s ở **1.0x** ➔ Gấp chữ nghe mù ở **1.0x ➔ 1.25x**.
4. **Step 4 — ⚡ Hot-Seat Q&A (2m)**: Trả lời to thành tiếng câu hỏi trực diện bằng ngôi thứ nhất (`I / We`).

## 🎧 AUDIO BLOCK 1 [TOEIC Part 3]: Office Supply Delivery Shortage & Supplier Reconciliation
> 🔗 **Audio Link**: [TOEIC Part 3 - Test 3 (Questions 7–9)](https://www.esl-lounge.com/student/toeic-test-practice/toeic-031-listening-part-three-3.php) | 📅 **Schedule**: Monday (New) ➔ Tuesday (Review)

### Step 1 & 2: 🧱 Chunked Transcript (Read First ➔ Audio-Text Sync at 1.0x)
* **Woman**: `The office supply delivery` / `just arrived,` / `but I think` / `there's a problem.` / `I've been checking the boxes` / `against our order form,` / `and we're short on` / `one of the items.`
* **Man**: `Which one?` / `We placed` / `a pretty big order` / `this time.`
* **Woman**: `Well,` / `we ordered five units` / `of two different products.` / `The color cartridges` / `are all here,` / `but the other item,` / `we only received three` / `instead of five.`
* **Man**: `That's frustrating.` / `We really need those` / `for the quarterly reports.` / `Did the packing slip` / `show the correct quantity?`
* **Woman**: `No, it says three.` / `So it looks like` / `a mistake on their end,` / `not a shipping error.` / `I think we should call the supplier` / `and ask them to either` / `send the missing items` / `or issue a partial refund.`
* **Man**: `Agreed.` / `I'll give them a call` / `right now` / `and sort it out.`

* **🔑 Key Chunks & 3W Snapshot**:
  * **Target Chunks**: `check against our order form` • `short on one of the items` • `packing slip` • `mistake on their end` • `issue a partial refund` • `sort it out`.
  * **3W Core**: Two colleagues checked an office supply delivery ➔ received only 3 of 5 units needed for quarterly reports, and the packing slip also showed 3 (vendor error) ➔ the man will call the supplier immediately for replacement or a partial refund.

### Step 3: 🎙️ Shadowing & Blind Speed Check
* [ ] **Shadowing (1.0x, eyes on text)**: Đọc đuổi 0.5s, chú ý nối âm `short-on-one-of-the-items`, `mistake-on-their-end`, `sort-it-out`.
* [ ] **Blind Check (1.0x ➔ 1.25x, eyes closed)**: Nhắm mắt nghe liền mạch, hiểu rõ ≥ 90% không bị khựng.

### Step 4: ⚡ "In-the-Room" Hot-Seat Q&A (1st-Person `I / We`)
* **Manager**: *"Did the office supplies arrive? We need everything ready for the quarterly reports this afternoon!"*

  * **Linear Axis**: `[1. Partial Status] ➔ [2. Root Cause] ➔ [3. Immediate Action]`
  * **1st-Person Hot-Seat Reflex**: *"The delivery just arrived,` / `and all five color cartridges are here,` / `but we're short two units` / `on the other item.` / `Since the packing slip` / `only lists three,` / `it's clearly an order-entry mistake` / `on the vendor's end.` / `I'm calling the supplier right now` / `to have them send` / `the missing units over` / `or issue a partial refund.`"*

## 🎧 AUDIO BLOCK 3 [TOEIC Part 4]: Flight 209 to Chicago & Departure Gate Change
> 🔗 **Audio Link**: [TOEIC Part 4 - Test 1 (Questions 1–3)](https://www.esl-lounge.com/student/toeic-test-practice/toeic-037-listening-part-four-1.php) | 📅 **Schedule**: Wednesday (New) ➔ Thursday (Review)

### Step 1 & 2: 🧱 Chunked Transcript (Read First ➔ Audio-Text Sync at 1.0x)
* **Speaker**: `Attention all passengers` / `on Flight 209` / `with service to Chicago.` / `We have an important update` / `regarding your departure gate.` / `Due to a maintenance issue` / `at Gate A4,` / `your flight will now be departing` / `from Gate B2.` / `That's Gate B2` / `in Terminal B.` / `Please note that` / `boarding will begin` / `in approximately twenty minutes,` / `so we ask that` / `you make your way` / `to the new gate immediately.` / `If you need assistance` / `or have questions about connections,` / `our staff will be available` / `at the new gate.` / `We apologize` / `for any inconvenience this may cause` / `and thank you` / `for your patience.`

* **🔑 Key Chunks & Linear Flow (`Update ➔ Cause ➔ Immediate Action`)**:
  * **Target Chunks**: `with service to Chicago` • `regarding your departure gate` • `due to a maintenance issue` • `will now be departing from` • `make your way to` • `questions about connections`.
  * **3W Core**: Airport announcement for Flight 209 to Chicago ➔ gate moved from A4 to B2 (Terminal B) due to a maintenance issue at A4; boarding starts in ~20 minutes ➔ passengers must head to Gate B2 immediately, where staff can help with connections.

### Step 3: 🎙️ Shadowing & Blind Speed Check
* [ ] **Shadowing (1.0x, eyes on text)**: Đọc đuổi 0.5s nguyên bài độc thoại không ngắt nghỉ, chú ý `will-now-be-departing-from`, `make-your-way-to-the-new-gate`.
* [ ] **Blind Check (1.0x ➔ 1.25x, eyes closed)**: Nhắm mắt nghe liền mạch từ đầu tới cuối, bắt dính số chuyến bay `209`, cổng `A4 ➔ B2`, `Terminal B`, `20 minutes`.

### Step 4: ⚡ "In-the-Room" Hot-Seat Q&A (1st-Person `I / We`)
* **Manager (returning from grabbing coffee at the airport)**: *"Sorry I missed the loudspeaker announcement! Why is everyone packing up and leaving Gate A4? What's going on with our flight to Chicago?"*
* <details><summary>💡 3-Second Linear Model Answer & Optional 15s Summary</summary>

  * **Linear Axis**: `[1. Direct Gate Change] ➔ [2. Reason] ➔ [3. Urgent Boarding Timeline]`
  * **1st-Person Hot-Seat Reflex**: *"We need to move right now` — `our flight to Chicago` / `was just reassigned to Gate B2` / `in Terminal B.` / `They announced there's a maintenance issue` / `here at Gate A4.` / `Boarding starts in about twenty minutes,` / `so let's make our way over to Gate B2` / `immediately!`"*
  * **Optional 15s 3W Summary**: *"An airline representative announced that Flight 209 to Chicago has moved from Gate A4 to Gate B2 in Terminal B due to a maintenance issue.
