## 🎯 How to Use This Reference for Self-Evaluation

When practicing **1-Minute Gist-Catching & Verbal Summary** (Drill 2 in [daily-listening-and-speaking-reflex-guide.md](file:///c:/Users/adm.thangvm/repos/thangvo-miscellaneous/english/daily-listening-and-speaking-reflex-guide.md)):

1. **Pass 1 (Gist Pass)**: Listen to the audio once through without reading the transcript. Focus only on the macro picture: **WHO** is speaking, **WHAT** is the obstacle, and what **ACTION** is taken.
2. **Pass 2 (Precision Pass)**: Listen a second time to catch specific constraints (numbers, items, root causes, deliverables).
3. **Instant Verbal Output (The 15-Second Rule)**: Stop the audio and immediately speak your summary out loud in English within 15–20 seconds using the **3W Framework**.
4. **Compare & Score**: Read the transcript, 3W Key Elements, and Benchmark Model Summary below to score your performance out of 10 points.

### 📊 Universal 10-Point Scoring Rubric

| Component | Max Points | Evaluation Criteria |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **1. Core Fact Accuracy (3W)** | **4 Pts** | Captured the core situation, conflict, and final resolution without missing key facts (1 pt per fact). |
| **2. Speed & Reflex** | **3 Pts** | Began speaking within 3 seconds of the audio ending; finished summary in under 20 seconds without long hesitations. |
| **3. Natural Phrasing & Chunks** | **3 Pts** | Used native phrasal verbs, idioms, and collocations; completely avoided word-by-word translation. |

## 📑 Clip 1: Office Supply Delivery Shortage & Supplier Reconciliation (Questions 7–9)

### 📜 Full Audio Transcript
* **Woman**: *"The office supply delivery just arrived, but I think there's a problem. I've been checking the boxes against our order form, and we're short on one of the items."*
* **Man**: *"Which one? We placed a pretty big order this time."*
* **Woman**: *"Well, we ordered five units of two different products. The color cartridges are all here, but the other item, we only received three instead of five."*
* **Man**: *"That's frustrating. We really need those for the quarterly reports. Did the packing slip show the correct quantity?"*
* **Woman**: *"No, it says three. So it looks like a mistake on their end, not a shipping error. I think we should call the supplier and ask them to either send the missing items or issue a partial refund."*
* **Man**: *"Agreed. I'll give them a call right now and sort it out."*

### 🔑 3W Key Elements
* **Who**: Two office colleagues verifying an incoming delivery of office supplies.
* **What**: An office supply order was short: while all five color cartridges arrived, they received only three of the five required units of another product needed for quarterly reports. Because the packing slip also listed three, it indicated a supplier order-entry error rather than a transit error.
* **Action/Next**: They agreed to contact the vendor for either a replacement delivery or a partial refund, and the man volunteered to call immediately to sort it out.

### 🎙️ Benchmark Model Verbal Summary (15 Seconds)
> *"An office supply delivery arrived short on items needed for quarterly reports, with only three of five units delivered. Since the packing slip showed three, they realized it was a mistake on the vendor's end, and the man agreed to call the supplier right away to request the missing items or get a refund."*

### 📋 Clip 1 Scoring Checklist (10 Points)
* [ ] **Fact 1 (1 pt)**: Issue: Delivery shortage discovered while cross-checking against the order form (`short on one of the items`).
* [ ] **Fact 2 (1 pt)**: Quantity gap: Received 5 color cartridges, but only 3 out of 5 of the other required product.
* [ ] **Fact 3 (1 pt)**: Cause identified: Packing slip printed "3", confirming a vendor order-entry error (`mistake on their end`).
* [ ] **Fact 4 (1 pt)**: Resolution: The man calls the supplier immediately to arrange replacement or a partial refund (`sort it out`).
* [ ] **Reflex & Flow (3 pts)**: Initiated summary within 3 seconds; finished smoothly in under 18 seconds without filler stalling.
* [ ] **Native Chunks (3 pts)**: Used workplace collocations: `check against our order form`, `short on [item]`, `packing slip`, `mistake on their end`, `issue a partial refund`, `sort it out`.

## 📑 Clip 2: Regional Sales Slump & Targeted Social Media Campaign (Questions 10–12)

### 📜 Full Audio Transcript
* **Man**: *"I've been looking at the quarterly sales figures, and the numbers from the West region are concerning. We're down almost fifteen percent compared to the same period last year."*
* **Woman**: *"I noticed that too. I think the problem is visibility. Our competitors have been much more active on social media lately, and we've barely posted anything in months."*
* **Man**: *"You might be onto something. What do you suggest?"*
* **Woman**: *"I think we should increase our online advertising budget and run a targeted campaign on the major platforms. We could focus on the twenty-five to forty age group since that's our core customer base."*
* **Man**: *"That sounds reasonable. Could you put together a proposal outlining the costs and expected reach? I'd like to present it to the director at next week's meeting."*
* **Woman**: *"Sure, I can have something ready by Friday. That should give you time to review it over the weekend."*

### 🔑 3W Key Elements
* **Who**: Two team members/managers reviewing regional sales numbers.
* **What**: West region quarterly sales dropped nearly 15% year-over-year, which the woman traced to poor online visibility and low social media activity compared to competitors.
* **Action/Next**: She recommended boosting their digital ad budget for a targeted campaign focused on their 25–40 demographic; the man asked her to draft a formal proposal by Friday so he can review it before pitching it to the director next week.

### 🎙️ Benchmark Model Verbal Summary (15 Seconds)
> *"West region sales dropped by nearly 15 percent due to poor brand visibility and inactive social media compared to competitors. The woman suggested running a targeted ad campaign aimed at their 25-to-40 demographic, and agreed to draft a cost proposal by Friday for next week's director meeting."*

### 📋 Clip 2 Scoring Checklist (10 Points)
* [ ] **Fact 1 (1 pt)**: Problem: West region quarterly sales down almost 15% year-over-year.
* [ ] **Fact 2 (1 pt)**: Root cause: Weak visibility / company has been inactive on social media while competitors were active.
* [ ] **Fact 3 (1 pt)**: Proposed strategy: Increase online ad spend with a targeted campaign for their core demographic (25–40 age group).
* [ ] **Fact 4 (1 pt)**: Deliverable & Timeline: Woman will prepare a proposal outlining costs and reach by Friday for the director's meeting.
* [ ] **Reflex & Flow (3 pts)**: Transitioned cleanly through problem $\rightarrow$ strategy $\rightarrow$ deliverable in under 18 seconds.
* [ ] **Native Chunks (3 pts)**: Used strategic business phrasing: `quarterly sales figures`, `down almost 15%`, `you might be onto something`, `targeted campaign`, `core customer base`, `put together a proposal`, `expected reach`.
