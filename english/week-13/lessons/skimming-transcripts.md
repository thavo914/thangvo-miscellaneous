## Topic 2: Navigating Vendor Constraints in Data Engineering

**Title**: *Blocked by Vendor Approval? Here's How to Handle It*
**Topic**: CDC/vendor constraint problem-solving — using modal perfect, passive, and professional language
**Target Duration**: 60 seconds
**Word Count**: ~128 words
**Speaking Speed**: ~130 WPM

### Transcript

`[00:00]`
If your team needs near-real-time data, CDC is the gold standard. But here's the problem — it **must be approved** by your database vendor first, and that can take weeks.

`[00:15]`
So what do you do? First, **reach out to** the vendor's account manager directly — not just the support queue. I'd recommend that you **prepare a clear technical justification** before the call. Vendors are much more likely to say yes when they understand the use case.

`[00:35]`
Second, don't wait and do nothing. **In the meantime**, set up a scheduled snapshot every 15 minutes. It's not perfect, but it **can be done** without any vendor involvement.

`[00:50]`
If they **push back on** the request, stay calm and **work around** it. Escalate, document, and keep moving.
