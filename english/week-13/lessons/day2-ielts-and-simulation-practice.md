# Day 2: IELTS & Simulation Practice (Sports GOAT Debate)

---

## 1. IELTS Speaking Practice Questions

**Part 1 (Interview)**
1. Are you a fan of any particular sport or team?
2. Do you enjoy watching sports with friends, or do you prefer watching alone?
3. Have you ever had a debate or disagreement with a friend about a sports topic?

**Part 2 (Cue Card)**
Describe a famous athlete or sports personality you admire.
You should say:
- Who they are and what sport they play
- What makes them stand out from others
- What achievements you find most impressive
- And explain why you admire them

**Part 3 (Analytical Discussion)**
1. Do you think stats and trophies are the best way to judge an athlete's greatness, or are there other factors to consider?
2. Why do you think sports debates like "Messi vs. Ronaldo" tend to be so passionate and long-lasting?

## 4. Interactive Shadowing & Pacing Guide

**Line 1** (from the overview dialogue):
> *"Even though Ronaldo scores more goals, Messi has more trophies and a World Cup."*

**Shadowing Breakdown**:
> `Even THOUGH / Ronaldo SCORES more goals, /`
> `Messi has MORE trophies / and a WORLD Cup.`

- Stress **THOUGH** to signal the concession.
- Stress **SCORES** to emphasize the contrast.
- Stress **MORE** and **WORLD** to highlight the key facts.

---

**Line 2** (from the overview dialogue):
> *"At the end of the day, we're lucky to have witnessed both of them."*

**Shadowing Breakdown**:
> `At the END of the DAY, /`
> `we're LUCKY to have WITnessed / BOTH of them.`

- Use a slight rising tone before the pause (`,`) after *day*.
- Stress **LUCKY** and **BOTH** to emphasize the positive, inclusive conclusion.
