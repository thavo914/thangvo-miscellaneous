## 🗣️ Core Lesson Dialogue – Topic 1: Debating Sports GOATs at Lunch

*Alex and Dan are on their lunch break in the office canteen.*

> **Alex**: Okay, I have to say it — Messi is the GOAT. **Even though** Ronaldo scores more goals, Messi has more trophies **and** a World Cup.
>
> **Dan**: Come on, you can't dismiss Ronaldo that easily. You have to **give credit to** him — five Ballon d'Ors, titles in three different leagues. **At the end of the day**, he proved himself everywhere he went.
>
> **Alex**: Sure, but if Ronaldo had Messi's vision and creativity, he still **wouldn't have** the same impact on a team. Messi makes **everyone around him** better.
>
> **Dan**: That's a fair point, but **despite** having fewer trophies at club level, Ronaldo's raw goal-scoring record is almost impossible to top. No one has **enough** data to **settle the debate** definitively.
>
> **Alex**: I think you can easily **make the case for** Messi just by watching him play. His touch and movement were **ahead of his time**. If he played in any other era, he'd **still dominate**.
>
> **Dan**: Look, **even though** I'll always back Ronaldo, I'll admit that **at the end of the day**, we're lucky to have witnessed **both** of them. This generation won the lottery.
>
> **Alex**: Now *that*, I fully agree with.

## 🗣️ Core Lesson Dialogue – Topic 2: Navigating Vendor Constraints in Data Engineering

*Linh and Khoa are in a sprint planning meeting.*

> **Linh**: So the business wants near-real-time data from the OLTP database, which means we need to enable CDC. The problem is, that **must be approved** by the vendor first.
>
> **Khoa**: Right. We should **reach out to** them this week and explain the use case clearly. I'd recommend that we **prepare a formal request** before the call.
>
> **Linh**: Agreed. And if they **push back on** it, what's the fallback? We can't just tell the business to wait three months for sign-off.
>
> **Khoa**: **In the meantime**, I'd suggest that we **set up** a scheduled snapshot every 15 minutes. It's not perfect, but it **can be done** without vendor involvement.
>
> **Linh**: That's a good **work-around**. But we still need to **get sign-off** on CDC eventually for it to be a proper real-time solution.
>
> **Khoa**: Exactly. The snapshot approach **must be seen** as temporary. I'd propose that we **escalate** to the vendor's account manager if the technical team is slow to respond.
>
> **Linh**: Good plan. Let's **get the request** drafted today so we're not blocked next sprint.
