## 1. Creative Dialogue Writing
**Instructions**: Write a new, original 10-turn conversation between you and a colleague at lunch. You are debating whether Messi or Ronaldo is the greatest footballer of all time.
**Requirement**: You must naturally include at least 2 of the target grammar rules (Second Conditional, Concession with Even Though/Despite, Quantifiers with more/fewer/less/enough) and 3 of the target collocations (*settle the debate, make the case for, give credit to, ahead of one's time, at the end of the day*).

## 2. Band 6.5-7.0 Cue Card Sample Answer

**Prompt**: Describe a famous athlete or sports personality you admire.

**Sample Answer**:
"I'd like to talk about Lionel Messi. **Even though** he's retired from club football at the highest level, I still consider him the greatest player I've ever watched. It's genuinely hard to **make the case for** anyone else when you look at what he's won — eight Ballon d'Ors, four Champions League titles, and a World Cup with Argentina.

What makes him truly special is that he makes his teammates better. If he were just a selfish goal-scorer, his teams **wouldn't have** the same chemistry. But he's a creator. You also have to **give credit to** Ronaldo, of course — scoring over 900 career goals is something no one has **enough** physical ability to replicate easily. Still, Messi's intelligence and touch feel like they were **ahead of his time**.

**At the end of the day**, we were all lucky to watch them both in the same era. Despite their rivalry, they pushed each other to be better, and football fans around the world benefited from that."

### Conversational Words Highlighted:
- **make the case for**: argue in support of (Vietnamese: lập luận cho / bào chữa cho)
- **give credit to**: acknowledge someone's achievement fairly (Vietnamese: công nhận / ghi nhận)
- **ahead of one's time**: more advanced or innovative than the current era (Vietnamese: đi trước thời đại)
- **at the end of the day**: ultimately / when everything is considered (Vietnamese: xét cho cùng)
