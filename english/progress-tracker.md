## Tips for Effective Tracking

1. **Be honest**: Real data helps you improve
2. **Track immediately**: Don't wait until end of day
3. **Review weekly**: Friday evening or Saturday morning
4. **Celebrate wins**: Even small progress counts
5. **Adjust when needed**: If something isn't working, change it
