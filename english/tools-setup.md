# 🛠️ Tools Setup Guide

Get your English learning toolkit ready. Follow these steps to configure all essential tools before Week 1.

## ✅ Pre-requisites Checklist

Before starting, ensure you have:
- [ ] Smartphone (iOS or Android)
- [ ] Computer with internet
- [ ] Headphones/earbuds
- [ ] Quiet practice space (at least 1 hour daily)
- [ ] Willingness to talk to yourself (seriously!)

## 1. Anki - Spaced Repetition System

### What is it?
Anki is a flashcard app that helps you remember vocabulary forever using scientifically-proven spaced repetition.

### Setup Steps

1. **Download Anki**
   - Desktop: [https://apps.ankiweb.net/](https://apps.ankiweb.net/)
   - iOS: AnkiMobile (paid, $24.99 - worth it!)
   - Android: AnkiDroid (free)

2. **Create an Account**
   - Sign up at [https://ankiweb.net/](https://ankiweb.net/)
   - Enables syncing across devices

3. **Create Your First Deck**
   - Deck name: "English - Data Engineering & Casino"
   - Or separate decks: "Business English", "Technical Terms", "Casino Vocabulary"

4. **Card Format** (CRITICAL!)
   - **Front**: Vietnamese prompt or English cue
   - **Back**: Full English sentence (NOT just a word!)
   - **Example**:
     - Front: "Giải thích lỗi ETL (context: meeting with manager)"
     - Back: "The ETL process failed due to a schema mismatch in the source database."

### Daily Commitment
- 15-30 minutes reviewing cards
- Add 10-15 new sentences weekly (Tuesdays)
- Review BEFORE new content, not after

### Resources
- [Sentence Mining Guide](https://refold.la/simplified/stage-2/a/sentence-mining/)
- [Anki Manual](https://docs.ankiweb.net/)

## 2. ChatGPT Voice Mode - AI Conversation Partner

### What is it?
Your 24/7 English speaking partner who never gets tired, always gives feedback, and costs less than one coffee shop visit.

### Setup Steps

1. **Subscribe to ChatGPT Plus** ($20/month)
   - Go to [https://chat.openai.com/](https://chat.openai.com/)
   - Click "Upgrade to Plus"
   - Worth every penny for voice mode

2. **Download Mobile App**
   - iOS: App Store → "ChatGPT"
   - Android: Google Play → "ChatGPT"

3. **Enable Voice Mode**
   - Open app → Tap headphone icon (bottom right)
   - Select voice (recommended: "Sky" or "Breeze" for clarity)
   - Grant microphone permissions

### Sample Prompts for Role-play (Wednesday)

#### Week 1: Professional Introduction
```
Act as a friendly but busy Casino Floor Manager. I am a new Data Engineer. 
Meet me at the coffee machine. Ask me what I do and how I can help the casino floor. 
Speak naturally and challenge me if my explanations are too technical.
```

#### General Feedback Prompt
```
After our conversation, please tell me:
1. What grammar mistakes did I make?
2. What pronunciation issues did you notice?
3. How can I sound more natural?
4. What phrases would a native speaker use instead?
```

### Usage Tips
- Use on commute, during walks, anywhere private
- 10-15 minute sessions work best
- Record your screen during practice for review

## 3. ELSA Speak - Pronunciation Coach

### What is it?
AI-powered app that analyzes your pronunciation and gives instant feedback.

### Setup Steps

1. **Download App**
   - iOS: App Store → "ELSA Speak"
   - Android: Google Play → "ELSA Speak"

2. **Create Account & Assessment**
   - Take initial pronunciation test
   - Identifies your weak sounds

3. **Daily Routine** (10-15 minutes)
   - Use as warm-up BEFORE other practice
   - Focus on your weak sounds
   - Best time: Early morning (warm up your mouth muscles)

### Recommended Use
- **Monday** (Shadowing day): Warm up before shadowing exercises
- **Daily**: 10 minutes as English activation routine

## 4. Otter.ai - Speech-to-Text Transcription

### What is it?
Records your speech and converts it to text. If the AI can't understand you, your pronunciation needs work!

### Setup Steps

1. **Create Account**
   - Go to [https://otter.ai/](https://otter.ai/)
   - Free tier: 100 minutes/month (sufficient for weekly checks)

2. **Download App**
   - iOS: App Store → "Otter"
   - Android: Google Play → "Otter Voice Meeting Notes"

3. **Grant Permissions**
   - Microphone access
   - Storage access (for recordings)

### Usage Pattern

#### Weekly Recording (Saturday Mini-Project)
1. Record your 3-5 minute presentation
2. Let Otter transcribe it
3. Review transcription accuracy:
   - Correct = Good pronunciation
   - Incorrect = Practice that word/phrase
4. Export and save in weekly folder

#### Daily Quick Check (Optional)
- Record 30 seconds of practice
- Check if Otter understood you
- Instant pronunciation feedback

## 💡 Success Tips

1. **Anki**: Do it FIRST thing, before email/social media
2. **ChatGPT**: Pretend it's a real person - use body language!
3. **ELSA**: Don't skip even when you feel good - warm up is crucial
4. **Otter**: Don't get discouraged by mistakes - use them to improve
5. **Recording**: Listen to your progress monthly - you'll be amazed!

## ❓ Troubleshooting

### "Anki cards are too hard to remember"
- You're learning isolated words. Switch to full sentences with context.

### "ChatGPT doesn't understand my accent"
- Use ELSA first to warm up
- Speak slightly slower with clear pronunciation
- Check Otter.ai to identify problem sounds

### "I don't have time for all these tools"
- Minimum viable setup: Anki (15 min) + ChatGPT (30 min) + Recording (5 min)
- Add others as habits form

### "It's expensive!"
- ChatGPT Plus: $20/month (one business meeting cost)
- ELSA: Use free tier
- Everything else: Free
- Total: ~$20-30/month for 6 months of transformation
