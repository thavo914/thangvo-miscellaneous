# 🍃 The Art of Caring Less: A Taoist Guide to Peace

**Core Message**: The modern world pressures us to constantly hustle, control outcomes, and compare ourselves to others, leading to inevitable stress and suffering. By applying Taoist principles like Wu Wei (effortless action), Ziran (spontaneity), and the balance of Yin and Yang, we can learn to detach from uncontrollable outcomes and flow naturally with life's changes.
