**Core Message**: In today's fast-paced, achievement-obsessed society, we often stretch ourselves to the point of burnout. By applying ancient Taoist principles like letting go, embracing simplicity, and finding balance over self-exploitation, we can regain tranquility and inner peace.

- **Relative Adverb "Where" to describe situations**: `We live in a world where [Subject] + [Verb]`
