# The Limits of Rationalism: From Socrates to Neural Networks

**Core Message:** This lesson explores the tension between rationalism—the belief that knowledge must be explicitly defined—and human intuition. It traces how this mindset shaped western civilization, its limitations in understanding complex systems like psychology, and how modern Artificial Intelligence (neural networks) relies on intuition rather than rigid rules.
