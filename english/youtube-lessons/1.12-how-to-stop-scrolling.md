# How to Stop Scrolling and Reclaim Your Time

**Core Message**: This video provides a practical, no-nonsense guide on how to stop mindlessly scrolling on your phone. The speaker emphasizes actionable steps like making daily to-do lists, deleting distracting apps, spending more time outside, talking to people instead of using your phone, and finding a meaningful hobby or project to work on. 

---

## Comprehensive Chunk-by-Chunk Analysis

### 📝 Transcript Chunk 1: The Power of Setting Plans
> "It's easier than you think to spend all the time that you really don't want to be on your phone doing something else which is why I don't want you to miss out on this because this could actually help you a lot... I want you to right now think of some things that you would like to do tomorrow and then just write them down or find some way you can actually remember to do those things because a lot of the time where I end up scrolling is when I'm not sure what to do but when I always have those little to-do lists of things that I can do it always ends up saving my days and me not feeling like I just rotted my brain for 5 hours."

#### 📚 Vocabulary & Idioms
- **To miss out on (something)**
  - *Phrasal Verb*
  - Bỏ lỡ (một cơ hội, một trải nghiệm tốt)
  - *Example*: You should come to the networking event tonight; you don't want to miss out on meeting the new director.
- **To end up (doing something)**
  - *Phrasal Verb*
  - Rốt cuộc lại (làm gì đó), dẫn đến kết cục là
  - *Example*: We couldn't decide on a restaurant, so we ended up eating pizza at home.
- **To rot your brain**
  - *Idiom (Informal)*
  - Làm lụi tàn trí não (do làm những việc vô bổ như xem TV/điện thoại quá nhiều)
  - *Example*: Spending five hours watching short videos on TikTok will just rot your brain.

#### 🧠 Grammar & Sentence Structure
- **Explaining the root cause:** `A lot of the time where I end up [Negative Action] is when I'm [Root Cause].`
  - *Rule*: This structure is excellent for self-reflection and explaining why a bad habit happens. 
  - *Example*: A lot of the time where I end up eating junk food is when I'm stressed from work.

#### 💡 Practical Application & Alternatives
- **How to use it**: Use "end up" in almost any daily conversation when telling a story about how your plans changed or how things unexpectedly turned out.
- **Alternative ways to say it**: 
  - *Instead of "miss out on":* Lose the opportunity, pass up on.
  - *Instead of "rotted my brain":* Wasted my time, numbed my mind.

### 📝 Transcript Chunk 2: Making Bad Habits Annoying
> "If scrolling isn't in your to-do list delete it delete the Instagram app delete Tik Tok delete YouTube I would even delete Chrome off my phone so I couldn't go on Google and search up YouTube. If you keep installing it back just delete it every time delete it every time make it so annoying to actually go in the app and start scrolling that you just don't want to do it it reminds me of this one short that I saw it was like when you're playing games with your friends but they suck so badly you'd rather just do the thing that you're procrastinating on."

#### 📚 Vocabulary & Idioms
- **To make it so annoying that...**
  - *Phrase*
  - Làm cho (việc gì đó) trở nên phiền phức đến mức...
  - *Example*: I hid my PlayStation in the closet to make it so annoying to set up that I just study instead.
- **To suck (at something)**
  - *Verb (Slang/Informal)*
  - Tệ hại, dở tệ
  - *Example*: I tried to play tennis yesterday, but I really suck at it.
- **To procrastinate**
  - *Verb*
  - Trì hoãn, chần chừ
  - *Example*: I always procrastinate when I have to write a difficult email to a client.

#### 🧠 Grammar & Sentence Structure
- **Expressing strong preference for the lesser evil:** `You'd rather just do [Boring/Hard Task] than [Annoying/Bad Task].`
  - *Rule*: `Would rather + Verb` is used to show a preference. In this context, it shows that the distraction became so bad that you prefer to do your actual work.
  - *Example*: The movie was so boring that I'd rather just do my laundry.

#### 💡 Practical Application & Alternatives
- **How to use it**: Use "procrastinate" in professional settings when admitting you've been putting off a task, but want to sound formal.
- **Alternative ways to say it**: 
  - *Instead of "procrastinating on":* Putting off, delaying.
  - *Instead of "they suck so badly":* They are terrible at it, they're so bad.

### 📝 Transcript Chunk 3: The Power of Persistence
> "Another extra thing about plans which gave me a lot more productive hours instead of scrolling were doing stuff that I'm okay with doing alone but inviting friends or people... to go out with me for sports practice for example. Even if they say no one day if you keep asking them I believe they're going to say yes eventually... Setting plans so when you're not sure what to do you can look at your to-do list... I believe that will help you at least cut off 70 60% of the time scrolling."

#### 📚 Vocabulary & Idioms
- **Productive hours**
  - *Noun Phrase*
  - Những giờ làm việc năng suất/có ích
  - *Example*: My most productive hours are usually early in the morning before everyone wakes up.
- **Eventually**
  - *Adverb*
  - Rốt cuộc, cuối cùng thì (sau một thời gian dài hoặc nhiều nỗ lực)
  - *Example*: It took six months of interviewing, but she eventually got the job.
- **To cut off (time/percentage)**
  - *Phrasal Verb*
  - Cắt giảm, giảm bớt (thời gian, chi phí)
  - *Example*: By automating this process, we can cut off 50% of the manual work.

#### 🧠 Grammar & Sentence Structure
- **Encouraging persistence:** `Even if they [Negative Response] one day, if you keep [Action-ing], they're going to [Positive Result] eventually.`
  - *Rule*: A great motivational structure combining a conditional concession ("Even if") with a cause-and-effect promise ("if you keep... they're going to").
  - *Example*: Even if they reject your proposal today, if you keep improving it, they're going to accept it eventually.

#### 💡 Practical Application & Alternatives
- **How to use it**: Use "eventually" to reassure a colleague or friend that success is coming, even if it's taking a long time.
- **Alternative ways to say it**: 
  - *Instead of "cut off 70%":* Reduce by 70%, eliminate 70% of.
  - *Instead of "eventually":* In the end, sooner or later.

#### 📚 Vocabulary & Idioms
- **To take advantage of (something)**
  - *Phrasal Verb*
  - Tận dụng (cơ hội, lợi thế)
  - *Example*: You should take advantage of the free training courses the company is offering.
- **A ton of (something)**
  - *Idiom (Informal)*
  - Rất nhiều, vô số
  - *Example*: We have a ton of data to analyze before the meeting tomorrow.
- **To keep someone entertained**
  - *Collocation*
  - Giữ cho ai đó được giải trí/vui vẻ (không bị chán)
  - *Example*: I bought some puzzles to keep the kids entertained during the long flight.

#### 🧠 Grammar & Sentence Structure
- **Proposing a personal goal:** `Try to make it a challenge to [Action] more than you normally do.`
  - *Rule*: A friendly, persuasive way to encourage someone (or yourself) to change a habit gradually.
  - *Example*: Try to make it a challenge to drink water more than you normally do.

#### 💡 Practical Application & Alternatives
- **How to use it**: Use "take advantage of" in performance reviews or mentoring sessions to encourage someone to use available resources. 
- **Alternative ways to say it**: 
  - *Instead of "a ton of opportunities":* A wealth of opportunities, countless chances.
  - *Instead of "take advantage of":* Make the most of, utilize.

### 📝 Transcript Chunk 5: Breaking the "Free Time" Reflex
> "A habit that I'm really glad I got into... when you're with people instead of just going on your phone actually try to talk to them. I know it can be rough I know you might not know exactly what to say... but it's a thousand times better to just say something than just go on your phone. That will really help you break the habit of when there's a sliver of free time where you don't know what to do you just boom whip out your phone but if you just put your phone away for a second... there's a lot more special moments."

#### 📚 Vocabulary & Idioms
- **To get into a habit**
  - *Idiom*
  - Hình thành thói quen
  - *Example*: Since working from home, I got into a habit of taking a walk every day at lunchtime.
- **A sliver of (free time/hope/chance)**
  - *Noun Phrase*
  - Một chút xíu, một tia (thời gian rảnh/hy vọng)
  - *Example*: We only have a sliver of time before the client arrives, so let's prepare quickly.
- **To whip out (your phone/wallet)**
  - *Phrasal Verb (Informal)*
  - Rút nhanh ra
  - *Example*: As soon as the celebrity walked in, everyone whipped out their phones to take pictures.
- **To break the habit**
  - *Collocation*
  - Bỏ thói quen xấu
  - *Example*: It's hard to break the habit of checking emails late at night, but it's necessary for mental health.

#### 🧠 Grammar & Sentence Structure
- **Hyperbolic comparison:** `It's a thousand times better to [Action A] than just [Action B].`
  - *Rule*: Using "a thousand times" is a common exaggeration in spoken English to strongly advise someone to choose one option over another.
  - *Example*: It's a thousand times better to ask for help than just sit there struggling for hours.

#### 💡 Practical Application & Alternatives
- **How to use it**: Use "a sliver of" to sound very natural and fluent when describing a tiny amount of something abstract.
- **Alternative ways to say it**: 
  - *Instead of "whip out":* Pull out quickly, grab.
  - *Instead of "it can be rough":* It can be tough, it can be awkward.

### 📝 Transcript Chunk 6: The Final Solution
> "But if there still is some sliver of chance you can go on your phone and start scrolling... this last thing will nail the coffin and that's to have something to work on whether that's a sport whether that's a job a business going to the gym for me it's right now having this YouTube channel... think of all the time we really spend on our phones today I probably spent 6 hours on my phone alone and imagine if I spent one or two of those hours doing anything else literally I could clean the room I could clean my house... #### 📚 Vocabulary & Idioms
- **To nail the coffin (Put the final nail in the coffin)**
  - *Idiom*
  - Đòn quyết định, dấu chấm hết (giải quyết triệt để một vấn đề hoặc làm hỏng hoàn toàn thứ gì đó)
  - *Example*: The recent scandal put the final nail in the coffin for the CEO's career. (In the video's context, it means ending the bad habit completely).
- **Whether that's [A], whether that's [B]**
  - *Conjunction phrase*
  - Cho dù đó là A hay là B (đưa ra nhiều lựa chọn)
  - *Example*: We need to improve our skills, whether that's taking online courses or finding a mentor.
- **Literally**
  - *Adverb*
  - Thật vậy, đúng nghĩa đen (thường dùng để nhấn mạnh)
  - *Example*: I was literally shocked when they announced the new promotion.

#### 🧠 Grammar & Sentence Structure
- **Reflective Imagination:** `Imagine if I spent [Time] doing [Productive Action] else. I could [Possibility 1], I could [Possibility 2].`
  - *Rule*: A great conversational pattern for brainstorming or reflecting on missed potential.
  - *Example*: Imagine if we spent that budget on marketing instead. We could reach more clients, we could upgrade our software.

#### 💡 Practical Application & Alternatives
- **How to use it**: Use "I think we can both agree that..." to build consensus in a meeting before proposing a solution.
- **Alternative ways to say it**: 
  - *Instead of "nail the coffin":* Seal the deal, put an end to it completely.
  - *Instead of "have something to work on":* Have a project, have a focus.

### 📝 Transcript Chunk 7: Final Motivation
> "We don't need to watch another video we don't need to scroll and forget that this video even existed forget this channel forget my name... forget that all the points I try to bring up to you to try to help you I'm not perfect but this helped me a lot and if this sort of helped you too it would really mean a lot if you subscribed to this channel... hope you got something out of it you could think about about something and I'll see you later soon hopefully peace goodbye."

#### 📚 Vocabulary & Idioms
- **To bring up (a point/a topic)**
  - *Phrasal Verb*
  - Đề cập đến, đưa ra (một ý kiến, chủ đề)
  - *Example*: In the meeting, Sarah brought up some valid concerns about the deadline.
- **To get something out of it**
  - *Idiom*
  - Nhận được giá trị/lợi ích gì đó (từ một trải nghiệm, cuốn sách, video)
  - *Example*: It was a long workshop, but I really got a lot out of it.
- **It would mean a lot (to me) if...**
  - *Phrase*
  - Sẽ rất có ý nghĩa (với tôi) nếu...
  - *Example*: It would mean a lot to me if you could review my code before I submit it.

#### 🧠 Grammar & Sentence Structure
- **Humble Advice Structuring:** `I'm not perfect but this helped me a lot, and if this sort of helped you too, it would [Positive Reaction].`
  - *Rule*: A very polite, non-arrogant way to offer advice or ask for support. 
  - *Example*: I'm not a senior developer yet, but this framework helped me a lot, and if it helps you too, it would be great to implement it.

#### 💡 Practical Application & Alternatives
- **How to use it**: Use "bring up" in emails or calls to politely mention an issue (e.g., "I just wanted to bring up the budget for next month").
- **Alternative ways to say it**: 
  - *Instead of "got something out of it":* Found it useful, learned something from it.
  - *Instead of "bring up":* Mention, raise.
