# Vocabulary Flashcards: Sport against poverty

## Flashcards
---
**Front:**
What is the noun for "severe emotional shock and pain caused by an extremely upsetting experience"?
**Back:**
[trauma] /ˈtrɔː.mə/ | noun | severe emotional shock and pain
**Episode:** "Most of the surfers have experienced trauma – emotional pain and shock..."
**Personal:** Her early life in the warzone left her with deep trauma.
---
**Front:**
What is the noun for "how a person feels about themselves, their personality, achievements and value"?
**Back:**
[self-image] /ˌselfˈɪm.ɪdʒ/ | noun | how you view your own value and ability
**Episode:** "This has given them a negative self-image..."
**Personal:** Regular exercise really helps boost my self-image.
---
**Front:**
What is the adjective for "difficult or demanding in a way that tests your ability or determination"?
**Back:**
[challenging] /ˈtʃæl.ɪn.dʒɪŋ/ | adjective | demanding or testing ability
**Episode:** "Surfing helps kids improve their self-image because it’s challenging..."
**Personal:** Learning to code has been one of the most challenging things I've ever done.
---
**Front:**
What is the noun for "an experienced and trusted advisor who helps a younger, less experienced person"?
**Back:**
[mentor] /ˈmen.tɔːr/ | noun | trusted advisor
**Episode:** "...be recognised by a coach or a mentor is very very good for your confidence as well."
**Personal:** I meet with my career mentor once a month for guidance.
---
**Front:**
What is the plural noun for "important skills everyone needs to solve everyday problems and issues"?
**Back:**
[life skills] /ˈlaɪf ˌskɪlz/ | plural noun | basic skills needed for everyday living
**Episode:** "...this teaches children important life skills – the basic skills needed to solve problems commonly encountered in everyday life."
**Personal:** Schools should teach practical life skills like personal finance.
---
**Front:**
What is the noun for "identifying the things you want to achieve and planning how to accomplish them"?
**Back:**
[goal setting] /ˈɡoʊl ˌset.ɪŋ/ | noun | planning what to achieve
**Episode:** "One important life skill is goal setting – deciding what things you want to achieve and how you plan to achieve them."
**Personal:** We focus on quarterly goal setting to keep our team focused.
