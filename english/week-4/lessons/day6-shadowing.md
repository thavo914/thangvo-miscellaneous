# Day 6: Shadowing Protocol

**Target Clip (Tim Conibear - Waves for Changes):**
> "Surfing also is quite difficult so you’re learning a very challenging skill that takes a lot of confidence. Very small successes which children have when they go into the water elicit a really big emotional response. If you come from a background of trauma quite often you’ll have a negative self-image and being able to try something new, achieve something new, be recognised by a coach or a mentor is very very good for your confidence as well."

## Stage 1: Auditory Priming (5 min)
- [ ] **Action:** Put away the transcript.
- [ ] **Action:** Listen to the 45-second target clip 3 times in a row. 
- [ ] **Goal:** Focus entirely on the music of the language—the rhythm, reductions, and intonation.

## Stage 2: Transcribed Echoing (15 min)
- [ ] **Action:** Open [lesson-transcript.md](./lesson-transcript.md).
- [ ] **Action:** Play the clip sentence by sentence. Pause after each sentence and repeat it aloud.
- [ ] **Goal:** Match the speaker's exact pronunciation, including connected speech features noted in the transcript. Do this 3 times for the whole clip.

## Stage 3: Synchronous Shadowing (20 min)
- [ ] **Action:** Keep the transcript open. 
- [ ] **Action:** Play the audio and speak *along* with the speaker, staying about 0.5 seconds behind them. 
- [ ] **Goal:** Blend your voice with the speaker's. Aim for at least 10 repetitions of the entire clip. It will feel chaotic at first—keep pushing through.

## Stage 4: Blind Shadowing (15 min)
- [ ] **Action:** Put the transcript away. Look away from the screen.
- [ ] **Action:** Shadow the speaker using ONLY the audio as your guide. 
- [ ] **Action:** Record your final 2 attempts on your phone.
- [ ] **Review:** Listen to your recordings. Are you hitting the primary stress points? Are you linking words properly?
