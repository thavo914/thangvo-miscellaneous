## Round 1 — Non-Technical Stakeholder (15 min)

**Setup**: The casino floor manager has no data background. They only care about business outcomes.

### Prompt for AI:
> *"Act as a Casino Floor Manager at a large resort casino. You have just heard that the data engineering team wants to implement a new real-time analytics pipeline. You are curious but skeptical — you don't understand technical terms. Ask me at least 4 questions about what this system does, how it affects your daily operations, and whether it is worth the investment. Use plain business language. After the conversation, give me feedback on: (1) how clearly I explained technical things without jargon, and (2) 2 phrases I could have used to sound more professional."*

### Your Goal:
- Explain what you do **without** using: pipeline, ETL, schema, API, SQL
- Use analogies (e.g. "Think of it like a conveyor belt for data...")
- Use at least 2 episode words naturally

## Round 2 — Peer Expert Discussion (15 min)

**Setup**: You are in a technical design meeting with a senior data engineer peer.

### Prompt for AI:
> *"Act as a Senior Data Engineer with 10 years of experience in cloud data warehousing (Snowflake, dbt, Airflow). I am a Mid-Level Data Engineer at a casino company. We are discussing whether to use a batch or streaming approach for our player session data ingestion. Challenge my assumptions. Ask about trade-offs, latency requirements, and failure scenarios. After the conversation, highlight 2 moments where my English was unclear or too informal for a technical meeting, and suggest more precise alternatives."*

### Your Goal:
- Use technical vocabulary confidently (pipeline, watermark, incremental load, idempotency)
- Incorporate episode words (challenging, resilience, goal setting) where natural
- Structure your arguments clearly: **Claim → Reason → Example**

## Round 3 — Job Interview (20 min)

**Setup**: You are interviewing for a Senior Data Engineer role at a casino & resort group.

### Prompt for AI:
> *"You are a hiring manager at a large casino & hospitality company, interviewing me for a Senior Data Engineer position. You care about both technical skills and soft skills — especially how I collaborate with non-technical teams. Ask me these 3 behavioral questions one at a time, wait for my full answer, then respond naturally before moving to the next: (1) 'Tell me about a challenging data project you led — what was the impact?' (2) 'Describe a time when you had to mentor a junior team member. What approach did you take?' (3) 'How do you handle situations where data quality issues affect business decisions?' After all 3, evaluate my overall performance: vocabulary range, answer structure (STAR method), and professional confidence. Suggest 2 phrases that would have made my answers stronger."*

### STAR Reminder:
| Element | What to say |
| :--- | :--- |
| **S**ituation | Set the scene briefly |
| **T**ask | What was your responsibility? |
| **A**ction | What specific steps did YOU take? |
| **R**esult | What was the measurable outcome? |
