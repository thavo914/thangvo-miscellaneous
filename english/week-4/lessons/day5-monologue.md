# Day 5: Monologue

## Phase 1: Warm-up (15 min)
- [ ] **Lexical Labeling:** Look around your room and name 10 objects in English.
- [ ] **Syntactic Narration:** Describe aloud exactly what you are doing right now (e.g., "I am sitting at my desk, looking at the screen, preparing to record my voice...").

## Phase 2: Recorded Monologue (30 min)
**Topic:** How can unconventional activities (like sports, arts, or hobbies) help people, especially children, overcome challenges and build life skills? Have you ever experienced this personally?

*Rules:*
- Speak continuously for 3-5 minutes.
- DO NOT write a script. Bullet points are okay.
- Try to use at least 3 target vocabulary words (*trauma, self-image, challenging, mentor, life skills, goal setting*).
- Record yourself on your phone or computer.

### Recommended Structure:
| Section | Time | Content |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Introduction** | 0:00 - 0:30 | Hook the listener. State your main view on the topic. |
| **Body Paragraph 1** | 0:30 - 1:30 | Discuss the general benefits of sports/hobbies on mental health. |
| **Body Paragraph 2** | 1:30 - 2:30 | Share a personal story or a specific example (like the ones from the episode). |
| **Conclusion** | 2:30 - 3:00 | Summarize your main point and end with a strong closing thought. |
