## Phase 2 — Recorded Monologue (30 min)

> **Choose ONE topic below**. Record yourself speaking for **2–3 minutes** without stopping.

### Topic Options

**Option A — Problem Story**
> *"Describe the most challenging data engineering problem you have faced at the casino. What was the impact? How did you solve it? What did it teach you about resilience?"*

**Option B — Explaining to Non-Technical Audience**
> *"Imagine you are presenting to a casino floor manager who has never heard of data pipelines. Explain what you do and why it matters for the business — using analogies from everyday life."*

**Option C — Mentorship / Growth**
> *"Describe a moment where someone acted as your mentor in your data career, OR describe how you help junior engineers develop their life skills in data engineering."*

**Option D — Vision**
> *"What is your vision for data engineering at your casino in the next 3 years? What specific goal setting are you doing to get there?"*

### Timing Structure

| Time | Section | What to say |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 0:00 – 0:30 | **Hook** | One strong opening sentence. State the topic clearly. |
| 0:30 – 1:30 | **Main Point** | Your core argument or story. Use at least 2 episode words. |
| 1:30 – 2:30 | **Concrete Example** | Specific situation from your work. Numbers and names are OK. |
| 2:30 – 3:00 | **Conclusion** | One sentence takeaway or call to action. |
