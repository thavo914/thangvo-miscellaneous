# Day 5 — Monologue + Shadowing: Sport against poverty

**Goal**: Speak about the episode topic in your own words (Monologue), then internalize the speaker's rhythm and connected speech (Shadowing).
**Time**: ~120 min total — split into two sittings if needed (Monologue in the morning, Shadowing in the evening works well).

---

## PART A — Monologue (60 min)

### Phase 1: Warm-up (15 min)
*Goal: Relax the vocal cords and activate your immediate English lexicon.*

- [ ] **Lexical Labeling**: Describe your workspace aloud for 1 minute.

---

### Phase 2: The Monologue (30 min)
*Goal: Fluency and creative application of target vocabulary.*

**Topic**: *"How can unconventional activities (like surfing or horse riding) build **life skills** and **resilience** in children facing **trauma**?"*

*Instructions*:
- Aim for 3–5 minutes of continuous speaking.
- Use at least 4 target words from the episode vocabulary.
- Record the first attempt. Listen. Re-record immediately. **Speed beats perfection.**

**Structure Template**:
| Section | Goal | Key Phrase to Use |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Introduction** | Hook & Viewpoint | *"Unconventional sports are more than just hobbies; they are..."* |
| **Example 1** | Describe a specific project (e.g., Cape Town surfing) | *"For children who have experienced **trauma**..."* |
| **Personal link** | Connect to your own experience or a goal | *"In my own professional life, **goal setting** works by..."* |
| **Conclusion** | Final punchy thought | *"Ultimately, building a positive **self-image** requires..."* |

**Record to file**: `week4-day5-monologue-v1.m4a` → listen → `week4-day5-monologue-v2.m4a`

## PART B — Shadowing (55 min)

**Clip**: Refer to `lesson-transcript.md` → `[TARGET]` section (~45s, Tim Conibear's primary clip).

---

### Stage 1 — Auditory Priming (5 min)
> No transcript. No reading. Just listen.

- Play the primary shadowing clip **3 times**.
- Close your eyes. Focus on rhythm, stress, and emotional tone.
- Write 3 words that describe how Tim sounds:

```text
1. _______________  2. _______________  3. _______________
```

---

### Stage 2 — Transcribed Echoing (15 min)
> Open the annotated transcript. Read along sentence by sentence.

- After each sentence, **pause and repeat** from memory.
- Pay attention to: `_` catenation · `(t)`/`(d)` elision · **BOLD** stress · [up]/[down] intonation

---

### Stage 3 — Synchronous Shadowing (20 min)
> Speak **0.5 seconds behind** the audio. Transcript in front of you.

| Round | Focus | Reps |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1–3 | Follow along, don't worry about accuracy | 3× |
| 4–6 | Match stress and connected speech patterns | 3× |
| 7–10 | Match emotional tone and pace exactly | 4×+ |

> 💡 If you fall behind, skip ahead — don't stop. Keeping pace is the training.

---

### Stage 4 — Blind Shadowing (15 min)
> No transcript. Record both attempts.

- **Attempt 1**: Shadow from memory. Focus on fluency.
- **Attempt 2**: After a short break, shadow again. Notice what improved.

**Record to file**: `week4-day5-shadow-attempt1.m4a` and `week4-day5-shadow-attempt2.m4a`
