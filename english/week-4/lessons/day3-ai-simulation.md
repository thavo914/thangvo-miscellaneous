# Day 3: AI Simulation (60 min)

## Guidelines
- Roleplay as an **Expert Founder** or **Social Worker**.
- AI Level: **B1-B2 Intermediate**.
- Request feedback on **Emotional Vocabulary** and **Empathy**.

---

## Round 1: The Journalist Interview (15 min)
**Role**: You are Tim Conibear (founder of Waves for Changes). The AI is a journalist from *People Fixing the World*.
**Prompt**:
> "I want to practice my interview English. I am Tim Conibear, founder of the surfing project in Cape Town. You are a journalist interviewing me for a BBC programme. Ask me how surfing helps children with 'trauma' and a 'negative self-image'. Challenge me on why surfing is better than traditional therapy. After 3 exchanges, give me feedback on my usage of the words 'challenging' and 'mentor'. Suggest 2 more descriptive ways to talk about 'success' in sports."

---

## Round 2: Pitching for Funding (15 min)
**Role**: You are an organizer for the Ebony Club in Brixton. The AI is a skeptical city council member.
**Topic**: Defending unconventional sports (Horse Riding).
**Prompt**:
> "You are a skeptical city council member who thinks horse riding is only for 'the elite'. I am a volunteer for the Ebony Club in Brixton. I am asking for funding to expand our program to help more disadvantaged youth. I must explain how this builds 'life skills' and 'resilience'. Start by asking: 'Why should we spend taxpayer money on horses when we need more books in schools?' At the end, tell me if you are 'convinced' by my argument and point out 3 strong phrases I used."

---

## Round 3: The Startup Consultant (20 min)
**Role**: You are Oliver Percovich (founder of Skateistan). The AI is a startup consultant helping you expand 'Skateistan' to a new country.
**Topic**: Scaling social impact.
**Prompt**:
> "I am Oliver Percovich, the founder of Skateistan. You are a business consultant. I want to expand my skateboarding project to another country with low-income backgrounds. We need to focus on 'goal setting' for girls and children with disabilities. Ask me 3 questions about my 'founding story' in Afghanistan and how we ensure 'confidence' in our students. Evaluate my responses for 'social impact' vocabulary and grammatical precision. Suggest 2 more professional phrases I could use for a donor report."

---

## Review (10 min)
1. Look at the "improved" phrases the AI gave you.
2. Record yourself saying them **3 times** each with natural intonation.

---

## Day 3 Deliverables
- [ ] Journalist interview (3+ exchanges).
- [ ] Council debate completed.
- [ ] Expansion consultation completed.

> **Key Insight for Today**: When talking about social projects, "why" matters as much as "what". Use emotional vocabulary to connect your actions to human results.
