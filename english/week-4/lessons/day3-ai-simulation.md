# Day 3: AI Simulation

## Prompt Setup
Copy and paste this into ChatGPT or Claude:
> Act as my B1-B2 level English conversation partner. We are using the "Sport against poverty" BBC 6 Minute English episode as our foundation. 
> Rules: Do not correct my mistakes during the conversation. Keep responses concise. Ask one question at a time. After we finish all 3 rounds, provide a feedback summary on my grammar, vocabulary usage, and naturalness.

## Round 1 - Discussion (15 min)
**Role:** AI acts as a curious colleague.
**Topic:** Have you ever tried a sport that helped you build confidence or deal with stress? Do you agree that sports can solve deep psychological trauma?

## Round 2 - Expert Role-play (15 min)
**Role:** You are Tim Conibear (founder of Waves for Changes) or Oliver Percovich (founder of Skateistan). AI is a journalist interviewing you about your project.
**Topic:** Explain how your chosen sport helps kids and what challenges you faced starting the organization. Try to use the words: *challenging*, *trauma*, *self-image*, *mentor*.

## Round 3 - Devil's Advocate / Startup Scenario (20 min)
**Role:** AI acts as a skeptical city council member. You are asking for funding to start a new sporting club in a disadvantaged neighborhood. 
**Topic:** Defend why funding should go to a sports program instead of traditional education or housing. Explain the concepts of *life skills* and *goal setting*.

## Review (10 min)
- [ ] Look at the feedback provided by the AI at the end of the simulation.
- [ ] Pick 3 "Improved" sentences the AI suggested.
- [ ] Read each improved sentence aloud loudly 3 times.
