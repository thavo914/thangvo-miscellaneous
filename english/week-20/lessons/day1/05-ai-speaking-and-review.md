# Day 1 - Exercise 5: AI Speaking Simulation & Daily Review (75 Mins)

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 3 (60 Mins)**: Conversational speaking practice with AI via ChatGPT Voice Mode.  
> **Block 4 (15 Mins)**: Anki logging, error tracking, and day-end consolidation.

---

## 🤖 Block 3: AI Speaking Practice (60 Mins)

Open ChatGPT Voice Mode on your mobile phone or desktop app. Choose a natural voice (e.g., *Juniper*, *Breeze*, or *Cove*).

### 🎯 Primary Scenario: Morning Standup & Urgent Review
Start your roleplay with the following prompt:

```text
Let's roleplay a morning engineering standup. You are my teammate Sarah. Ask me: 'Morning Thang! How are things looking on the streaming ingestion task?' I will share my update using 'I'm going to go ahead and deploy', ask you for a quick check using 'Would you be able to go have a quick look now, please?', and negotiate our release scope. Correct my pronunciation and natural flow after each turn.
```

---

### Suggested Exercises (AI Prompts):

1. **Rapid Q&A:**
   > "Act as my English tutor. Ask me random English questions ranging from easy to advanced (topics: daily life, work, opinions, etc.). I will answer in English as quickly as possible. After I answer, please correct my grammar and vocabulary, and suggest more natural, native-like ways to express my ideas."

2. **Instant Translation (Reverse Shadowing):**
   > "Give me a common Vietnamese situation or daily phrase. I will try to translate it into English out loud within 3–5 seconds. After my attempt, please provide the most natural English translation so I can compare."

3. **Role-play:**
   > "Let's do a role-play. Please choose one of these topics: ordering at a restaurant, a job interview, traveling/at the airport, or discussing work with a colleague. We will have a back-and-forth conversation entirely in English."

## 📝 Block 4: Review & Consolidation (15 Mins)

### 1. Anki / Flashcard Logging
Export the following high-frequency items into your Anki deck:

| Front (Prompt / Vietnamese) | Back (Native Target Structure) |
| :--- | :--- |
| *Tôi chuẩn bị tiến hành triển khai bản vá ngay bây giờ* | `I'm going to go ahead and deploy the patch right now.` |
| *Liệu bạn có thể thu xếp nghía qua xem giúp mình bây giờ được không?* | `Would you be able to go have a quick look now, please?` |
| *Tôi nghĩ tôi có thể thu xếp được, miễn là...* | `I think I can make that work, as long as...` |
| *Mọi người trong team đều muốn làm tròn phần việc của mình* | `Everyone on the team is eager to pull their weight.` |
| *Khi cuộc họp kết thúc / bế mạc* | `at the conclusion of the meeting` |
