# Day 4 - Exercise 5: AI Technical Speaking Simulation & Daily Review (75 Mins)

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 3 (60 Mins)**: Conversational speaking practice with AI via ChatGPT Voice Mode focused on cloud costs, hardware diagnostics, and architecture trade-offs.  
> **Block 4 (15 Mins)**: Technical Anki logging, error tracking, and day-end consolidation.

---

## 🤖 Block 3: AI Speaking Practice (60 Mins)

Open ChatGPT Voice Mode. Choose a confident, professional voice.

### 🎯 Primary Technical Scenario: Addressing Vendor Price Hikes & Flaky Services
Start your voice roleplay with the following prompt:

```text
Let's roleplay a technical review meeting. You are Alex, the Principal Architect. Tell me: 'The managed database vendor just announced a 25% price increase. How do you propose we handle this?' I will explain our infrastructure options using 'a money pit', 'throw money down the drain', 'act up', and 'as those things often do', and propose migrating cold data. Challenge my cost assumptions and correct my English after each turn.
```

---

### Suggested Exercises (AI Prompts):

1. **Rapid Q&A:**
   > "Act as my English tutor. Ask me random English questions ranging from easy to advanced (topics: daily life, work, opinions, etc.). I will answer in English as quickly as possible. After I answer, please correct my grammar and vocabulary, and suggest more natural, native-like ways to express my ideas."

2. **Instant Translation (Reverse Shadowing):**
   > "Give me a common Vietnamese situation or daily phrase. I will try to translate it into English out loud within 3–5 seconds. After my attempt, please provide the most natural English translation so I can compare."

3. **Role-play:**
   > "Let's do a role-play. Please choose one of these topics: ordering at a restaurant, a job interview, traveling/at the airport, or discussing work with a colleague. We will have a back-and-forth conversation entirely in English."

## 📝 Block 4: Review & Consolidation (15 Mins)

### 1. Anki / Flashcard Logging
Add these high-utility technical vocabulary cards to your deck:

| Front (Prompt / Vietnamese) | Back (Native Target Structure) |
| :--- | :--- |
| *Cỗ máy ngốn tiền / Hố không đáy bảo trì* | `a money pit` |
| *Ném tiền qua cửa sổ / Lãng phí ngân sách* | `throw money down the drain` |
| *Nhà cung cấp đám mây đã tăng giá* | `the cloud vendor has raised its prices` |
| *Hệ thống / Phần cứng dở chứng, chập chờn* | `act up` |
| *Như mấy chuyện đó thường hay bị* | `as those things often do` |
| *Cuộc họp sẽ được tổ chức trên Zoom* | `The meeting will be held on Zoom.` |

### 2. Error Log & Self-Evaluation Checklist
- [ ] Did I avoid saying *will hold on Zoom* and use *will be held on Zoom* instead?
- [ ] Did I correctly match `often do` for action verbs and `often are` for adjectives?
- [ ] Did I use `acting up` instead of simply saying *has a bug*?
- [ ] Did I practice speaking aloud with AI for at least 25 minutes?
