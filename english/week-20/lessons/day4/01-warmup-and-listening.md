# Day 4 - Exercise 1: Warm-up Reflex & Active Listening (35–45 Mins)

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 1 Focus**: Focused acoustic decoding (20–30 mins) + Technical context immersion (15 mins) as we launch Topic 2: Cloud Cost Defense & Infrastructure Reliability.

---

## 🎧 Part 1: Active Listening - TOEIC Dictation (20–30 Mins)

### Goal
Decode fast-paced technical and commercial announcements, vendor contract discussions, and equipment maintenance updates.

### Instructions
1. Select 3 TOEIC listening clips (Parts 3 or 4) focusing on budget cutbacks, service price adjustments, or IT system maintenance.
2. Complete full word-for-word dictation, pausing as necessary.
3. Compare with the audio script and highlight weak vowels, elisions, and technical terminology in red.

---

## 🎙️ Part 2: Technical Context Immersion - System Diagnosis Out Loud (15 Mins)

### Goal
Warm up your vocal cords by narrating a real technical trade-off regarding system performance and cloud costs.

### Instructions
Speak aloud for 3 minutes about a cluster or software service in your infrastructure that consumes excessive budget or requires frequent maintenance. Incorporate at least two target phrases:
- `a money pit`
- `throw money down the drain`
- `has raised its prices`
- `act up`
- `as those things often do`

### 💡 Worked Example (Speaking Out Loud)
> *"Looking at our AWS bill this month, our provisioned RDS database has essentially become **a money pit**. We're paying for high provisioned IOPS that sit idle 80% of the time, which is like **throwing money down the drain**. To make matters worse, the cloud vendor **has raised its prices** on old instance families by nearly 15%. Over the weekend, the secondary replica was **acting up** due to connection pool exhaustion, **as unoptimized database clusters often do**. We need to migrate historical audit tables to S3 storage immediately."*
