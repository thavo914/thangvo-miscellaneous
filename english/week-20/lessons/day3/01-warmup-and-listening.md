# Day 3 - Exercise 1: Warm-up Reflex & Active Listening (35–45 Mins)

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 1 Focus**: Dramatic situational adaptability (15 mins) + Intensive listening decoding (20–30 mins).

---

## 🤖 Part 1: Speaking Reflex - Unconventional AI Voice Roleplay (15 Mins)

### Goal
Break rigid speaking patterns and build conversational poise by roleplaying a high-friction or demanding workplace scenario with AI.

### Prompt to Speak Out Loud or Use in Voice Mode:
> *"Act as an impatient project manager who wants me to take on weekend on-call shifts and interview five candidates this week. I will diplomatically decline extra shifts while offering reasonable alternatives using 'That won't be necessary', 'not be available as much as the job requires', and 'at the conclusion of the meeting'. Keep pushing back to test my boundary-setting skills."*

### 💡 Worked Example (Opening the Roleplay)
> *"Hi Mark, thanks for reaching out. While I understand we're in a rush to **fill the position**, taking on weekend shifts on top of the warehouse migration simply isn't feasible right now. I will **not be available as much as the job requires** over the weekend. However, I can help **conduct the interviews** on Thursday morning. As for booking an emergency planning call tonight, **that won't be necessary**; I will summarize my candidate feedback **at the conclusion of the meeting**."*

---

## 🎧 Part 2: Active Listening - TOEIC Dictation (20–30 Mins)

### Goal
Sharpen listening comprehension of boundary-setting, polite negotiations, and scheduling nuances in workplace dialogues.

### Instructions
1. Select 3 TOEIC Part 3 audio dialogues dealing with hiring, schedules, or workload.
2. Transcribe the conversations word-for-word onto paper or screen.
3. Check against the audio script and highlight missed phonological contractions and prepositions in red.
