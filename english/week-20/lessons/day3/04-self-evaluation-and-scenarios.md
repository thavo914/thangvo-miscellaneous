## 🎯 Situational Engineering Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Setting On-Call Boundaries During a Sprint Crunch
* **Situation**: Your manager asks if you can take an extra on-call rotation this weekend to support an ad-hoc marketing push, but you are already scheduled to run database index maintenance on Friday night.
* **Your Task**: Politely decline the weekend shift while explaining your Friday commitments using `not be available as much as the job requires` and `That won't be necessary`.

<details>
<summary>💡 Click to View Model Workplace Response</summary>

> *"Hi Mark, thanks for checking in. I'm already scheduled to lead our database re-indexing on Friday night, so I will **not be available as much as the job requires** over the weekend. However, I've already tested our automated alerts, so having a dedicated engineer on standby 24/7 **won't be necessary**; the system will page the secondary rotation only if critical thresholds are breached."*
</details>

---

### Scenario 2: Delegating Candidate Interview During an Outage
* **Situation**: You are supposed to interview a candidate in 30 minutes, but a sudden ingestion pipeline failure requires your immediate attention.
* **Your Task**: Ask your senior teammate to conduct the interview on your behalf, explaining that you will share the feedback notes at the conclusion of the meeting.

<details>
<summary>💡 Click to View Model Workplace Response</summary>

> *"Hey Sarah, our streaming consumer just crashed and dropped thousands of events. **Would you be able to conduct the candidate interview** at 2 PM on my behalf, please? I need to stay focused on debugging the offset failure. I already had the rubric prepared, and we can sync up on candidate notes **at the conclusion of the meeting**."*
</details>

## 📋 Comprehensive Self-Evaluation Checklist

Check off each milestone to verify your Topic 1 mastery before we transition to Topic 2:

- [ ] Can I use `Would you be able to...` automatically when asking for urgent favors?
- [ ] Do I drop `to/and` instinctively when using `go + [Bare Verb]` in the base form (*go check, go grab*)?
- [ ] Can I use `I think I can make that work, as long as...` to accept requests conditionally?
- [ ] Did I eliminate verb stacking (*going ahead and starting installing* ❌)?
- [ ] Can I transcreate Vietnamese workplace dialogues into English without literal word-by-word stumbling?
