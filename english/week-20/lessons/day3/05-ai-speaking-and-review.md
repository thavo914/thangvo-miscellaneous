## 🤖 Block 3: AI Speaking Practice (60 Mins)

Open ChatGPT Voice Mode and practice speaking with AI using today's translated dialogue and monologue.

### 🎯 Primary Scenario: Boundary Setting & Task Delegation
Start your voice session with this prompt:

```text
Let's roleplay an engineering conversation. You are my Engineering Lead, Hoang. You want me to conduct three candidate interviews next week, but I am on-call and optimizing our database. I will set boundaries politely using 'not be available as much as the job requires' and 'That won't be necessary', and propose a compromise. Correct my phrasing after each response.
```

---

### Suggested Exercises (AI Prompts):

1. **Rapid Q&A:**
   > "Act as my English tutor. Ask me random English questions ranging from easy to advanced (topics: daily life, work, opinions, etc.). I will answer in English as quickly as possible. After I answer, please correct my grammar and vocabulary, and suggest more natural, native-like ways to express my ideas."

2. **Instant Translation (Reverse Shadowing):**
   > "Give me a common Vietnamese situation or daily phrase. I will try to translate it into English out loud within 3–5 seconds. After my attempt, please provide the most natural English translation so I can compare."

3. **Role-play:**
   > "Let's do a role-play. Please choose one of these topics: ordering at a restaurant, a job interview, traveling/at the airport, or discussing work with a colleague. We will have a back-and-forth conversation entirely in English."

## 📝 Block 4: Topic 1 Review & Consolidation (15 Mins)

### 1. Anki / Flashcard Logging
Add these boundary and hiring expressions to your deck:
* Front: *Đang tìm kiếm nhân sự mới* → Back: `be looking to hire`
* Front: *Lấp vào vị trí còn trống* → Back: `fill the position`
* Front: *Tiến hành các buổi phỏng vấn* → Back: `conduct the interviews`
* Front: *Không thể đáp ứng thời gian nhiều như công việc đòi hỏi* → Back: `not be available as much as the job requires`
* Front: *Không cần thiết phải làm vậy đâu* → Back: `That won't be necessary.`
