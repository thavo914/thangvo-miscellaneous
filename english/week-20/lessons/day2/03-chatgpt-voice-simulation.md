# Day 2 - Exercise 3: ChatGPT Voice Simulation Prompt

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 3 Setup**: Launch ChatGPT Voice Mode and paste this exact prompt into the chat window to initiate a realistic workplace negotiation simulation.

---

## 🎙️ Voice Simulation Prompt

Copy and paste the prompt below into ChatGPT:

```text
Act as my product manager. Tell me you need three extra metrics delivered by tomorrow morning. I will negotiate the scope and deadline with you using phrases like I think I can make that work, meet halfway, and as long as. Challenge my reasoning and keep the dialogue natural.
```

---

## 🎯 Target Performance Objectives
During this 15–20 minute voice session, aim to naturally produce:
1. `I think I can make that work, as long as...` (when agreeing under reasonable conditions).
2. `meet halfway` (when proposing a realistic trade-off).
3. `Would you be able to confirm...` (when clarifying business priorities politely).
4. `pull our weight` (when affirming your team's dedication to project quality).
