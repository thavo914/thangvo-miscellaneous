# Day 2 - Exercise 5: AI Speaking Practice & Review (75 Mins)

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 3 (60 Mins)**: Dynamic back-and-forth speaking practice with AI via ChatGPT Voice Mode.  
> **Block 4 (15 Mins)**: Feedback logging, error tracking, and Anki repetition.

---

## 🤖 Block 3: AI Speaking Simulation (60 Mins)

Use the simulation prompt from `03-chatgpt-voice-simulation.md` to conduct your primary negotiation roleplay.

### Suggested Exercises (AI Prompts):

1. **Rapid Q&A:**
   > "Act as my English tutor. Ask me random English questions ranging from easy to advanced (topics: daily life, work, opinions, etc.). I will answer in English as quickly as possible. After I answer, please correct my grammar and vocabulary, and suggest more natural, native-like ways to express my ideas."

2. **Instant Translation (Reverse Shadowing):**
   > "Give me a common Vietnamese situation or daily phrase. I will try to translate it into English out loud within 3–5 seconds. After my attempt, please provide the most natural English translation so I can compare."

3. **Role-play:**
   > "Let's do a role-play. Please choose one of these topics: ordering at a restaurant, a job interview, traveling/at the airport, or discussing work with a colleague. We will have a back-and-forth conversation entirely in English."

## 📝 Block 4: Review & Consolidation (15 Mins)

### 1. Anki / Flashcard Logging
Export the following cards to reinforce Day 2 target vocabulary:
* Front: *Tôi nghĩ tôi có thể thu xếp ổn thỏa, miễn là...* → Back: `I think I can make that work, as long as...`
* Front: *Tùy bạn quyết định đấy / Bạn có toàn quyền* → Back: `It's your call.`
* Front: *Thời gian xử lý rất gấp / Deadline gấp* → Back: `a tight turnaround`
* Front: *Thống nhất phương án thỏa hiệp / Mỗi bên nhượng bộ một nửa* → Back: `meet halfway`
* Front: *Nhận được lời khen ngợi nhiệt liệt / đánh giá xuất sắc* → Back: `receive a rave review`
