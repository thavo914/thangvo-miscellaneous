# Day 2 - Exercise 4: Interactive Shadowing & Pacing Guide

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 2 (Part C)**: Internalize native conversational rhythm, eliminate monotone delivery, and master breath boundaries using visual prosody marks.

---

## 🎵 Visual Rhythm & Stress Markers

Read the lines aloud, pausing slightly at each slash (`/`) and stressing the **BOLD CAPITALIZED** words with slightly higher pitch and volume.

### Line 1: Polite Urgent Request
> *"Would you be **ABLE** to / go have a **QUICK LOOK** now, / **PLEASE**? / I'll **GO CHECK** the consumer lag / on **GRAFANA** right now."*

* **Rhythm Breakdown**:
  * `Would you be` → Pronounced smoothly together: */wʊdʒə bi/*.
  * `go have a quick look` → Seamless chunk without pause: */ɡoʊ hæv ə kwɪk lʊk/*.
  * Notice the upward polite inflection on **`PLEASE`**, followed by a decisive downward pitch on **`GRAFANA right now`**.

---

### Line 2: Professional Deadline Compromise
> *"I think I can **MAKE THAT WORK**, / as long as he lets us **PUSH** / the **SECONDARY** reports / to **NEXT SPRINT**."*

* **Rhythm Breakdown**:
  * `I think I can` → Contracted and rapid: */aɪ θɪŋk aɪ kən/*.
  * **`MAKE THAT WORK`** → Strongly emphasized core verbal phrase.
  * Pause at `/ as long as /` to give the listener time to absorb your condition.

---

## 🔁 3-Step Shadowing Protocol

1. **Listen & Tap**: Tap your finger on the desk at every stressed word to match the native beat.
2. **Mute Shadowing**: Whisper along while silently miming mouth shapes and breathing points.
3. **Full Voice Shadowing**: Speak simultaneously at full volume 3–5 times until your delivery feels effortless.
