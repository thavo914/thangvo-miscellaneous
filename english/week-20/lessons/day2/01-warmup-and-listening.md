# Day 2 - Exercise 1: Warm-up Reflex & Active Listening (35–45 Mins)

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 1 Focus**: Rapid 60-second spontaneous descriptive fluency (15 mins) + Acoustic decoding through dictation (20–30 mins).

---

## ⏱️ Part 1: Speaking Reflex - The 60-Second Random Object Pitch (15 Mins)

### Goal
Eliminate vocal hesitation, overcome mental blankness, and maintain continuous rhythm for 60 seconds without pausing longer than 2 seconds.

### Instructions
1. Pick any physical object nearby (e.g., your desk microphone, mechanical keyboard, coffee mug, or notebook).
2. Start a 60-second timer on your phone.
3. Speak out loud without stopping, describing the object's features, how it supports your daily workflow, and how it helps you manage stressful deadlines and tight turnarounds.
4. Intentionally integrate at least two of today's expressions:
   - `I think I can make that work`
   - `It's your call`
   - `a mix of both`
   - `meet halfway`
   - `tight turnaround`

### 💡 Worked Example (The Mechanical Keyboard)
> *"This mechanical keyboard in front of me is an essential tool for my daily engineering productivity. Whenever our team faces a **tight turnaround** on an incident fix, having responsive tactile feedback keeps my typing fast and accurate. A colleague recently asked whether I preferred linear or clicky switches, and I told him **a mix of both** is ideal for coding long hours. In sprint planning, when our lead asks whether we should automate a script or document the workflow first, I usually say, '**It's your call**, but if we **meet halfway**, **I think I can make that work** by delivering the automated test today and wrapping up documentation tomorrow.' This keyboard has accompanied me through countless release cycles."*

---

## 🎧 Part 2: Active Listening - TOEIC Dictation (20–30 Mins)

### Goal
Refine auditory perception of conversational cadence, connected speech, and elision in workplace dialogues.

### Instructions
1. Select 3 short workplace listening passages (TOEIC Parts 3 or 4).
2. Listen once for general context; on the second pass, transcribe sentence-by-sentence word-for-word.
3. Compare against the transcript and circle missed linking words, reductions, and articles in red.
