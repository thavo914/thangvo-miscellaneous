# Day 5 - Exercise 1: Warm-up Reflex & Active Listening (35–45 Mins)

> [!NOTE]
> **Block 1 Focus**: Spoken agility through rapid dialectical argumentation (15 mins) + Intensive technical listening decoding (20–30 mins).

---

## 😈 Part 1: Speaking Reflex - The "Devil's Advocate" Challenge (15 Mins)

### Goal
Train cognitive flexibility and conversational fluency by stating an engineering opinion and immediately refuting it within 30 seconds using contrastive linkers (*However*, *Having said that*, *On the other hand*).

### Instructions
1. Pick a technical controversial statement below.
2. Spend 15 seconds arguing in favor of it.
3. Immediately pivot and spend 15 seconds arguing against it, integrating at least two target phrases:
   - `guarantee flexibility`
   - `the maximum amount of`
   - `there is something involved`
   - `trade-off between cost and latency`

#### Prompt Options:
* *Option A*: "All engineering teams should migrate away from managed databases to self-hosted open-source clusters to save costs."
* *Option B*: "We should store all analytical data in hot memory storage regardless of cost."

### 💡 Worked Example (Option A)
> *"**Argument Pro (15s)**: In my view, migrating to open-source database clusters makes complete sense because it allows us to **guarantee flexibility** across multi-cloud environments without being locked into a vendor that raises its prices annually. We can extract **the maximum amount of** value from our hardware budget without paying arbitrary license fees.  
> 
> **Devil's Advocate Counter (15s)**: **Having said that**, there is significant operational overhead **involved** in managing replication and automated failovers in-house. While self-hosting cuts software bills, the engineering hours required to troubleshoot when the cluster **acts up** often exceed the cloud subscription costs."*

---

## 🎧 Part 2: Active Listening - TOEIC Dictation (20–30 Mins)

### Goal
Decode fast spoken technical updates, server status reports, and enterprise negotiations.

### Instructions
1. Select 3 TOEIC listening clips (Parts 3 or 4) covering system upgrades, scheduling, or technical contracts.
2. Complete word-for-word dictation, pausing as necessary.
3. Compare against the transcript and highlight missed linking sounds, prepositions, and weak forms in red.
