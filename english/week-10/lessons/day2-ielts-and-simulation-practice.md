# Day 2: IELTS & Simulation Practice (Week 10 - Topic 1 Focus)

## 🗣️ 1. IELTS Speaking Practice Questions

### Part 1: Interview (Casual Questions)
1. *Do you enjoy catching up with your coworkers during breaks? Why or why not?*
2. *What do you usually like to do to keep active on the weekends?*
3. *Is there any new activity or restaurant you have been meaning to try recently?*

### Part 2: Cue Card (Long Turn)
> **Describe a pleasant or interesting casual conversation you had with a colleague at work.**
> 
> You should say:
> - Who you spoke to and when it happened
> - What you talked about
> - Where you had the conversation
> - And explain why you enjoyed or found this casual conversation interesting.

### Part 3: Analytical Discussion (Deeper Questions)
1. *Do you think having casual "coffee machine chats" plays an important part in team relationships?*
2. *How has the way colleagues communicate during work breaks changed due to remote work or hybrid policies?*

## 🤖 2. ChatGPT Voice Mode Simulation Prompt
Copy and paste this prompt into the ChatGPT mobile app, click the headphone icon (Voice Mode), and start practicing your spoken English in a natural office setting.

```text
Act as "Sarah", a friendly and energetic Marketing Manager at our casino operations office. I am a Data Engineer on your floor. 
We just bumped into each other at the office kitchen coffee machine on a Friday morning. 

Your goals:
1. Start the conversation with a warm greeting: "Hey! Getting your morning coffee?"
2. Chat casually with me about how the week went and ask what I am up to this weekend.
3. Challenge me slightly to explain my plans and describe my hobby (like running or gaming) naturally.
4. Keep your replies short and conversational (2-3 sentences max).
5. At the very end of our chat (when I say goodbye), provide brief, friendly feedback on:
   - Any grammar mistakes I made (especially on Future Continuous, Present Perfect, or "used to").
   - Any words I could change to sound more natural, native-like, and B1-B2 conversational.
```

## 🎵 4. Interactive Shadowing & Pacing Guide
*Practice reading these lines out loud. Slashes `/` represent brief natural pauses. **UPPERCASE** words indicate word stress and emphasis.*

### Line 1 (Alex - Sharing plans):
> "Well / this SATURDAY / I'll be participating in a RUNNING race nearby. // I USED to play video games all weekend / but NOW / I prefer to KEEP ACTIVE."

### Line 2 (Sarah - Expressing intent & lack of time):
> "Oh wow / that's IMPRESSIVE! // I have PLANS to check out that new ITALIAN restaurant downtown. // I've been MEANING to try it / but I haven't HAD the time."
