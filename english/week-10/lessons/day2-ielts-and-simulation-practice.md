## 🗣️ 1. IELTS Speaking Practice Questions

### Part 1: Interview (Casual Questions)
1. *Do you enjoy catching up with your coworkers during breaks? Why or why not?*
- Yes, I do. Deep work takes a lot of energy, so it is nice to take a break and chat with my coworkers.
- Absolutely! Catching up with coworkers is a great way to relax and recharge. It helps me feel connected to my team and get to know them better on a personal level.
- It depends. Some days I need to focus on my work, but other days I enjoy chatting with my colleagues. It really depends on how busy I am and how I'm feeling.
2. *What do you usually like to do to keep active on the weekends?*
- I'm currently training for a half marathon, so my weekends are pretty much booked with long runs and training sessions. I usually wake up early on Saturday morning to get my miles in before it gets too hot outside.
- To keep active, I try to balance physical exercise with mental rest. I usually go for a long run on Satyrday morning then spend the afternoon. Sunday I usually take a ride around the city to get some fresh air and sun shine.
- It depends. Some weekends I just want to sleep as much as possible, but other weekends I like to go for a long run or a bike ride.
3. *Is there any new activity or restaurant you have been meaning to try recently?*
- Yes, actually I have been meaning to register for a swimming class for a few weeks. I want to participate in a half ironman next year. I'm quite good at running and cycling, but I need to improve my swimming.
- Yes, there are some local retaurants I've been meaning to try, but I haven't had the time yet. I also want to go to some places I've never been to since I've just relocated to this city for 3 months.
- To be honest, there are many new activities I've been meaning to try recently, but I haven't had the time yet. I want to go to some local cafes, restaurants, and outdoor places.

### Part 2: Cue Card (Long Turn)
> **Describe a pleasant or interesting casual conversation you had with a colleague at work.**
> 
> You should say:
> - Who you spoke to and when it happened
> - What you talked about
> - Where you had the conversation
> - And explain why you enjoyed or found this casual conversation interesting.
1. I would like to take about a very helpful conversation I had with my colleague at work, just a few days ago. We were standing near the coffee machine taking a quick morning break. We started taking about work, specifically about a data warehouse I am currently building from scratch. I am working on the real-time streaming side of the project, and I wanted to ask for advice about using Python. We ened up having a great discussion about different ways to run unit and mock tests for the code. We had this conversation right there in the office kitchen while waiting for our drinks. I found this conversation really interesting and plesant for a few reasons. Since I mostly work as a solo engineer, it can sometimes feel a bit lonely. Having a smart teammate to talk to makes the complex problems much easier to think through and solve. Plus, it is always nice to take a break from staring at the screen and have a real, face-to-face conversation with someone, even if it's just for a few minutes.
