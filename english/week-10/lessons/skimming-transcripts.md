## 📹 Transcript 1: Topic 1 (Office Small Talk & Weekend Plans)

### 1. Document Format & Pacing
- **Title**: *Mastering Casual Office Small Talk & Sharing Weekend Plans*
- **Target Duration**: 60 seconds (Word Count: ~135 words)
- **Speaking Speed**: ~130 WPM (words per minute) for a steady, natural B1-B2 rhythm.

```text
[00:00] In a busy tech company, it's really important to connect with colleagues. 
I have just bumped into a manager from marketing in the kitchen. 
We decided to catch up on our weeks. 
She asked about my Saturday plans, and I shared that this Saturday, I'll be participating in a running race. 
In the past, I used to play video games all weekend, but recently, I've been trying to keep active. 
[00:20] I've been meaning to try a new Italian restaurant downtown, but I haven't had the time. 
Since it's supposed to rain on Sunday, I might just stay home and take it easy. 
[00:40] I'm really looking forward to resting because next week will be hectic. 
These brief coffee break chats play a huge part in building great office relationships. 
[00:60]
```

### 2. 10-Second Skimming Training
*Sweep your eyes across ONLY the bold keywords in under 10 seconds to grasp the main ideas.*

* Tech company / **connect** / colleagues.
* **Bumped into** / marketing manager / kitchen.
* **Catch up** / weeks.
* Saturday / **participating** / **running race**.
* Past / **used to play** / video games / now / **keep active**.
* **Meaning to try** / Italian restaurant / **haven't had time**.
* Sunday / **rain** / **take it easy** / home.
* **Looking forward to resting** / next week / hectic.
* **Coffee break chats** / **building relationships**.

#### Skimming Check:
1. *What is the speaker doing this Saturday?*
2. *Why does the speaker plan to stay home on Sunday?*

## 📹 Transcript 2: Topic 2 (Asking for Help & Explaining Simply)

### 1. Document Format & Pacing
- **Title**: *How to Ask for Technical Help & Describe Complex Pipelines Simply*
- **Target Duration**: 60 seconds (Word Count: ~135 words)
- **Speaking Speed**: ~130 WPM (words per minute) for a steady, natural B1-B2 rhythm.

```text
[00:00] At some point at work, you will inevitably run into a technical block. 
Yesterday, I was having some trouble with a database schema, and I was not sure how to fix the lag. 
I went to a senior engineer and asked: "I was wondering if you could take a quick look at this query when you have a minute?" 
[00:20] He was able to point me in the right direction. 
While debugging, a business manager walked over to ask about the delay. 
Explaining these pipelines simply is crucial. 
[00:40] If you put it simply, it's basically like a water filter trying to process too much water. 
Unless we optimize the database indexes, the system will remain slow. 
It's no rush, but keeping stakeholders in the loop is essential for smooth collaboration. 
[00:60]
```
