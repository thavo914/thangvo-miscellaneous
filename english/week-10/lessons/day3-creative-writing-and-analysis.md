## 🎓 2. Band 6.5 - 7.0 Cue Card Sample Answer
*Here is a realistic model response answering the Day 2 Part 2 Cue Card: **Describe a pleasant or interesting casual conversation you had with a colleague at work.***

### Model Answer:
> "To be honest, I'm generally a bit quiet at work, but last Friday morning, I had a really pleasant chat with Sarah, who is a manager in our marketing department. We happened to bump into each other in the office kitchen while we were both waiting for the coffee machine to brew.
> 
> We started by **catching up** on how exhausting the week had been. She then asked if I **had plans** for the weekend. I told her that **this Saturday, I'll be participating in** a running race. She was quite surprised because I **used to** spend all my free time gaming, but recently I've been trying to **keep active**. It was nice because she didn't just nod; she actually asked about my training and wished me luck.
> 
> We also talked about our favorite food spots. She mentioned a new Italian restaurant that has just opened downtown. I told her that **I've been meaning to** go there, **but I haven't had the time** yet. We realized we both love fresh pasta, which was a neat little connection. 
> 
> Overall, I really enjoyed the conversation because it was completely relaxed and didn't feel forced. In a busy tech office, it's nice to just **take it easy** for ten minutes and talk about normal life instead of database queries or pipelines."

### Key Conversational Phrases Used:
1. **catching up** (trò chuyện, cập nhật): *Talking about recent life events.*
2. **had plans** (có kế hoạch): *Intending to do something specific.*
3. **keep active** (vận động, năng động): *Staying physically healthy.*
4. **been meaning to** (đã định làm gì): *Intending to do an action but delaying it.*
5. **take it easy** (thư giãn, nghỉ ngơi): *Relaxing and not working hard.*
