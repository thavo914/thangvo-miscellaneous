## 🏃‍♂️ Running Details

### **Tuesday: Intervals**
- **Warm-up**: 2 km easy + dynamic drills
- **Workout**: 4 x 600m @ **5:30/km**
- **Recovery**: 2.5 min jog (or 400m very slow) between reps
- **Cool-down**: 2 km easy
- **Total**: ~7 km

### **Wednesday: Easy Run**
- **Distance**: 8 km
- **Pace**: 6:40/km (MAF)
- **Focus**: Relaxed, conversational pace.

### **Thursday: Medium-Long Run (Mixed)**
- **Distance**: 17 km
- **Structure**:
    - 7 km @ 6:40/km (MAF)
    - **3 km @ 5:50/km (Tempo)**
    - 7 km @ 6:40/km (MAF)
- **Note**: Keep the tempo section controlled.

### **Saturday: Long Run**
- **Distance**: 26 km
- **Pace**: 6:40/km (Pure MAF)
- **Focus**: Time on feet. Hydrate every 5km.
