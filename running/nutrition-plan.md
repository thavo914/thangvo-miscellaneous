# 🥗 Marathon Training Nutrition Plan (Weeks 3-14)
**Goal**: Fast glycogen replenishment using *Rice, Bread, Bananas, Eggs, Chocolate Milk*.

---

## 📅 Standard Week Menu (Tue-Fri)
*This remains consistent every week unless otherwise noted.*

| Day | Run Type | Immediate Recovery (0-30 min) | Main Recovery Meal (30-90 min) | Why? |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Tue** | **Intervals/Speed** | **Chocolate Milk** + **1/2 Banana** | **Rice (Large Portion)** + **2 Eggs** | Speed depletion needs liquid sugar ASAP + solid refuel. |
| **Wed** | **Easy Run** | **Water** + **1 Banana** | **Bread (Baguette)** + **2 Eggs** | Moderate depletion. Baguette/Eggs is a balanced recovery. |
| **Thu** | **Medium Run** | **Chocolate Milk** | **Rice (Moderate)** + **2 Eggs** | 3:1 Carb/Protein liquid immediate recovery is key here. |
| **Fri** | **Rest** | - | *Carb-focused Dinner (Rice/Bread)* | **Carb Load**: Eat a larger portion to prep for Saturday. |

## 🍳 Preparation Rules
1.  **Boil Eggs in Batch**: Boil 6-8 eggs on Mon/Thu to ensure they are ready for immediate consumption.
2.  **Bread vs Rice**: Bread is great for Wed because it digests faster after an Easy Run. Rice is better for Tue/Thu/Sat to ensure deep glycogen refilling.
3.  **Salt is Recovery**: For Long Runs (Sat), adding **soy sauce** or extra salt to your eggs is NOT optional. You need the sodium.
