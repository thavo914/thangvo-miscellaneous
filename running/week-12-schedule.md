# 📅 Week 12: Marathon Pace Introduction & 80-20 Rule Optimization

## 🎯 Week 12 Goals

Week 12 introduces **marathon pace practice** while optimizing the **80-20 rule**:
- **Speed Check**: 5 km time trial to assess fitness progress
- **Marathon Pace Introduction**: First practice at 6:24/km pace
- **80-20 Rule**: Proper intensity distribution (80% easy, 20% hard)
- **Volume Increase**: 61 km (+6 km from Week 11)
- **Race-Specific Training**: Transition to marathon-specific preparation

---

## 🏃‍♂️ Week 12 Running Schedule

### **Tuesday: Speed Check (Hard - 20%)**

::: {.speed-check}
**Workout**: 2km warm-up + 5km time trial + 1km cool-down
**Total Distance**: 8 km
**Duration**: 50-60 minutes
**Purpose**: Fitness assessment and pace calibration
:::

#### **Speed Check Protocol:**
- **Warm-up**: 2 km @ 6:30/km (MAF pace)
- **Time Trial**: 5 km with progressive pace
- **Cool-down**: 1 km @ 6:30/km (MAF pace)

#### **5km Time Trial Pacing:**
| Km | Target Pace | Cumulative Time | HR Zone |
|----|-------------|----------------|---------|
| 1 | 5:45/km | 5:45 | 160-165 bpm |
| 2 | 5:40/km | 11:25 | 165-169 bpm |
| 3 | 5:35/km | 17:00 | 169-173 bpm |
| 4 | 5:30/km | 22:30 | 173-177 bpm |
| 5 | 5:25/km | 27:55 | 177-180 bpm |

#### **Heart Rate Monitoring:**
- **Warm-up/Cool-down**: 143-153 bpm (MAF zone)
- **Time Trial**: 160-180 bpm (progressive increase)
- **Recovery**: Should return to <120 bpm within 3 minutes

---

### **Wednesday: Easy Run (Easy - 80%)**

::: {.workout-box}
**Workout**: Easy Run
**Distance**: 8 km
**Pace**: 6:30/km (MAF pace)
**Heart Rate**: 143-153 bpm (MAF zone)
**Duration**: 45-55 minutes
**Purpose**: Recovery after speed check
:::

#### **Key Points:**
- **Stay in MAF zone**: Don't let HR drift above 153 bpm
- **Easy effort**: Should feel comfortable and conversational
- **Recovery focus**: Help body adapt to speed check stimulus

---

### **Thursday: Medium-Long Run with Marathon Pace (Mixed)**

::: {.marathon-pace}
**Workout**: 6 km MAF + 4 km @ 6:24/km + 6 km MAF
**Total Distance**: 16 km
**Duration**: 100-110 minutes
**Purpose**: First marathon pace practice
:::

#### **Workout Breakdown:**
- **First 6 km**: 6:30/km (MAF pace) - 143-153 bpm
- **Middle 4 km**: 6:24/km (Marathon pace) - 155-165 bpm
- **Final 6 km**: 6:30/km (MAF pace) - 143-153 bpm

#### **Marathon Pace Guidelines:**
- **Target pace**: 6:24/km (sub-4:30 marathon pace)
- **Feel**: Comfortably hard, sustainable for long periods
- **Form**: Smooth, efficient running with good posture
- **Breathing**: Controlled, not labored

#### **Marathon Pace Strategy:**
- **Start conservatively**: First km at 6:30/km, then build to 6:24/km
- **Maintain consistency**: Hold 6:24/km for the 4 km section
- **Finish strong**: Last km can be slightly faster (6:20/km)
- **Recovery**: Return to MAF pace immediately after

---

### **Friday: Complete Rest**

::: {.callout-note}
**Workout**: Complete Rest

**Alternative**: Light mobility only if feeling good

#### **Recovery Activities:**
- **Sleep**: 8-9 hours
- **Hydration**: 2.5-3L water
- **Light mobility**: Ankle circles, hip circles (optional)
- **Mental preparation**: Review marathon pace strategy

---

### **Saturday: Easy Run (Easy - 80%)**

::: {.workout-box}
**Workout**: Easy Run
**Distance**: 8 km
**Pace**: 6:30/km (MAF pace)
**Heart Rate**: 143-153 bpm (MAF zone)
**Duration**: 45-55 minutes
**Purpose**: Preparation for long run
:::

#### **Key Points:**

---

### **Sunday: Long Run with Marathon Pace (Mixed)**

::: {.marathon-pace}
**Workout**: 19 km MAF + 6 km @ 6:24/km
**Total Distance**: 25 km
**Duration**: 160-170 minutes
**Purpose**: Extended marathon pace practice
:::

#### **Workout Breakdown:**
- **First 19 km**: 6:30/km (MAF pace) - 143-153 bpm
- **Final 6 km**: 6:24/km (Marathon pace) - 155-165 bpm

#### **Long Run Strategy:**
- **Start easy**: First 5 km at 6:35/km, then settle into 6:30/km
- **Build endurance**: Maintain MAF pace for 19 km
- **Marathon pace finish**: Last 6 km at target marathon pace
- **Mental toughness**: Practice race-day pacing strategy

#### **Marathon Pace Execution:**
- **Transition smoothly**: From 6:30/km to 6:24/km
- **Maintain form**: Good posture and efficient stride
- **Stay relaxed**: Don't tense up at faster pace
- **Finish strong**: Last km can be 6:20/km if feeling good

## ❤️ Heart Rate Zones Reference (Age 32)

### **MAF Heart Rate Calculation:**
- **Base MAF HR**: 180 - 32 = 148 bpm
- **MAF Zone**: 143-153 bpm (MAF ± 5 bpm)
- **Max HR Estimate**: ~188 bpm (220 - age)

### **Training Zones:**
| Zone | Heart Rate | % Max HR | Purpose | Workout Type |
|------|------------|----------|---------|--------------|
| **Recovery** | 120-140 bpm | 60-70% | Active recovery | Recovery jogging |
| **MAF** | 143-153 bpm | 75-80% | Aerobic base | Easy runs, long runs |
| **Marathon Pace** | 155-165 bpm | 82-88% | Race pace | Marathon pace sections |
| **Threshold** | 160-169 bpm | 85-90% | Lactate threshold | Tempo runs |
| **VO₂ Max** | 160-175 bpm | 85-90% | Anaerobic capacity | Intervals |
| **Speed Check** | 160-180 bpm | 85-96% | Max effort | Time trials |

### **Heart Rate Monitoring Tips:**
- **Use chest strap**: More accurate than wrist sensors
- **Monitor trends**: Watch for elevated resting HR
- **Stay in zone**: If HR drifts above zone, slow down
- **Recovery check**: HR should return to <120 bpm within 3 minutes
- **Marathon pace**: Aim for 155-165 bpm during race pace sections
