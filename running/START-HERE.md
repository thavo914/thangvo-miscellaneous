## 🎯 Training Principles

### **80-20 Rule**
- **80% Easy**: Most runs at MAF pace (6:25/km)
- **20% Hard**: Intervals, tempo, marathon pace work
- **Purpose**: Optimal adaptation and recovery

### **MAF Method**
- Easy pace builds aerobic base
- Develops fat-burning efficiency
- Lowers injury risk
- Foundation for speed work

### **Progressive Overload**
- Volume increases gradually each week
- Peak at Week 12 (77 km/week)
- 3-week taper before race
