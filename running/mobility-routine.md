# 🧘 Recovery & Mobility Routine
**Focus**: Resetting tight muscles. (Best after Long Runs or on Fridays)
**Time**: 15 Minutes

## The Routine

1.  **Couch Stretch (Hip Flexors)**
    *   **Time**: 90s each side.
    *   **Why**: Crucial for runners who sit all day. Opens up the front of the hip to improve stride extension.

2.  **Pigeon Pose (Glutes)**
    *   **Time**: 2 mins each side.
    *   **Why**: Deep glute stretch to release tension from long miles and prevent piriformis issues.

3.  **Cat-Cow (Spine)**
    *   **Reps**: 12 slow repetitions.
    *   **Why**: Mobilizes the spine and releases lower back stiffness.

4.  **Downward Dog (Calves/Hamstrings)**
    *   **Time**: Hold for 60s, "pedaling" the feet.
    *   **Why**: Stretches the entire posterior chain (calves, hamstrings, back).

5.  **Wall Slide (Upper Back)**
    *   **Reps**: 15 reps.
    *   **Why**: Opens up the chest for better breathing and posture (combating the "runner's hunch").

## When to use
*   **Fridays**: As a standalone recovery session.
*   **Sundays**: Immediately after your Long Run to prevent stiffness.
