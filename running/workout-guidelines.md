# 🏃‍♂️ Workout Guidelines & Execution
**15-Week Marathon Training Plan**

## 🔥 Warm-Up & Cool-Down Protocol

### **Standard Warm-Up (10 minutes)**
- **Minutes 1-3**: Easy jogging at MAF pace (6:40/km)
- **Minutes 4-6**: Dynamic stretching and mobility
  - Leg swings (forward/back, side-to-side)
  - Hip circles and arm circles
  - Walking knee hugs and ankle mobility
- **Minutes 7-10**: Progressive pace increase to workout pace

### **Dynamic Stretches to Include**
- **Hip Flexor Stretch**: Walking knee hugs (10 each leg)
- **Hamstring Mobility**: Walking toe touches (10 reps)
- **Ankle Mobility**: Heel/toe walks (10 each)
- **Arm Circles**: Forward and backward (10 each direction)
- **Leg Swings**: Forward/back and side-to-side (10 each)

### **Cool-Down (10 minutes)**
- **Minutes 1-5**: Easy jogging at MAF pace (6:40/km)
- **Minutes 6-10**: Walking and static stretching
- **Focus**: Gradually lower heart rate and flush metabolites

## ⚡ High-Intensity Workouts

### **Interval Training (All Tuesdays Except Speed Check Weeks)**

#### **400m Intervals (Weeks 1, 2, 3, 4)**
- **Format**: 5×400m @ 5:30/km with 200m jog recovery
- **Target Pace**: 5:30/km (1:22 per 400m)
- **Recovery**: 200m jog @ 7:00/km (~1:24 recovery)
- **Total Work**: 2 km at interval pace
- **Total Recovery**: 1 km at recovery pace
- **Heart Rate**: 170-175 bpm during intervals
- **Purpose**: Build VO2 max and running economy in base phase

#### **600m Intervals (Weeks 6, 7, 8, 9)**
- **Format**: 4×600m @ 5:30/km with 300m jog recovery
- **Target Pace**: 5:30/km (3:18 per 600m)
- **Recovery**: 300m jog @ 7:00/km (~2:06 recovery)
- **Total Work**: 2.4 km at interval pace
- **Total Recovery**: 1.2 km at recovery pace
- **Heart Rate**: 170-175 bpm during intervals
- **Purpose**: Extend VO2 max intervals during build phase

#### **1000m Intervals (Weeks 10, 11, 13)**
- **Format**: 3×1000m @ 5:30/km with 2' jog recovery
- **Target Pace**: 5:30/km (5:30 per 1000m)
- **Recovery**: 2' jog @ 7:00/km (~400m recovery)
- **Total Work**: 3 km at interval pace
- **Total Recovery**: 1.2 km at recovery pace
- **Heart Rate**: 170-175 bpm during intervals
- **Purpose**: Peak phase VO2 max development

#### **Light Intervals (Week 14 - Race Week)**
- **Format**: 3×400m @ 5:30/km with 200m jog recovery
- **Purpose**: Light sharpening, maintain leg turnover

## 🏃‍♂️ Tempo Training

### **Option A: Tuesday Tempo Runs (Treadmill Alternative)**

When weather forces treadmill use, substitute intervals with tempo runs:

#### **Progressive Tempo Build-Up**
- **Weeks 1-2**: 20' @ 5:50/km (3.4 km tempo work)
- **Week 3**: 22' @ 5:50/km (3.8 km tempo work)
- **Week 4**: 25' @ 5:50/km (4.3 km tempo work)
- **Weeks 6-7**: 28' @ 5:50/km (4.8 km tempo work)
- **Weeks 8-9**: 30' @ 5:50/km (5.2 km tempo work)
- **Weeks 10-11**: 32' @ 5:50/km (5.5 km tempo work)
- **Week 13**: 25' @ 5:50/km (4.3 km tempo work, taper)

#### **Treadmill Execution**
- **Speed Setting**: 10.3 km/h (5:50/km pace)
- **Incline**: 1% (simulates outdoor resistance)
- **Warm-up**: 2 km @ 6:40/km (9.0 km/h)
- **Cool-down**: 1 km @ 6:40/km (9.0 km/h)
- **Benefit**: Set speed once, maintain for entire tempo portion

### **Option B: Tempo in Medium-Long Runs**
- **Purpose**: Threshold development during endurance runs
- **Integration**: 3-4km tempo sections within MLR (Weeks 5-7, 10-11)
- **Benefit**: Race-specific pacing practice

### **Option C: Tempo in Long Runs**
- **Purpose**: Race-specific endurance at threshold
- **Integration**: 4-5km tempo sections within LR (Weeks 6, 7, 9)
- **Benefit**: Teaches pacing control during long efforts

#### **Tempo Execution Guidelines**
- **Pace Control**: Start slightly slower than target, build to target pace
- **Effort Level**: "Comfortably hard" - you can speak in short phrases
- **Heart Rate**: 160-169 bpm (85-90% of maximum heart rate)
- **Focus**: Maintain consistent pace throughout the tempo portion

## 🏃‍♂️ Easy Runs (MAF Pace)

### **Target Pace: 6:40/km** *(Adjusted for sustainable aerobic development)*

#### **Execution Guidelines**
- **Effort Level**: Very comfortable, conversational pace
- **Heart Rate**: 143-153 bpm (MAF zone)
- **Breathing**: Easy, relaxed breathing pattern
- **Form**: Focus on good running form and relaxation

#### **When to Use MAF Pace**
- **Wednesday Easy Runs**: 8 km recovery from Tuesday's hard session
- **Saturday Easy Runs**: 8 km maintenance and recovery
- **Long Run Base**: Base portion of long runs at MAF pace
- **MLR Base**: Base portion of medium-long runs at MAF pace
- **Recovery Runs**: Any additional recovery sessions

#### **MAF Pace Benefits**
- **Aerobic Development**: Builds fat-burning efficiency at sustainable effort
- **Recovery**: Promotes active recovery between hard sessions
- **Injury Prevention**: Truly easy pace reduces injury risk
- **Endurance Base**: Solid foundation allows for quality tempo/MP work

## 🏁 Speed Check Guidelines (Weeks 5 & 12)

### **5 km Time Trial Protocol**

#### **Warm-Up Protocol**
- **2 km**: Easy jog at MAF pace (6:40/km, 143-153 bpm)
- **Dynamic Stretches**: 5 minutes of mobility work
- **Strides**: 4-6×100m progressive accelerations

#### **5 km Time Trial Execution**
- **Pace Progression**: Gradually increase effort each kilometer
- **Heart Rate Targets**: Build from 85% to 96% HRmax
- **Mental Focus**: Stay relaxed and maintain form
- **Pace Control**: Don't start too fast

#### **Target Pace & Heart Rate Progression**
| Km | Target Pace | Cumulative Time | HR Zone (bpm) |
|----|-------------|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | 5:20/km | 5:20 | 160-165 (85-88% HRmax) |
| 2 | 5:15/km | 10:35 | 165-169 (88-90% HRmax) |
| 3 | 5:15/km | 15:50 | 169-173 (90-92% HRmax) |
| 4 | 5:10/km | 21:00 | 173-177 (92-94% HRmax) |
| 5 | 5:10/km | 26:10 | 177-180 (94-96% HRmax) |

**Target Time**: Sub-26:30 (supports sub-4:30 marathon goal)

#### **Cool-Down Protocol**
- **1 km**: Easy jog at MAF pace (6:40/km, 143-153 bpm)
- **Static Stretching**: 5 minutes of post-run stretching
- **Recovery**: Light walking and hydration

## 🎯 Marathon Pace (6:24/km) Execution

### **Target Pace: 6:24/km (Sub-4:30 Marathon)**

#### **Marathon Pace Guidelines**
- **Target pace**: 6:24/km (4:29:53 marathon finish)
- **Feel**: Comfortably hard, sustainable for long periods
- **Form**: Smooth, efficient running with good posture
- **Breathing**: Controlled, not labored
- **Heart Rate**: 155-165 bpm

#### **Marathon Pace Strategy**
- **Start conservatively**: First km at 6:30/km, then build to 6:24/km
- **Maintain consistency**: Hold 6:24/km throughout the section
- **Finish strong**: Last km can be slightly faster (6:20/km) if feeling good
- **Recovery**: Return to MAF pace immediately after

#### **Mental Approach**
- **Visualization**: Practice race-day scenarios
- **Confidence**: Build belief that this pace is sustainable
- **Relaxation**: Stay loose and avoid tension
- **Focus**: Maintain form and rhythm

## 🎯 Pace Control & Execution Tips

### **Pace Discipline**
- **Use GPS Watch**: Monitor pace throughout workouts
- **Heart Rate Monitor**: Stay in target zones
- **Perceived Effort**: Learn to gauge effort without technology
- **Consistency**: Maintain target pace throughout workout

### **Mental Strategies**
- **Break Down Workouts**: Focus on one interval/segment at a time
- **Positive Self-Talk**: Use encouraging phrases during hard efforts
- **Visualization**: Picture successful workout completion
- **Breathing Focus**: Concentrate on rhythmic breathing patterns

### **Form Focus**
- **Posture**: Keep chest up and shoulders relaxed
- **Arm Swing**: Natural, relaxed arm movement
- **Foot Strike**: Mid-foot landing with quick turnover
- **Relaxation**: Stay loose and avoid tension

## ⚠️ Workout Modifications & Safety

### **When to Modify Workouts**
- **Injury Signs**: Pain, swelling, or reduced range of motion
- **Illness**: Fever, fatigue, or respiratory symptoms
- **Extreme Weather**: Heat index >90°F or wind chill <-20°F
- **Poor Recovery**: Persistent fatigue or elevated resting heart rate

### **Modification Options**
- **Reduce Volume**: Cut distance by 20-30%
- **Reduce Intensity**: Slow pace by 10-15 seconds/km
- **Skip Workout**: Take complete rest if needed
- **Cross-Train**: Low-impact alternatives (cycling, swimming)

### **Safety Guidelines**
- **Hydration**: Drink water before, during, and after workouts
- **Weather Awareness**: Check conditions before heading out
- **Route Safety**: Choose well-lit, safe running paths
- **Emergency Plan**: Carry phone and ID on long runs

## 📊 Workout Quality Indicators

### **Good Workout Signs**
- **Consistent Pace**: Maintain target pace throughout
- **Strong Finish**: Feel strong in final segments
- **Quick Recovery**: Return to normal within 24 hours
- **Motivation**: Look forward to next workout

### **Poor Workout Signs**
- **Pace Decline**: Unable to maintain target pace
- **Excessive Fatigue**: Feel drained for extended periods
- **Poor Recovery**: Soreness lasting >48 hours
- **Loss of Motivation**: Dread upcoming workouts

### **Adjustment Strategies**
- **Volume Reduction**: Cut weekly distance by 10-20%
- **Intensity Reduction**: Slow all paces by 10-15 seconds/km
- **Additional Rest**: Add extra rest day or cross-training
- **Professional Help**: Consult coach or medical professional

## ❤️ Heart Rate Zones Reference

### **MAF Heart Rate Calculation (Age 32)**
- **Base MAF HR**: 180 - 32 = 148 bpm
- **MAF Zone**: 143-153 bpm (MAF ± 5 bpm)
- **Max HR Estimate**: ~188 bpm (220 - age)

### **Training Zones**
| Zone | Heart Rate | % Max HR | Pace | Purpose | Workout Type |
|------|------------|----------|------|---------|--------------|
| **Recovery** | 120-140 bpm | 60-70% | 7:00/km | Active recovery | Recovery jogging |
| **MAF** | 143-153 bpm | 75-80% | 6:40/km | Aerobic base | Easy runs, long runs |
| **Marathon Pace** | 155-165 bpm | 82-88% | 6:24/km | Race pace | Marathon pace sections |
| **Threshold** | 160-169 bpm | 85-90% | 5:50/km | Lactate threshold | Tempo runs |
| **VO₂ Max** | 170-175 bpm | 90-93% | 5:30/km | Anaerobic capacity | Intervals |
