# 📅 Week 1: Transition Week Schedule
**Dates**: December 9-15, 2025  
**Purpose**: Smooth transition after illness recovery  
**Total Volume**: 55 km  
**Long Run**: 22 km

---

## 🎯 Week 1 Goals

- **Transition**: Build from current 50 km/week → 55 km/week
- **Test Fitness**: Assess readiness for Week 12 jump
- **Long Run**: Match your current longest distance (22 km)
- **Recovery Focus**: Ensure you're fully recovered from illness

---

### **Tuesday, December 10: Tempo Run**
**Workout**: Tempo Run with Warm-up and Cool-down

| Segment | Distance | Pace | Duration | Heart Rate |
|---------|----------|------|----------|------------|
| Warm-up | 2 km | 6:50/km (MAF) | ~14 min | 143-153 bpm |
| **Tempo** | **~4 km** | **6:10/km** | **24 minutes** | **160-169 bpm** |
| Cool-down | 2 km | 6:50/km (MAF) | ~14 min | 143-153 bpm |

**Total Distance**: ~8 km  
**Total Duration**: 50-55 minutes

**Tempo Execution:**
- Start slightly slower, build to 6:10/km
- Should feel "comfortably hard"
- Can speak in short phrases
- Maintain consistent pace throughout

**Post-Run:**
- 10 minutes walking
- Light stretching (5 minutes)
- Foam rolling (10 minutes) - focus on legs

### **Sunday, December 15: Long Run** 🏃‍♂️
**Workout**: Long Run

- **Distance**: 22 km
- **Pace**: 6:50/km (MAF pace)
- **Heart Rate**: 143-153 bpm
- **Duration**: ~2.5 hours (150 minutes)
- **Effort**: Comfortable, sustainable

**Purpose**: Build endurance, test fitness after illness recovery

**Strategy:**
- Start easy: First 2 km at 7:00/km
- Settle into MAF pace: 6:50/km for remainder
- Maintain consistent pace
- Stay relaxed and comfortable

**Hydration & Fueling:**
- **Pre-run**: 300-400ml water 1-2 hours before

- **Post-run**: 500-600ml water + recovery meal

**Post-Run Recovery:**
- 15 minutes walking
- Comprehensive stretching (20 minutes)
- Foam rolling (20 minutes)
- Recovery meal within 30 minutes
- Rest for remainder of day

## ✅ Week 1 Success Indicators

### **You're Ready for Week 12 if:**
- ✅ All runs completed at comfortable effort
- ✅ Recovery within 24 hours between runs
- ✅ Long run (22 km) felt manageable
- ✅ No unusual fatigue or soreness
- ✅ Resting heart rate normal
- ✅ Feeling energized, not drained
- ✅ Eager for next week's training

### **Consider Another Transition Week if:**
- ⚠️ Excessive fatigue lasting >48 hours
- ⚠️ Long run felt very difficult
- ⚠️ Unusual soreness or pain
- ⚠️ Elevated resting heart rate
- ⚠️ Loss of motivation
- ⚠️ Difficulty sleeping

## 🍎 Nutrition Guidelines

### **Daily:**
- **Hydration**: 2.5-3L water throughout day
- **Meals**: Balanced nutrition with carbs, protein, vegetables
- **Snacks**: Healthy snacks between meals

### **Pre-Run (2-3 hours):**
- **Main meal**: Rice/noodles + protein + vegetables
- **Example**: Rice + chicken + vegetables + banana

### **Pre-Run (30-60 minutes):**
- **Light snack**: Banana + 200ml water/coconut water
- Avoid heavy meals

### **During Run:**
- **Water**: Small sips every 20-30 minutes (for runs >60 minutes)
- **Long Run (22 km)**: Consider bringing water bottle

### **Post-Run (within 30 minutes):**
- **Recovery meal**: Carbs + protein
- **Example**: Rice + meat + vegetables + banana
- **Hydration**: 500ml water + 200ml coconut water

## 🎯 Week 1 Mindset

### **Focus Areas:**
- **Patience**: This is a transition week, not a test
- **Recovery**: Prioritize recovery from recent illness
- **Listening**: Pay attention to how your body responds
- **Consistency**: Complete all scheduled runs
- **Easy Effort**: Don't push too hard - stay in MAF zone

### **Success = Feeling Strong and Recovered**
- All runs completed comfortably
- Good recovery between sessions
- Energy levels maintained
- Ready for increased volume next week

## 📞 End of Week Assessment

**After completing Week 1, assess:**

1. **How did all runs feel?**
   - Comfortable: ✅ Ready for Week 12
   - Difficult: ⚠️ Consider Week 11 level first

2. **Recovery between runs?**
   - Good (24 hours): ✅ Ready
   - Slow (>48 hours): ⚠️ Need more time

3. **Long run (22 km)?**
   - Manageable: ✅ Ready
   - Very hard: ⚠️ Consider another transition week

4. **Overall energy?**
   - Strong: ✅ Ready
   - Drained: ⚠️ Rest more
