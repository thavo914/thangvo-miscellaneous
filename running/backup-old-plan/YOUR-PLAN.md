## 🚨 Red Flags to Watch

**If you experience any of these, reduce volume or rest:**
- ❌ Persistent fatigue >48 hours
- ❌ Elevated resting heart rate
- ❌ Unusual pain or soreness
- ❌ Loss of motivation
- ❌ Difficulty sleeping
- ❌ Lingering effects from illness

**Action**: Reduce volume 10-20%, add rest day, or extend transition period

## 📞 Weekly Check-Ins

**After Each Week, Ask Yourself:**
1. How did all runs feel? (Easy/Comfortable/Difficult)
2. Recovery between runs? (Good/Slow)
3. Energy levels? (Strong/Moderate/Drained)
4. Any pain or unusual soreness?
5. Resting heart rate normal?

**Use answers to adjust:**
- Feeling strong → Continue plan
- Feeling drained → Reduce volume or add rest
- Pain/injury → Rest and consult healthcare provider
