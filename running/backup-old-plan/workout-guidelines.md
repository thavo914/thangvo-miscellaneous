# 🏃‍♂️ Workout Guidelines & Execution

## 🔥 Warm-Up & Cool-Down Protocol

### **Standard Warm-Up (10 minutes)**
- **Minutes 1-3**: Easy jogging at MAF pace (6:50/km)
- **Minutes 4-6**: Dynamic stretching and mobility
  - Leg swings (forward/back, side-to-side)
  - Hip circles and arm circles
  - Walking knee hugs and ankle mobility
- **Minutes 7-10**: Progressive pace increase to workout pace

### **Dynamic Stretches to Include**
- **Hip Flexor Stretch**: Walking knee hugs (10 each leg)
- **Hamstring Mobility**: Walking toe touches (10 reps)
- **Ankle Mobility**: Heel/toe walks (10 each)
- **Arm Circles**: Forward and backward (10 each direction)
- **Leg Swings**: Forward/back and side-to-side (10 each)

### **Cool-Down (10 minutes)**
- **Minutes 1-5**: Easy jogging at MAF pace
- **Minutes 6-10**: Walking and static stretching
- **Focus**: Gradually lower heart rate and flush metabolites

## ⚡ High-Intensity Workouts

### **Interval Training (Odd Weeks)**

#### **400m Intervals (Weeks 1, 3, 5, 7)**
- **Format**: 5×400m @ 6:00/km with 200m jog recovery
- **Target Pace**: 6:00/km (1:30 per 400m)
- **Recovery**: 200m jog @ 8:30/km (~1:42 recovery)
- **Total Work**: 2.5 km at interval pace
- **Total Recovery**: 1 km at recovery pace

#### **600m Intervals (Weeks 9, 11)**
- **Format**: 4×600m @ 6:00/km with 300m jog recovery
- **Target Pace**: 6:00/km (3:36 per 600m)
- **Recovery**: 300m jog @ 8:30/km (~2:33 recovery)
- **Total Work**: 2.4 km at interval pace
- **Total Recovery**: 1.2 km at recovery pace

#### **1000m Intervals (Weeks 17, 19, 21)**
- **Format**: 3×1000m @ 5:55/km with 2′ jog recovery
- **Target Pace**: 5:55/km (5:55 per 1000m)
- **Recovery**: 2′ jog @ 8:30/km (~400m recovery)
- **Total Work**: 3 km at interval pace
- **Total Recovery**: 1.2 km at recovery pace

#### **400m Taper Intervals (Weeks 23, 25)**
- **Format**: 2×400m @ 5:55-6:00/km with 200m jog recovery
- **Target Pace**: 5:55-6:00/km (1:28-1:30 per 400m)
- **Recovery**: 200m jog @ 8:30/km (~1:42 recovery)
- **Total Work**: 800m at interval pace
- **Total Recovery**: 400m at recovery pace

### **Tempo Training (Even Weeks)**

#### **Progressive Tempo Build-Up**
- **Week 2**: 15′ @ 6:10/km
- **Week 4**: 17′ @ 6:10/km
- **Week 6**: Speed check (5 km time trial)
- **Week 8**: 24′ @ 6:10/km
- **Week 10**: 28′ @ 6:10/km
- **Week 12**: Speed check (5 km time trial)
- **Week 14**: 33′ @ 6:10/km
- **Week 16**: 35′ @ 6:10/km
- **Week 18**: Speed check (5 km time trial)
- **Week 20**: 40′ @ 6:05/km
- **Week 22**: 32′ @ 6:05/km
- **Week 24**: Speed check (5 km time trial)
- **Week 26**: 10′ @ 6:10/km

#### **Tempo Execution Guidelines**
- **Pace Control**: Start slightly slower than target, build to target pace
- **Effort Level**: "Comfortably hard" - you can speak in short phrases
- **Heart Rate**: 85-90% of maximum heart rate
- **Focus**: Maintain consistent pace throughout the tempo portion

## 🏃‍♂️ Easy Runs (MAF Pace)

### **Target Pace: 6:50/km**

#### **Execution Guidelines**
- **Effort Level**: Very comfortable, conversational pace
- **Heart Rate**: 143-153 bpm (MAF zone)
- **Breathing**: Easy, relaxed breathing pattern
- **Form**: Focus on good running form and relaxation

#### **When to Use MAF Pace**
- **Wednesday Easy Runs**: 8 km recovery from Tuesday's hard session
- **Saturday Easy Runs**: 8 km maintenance and recovery
- **Long Run Base**: Majority of long runs at MAF pace
- **Recovery Runs**: Any additional recovery sessions

#### **MAF Pace Benefits**
- **Aerobic Development**: Builds fat-burning efficiency
- **Recovery**: Promotes active recovery between hard sessions
- **Injury Prevention**: Low stress allows tissue adaptation
- **Endurance Base**: Foundation for all other training

- **Weeks 7-8**: 20-21 km

- **Primary Pace**: MAF pace (6:50/km)
- **Marathon-Pace Sections**: Weeks 12, 16, 20 include marathon-pace work
- **Effort Level**: Comfortable, sustainable for entire distance

#### **Marathon-Pace Integration**
- **Week 12**: 19 MAF + 6 @ 6:24

- **Start Slow**: First 2-3 km at MAF pace to warm up
- **Pace Consistency**: Maintain target pace throughout
- **Mental Approach**: Break into manageable segments
- **Fueling**: Practice race-day nutrition strategy

## 🏁 Speed Check Guidelines

### **Speed Check Weeks: 6, 12, 18, 24**

#### **Warm-Up Protocol**
- **2 km**: Easy jog at MAF pace (143-153 bpm)
- **Dynamic Stretches**: 5 minutes of mobility work
- **Strides**: 4-6×100m progressive accelerations

#### **5 km Time Trial Execution**
- **Pace Progression**: Gradually increase effort each kilometer
- **Heart Rate Targets**: Build from 85% to 96% HRmax
- **Mental Focus**: Stay relaxed and maintain form
- **Pace Control**: Don't start too fast

#### **Target Pace & Heart Rate Progression**
| Km | Target Pace | Cumulative Time | HR Zone (bpm) |
|----|-------------|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | 5:45 | 5:45 | 160–165 (85–88% HRmax) |
| 2 | 5:40 | 11:25 | 165–169 (88–90% HRmax) |
| 3 | 5:35 | 17:00 | 169–173 (90–92% HRmax) |
| 4 | 5:30 | 22:30 | 173–177 (92–94% HRmax) |
| 5 | 5:25 | 27:55 | 177–180 (94–96% HRmax) |

#### **Cool-Down Protocol**
- **1 km**: Easy jog at MAF pace (143-153 bpm)
- **Static Stretching**: 5 minutes of post-run stretching
- **Recovery**: Light walking and hydration

## 🎯 Pace Control & Execution Tips

### **Pace Discipline**
- **Use GPS Watch**: Monitor pace throughout workouts
- **Heart Rate Monitor**: Stay in target zones
- **Perceived Effort**: Learn to gauge effort without technology
- **Consistency**: Maintain target pace throughout workout

### **Mental Strategies**
- **Break Down Workouts**: Focus on one interval/segment at a time
- **Positive Self-Talk**: Use encouraging phrases during hard efforts
- **Visualization**: Picture successful workout completion
- **Breathing Focus**: Concentrate on rhythmic breathing patterns

### **Form Focus**
- **Posture**: Keep chest up and shoulders relaxed
- **Arm Swing**: Natural, relaxed arm movement
- **Foot Strike**: Mid-foot landing with quick turnover
- **Relaxation**: Stay loose and avoid tension

## ⚠️ Workout Modifications & Safety

### **When to Modify Workouts**
- **Injury Signs**: Pain, swelling, or reduced range of motion
- **Illness**: Fever, fatigue, or respiratory symptoms
- **Extreme Weather**: Heat index >90°F or wind chill <-20°F
- **Poor Recovery**: Persistent fatigue or elevated resting heart rate

### **Modification Options**
- **Reduce Volume**: Cut distance by 20-30%
- **Reduce Intensity**: Slow pace by 10-15 seconds/km
- **Skip Workout**: Take complete rest if needed
- **Cross-Train**: Low-impact alternatives (cycling, swimming)

### **Safety Guidelines**
- **Hydration**: Drink water before, during, and after workouts
- **Weather Awareness**: Check conditions before heading out
- **Route Safety**: Choose well-lit, safe running paths
- **Emergency Plan**: Carry phone and ID on long runs

## 📊 Workout Quality Indicators

### **Good Workout Signs**
- **Consistent Pace**: Maintain target pace throughout
- **Strong Finish**: Feel strong in final segments
- **Quick Recovery**: Return to normal within 24 hours
- **Motivation**: Look forward to next workout

### **Poor Workout Signs**
- **Pace Decline**: Unable to maintain target pace
- **Excessive Fatigue**: Feel drained for extended periods
- **Poor Recovery**: Soreness lasting >48 hours
- **Loss of Motivation**: Dread upcoming workouts

### **Adjustment Strategies**
- **Volume Reduction**: Cut weekly distance by 10-20%
- **Intensity Reduction**: Slow all paces by 10-15 seconds/km
- **Additional Rest**: Add extra rest day or cross-training
- **Professional Help**: Consult coach or medical professional
