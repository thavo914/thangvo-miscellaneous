# 📉 14-Week Strength Periodization Strategy
**Supporting Your Journey from Base to Race Day**

This guide tells you exactly how to adjust the **Mini-Max Strength Plan** based on where you are in your marathon training.

---

## 🏗️ Phase 1: Base Building (Weeks 1–4)
**Goal**: Build capacity and perfect form.
- **W1–2**: 2 sets per exercise. Focus on slow, controlled movements.
- **W3–4**: 3 sets per exercise. Increase resistance band tension if it feels easy.

## 🚀 Phase 2: Build Phase (Weeks 5–8)
**Goal**: Support increasing mileage and intensities.
- **W5–8**: 3 sets. 
- **Emphasis**: **Glute Medius** (Monster Walks) and **Ankle Stability**. As you add Tempo runs, your knees and ankles need extra support to handle the higher impact.

## ⚡ Phase 3: Peak Phase (Weeks 9–12)
**Goal**: Durability & Maintenance (Mileage is at 70–80 km/week).
- **W9–12**: **Reduce to 2 sets.** 
- **The Golden Rule**: Strength work should feel like a "recharge," not a "drain." If you feel heavy on your runs, drop to 1 set of the most important moves (Stability & Core).

## 🏁 Phase 4: The Taper (Weeks 13–14)
**Goal**: Freshness and glycogen restoration.
- **W13**: 1 set of each exercise. Keep the movements snappy.
- **W14 (Race Week)**: 1 set of **Stability only** on Monday. **Stop all strength training by Tuesday.** Use the Mobility Routine only.

## ⚠️ Fatigue Management & "Scaling Down"

### When to scale down to "Stability Only":
If you experience any of the following, do only **1 set of Single-Leg Balance and Dead Bugs**, then stop:
1.  **Dull leg ache** that lasts more than 48 hours.
2.  **Long Run fatigue** that makes you struggle to finish your scheduled distance.
3.  **Busy Schedule**: If you only have 10 minutes, skip the bands and do the **Stability** moves.

### Taper Guidance (The Final 14 Days):
- **DO**: Keep doing single-leg stability and activation (1 set). It keeps the brain-to-muscle connection sharp.
- **AVOID**: New exercises, heavier bands, or going to "failure."
- **REPLACE**: From Thursday of Race Week, replace all strength with the **15-minute Mobility Routine**.
