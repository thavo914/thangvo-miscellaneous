## 🥗 Recovery Nutrition Plan (Week 3)
**Focus**: Fast glycogen replenishment using *Rice, Bread, Bananas, Eggs, Chocolate Milk*.

> [!NOTE]
> **Gel Rule Logic**: For your 26 km Long Run, I selected **2 Gels** (meeting the 25–28 km rule) to ensure safety against bonking.

| Day | Run Type & Distance | Gels | Immediate Recovery (0-30 min) | Main Recovery Meal (30-90 min) | Why? |
|:---|:---|:---:|:---|:---|:---|
| **Mon** | **Rest** | - | - | *Standard Balanced Eating* | No glycogen depletion to restore. |
| **Tue** | **Intervals** (~6-7 km) | 0 | **Chocolate Milk** + **1/2 Banana** | **Rice (Large Portion)** + **2 Eggs** | High intensity drains glycogen fast. Liquid carbs (Choc Milk) hit bloodstream quickest. Large rice portion refills muscle stores. |
| **Wed** | **Easy Run** (8 km) | 0 | **Water** + **1 Banana** | **Bread (Baguette)** + **2 Eggs** | Lower depletion. Banana provides enough immediate potassium/carbs. Baguette is great for moderate refueling. |
| **Thu** | **Medium Run** (14 km) | 0 | **Chocolate Milk** | **Rice (Moderate)** + **2 Eggs** | 14km is significant depletion. Chocolate milk provides ideal 3:1 carb:protein ratio for immediate repair. |
| **Fri** | **Rest** | - | - | *Carb-focused Dinner (Rice/Bread)* | **Carb Load**: Eat a slightly larger serving of Rice/Bread at dinner to prep for Saturday. |
| **Sat** | **Long Run** (26 km) | **2** | **Chocolate Milk** + **1 Banana** | **Rice (Extra Large)** + **3 Eggs** + **Soy Sauce/Salt** | **Critical Window**. 26km depletes ~1500+ calories. Immediate liquid carbs + banana are distinct hydration/electrolyte sources. 3 Eggs provides extra protein for muscle repair; Salt aids hydration. |
| **Sun** | **Rest** | - | - | *Standard Balanced Eating* | Focus on hydration. |

### 🍳 Preparation Tips
*   **Eggs**: Boil 6-8 eggs on Monday morning to have them ready for Tue/Wed/Thu post-run.
*   **Chocolate Milk**: Keep cold. Drink it *before* you shower.
*   **Timing**: The "Main Satisfaction Meal" is most effective if eaten within 90 minutes. Don't wait 3 hours!
*   **Salt**: For the Saturday post-run meal, don't be afraid to add extra salt or soy sauce to your eggs/rice.
