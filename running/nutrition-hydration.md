## 🚰 During-Workout Hydration

### **Hydration Guidelines by Workout Type**

#### **Easy Runs (≤10 km)**
- **Water**: Usually not needed unless >60 minutes
- **Electrolytes**: Not typically required
- **Timing**: Drink to thirst

#### **Medium-Long Runs (10-20 km)**
- **Water**: 250-500 ml per hour
- **Electrolytes**: Light electrolyte replacement
- **Timing**: Every 20-30 minutes

#### **Long Runs (≥20 km)**
- **Water**: 500-750 ml per hour
- **Electrolytes**: 300-600 mg sodium per hour
- **Timing**: Every 15-20 minutes

### **Hydration Strategy for Long Runs**

#### **Pre-Run Hydration**
- **2-3 hours before**: 500-750 ml water
- **1 hour before**: 250-500 ml water
- **15 minutes before**: 100-200 ml water

#### **During Run Hydration**
- **First hour**: Water only
- **After 1 hour**: Water + electrolytes
- **Every 15-20 minutes**: 100-200 ml fluid

#### **Post-Run Hydration**
- **Immediate**: 500-750 ml water
- **Within 1 hour**: 500-750 ml water + electrolytes
- **Next 2-4 hours**: Continue drinking to thirst

## 🍯 During-Workout Fueling

### **Carbohydrate Requirements**

#### **Workouts <60 minutes**
- **Fueling**: Usually not needed
- **Exception**: If running fasted or low glycogen

#### **Workouts 60-90 minutes**
- **Carbohydrates**: 30-45 g per hour
- **Timing**: Start fueling after 45 minutes
- **Format**: Gels, chews, or sports drinks

#### **Workouts 90+ minutes**
- **Carbohydrates**: 40-60 g per hour
- **Timing**: Start fueling after 30-45 minutes
- **Format**: Gels, chews, sports drinks, or real food

### **Fueling Strategy for Long Runs**

#### **Progressive Fueling Build-Up**
- **Weeks 1-8**: Practice basic hydration
- **Weeks 9-16**: Add carbohydrate fueling (30-45 g/hour)
- **Weeks 17-22**: Increase to race-day levels (50-70 g/hour)
- **Weeks 23-26**: Perfect race-day nutrition strategy

#### **Fueling Schedule Example (Long Run)**
- **0-30 minutes**: Water only
- **30 minutes**: First gel (25 g carbs)
- **60 minutes**: Second gel (25 g carbs)
- **90 minutes**: Third gel (25 g carbs)
- **120 minutes**: Fourth gel (25 g carbs)
- **Continue**: Every 30 minutes thereafter

### **Fueling Products & Alternatives**

#### **Commercial Products**
- **Energy Gels**: 20-25 g carbs per packet
- **Energy Chews**: 20-25 g carbs per serving
- **Sports Drinks**: 15-20 g carbs per 250 ml
- **Energy Bars**: 20-30 g carbs per bar

#### **Natural Alternatives**
- **Dates**: 18 g carbs per 2 dates
- **Banana**: 27 g carbs per medium banana
- **Honey**: 17 g carbs per tablespoon
- **Maple Syrup**: 13 g carbs per tablespoon

## 🥤 Hydration Strategy

### **Daily Hydration Needs**

#### **Training Days**
- **Base Requirement**: 1,845-2,153 ml (30-35 ml per kg for 61.5kg)
- **Additional**: 500-1000 ml per hour of exercise
- **Total**: 3-5 liters per day

#### **Rest Days**
- **Base Requirement**: 1,845-2,153 ml (30-35 ml per kg for 61.5kg)
- **Total**: 2-3 liters per day

### **Electrolyte Requirements**

#### **Sodium (Most Important)**
- **Daily**: 2,300-5,000 mg
- **During Exercise**: 300-800 mg per hour
- **Sources**: Sports drinks, electrolyte tablets, salt

#### **Potassium**
- **Daily**: 3,500-4,700 mg
- **During Exercise**: 200-400 mg per hour
- **Sources**: Bananas, potatoes, sports drinks

#### **Magnesium**
- **Daily**: 310-420 mg
- **During Exercise**: 50-100 mg per hour
- **Sources**: Nuts, seeds, dark chocolate

### **Hydration Monitoring**

#### **Urine Color Chart**
- **Clear**: Over-hydrated, reduce intake
- **Light Yellow**: Well-hydrated
- **Yellow**: Slightly dehydrated
- **Dark Yellow**: Dehydrated, increase intake
- **Brown**: Severely dehydrated, seek medical attention

#### **Weight Monitoring**
- **Pre-Run**: Weigh yourself
- **Post-Run**: Weigh yourself
- **Weight Loss**: Replace with 1.5× the weight lost in fluid
- **Example**: Lost 1 kg = drink 1.5 liters

## ⚠️ Common Nutrition Mistakes

### **Pre-Race Mistakes**
- **Eating too much**: Can cause GI distress
- **Trying new foods**: Stick to familiar foods
- **Not eating enough**: Can lead to bonking
- **Poor timing**: Too close to race start

### **During Race Mistakes**
- **Starting fueling too late**: Begin at 10 km
- **Inconsistent fueling**: Stick to schedule
- **Over-hydration**: Can cause hyponatremia
- **Under-hydration**: Can cause dehydration

### **Training Mistakes**
- **Skipping pre-workout fuel**: Can limit performance
- **Not practicing race nutrition**: Test everything in training
- **Ignoring hydration**: Can limit training quality
- **Poor recovery nutrition**: Can limit adaptation

## 🔬 Individualization & Testing

### **Personalized Nutrition Plan**

#### **Factors to Consider**
- **Body Weight**: Larger athletes need more fuel
- **Metabolism**: Fast metabolizers may need more frequent fueling
- **GI Tolerance**: Test different products and timing
- **Climate**: Hot weather increases hydration needs

#### **Testing Protocol**
- **Week 1-4**: Test basic hydration
- **Week 5-8**: Test carbohydrate fueling
- **Week 9-16**: Test race-day nutrition strategy
- **Week 17-22**: Perfect race-day plan
- **Week 23-26**: Finalize and practice

### **GI Distress Prevention**

#### **Common Causes**
- **High fiber foods**: Avoid before/during exercise
- **High fat foods**: Slow digestion
- **New foods**: Test in training first
- **Dehydration**: Can cause GI issues

#### **Prevention Strategies**
- **Test everything**: Don't try new foods on race day
- **Timing**: Allow 2-3 hours for digestion
- **Portion control**: Don't overeat
- **Hydration**: Stay well-hydrated
