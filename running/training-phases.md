# 🎯 Training Phases & Progression Strategy
**14-Week Marathon Training Plan**

## 📊 Phase Overview

The 14-week marathon training plan is divided into four distinct phases:

1. **Base Phase** (Weeks 1-4): Aerobic foundation building
2. **Build Phase** (Weeks 5-8): Strength, endurance, and marathon pace introduction
3. **Peak Phase** (Weeks 9-12): Maximum training stimulus
4. **Taper & Race Week** (Weeks 13-14): Recovery and peak performance

---

## 🏗️ Phase 1: Base Building (Weeks 1-4)

### **Primary Goals**
- Build aerobic foundation with MAF-pace running (6:25/km)
- Establish consistent training routine
- Develop basic endurance and running economy
- Introduce structured speed work gradually

### **Key Workout Progression**

#### **High-Intensity Days (Tuesdays - Intervals Only)**
- **Weeks 1-4**: 5×400m intervals @ 5:30/km (VO2 max foundation)
- **Tempo Work**: Integrated into Thursday MLR and Sunday LR (Week 5+)
- **Recovery**: 200m jog @ 7:00/km between intervals
- **Rationale**: Focus Tuesday on VO2 max, get threshold work in MLR/LR

#### **Distance Progression**
- **Medium-Long Run**: 14 km → 15 km
- **Long Run**: 24 km → 25 km
- **Weekly Volume**: 60 km → 64 km

### **Training Rationale**
- **MAF pace (6:40/km)**: Truly easy pace builds fat-burning efficiency and capillary density
- **Progressive overload**: Gradual distance increases minimize injury risk
- **Interval introduction**: Prepares body for higher intensity work
- **Tempo building**: Develops lactate threshold gradually
- **Polarized approach**: Easy runs are truly easy, quality work added to MLR/LR

### **Success Indicators**
- Comfortable completion of 25 km long runs
- Consistent MAF pace maintenance
- Recovery within 24 hours between workouts
- No persistent fatigue or soreness

---

## 🚀 Phase 2: Build Phase (Weeks 5-8)

### **Primary Goals**
- Increase overall training volume and intensity
- Develop marathon-specific endurance
- Introduce marathon-pace practice (6:24/km)
- Build mental and physical resilience

### **Key Workout Progression**

#### **High-Intensity Days**
- **Week 5 (Tuesday)**: 5 km Speed Check (special assessment)
- **Weeks 6-8 (Tuesday)**: 4×600m intervals @ 5:30/km (longer VO2 max work)
- **Tempo Work**: Now integrated into Thursday MLR (3km) starting Week 5
- **Long Run Quality**: Tempo sections added to Sunday LR starting Week 6
- **Marathon Pace**: First introduction in Week 8 (MLR & LR)

#### **Tempo Integration in MLR/LR (Build Phase Innovation)**
- **Week 5**: MLR adds 3km tempo
- **Week 6**: MLR adds 4km tempo, LR adds 5km tempo
- **Week 7**: MLR adds 4km tempo, LR adds 6km tempo

#### **Marathon-Pace Integration**
- **Week 8**: First introduction of 6:24/km pace
  - Thursday MLR: 5.5 MAF + 3 Tempo + 4 MP + 5.5 MAF (18 km)
  - Sunday Long Run: 16 MAF + 4 Tempo + 8 MP + 2 MAF (30 km)

#### **Distance Progression**
- **Medium-Long Run**: 16 km → 18 km (with quality sections)
- **Long Run**: 26 km → 30 km (with quality sections)
- **Weekly Volume**: 66 km → 73 km
- **Quality Work**: Increased tempo/MP sections maintain 70-80% easy split

### **Training Rationale**
- **Longer intervals**: Build anaerobic capacity and running economy
- **Marathon-pace practice**: Teaches neuromuscular pattern at goal pace
- **Tempo integration**: Regular threshold work in MLR/LR builds race fitness
- **Increased volume**: Develops endurance without overtraining
- **Pace specificity**: Critical for sub-4:30 marathon target
- **Polarized training**: Easy pace (6:40) + quality tempo/MP = optimal stimulus

### **Success Indicators**
- Comfortable completion of 28 km long runs
- Marathon-pace sections feel sustainable
- Recovery between hard workouts improves
- Weekly volume increases without injury

---

## ⚡ Phase 3: Peak Phase (Weeks 9-12)

### **Primary Goals**
- Hone race-pace feel and confidence
- Maintain peak fitness while avoiding over-fatigue
- Perfect marathon-pace execution
- Build mental toughness for race day

### **Key Workout Progression**

#### **High-Intensity Days (Tuesdays)**
- **Week 9**: 4×600m intervals @ 5:30/km (continued VO2 max)
- **Week 10**: 3×1000m intervals @ 5:30/km (peak VO2 max work)
- **Week 11**: 3×1000m intervals @ 5:30/km (maintain fitness)
- **Week 12**: 5 km Speed Check (final fitness assessment)
- **Tempo/MP Work**: Integrated into Thursday MLR & Sunday LR
- **Focus**: Quality intervals + distributed tempo/MP work

#### **Marathon-Pace Mastery**
- **Week 9**: 5 km @ marathon pace in MLR (19 km total), 30 km long run at MAF
- **Week 11**: 8 km @ marathon pace in long run (33 km total, peak MP work)
- **Week 12**: Peak long run (35 km) at MAF pace

#### **Distance Progression**
- **Medium-Long Run**: 19 km → 21 km
- **Long Run**: 30 km → 35 km
- **Weekly Volume**: 72 km → 80 km (peak)

### **Training Rationale**
- **VO2-max focus**: Improves maximum oxygen uptake and recovery
- **Marathon-pace confidence**: Builds mental and physical comfort at goal pace
- **Peak quality work**: 25-35% of weekly volume at tempo/MP
- **Peak volume**: Maximum training stimulus before taper
- **Race simulation**: Long runs with tempo/MP prepare for marathon demands
- **Polarized peak**: Easy base (6:40) + high quality volume = race readiness

### **Success Indicators**
- Marathon-pace sections feel comfortable and sustainable
- 35 km long runs completed without excessive fatigue
- Consistent pace maintenance throughout long runs
- Strong recovery between workouts

---

## 🎯 Phase 4: Taper & Race Week (Weeks 13-15)

### **Primary Goals**
- Reduce training volume while maintaining sharpness
- Maximize freshness and recovery for race day
- Perfect race-day preparation and strategy
- Achieve peak performance on race day

### **Key Workout Progression**

#### **High-Intensity Days**
- **Week 13 (Tuesday)**: 3×1000m intervals @ 5:30/km (reduced volume)
- **Week 14 (Tuesday)**: 3×400m light intervals @ 5:30/km (sharpening)
- **Quality in MLR/LR**: Maintained but reduced (4-6km MP sections)

#### **Distance Reduction**
- **Week 13**: Initial taper (70 km, -12.5% from peak)
- **Week 14**: Race week (62.2 km including race, -22% from peak)

#### **Long Run Taper**
- **Week 13**: 30 km (-14% from peak)
- **Week 14**: Race day (42.2 km)

### **Training Rationale**
- **Volume reduction**: Allows full recovery and glycogen restoration
- **Intensity maintenance**: Preserves fitness while reducing fatigue
- **Race preparation**: Final week focuses on race-day readiness
- **Peak performance**: Optimal balance of fitness and freshness

### **Success Indicators**
- Feeling fresh and energetic by race week
- No lingering fatigue or soreness
- Strong desire to run and race
- Optimal sleep and recovery

---

## 🔄 Phase Transitions & Recovery

### **Between Phase 1 & 2**
- **Speed check**: Week 5 assesses fitness progress
- **Volume jump**: Continue gradual increases
- **Intensity increase**: Longer intervals require proper adaptation

### **Between Phase 2 & 3**
- **Marathon-pace integration**: New stimulus requires careful monitoring
- **Volume peak**: Highest training load, watch for overtraining signs
- **Recovery importance**: Adequate rest between hard sessions critical

### **Between Phase 3 & 4**
- **Taper timing**: Begin taper when feeling strong, not when exhausted
- **Volume reduction**: Gradual decrease prevents detraining
- **Race preparation**: Focus shifts from training to race execution

## 📈 Phase-Specific Metrics

### **Base Phase Success Metrics**
- **Weekly consistency**: 5+ runs per week
- **MAF pace maintenance**: 6:40/km ± 10 seconds, HR 143-153 bpm
- **Recovery time**: <24 hours between hard sessions

### **Build Phase Success Metrics**
- **MAF pace comfort**: 6:40/km feels easy at HR <153 bpm
- **Tempo pace**: 5:50/km feels "comfortably hard" for 3-6km sections
- **Marathon-pace comfort**: 6:24/km feels sustainable for 4-8km sections
- **Volume tolerance**: Weekly increases without injury
- **Interval quality**: Maintain pace through all reps

### **Peak Phase Success Metrics**
- **Long run completion**: 35 km with 12km @ MP without excessive fatigue
- **Quality tolerance**: Handle 25-35% hard running weekly
- **Pace consistency**: Maintain target paces throughout quality sections
- **Recovery quality**: Strong between all workouts despite high quality volume

### **Taper Phase Success Metrics**
- **Freshness**: Feeling energetic and ready to race
- **Recovery**: No persistent fatigue or soreness
- **Readiness**: Strong desire to run and compete

---

## ⚠️ Phase-Specific Warnings

### **Base Phase Risks**
- **Too much too soon**: Resist increasing volume beyond plan
- **Neglecting recovery**: Easy days must be truly easy (6:40/km)
- **Pace discipline**: Don't run MAF runs too fast - keep HR <153 bpm
- **Ego running**: Accept that 6:40/km MAF pace will improve your marathon time

### **Build Phase Risks**
- **Tempo/MP overuse**: Don't add extra quality beyond plan
- **Volume overload**: Monitor cumulative fatigue with added quality
- **Recovery neglect**: Hard days require truly easy days (6:40/km)
- **Pace confusion**: Keep easy runs easy, don't drift toward tempo pace

### **Peak Phase Risks**
- **Overtraining**: Peak volume + quality (30-35% hard) can lead to burnout
- **Race-pace obsession**: Don't neglect MAF pace runs (6:40/km)
- **Injury risk**: High quality volume increases injury susceptibility
- **Incomplete recovery**: 65-70% easy split requires diligent recovery between sessions

### **Taper Phase Risks**
- **Detraining fear**: Trust the taper process
- **Over-resting**: Maintain some activity and intensity
- **Race-week anxiety**: Stick to the plan, don't add workouts
