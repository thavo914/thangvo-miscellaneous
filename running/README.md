# 🏃‍♂️ Marathon Training Plan - 14 Weeks
**Target Race**: March 22, 2026  
**Start Date**: December 9, 2025

A comprehensive 14-week Full Marathon training plan following the MAF (Maximum Aerobic Function) method and 80-20 rule, designed to help you achieve a sub-4:30 marathon safely and effectively.

## 📋 Plan Overview

- **Duration**: 14 weeks
- **Runs per week**: 5 runs + 2 rest days
- **Target**: Sub-4:30 marathon completion (6:24/km pace)
- **Methodology**: MAF-based training with 80-20 intensity distribution
- **Starting Fitness**: 50 km/week, 22 km longest run, easy pace 6:20-6:30/km

## 🎯 Training Paces

Based on your current fitness level:

- **Easy/MAF Pace**: 6:25/km (143-153 bpm) - Your current comfortable pace
- **Marathon Pace**: 6:24/km (sub-4:30 goal pace)
- **Tempo Pace**: 5:50/km (threshold, 160-169 bpm)
- **Interval Pace**: 5:30/km (VO2 max, 170-175 bpm)
- **Recovery Jog**: 7:00/km (between intervals)

## 🎯 Key Training Principles

- **MAF Pace (6:25/km)**: Builds aerobic base and fat-burning efficiency at your current comfortable pace
- **80-20 Rule**: 80% easy running, 20% hard running for optimal adaptation
- **Progressive Overload**: Weekly distance increases for gradual adaptation (56 km → 77 km peak)
- **Recovery Focus**: Strategic rest days and easy runs
- **Specificity**: Marathon-pace practice (6:24/km) in long runs
- **Taper Strategy**: 3-week taper for optimal race performance

- **Marathon Pace Work**: Introduced in Week 8, practiced through Week 11

## 📈 Volume Progression

- **Weeks 1-4**: Base building (56-63 km/week)
- **Weeks 5-8**: Build phase (65-69 km/week) + marathon pace introduction
- **Weeks 9-12**: Peak phase (72-77 km/week) + marathon pace mastery

## ⚠️ Important Notes

- **Practice race nutrition** during long runs
- **Include strength work** for injury prevention
- **Maintain 80-20 rule**: 80% easy, 20% hard throughout training

## ❤️ Heart Rate Zones Reference

### **MAF Heart Rate Calculation (Age 32)**
- **Base MAF HR**: 180 - 32 = 148 bpm
- **MAF Zone**: 143-153 bpm (MAF ± 5 bpm)
- **Max HR Estimate**: ~188 bpm (220 - age)

### **Training Zones**
| Zone | Heart Rate | % Max HR | Purpose | Workout Type |
|------|------------|----------|---------|--------------|
| **Recovery** | 120-140 bpm | 60-70% | Active recovery, easy runs | Recovery jogging |
| **MAF** | 143-153 bpm | 75-80% | Aerobic base building | Easy runs, long runs (6:25/km) |
| **Marathon Pace** | 155-165 bpm | 82-88% | Race pace | Marathon pace sections (6:24/km) |
| **Threshold** | 160-169 bpm | 85-90% | Lactate threshold | Tempo runs (5:50/km) |
| **VO₂ Max** | 170-175 bpm | 90-93% | Anaerobic capacity | Intervals (5:30/km) |

### **Heart Rate Monitoring Tips**
- **Use a chest strap**: More accurate than wrist-based sensors
- **Monitor trends**: Watch for elevated resting HR (overtraining sign)
- **Stay in zone**: If HR drifts above MAF zone, slow down
- **Recovery check**: HR should return to <120 bpm within 2-3 minutes post-workout
