## 🧠 Coaching Notes

**This week's focus**: 28 km long run — your longest yet. Patience is your superpower.

- 2×15 min tempo should feel "hard but manageable." If Rep 2 drops more than 5s/km from Rep 1, reduce to 2×12 min next week.
- **Thursday before 28 km Saturday**: The Fri rest day between Thu MLR and Sat LR is your key recovery buffer. Don't overextend Thursday.
- **28 km tip**: The final 6–8 km will test your aerobic base. If you maintained < 147 bpm through km 20, you'll have reserves.
- **Watch on Garmin**: "Acute Load" metric should be orange (high) after this week. Garmin may suggest "Hold" — that's correct.
