## 🧠 Coaching Notes

**This week's focus**: Let the adaptation set in. The work was done in Weeks 1–3.

- Recovery weeks are **not optional** — they are when your body actually builds fitness.
- **Goal HRV target**: By end of this week (Saturday/Sunday), your HRV should be trending back toward or above 50ms. This is your green light for Build Phase.
- **If HRV doesn't recover**: Extend recovery by adding one more easy week before starting Week 5. Never start Build Phase with HRV consistently below 45ms.
- **Watch on Garmin**: "Training Readiness" score should be recovering throughout this week — aim for 60+ by Saturday.
- Sleep, nutrition, and stress management matter MORE this week than the runs.
- Continue Magnesium Glycinate 300–400mg before bed 🌙
