**This week's focus**: Running 5 km at race pace while fatigued from Week 13 is the point.

- On race day you'll be fresh from the taper. 6:24/km will feel significantly easier than it does now.
- **28 km LR**: After last week's 35 km, 28 km will feel manageable. That's the plan working.
- **Taper starts next week**: The mental challenge of tapering (feeling sluggish, restless, losing fitness anxiety) is universal and temporary. Trust the process.
- Begin thinking about race logistics: early breakfast, gear, weather, start pace plan.
- **Watch on Garmin**: "Training Readiness" and "Body Battery" should be moderate this week. By end of Week 15 they should be high.
