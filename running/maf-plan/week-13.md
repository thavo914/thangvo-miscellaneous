## 🧠 Coaching Notes

**This week's focus**: 35 km long run is your marathon confidence builder. Trust the pace, not the feeling.

- **35 km at MAF pace** takes approximately 3:50–4:00 hours. If you cross this finish line upright and controlled, you're ready to race.
- **The last 5 km of 35 km**: HR will drift to 145–147 bpm even at easy pace. Normal. Slow down and walk if needed. **Do not fight the ceiling.**
- **Why Fri rest before 35 km matters**: Arriving at your longest run ever with a full rest day before it is the entire point of Option B schedule. You should feel fresher than expected at km 0.
- **Sunday + Monday double rest**: After 35 km, both days are mandatory. Do nothing active. Sleep. Eat. Let the adaptation happen.
- ⚠️ If Saturday morning HRV < 45ms → reduce LR to 28–30 km. Never push 35 km on low HRV.
