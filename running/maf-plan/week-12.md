## 🧠 Coaching Notes

**This week's focus**: Arrive at Weeks 13–14 with HRV ≥ 50ms and full mental freshness.

- Weeks 13–14 are the hardest weeks of the plan (marathon pace segments, 35 km LR, race rehearsal). Arriving fresh is not optional — it is training.
- **HRV target**: ≥ 50ms by Thursday. If it doesn't recover by Saturday, Week 13's LR must be shortened.
- **Key marker**: Notice how 20 km feels vs Week 1's 20 km. If it feels easy and your pace is noticeably faster at the same HR — your aerobic base is dramatically stronger. That's MAF working.
- **Mental reset**: Visualize your marathon during the 20 km run. What will the first 10 km feel like? Does 6:24/km feel controlled in your legs right now?
