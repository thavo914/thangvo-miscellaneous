## 🧠 Coaching Notes

**This week's focus**: Feel the aerobic base being built. Pay attention to cardiac drift.

- **Cardiac drift**: Normal for HR to rise 5–10 bpm in the final 30–40 min of a long run at the same pace. If HR climbs above 147, slow down — do not hold pace and hope HR stabilizes.
- **MAF progress marker**: Note your average pace at 143–145 bpm. Record it. Future improvement = faster pace at same HR.
- **Thu MLR advantage**: Full rest day (Fri) before Saturday's LR means you arrive at your most important session of the week well-rested.
- **Watch on Garmin**: Monitor "Body Battery" overnight. If it doesn't recover above 60, focus on sleep quality.
