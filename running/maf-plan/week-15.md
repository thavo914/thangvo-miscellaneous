## 🧠 Coaching Notes

**This week's focus**: Less is more. Trust the fitness you've built. The taper IS training.

- **Taper anxiety is real**: You'll feel sluggish in first few days after reducing volume. Legs may feel "heavy" or "flat." This passes. By Day 4–5, freshness returns.
- **Do NOT**: Add extra easy km because "it feels too little." Do NOT do extra workouts because "I have energy." Taper correctly = race well.
- **Sleep**: Make this your primary intervention. 8+ hours nightly. Early bedtime. Magnesium Glycinate 300–400mg every night.
- **HRV target**: Should rise steadily — 50ms by Thursday, 52–55ms by Saturday. If not rising, focus exclusively on sleep quality.
- **Garmin watch**: "Training Readiness" should begin climbing. "Body Battery" should recover to 80+ overnight.
