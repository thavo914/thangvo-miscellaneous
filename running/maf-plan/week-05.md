## 🧠 Coaching Notes

**This week's focus**: Your first tempo — stay disciplined, don't race the reps.

- 3×8 min is the gentlest tempo introduction possible. Recovery jogs are full recovery (walk if needed). This is not a time trial.
- **Wednesday HRV is your feedback loop**: The morning after Tuesday's first tempo reveals your stress response. If HRV tanks below 45ms, note it — tempo = high stress signal.
- **Thursday MLR gating**: Check HRV on Thursday morning. If still recovering from Tuesday, shorten MLR to 9–10 km.
- **Watch on Garmin**: After tempo reps, check "Recovery Time" estimate. A suggestion of 20–30h after 3×8 min is healthy.
