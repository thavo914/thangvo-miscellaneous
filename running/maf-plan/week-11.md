## 🧠 Coaching Notes

**This week's focus**: Learning to feel marathon race pace — critical neural programming.

- **Cruise intervals at 6:24/km** teach your brain and body to recognize race pace. The 90s recovery is deliberately short — feel the reps accumulate.
- **32 km long run**: You are now training at true marathon stimulus distances. Fat metabolism, mental resilience, and aerobic ceiling are all being tested.
- **Fri rest + Sat LR** is the most beneficial combination in this schedule. Arriving at 32 km fresh makes the quality of the aerobic stimulus far higher.
- **Watch on Garmin**: After the 32 km LR, check "Aerobic TE" — it should be 3.0–4.0 (Improving Significantly).
