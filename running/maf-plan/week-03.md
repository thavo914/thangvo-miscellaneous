## 🧠 Coaching Notes

**This week's focus**: 24 km long run is the longest of base phase — respect it.

- By Week 3, your aerobic system is adapting. Your pace at 145 bpm should be improving vs Week 1 — this is MAF progress.
- **Long run nutrition**: For 24 km+, consider a gel or banana at the 60–75 min mark.
- **Key recovery marker**: Saturday night HRV and Sunday morning HRV. If the 24 km LR tanks your HRV for both rest days, you may need to run slightly slower.
- With Thu MLR + Fri rest + Sat LR + Sun rest + Mon rest — you have the best possible recovery structure for your HRV profile.
- Never apply Finish Strong on both Thursday AND Saturday the same week (no back-to-back rule).

## 💪 Strength Sessions — Phase 1: Stability & Activation

> 🚦 **HRV Gate**: If morning HRV < 45ms → skip strength, do 10 min light mobility only.

### Session A — Wednesday (after easy run)
*~35 min total | Focus cue: "Wake up the glutes and lock in the core."*

**Warm-up** (5 min): Leg swings 10/side · Hip circles 10/side · Glute bridge 10 reps

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Tempo | Cue |
|----------|------------|-------|-----|
| Glute bridge | 2 × 15 | 3s down, 1s hold | Drive hips fully up, squeeze at top |
| Dead bug | 2 × 10/side | Controlled | Lower back flat on floor throughout |
| Side plank | 2 × 25 sec/side | Hold | Hips stacked, no sagging |
| Clamshell | 2 × 15/side | Controlled | Resist rotating hips back |

**Cooldown** (5 min): Hamstring stretch 30s/side · Hip flexor stretch 30s/side · Calf stretch 30s/side
