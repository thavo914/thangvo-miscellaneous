# 💪 Runner-Specific Strength Training Guide
**Method**: Bodyweight only | **Goal**: Injury prevention + running economy | **NOT hypertrophy**

---

## 📅 Weekly Scheduling Rules

| Day | Strength? | Reason |
|-----|-----------|--------|
| Mon | ❌ Never | Rest day |
| Tue | ❌ Never | Hard quality run day — protect neuromuscular readiness |
| **Wed** | ✅ **Session A** | After easy run — primary strength day |
| **Thu** | ✅ **Session B** | After MLR — secondary strength day |
| Fri | ❌ Never | Rest day before LR |
| Sat | ❌ Never | Long Run day |
| Sun | ❌ Never | Rest day |

> **Order always**: Run first → eat/rest 20–30 min → strength session. Never reverse this.

---

## 🚦 HRV Gate for Strength

| Morning HRV | Action |
|-------------|--------|
| < 45ms | ❌ Skip strength entirely → 10 min light mobility only |
| 45–49ms | ✅ Proceed but reduce to 2 sets per exercise |
| ≥ 50ms | ✅ Full session as planned |

---

## 📋 Session Template (Every Strength Day)

**Total time: 30–40 min**

| Block | Content | Duration |
|-------|---------|----------|
| Warm-up | Leg swings (10/side), hip circles (10/side), glute bridges (10 reps, slow) | 5 min |
| Main Work | 3–4 exercises, phase-appropriate sets/reps | 20–25 min |
| Cooldown | Hamstring stretch (30s/side), hip flexor stretch (30s/side), calf stretch (30s/side) | 5–10 min |

---

## 🏗️ Phase 1 — Base (Weeks 1–4): Stability & Activation

**Focus**: Glute activation, single-leg stability, core anti-rotation  
**Sets/Reps**: 2 sets × 10–15 reps | Tempo: 3 sec down, 1 sec hold, 1 sec up | Rest: 60 sec between sets

### Session A (Wednesday — after easy run)
*Focus cue: "Wake up the glutes and lock in the core."*

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Tempo | Notes |
|----------|------------|-------|-------|
| Glute bridge | 2 × 15 | 3s down | Drive hips fully up, squeeze at top |
| Dead bug | 2 × 10/side | Controlled | Lower leg + opposite arm, keep lower back flat |
| Side plank | 2 × 25 sec/side | Hold | Hips stacked, no sagging |
| Clamshell | 2 × 15/side | Controlled | Resist rotating hips back |

**Estimated duration**: ~33 min incl. warm-up/cooldown

---

### Session B (Thursday — after MLR)
*Focus cue: "Build single-leg stability that holds at km 30."*

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Tempo | Notes |
|----------|------------|-------|-------|
| Single-leg glute bridge | 2 × 10/side | 3s down | Keep pelvis level throughout |
| Step-up (bodyweight) | 2 × 12/side | Controlled | Full hip extension at top |
| Wall sit | 2 × 40 sec | Hold | 90° knee angle, back flat on wall |
| Clamshell | 2 × 15/side | Controlled | Alternate side each set |

**Estimated duration**: ~35 min incl. warm-up/cooldown

---

### ♻️ Recovery Week (Week 4) — Session A only (Wednesday)
*Session B (Thursday) is SKIPPED. Volume reduced 40%.*

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|----------|------------|-------|
| Glute bridge | 2 × 10 | Easy, simple glute activation |
| Dead bug | 2 × 8/side | Controlled |
| Side plank | 1 × 20 sec/side | Maintenance only |

**Estimated duration**: ~25 min

## 🔨 Phase 2 — Build (Weeks 5–10): Strength & Single-Leg Power

**Focus**: Single-leg strength, hip drive, running posture endurance  
**Sets/Reps**: 3 sets × 8–12 reps | Tempo: controlled (2s down) | Rest: 75 sec between sets  
**RIR**: Stop 2 reps short of failure always

### Session A (Wednesday — after easy run)
*Focus cue: "Power through the hip — every rep is a stride."*

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Tempo | Notes |
|----------|------------|-------|-------|
| Bulgarian split squat | 3 × 10/side | 2s down | Rear foot elevated on chair/bench, chest tall |
| Single-leg RDL (bodyweight) | 3 × 8/side | 2s down | Hinge at hip, slight knee bend, back flat |
| Hip thrust (unloaded) | 3 × 12 | 1s hold | Full hip extension, squeeze glutes |
| Plank | 3 × 40 sec | Hold | Elbows under shoulders, neutral spine |

**Estimated duration**: ~38 min incl. warm-up/cooldown

---

### Session B (Thursday — after MLR)
*Focus cue: "Protect the hamstrings that keep you upright at km 38."*

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Tempo | Notes |
|----------|------------|-------|-------|
| Nordic hamstring curl (assisted) | 3 × 6 | Very slow 4s down | Use hands to catch if needed — eccentric only |
| Reverse lunge with knee drive | 3 × 10/side | Controlled | Drive knee up at top, don't rush |
| Copenhagen plank | 3 × 20 sec/side | Hold | Inner thigh on chair, outer leg free |
| Plank variation (reach) | 3 × 8/side | Controlled | Plank + opposite arm forward reach |

**Estimated duration**: ~40 min incl. warm-up/cooldown

---

### ♻️ Recovery Week (Week 8) — Session A only (Wednesday)
*Session B (Thursday) is SKIPPED. Volume reduced 40%.*

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|----------|------------|-------|
| Bulgarian split squat | 2 × 8/side | Slightly easier range of motion |
| Single-leg RDL | 2 × 6/side | Focus on balance, not depth |
| Plank | 2 × 30 sec | Maintenance |

**Estimated duration**: ~28 min

## ⚡ Phase 3 — Peak (Weeks 11–14): Power Endurance

**Focus**: Late-race fatigue resistance, elastic strength, force absorption  
**Sets/Reps**: 3 sets, strength reps (8) and power reps (5) mixed | Rest: 90 sec (longer, needed for power)  
**RIR**: 2 — never train to failure

### Session A (Wednesday — after easy run)
*Focus cue: "Train the muscles that carry you through the wall."*

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Tempo | Notes |
|----------|------------|-------|-------|
| Jump squat | 3 × 5 | Explosive up, soft land | **Low volume only** — land quietly, knees tracking toes |
| Single-leg hop to stick | 3 × 5/side | Stick landing 2 sec | Small hop, absorb cleanly, no wobble |
| Bulgarian split squat | 3 × 8/side | 2s down | Maintain Phase 2 baseline |
| Copenhagen plank | 3 × 25 sec/side | Hold | Progress from Phase 2 |

**Estimated duration**: ~40 min incl. warm-up/cooldown

---

### Session B (Thursday — after MLR)
*Focus cue: "Build the engine that keeps stride length at km 35."*

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Tempo | Notes |
|----------|------------|-------|-------|
| Step-up with drive (explosive) | 3 × 8/side | Explosive but controlled | Drive knee up aggressively at top |
| Single-leg RDL | 3 × 8/side | 2s down | Maintain Phase 2 baseline |
| Nordic hamstring curl | 3 × 6 | 4s down | Same as Phase 2, add focus on control |
| Plank | 3 × 45 sec | Hold | Progress from Phase 2 |

**Estimated duration**: ~40 min incl. warm-up/cooldown

---

### ♻️ Recovery Week (Week 12) — Session A only (Wednesday)
*Session B (Thursday) is SKIPPED. Volume reduced 40%.*

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|----------|------------|-------|
| Bulgarian split squat | 2 × 8/side | No power, just strength |
| Single-leg RDL | 2 × 8/side | Controlled |
| Plank | 2 × 35 sec | Maintenance |

**Estimated duration**: ~28 min

## 🎯 Phase 4 — Taper (Weeks 15–16): Maintenance Only

**Focus**: Maintain neuromuscular readiness, zero accumulated fatigue  
**Sets/Reps**: 2 sets × 8 reps | No jumping, no new movements | Rest: 60 sec

### Session A only (Wednesday — 1 session per week)
*Focus cue: "Stay sharp without adding fatigue — just maintenance."*

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Tempo | Notes |
|----------|------------|-------|-------|
| Bulgarian split squat | 2 × 8/side | 2s down | Below max range, easy execution |
| Single-leg RDL | 2 × 8/side | 2s down | Controlled, no depth push |
| Plank | 2 × 40 sec | Hold | Easy, relaxed |

**Estimated duration**: ~30 min incl. warm-up/cooldown

> **Week 16 (Race Week)**: Skip strength entirely. Light mobility (10 min) only if needed.

---

## 🎯 Key Muscles Priority Reminder

| Priority | Muscle | Why It Matters |
|----------|--------|----------------|
| 1 | **Glutes (max + med)** | Primary power source, prevents knee collapse |
| 2 | **Hamstrings** | Protects knee, improves stride length |
| 3 | **Hip flexors** | Running posture + knee lift when fatigued |
| 4 | **Core (anti-rotation)** | Maintains form at km 35+ |
| 5 | **Calves / soleus** | Absorbs impact, reduces shin splints risk |

---

## ❌ What to Avoid — Always

- Heavy bilateral squats or deadlifts (high recovery cost, not needed)
- Upper body volume (wastes recovery budget)
- Training to failure (always stop 2 reps short — RIR 2)
- Strength the day before or after LR (Fri and Sun are always rest)
- Any new movements in Taper phase (Weeks 15–16)
