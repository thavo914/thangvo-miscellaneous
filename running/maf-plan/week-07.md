## 🧠 Coaching Notes

**This week's focus**: Moving from 4×8 min to 2×12 min — sustained tempo is a key mental shift.

- The 12-min rep feels harder because you can't coast. Focus on **even splits** — don't start hot.
- **26 km LR**: Break into thirds (9 + 9 + 8 km). Stay patient in the first 18 km.
- **Saturday advantage**: With a full Fri rest day going in and Sun + Mon rest coming out, your 26 km long run has the best surrounding recovery structure possible. This is ideal for your HRV profile.
