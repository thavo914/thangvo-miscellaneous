## 🧠 Coaching Notes

**This week's focus**: 30 km long run — your single biggest confidence builder.

- **30 km at MAF pace** trains fat metabolism, mental toughness, and builds your aerobic buffer for the marathon's final 12 km.
- **Fri rest before Sat 30 km**: Arriving at your most demanding run of the plan with a fresh rest day matters — this is why Option B schedule is ideal for you.
- If HR is stable under 147 through 25 km, your aerobic base is excellent. If it's drifting significantly from km 20 onward, focus Peak Phase on more aerobic volume rather than adding intensity.
- After this week you enter Peak Phase (Weeks 11–14) — Tuesday shifts to race-specific intervals. The training ramps up. Trust the base you've built.
- **Garmin check**: "VO2 Max estimate" should be trending upward. MAF training genuinely improves VO2 Max over 10+ weeks. 🎉
