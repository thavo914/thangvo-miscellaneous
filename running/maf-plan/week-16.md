### Saturday — MARATHON RACE DAY 🏆

**Pre-Race Protocol (3–4 hours before start):**
| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| –3.5h | Wake up. Eat race breakfast (rice/banana/oatmeal, 400–600 kcal) |
| –2.5h | Arrive at venue. Warm up lightly (10 min easy walk/jog) |
| –45 min | Take first gel if using |
| –20 min | Dynamic warm-up, strides |
| –5 min | At start line, deep breathing |

**Race Execution Plan**:
| Segment | Km | Target Pace | Strategy |
|---------|----|-------------|----------|
| Early Phase | 0–10 km | 6:30–6:35/km | **Run slower than target pace** — let others go |
| Middle Phase | 10–30 km | 6:24–6:28/km | Find your rhythm, stay consistent |
| Late Phase | 30–38 km | 6:24/km or faster | Aerobic base pays off here |
| Final Push | 38–42.2 km | Best effort | Race to the finish |

**In-Race Nutrition**:
- Gel or banana at km 10, 20, 30, and 36
- Water at every station — sip, don't gulp
- Salt/electrolyte at km 25–30 if available

**If feeling bad at km 35+**: Slow to 6:45–7:00/km for 2–3 km to recover, then re-accelerate. Do not stop unless injured.

**Target Finish**: **< 4:30:00** 🎯

## 🧠 Race Week Coaching Notes

**Your only job this week: rest, stay calm, and trust 16 weeks of work.**

- **HRV race week targets**:
  - Wednesday morning: ≥ 52ms
  - Friday morning: ≥ 54ms
  - Saturday morning: ≥ 55ms (green light for your best race)
  - If Saturday HRV < 48ms → still race, but be conservative in first 15 km
- **Sleep**: The most important race prep. If you have one bad night (Friday), don't panic — Thursday's sleep matters more physiologically.
- **Garmin on race day**: Consider running by feel and pace only. Staring at HR during a race can cause anxiety. Trust your training.
- You cannot gain fitness in race week. Every decision should protect what you've already built.

---

## 💪 Strength — Race Week: SKIP ALL SESSIONS ❌

> **No strength training this week.** Zero. Not even "light" strength.
>
> If you feel restless and need to move on Wednesday or Thursday:
> - **10 min light mobility only**: Leg swings, hip circles, glute bridges (bodyweight), hamstring stretch
> - This is NOT a strength session. It is nervous system calming and blood flow only.

**Why**: Strength creates micro-damage in muscle fibers. Any strength this week risks arriving at the start line with residual soreness or reduced muscle recruitment. The neuromuscular fitness from 15 weeks of training is fully banked — nothing you do now adds to it, but you can subtract from it.

## 🎯 Post-Race Protocol

| Action | Timing |
|--------|--------|
| Keep walking for 10–15 min | Immediately after finish |
| Eat immediately (banana, bar, protein) | Within 30 min |
| Hydrate with electrolytes | Throughout |
| Avoid sitting/lying down immediately | First 30 min |
| Ice/compression on legs | If available |
| No running for 7–10 days | Post-marathon recovery |
| Gentle walking Day 1–3 | Light movement only |
| Easy jogging resumes | Day 10–14 post-race |
