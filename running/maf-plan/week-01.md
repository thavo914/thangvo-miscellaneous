## 🧠 Coaching Notes

**This week's focus**: Trust the process. In base phase, slow feels wrong but it's right.

- Your MAF HR is 147 bpm — treat it as an **absolute ceiling**, not a target
- If your GPS pace shows 7:00–7:30/km to stay under 147 bpm, that is **correct and expected**
- **Why Thu MLR + Sat LR?** You now get a full rest day (Fri) between runs, AND two rest days after the LR (Sun + Mon) before Tuesday. This is ideal for your recovery profile.
- **Watch on Garmin**: Check HRV every morning. Thursday's MLR will be gated by Tuesday+Wednesday's HRV trend.
- Magnesium Glycinate 300–400mg before bed nightly 🌙

## 💪 Strength Sessions — Phase 1: Stability & Activation

> 🚦 **HRV Gate**: If morning HRV < 45ms → skip strength, do 10 min light mobility only. See [strength-guide.md](strength-guide.md) for full details.

### Session A — Wednesday (after easy run)
*~35 min total | Focus cue: "Wake up the glutes and lock in the core."*

**Warm-up** (5 min): Leg swings 10/side · Hip circles 10/side · Glute bridge 10 reps

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Tempo | Cue |
|----------|------------|-------|-----|
| Glute bridge | 2 × 15 | 3s down, 1s hold | Drive hips fully up, squeeze at top |
| Dead bug | 2 × 10/side | Controlled | Lower back flat on floor throughout |
| Side plank | 2 × 25 sec/side | Hold | Hips stacked, no sagging |
| Clamshell | 2 × 15/side | Controlled | Resist rotating hips back |

**Cooldown** (5 min): Hamstring stretch 30s/side · Hip flexor stretch 30s/side · Calf stretch 30s/side
