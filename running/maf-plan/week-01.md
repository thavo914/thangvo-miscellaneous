## 📋 Session Details

### Tuesday — Easy Zone 2 (6 km)
- **Full Session**: 6 km continuous at < 147 bpm
- **Pace**: Let HR dictate (~6:40–7:20/km)
- **RPE**: 4 (Conversational, easy effort)
- **Focus**: Smooth stride, relaxed upper body. Walk if HR approaches 147.

---

### Wednesday — Easy Zone 2 (6 km)
- **Full Session**: 6 km at < 147 bpm
- **RPE**: 4
- **Focus**: Maintain consistent low HR. Followed by Strength Session A.

---

### Thursday — Medium Long Run / MLR (10 km)
- **Full Session**: 10 km at < 147 bpm
- **RPE**: 4–5
- **Finish Strong eligibility**: ✅ If HRV ≥ 50ms + Garmin "Balanced" 2+ days
  - Final 1 km at MRP (6:24/km) — **Base phase: MRP only, no tempo**
  - Follow immediately with 1 km easy flush (< 140 bpm)
  - Total with Finish Strong: ~11 km

---

### Saturday — Long Run: Trail Run 🌲 (14 km)
- **Full Session**: 14 km Trail Run strictly at < 147 bpm
- **Terrain Strategy**:
  - **Uphills**: Power walk / hike all steep uphills to prevent HR spikes above 147.
  - **Downhills**: Keep stride short and controlled to protect knees and quads.
  - **Pace**: Ignore GPS pace completely; terrain variations make pace irrelevant. Focus 100% on HR and RPE.
- **Hydration/Fueling**: Carry handheld bottle or vest (water/electrolytes). Sip every 15–20 mins.
- **Post-Run**: Eat within 45 min (carbs + protein) to kickstart recovery.

---

### Sunday — Recovery Shakeout Run (4 km)
- **Full Session**: 4 km ultra-easy jog at < 140 bpm
- **Purpose**: Active recovery to flush legs after Saturday's trail run.
- **RPE**: 3 (Very light effort)

## 🧠 Coaching Notes

**This week's focus**: Base building on 5 days/week with Saturday trail long run.

- **Trail Running HR Management**: Trail running increases heart rate significantly on climbs. **Do not hesitate to power hike uphill** to maintain your $<147$ bpm ceiling.
- **5-Day Flow**: Tue (6km) $\rightarrow$ Wed (6km) $\rightarrow$ Thu (10km MLR) $\rightarrow$ Fri (Rest) $\rightarrow$ Sat (14km Trail LR) $\rightarrow$ Sun (4km Recovery).
- **Rest Days**: Monday (full rest after Sunday shakeout) and Friday (fresh legs before Saturday trail LR).
- Magnesium Glycinate 300–400mg before bed nightly 🌙

## 💪 Strength Sessions — Phase 1: Stability & Activation

> 🚦 **HRV Gate**: If morning HRV < 45ms → skip strength, do 10 min light mobility only. See [strength-guide.md](strength-guide.md) for full details.

### Session A — Wednesday (after easy run)
*~35 min total | Focus cue: "Wake up the glutes and lock in the core."*

**Warm-up** (5 min): Leg swings 10/side · Hip circles 10/side · Glute bridge 10 reps

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Tempo | Cue |
|----------|------------|-------|-----|
| Glute bridge | 2 × 15 | 3s down, 1s hold | Drive hips fully up, squeeze at top |
| Dead bug | 2 × 10/side | Controlled | Lower back flat on floor throughout |
| Side plank | 2 × 25 sec/side | Hold | Hips stacked, no sagging |
| Clamshell | 2 × 15/side | Controlled | Resist rotating hips back |

**Cooldown** (5 min): Hamstring stretch 30s/side · Hip flexor stretch 30s/side · Calf stretch 30s/side

---

### Session B — Thursday (after MLR)
*~35 min total | Focus cue: "Build single-leg stability for uneven trails."*

**Warm-up** (5 min): Leg swings 10/side · Hip circles 10/side · Glute bridge 10 reps

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Tempo | Cue |
|----------|------------|-------|-----|
| Single-leg glute bridge | 2 × 10/side | 3s down | Keep pelvis level throughout |
| Step-up (bodyweight) | 2 × 12/side | Controlled | Full hip extension at top |
| Wall sit | 2 × 40 sec | Hold | 90° knee angle, back flat on wall |
| Clamshell | 2 × 15/side | Controlled | Alternate side each set |

**Cooldown** (5 min): Same as Session A
