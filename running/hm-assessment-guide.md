# 🏃‍♂️ Half Marathon Assessment Guide
**Today's HM: Fitness Test for Marathon Training Plan**

This half marathon is an excellent opportunity to assess your current fitness level and validate your training plan. Since it's your first long run after 5 weeks (including illness recovery), this will give us valuable data.

---

## 🎯 Purpose of Today's HM

1. **Fitness Assessment**: Determine your current endurance level
2. **Pace Validation**: Confirm your easy pace and marathon pace
3. **Recovery Status**: Check how well you've recovered from illness
4. **Plan Validation**: Verify if the training plan matches your current fitness

---

## 📊 What to Monitor During the HM

### **Key Metrics to Track:**

1. **Average Pace**
   - What pace can you comfortably maintain?
   - How does this compare to your 6:20-6:30/km easy pace?

2. **Heart Rate**
   - Average HR during the run
   - Does it stay in MAF zone (143-153 bpm) or higher?
   - How does your HR respond to pace changes?

3. **Effort Level**
   - How does the effort feel?
   - Comfortable, moderate, or hard?
   - Can you hold a conversation?

4. **Pace Consistency**
   - Can you maintain steady pace throughout?
   - Do you fade in the later kilometers?

5. **Recovery**
   - How do you feel after finishing?
   - Recovery time to normal breathing/HR
   - Soreness level post-run

---

## 🎯 Recommended Approach

### **Option 1: MAF Pace Run (Recommended)**

Since this is your first long run after illness, treat it as a MAF pace assessment:

- **Target Pace**: 6:25/km (your planned easy/MAF pace)
- **Heart Rate**: 143-153 bpm
- **Effort**: Comfortable, conversational
- **Focus**: Can you complete 21.1 km comfortably at this pace?

**This tells us**: If you can comfortably complete a HM at MAF pace, you're ready for the training plan.

---

### **Option 2: Marathon Pace Test**

If you're feeling strong and fully recovered, you could test marathon pace:

- **Target Pace**: 6:24/km (your goal marathon pace)
- **Strategy**: 
  - First 5 km: 6:30/km (warm-up)
  - Next 10 km: 6:24/km (marathon pace test)
  - Last 6.1 km: 6:30/km or finish strong if feeling good

**This tells us**: If you can hold marathon pace for 10 km comfortably, you're in great shape for the plan.

---

### **Option 3: Progressive Run**

Start easy and gradually increase pace:

- **Km 1-5**: 6:40/km (easy warm-up)
- **Km 6-10**: 6:25/km (MAF pace)
- **Km 11-15**: 6:24/km (marathon pace)
- **Km 16-21.1**: Finish at comfortable pace or push if feeling good

**This tells us**: Your ability to run different paces and how you feel at each intensity.

---

## ✅ Post-HM Assessment Questions

After your HM, answer these questions:

### **Pace & Performance:**
1. What was your average pace? ________ km/min
2. Did you maintain consistent pace throughout? Yes/No
3. How did the pace feel? (Comfortable/Moderate/Hard)
4. What was your finishing time? ________

### **Heart Rate:**
5. What was your average heart rate? ________ bpm
6. Did you stay in MAF zone (143-153 bpm)? Yes/No/Partly
7. How quickly did your HR return to normal after finishing? ________ minutes

### **Effort & Feel:**
8. How did the effort feel overall? (1-10, where 5 = comfortable)
9. Could you have run faster? Yes/No
10. Could you have run longer? Yes/No/Not sure

### **Recovery:**
11. How do you feel immediately after? (Good/Fatigued/Exhausted)
12. Any pain or unusual soreness? Yes/No - If yes, where?
13. How long until you felt recovered? ________

### **Plan Validation:**
14. Did this HM feel appropriate for your current fitness? Yes/No
15. Based on this run, are you ready to start the training plan? Yes/No/Not sure

## 📋 How Results Inform the Training Plan

### **If HM Felt Comfortable at 6:25/km:**
- ✅ **You're ready for the plan!**
- ✅ Start Week 1 as planned (56 km/week, 22 km long run)
- ✅ Your current fitness matches the plan's starting point

### **If HM Was Challenging at 6:25/km:**
- ⚠️ Consider a gentler start
- Option: Modify Week 1 to 50-52 km instead of 56 km
- Focus on building base for 1-2 weeks before increasing volume

### **If HM Was Easy at 6:25/km:**
- ✅ **You might be ready to start stronger!**
- Option: Could potentially start at Week 2 level (58 km)
- Or proceed as planned - conservative is better than aggressive

### **If You Held 6:24/km Comfortably for 10+ km:**
- ✅ **Excellent fitness!**
- ✅ Marathon pace feels good - great sign for your goal
- ✅ Plan is well-suited to your current level

### **If HM Pace Was Faster Than Expected:**
- Note: Your easy pace might actually be faster than 6:25/km
- We may need to adjust training paces upward
- Still stay conservative - aerobic base is most important

## ⚠️ Important Notes

### **After Illness Recovery:**
- **Don't push too hard** - This is a fitness test, not a race
- **Listen to your body** - If something doesn't feel right, slow down or stop
- **Focus on completion**, not time
- **Monitor recovery** - How you recover tells us a lot

### **Safety First:**
- If you experience unusual pain, stop
- If you feel dizzy or unwell, stop
- If you're excessively fatigued, slow down or walk
- Remember: You're coming back from illness - be smart!

   - Stretching: Light stretching, don't overdo it

2. **Record Your Data**:
   - Fill out the assessment questions above
   - Note any observations about how you felt
   - Track your recovery over next 24-48 hours

3. **Plan Adjustment**:
   - Share your results if you want plan modifications
