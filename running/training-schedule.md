# 📅 Complete 26-Week Training Schedule

## 🗓️ Weekly Schedule Overview

- **Monday**: Complete rest / light active recovery
- **Tuesday**: HI Day (odd weeks = Interval, even weeks = Tempo) + 10′ warm-up + 10′ cool-down
- **Wednesday**: Easy Run (MAF pace)
- **Thursday**: Medium-Long Run (MAF pace)
- **Friday**: Light rest / mobility & foam-roll
- **Saturday**: Easy Run (MAF pace)
- **Sunday**: Long Run (MAF pace, morning)

## 🏁 Speed Check Guidelines

**Speed-check weeks (6, 12, 18, 24):**

- **Warm-up 2 km** and **cool-down 1 km** both at MAF zone 143–153 bpm
- In the 5 km TT, gradually increase HR as shown in the table below, reaching ~95–96% HRmax by the final km while maintaining pace

**Recovery weeks (CB-1, CB-2):**

- **CB-1**: Light recovery week with strides and reduced volume
- **CB-2**: Light tempo introduction with gradual volume increase
- Both weeks help prevent overtraining and allow adaptation

| Km | Target Pace | Cumulative Time | HR Zone (bpm) |
|----|-------------|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | 5:45 | 5:45 | 160–165 (85–88% HRmax) |
| 2 | 5:40 | 11:25 | 165–169 (88–90% HRmax) |
| 3 | 5:35 | 17:00 | 169–173 (90–92% HRmax) |
| 4 | 5:30 | 22:30 | 173–177 (92–94% HRmax) |
| 5 | 5:25 | 27:55 | 177–180 (94–96% HRmax) |
