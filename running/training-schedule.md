## 🗓️ Weekly Schedule Overview

- **Monday**: **Strength A** (Glutes) + Recovery (No Running)
- **Tuesday**: **Strength C** (Post-Run Posture) + High Intensity Day (Intervals)
- **Wednesday**: Easy Run + **Strength B** (Core/Post-Run)
- **Thursday**: Medium-Long Run (MAF pace)
- **Friday**: **Strength D** (Arms) (No Running)
- **Saturday**: **Long Run** (MAF pace + quality sections)
- **Sunday**: Complete Rest / [Mobility](mobility-routine.md)

## 🎯 Training Paces (Based on Your Current Fitness)

- **Easy/MAF Pace**: 6:40/km (143-153 bpm) - *Adjusted for sustainable aerobic development*
- **Marathon Pace**: 6:24/km (sub-4:30 goal pace)
- **Tempo Pace**: 5:50/km (threshold, 160-169 bpm)
- **Interval Pace**: 5:30/km (VO2 max, 170-175 bpm)
- **Recovery Jog**: 7:00/km (between intervals)

## 📊 Training Principles

- **80-20 Rule**: 80% easy running, 20% hard running
- **MAF Method**: Build aerobic base with easy pace
- **Progressive Overload**: Gradual volume increases
- **5 Days/Week Running**: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Saturday, Sunday
- **2 Rest Days**: Monday (complete rest), Friday (light mobility)

## 🌦️ Tuesday Workout Decision Guide

### **Choose INTERVALS (Primary Option)**
✅ Weather allows outdoor running  
✅ Access to track or safe flat path  
✅ Conditions are reasonable (not icy/extreme)

**Benefit**: VO2 max development, race-specific outdoor adaptation

### **Choose TEMPO (Treadmill Alternative)**
✅ Bad weather (ice, heavy snow, extreme heat/cold)  
✅ Treadmill-only access that day  
✅ Safety concerns for outdoor intervals

**Benefit**: Easier to execute on treadmill (set speed once), still builds threshold

**Note**: Both options provide quality training stimulus. Choose based on practical conditions!

## 🔄 Optional Cutback Weeks (use up to 2x if needed)

If a long run feels too much or recovery lags, replace any upcoming week with a cutback:
- **Volume**: Reduce total by ~15-20% (e.g., 58 km → ~46-49 km)
- **Long Run**: Reduce by 4-6 km (e.g., 24 km → 18-20 km; 30 km → 24-26 km)
- **Intensity**: Keep Tuesday light—either strides or short tempo (15-20') instead of full intervals/tempo
- **MLR**: Reduce by 2-4 km
- **Goal**: Arrive fresh the following week; return to the planned progression once recovered
