## 🚨 Warning Signs to Watch

**Reduce volume immediately if you notice:**
- Elevated resting heart rate (5+ bpm above normal)
- Difficulty maintaining MAF pace (6:40/km feels hard)
- Persistent muscle soreness beyond 48 hours
- Poor sleep quality
- Low motivation or irritability

**Action**: Skip Thursday MLR or reduce Saturday LR to 22-23 km
