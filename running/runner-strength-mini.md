## 📅 Weekly Scheduling (4-Day Run Cycle)

To balance stress and recovery, place these sessions on your non-running days or after easy runs.

| Day | Run Type | Strength/Stability |
|:---:|:---|:---|
| **Mon** | Rest / Recovery | **Session A: Glutes & Stability** |
| **Tue** | **Speed/Tempo Run** | **AM: Session C (Posture)** |
| **Wed** | **Easy Run** | **PM: Session B (Core)** |
| **Thu** | **Medium Run** | Rest |
| **Fri** | Rest | **AM: Session D (Arms)** |
| **Sat** | **Long Run** | Rest |
| **Sun** | Rest | **Optional: Mobility Routine** |

## ⚡ Session A: The "Glute & Stability" Powerhouse
**Focus**: Hips, Glutes, Ankle Stability. (Reduces Knee Pain & IT Band issues)
**Time**: 20–25 Minutes | **Equipment**: Mini Resistance Band

| Exercise | Instructions | Sets/Reps | Rest |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Banded Clamshells** | Band above knees. Keep hips stacked; lift top knee. | 3 x 15-20 / side | 30s |
| **Banded Monster Walk** | Band at ankles. Side-steps in a partial squat. | 3 x 15 steps / side | 45s |
| **Single-Leg Calf Raises** | Slow down (3s). Use a wall for balance. | 3 x 12-15 / side | 30s |
| **Single-Leg Balance** | Bench/Ground. Eyes closed for 15s to challenge ankle. | 3 x 45s / side | 30s |
| **Lateral Band Walk** | Band at mid-shin. Maintain tension. | 2 x 20 steps / side | 45s |

> [!TIP]
> **Why Ankle Stability?** Strong ankles absorb the impact of every stride, preventing the "collapsing" form that leads to knee and hip pain after 30km.

---

## ⚡ Session B: The "Endurance & Form" Foundation
**Focus**: Hamstrings, Core, Back. (Prevents Lower Back Pain & "The Hunch" in late race)
**Time**: 20–25 Minutes | **Equipment**: Bodyweight (Bands optional)

| Exercise | Instructions | Sets/Reps | Rest |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Single-Leg RDL** | Hinge at hip. Keep back flat. Focus on balance. | 3 x 10 / side | 45s |
| **Banded Glute Bridge** | Band above knees. Push knees out against band. | 3 x 20 reps | 30s |
| **Dead Bugs** | Lower opposite arm/leg slowly. Keep back flat on floor. | 3 x 12 / side | 30s |
| **Bird-Dog** | Focus on a straight line from finger to toe. | 3 x 12 / side | 30s |
| **Plank (Banded Optional)** | Band at wrists. Tap hands forward/side to challenge core. | 3 x 60s | 45s |

## ☀️ Session C: Morning Posture & Back (Morning Routine)
**Focus**: Upper Back, Scapular Stability. (Prevents "The Slouch" as you tire)
**Time**: 12–15 Minutes | **Equipment**: Resistance Band

| Exercise | Instructions | Sets/Reps | Rest |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Band Pull-Aparts** | Arms straight at chest height. Pull band apart to squeeze shoulder blades. | 2 x 15-20 | 30s |
| **Wall Slides** | Back/arms against wall. Slide arms up to 'Y' and down to 'W'. | 2 x 10-12 | 30s |
| **Scapular Push-ups** | In plank, move only shoulder blades (don't bend elbows). | 2 x 15 | 30s |
| **Chin Tucks** | Standing tall, pull chin straight back to align neck. | 2 x 10 (hold 3s) | 20s |

---

## ☀️ Session D: Morning Arm Endurance (Morning Routine)
**Focus**: Shoulder Endurance, Arm Swing Efficiency. (Keeps arms pumping at km 32+)
**Time**: 12–15 Minutes | **Equipment**: Resistance Band

| Exercise | Instructions | Sets/Reps | Rest |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Banded Rows** | Standing on band, pull back to hips. Focus on posture. | 2 x 15-20 | 30s |
| **Running Arms (Simulated)** | Standing, use light bands to simulate aggressive arm swing. | 2 x 60 seconds | 45s |
| **Shoulder Taps** | In high plank, tap opposite shoulder without moving hips. | 2 x 12 / side | 30s |
| **Banded Face Pulls** | Pull band toward forehead, elbows high and wide. | 2 x 15 | 30s |

## 🛡️ Smart Plank Selector (Post-Run Core Safety)
**Auto-select your core finisher based on how tired your legs are.**

| IF you just finished... | AND your Fatigue level is... | DO THIS Plank Variation (Lower Back Safety) | Why? |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Long Run (25km+)** | **High** (Legs shaking) | **Dead Bugs** (3 sets x 12 reps) | Lying on your back protects the spine when tired. |
| **Intervals / Tempo** | **Medium** (Winded) | **Side Planks** (3 sets x 30s) | Targets obliques without sagging the lower back. |
| **Easy Run** | **Low** (Fresh) | **Plank w/ Shoulder Taps** (3 sets x 20) | Adds instability to build dynamic strength. |
| **Recovery Run** | **Very High** (Exhausted) | **SKIP CORE** | Rest is better than bad form. |

> [!WARNING]
> **Safety Check**: If your lower back "sags" or hurts during a plank, **STOP IMMEDIATELY**. Switch to Dead Bugs.

1. **Couch Stretch (Hip Flexors)**: 90s each side. *Crucial for runners who sit all day.*
2. **Pigeon Pose (Glutes)**: 2 mins each side.
3. **Cat-Cow (Spine)**: 12 slow repetitions.
4. **Downward Dog (Calves/Hamstrings)**: Hold for 60s, "pedaling" the feet.
5. **Wall Slide (Upper Back)**: 15 reps to open up chest for better breathing.

## 📈 Progression & Form Tips
- **Form First**: If your form breaks, stop the rep. Quality > Quantity.
- **Micro-Progressions**: For Banded exercises, move to a heavier band once you can easily complete 20 reps with perfect form.
- **Listen to Training Fatigue**: If your legs feel heavy before a Tempo run, do 1 set of stability only instead of 3.

---

**Target Goal**: Consistency. Doing 15 minutes twice a week is better than one 60-minute session once a month.
